# Govindaraja Perumal — Thiruchitrakoodam, Chidambaram

Master list confirmed. Shrine inside the Thillai Nataraja temple complex, Chidambaram, Cuddalore district.

- One of the 108 Divya Desams (Chola Nadu); also called Thiruchitrakoodam / Thillai Thiruchitrakoodam.
- Moolavar: Govindaraja Perumal, reclining; tradition says he looks toward Nataraja's shrine, having witnessed/judged the dance contest of Shiva and Parvati.
- Consort: Pundarikavalli Thayar, separate shrine.
- Alvars: Kulasekhara Alvar and Thirumangai Alvar (Dinamalar). Kulasekhara's verses on Thillai Chitrakoodam recount the Rama story (wisdomlib essay "Temples and Cult of Shri Rama in Tamil-Nadu"). Pasuram counts not verified.
- Coordinates (Wikipedia): 11°23′58″N 79°41′36″E → 11.3994, 79.6933.
- History: TN Temples Project — installed by the Pallava king Nandivarman (mid–late 8th c.). Tradition: in the 12th c. under Kulothunga Chola II the image was removed; the festival image was carried to Tirupati (tradition associates Ramanuja), where Govindaraja is still worshipped; the shrine was reinstated in the mid-16th c. under Krishnappa Nayak. Present structure largely 12th–13th c. with later additions (sources differ).
- Tank: Pundarika Pushkarini (per temple blogs).
- Festivals: 10-day Chithirai Vasantha Utsavam (Apr–May); Gajendra Moksham.
- Vimana / gopuram name not verified.
