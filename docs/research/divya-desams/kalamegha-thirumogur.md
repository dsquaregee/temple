# Kalamegha Perumal Temple, Thirumohur (Thirumogur)

Divya Desam, Pandya Nadu. Master list confirmed (Thirumohur, Madurai district).

Research note: WebFetch blocked and the shared search budget ran out; facts from search-result
text of the sources below. Coordinates are approximate (not verified from an infobox).

## Deity
- Vishnu as Kalamegha Perumal ("the dark rain-cloud lord"); consort Mohanavalli Thayar in a
  separate shrine. Temple also called Mohanapuram / Mohana Kshetram.
- Separate shrine of Chakrathalvar (Sudarshana): sixteen arms bearing sixteen weapons, with
  Narasimha (Yoga Narasimha) carved on the back of the same image.

## Alvars
- Nammalvar sang the temple (Tiruvaimozhi decad on Thirumogur — refuge in the lord protects
  from demonic threats). Thirumangai Alvar is also commonly listed (not confirmed this session).

## Location
- Thirumohur village near Othakadai, roughly 10–12 km north-east of Madurai.
- Coordinates (approximate): 9.960 N, 78.218 E.

## Legends
- Vishnu took the form of Mohini here to draw away the asuras so the devas received the amrita
  (Wikipedia, AstroVed). Another account: a drop of amrita fell into the tank, and the lord
  appeared on its southern side. Another: Brahma worshipped here after Vishnu recovered the
  Vedas from Madhu and Kaitabha.

## Festivals
- Standard Vaishnava calendar (Vaikunta Ekadashi etc.); the Chakrathalvar shrine draws
  devotees (specific festival dates not verified).
