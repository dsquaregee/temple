# Loganatha Perumal Temple, Thirukannangudi

Divya Desam, Chola Nadu. Master list name/place confirmed (near Sikkal).

## Facts
- Deities: moolavar Loganatha Perumal with Loganayagi; utsavar Damodara Narayana Perumal with Aravindavalli.
- Coordinates (Wikipedia): 10°45′23.7″N 79°45′48.7″E → 10.7566, 79.7635. About 2 km from Sikkal, off the Tiruvarur–Nagapattinam highway.
- Alvar: Thirumangai Alvar, 10 pasurams (Periya Thirumozhi 1748–1757).
- One of the Pancha Krishnaranya Kshetrams (also listed among Pancha Narayana kshetrams).
- History: Wikipedia — built by the Medieval Cholas in the late 9th century (Astroved says 8th).
- Architecture: east-facing; five-tiered rajagopuram; Utpala Vimanam; tank Shravana Theertham (also Nithya Pushkarini per TN Temples Project; Astroved says the tank lies outside the complex).
- Sthala vriksham: Magizha tree, said never to die; seen behind the temple.
- Legends: Sage Vasishta worshipped a butter image of Krishna that did not melt; Krishna came as a child and swallowed it; Vasishta pursued him here, where rishis in penance (or Vasishta) bound the child to the Magizha tree by the power of devotion. The utsava image shows Krishna bound to the tree with a rope (Damodara). Thirumangai Alvar, tired and thirsty, rested under the Magizha tree and was given food and water by the Lord, who appeared bearing conch and discus and vanished.

## Not verified
- Festival calendar (only generic Vaishnava observances assumed: Vaikunta Ekadashi); the four "wonders" of Thirukannangudi sayings.
