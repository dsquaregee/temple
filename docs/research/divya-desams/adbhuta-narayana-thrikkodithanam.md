# Adbhuta Narayana Temple, Thrikkodithanam (Thirukkadithanam) — Malai Nadu Divya Desam

- Presiding deity: Adbhuta Narayana (Mahavishnu), black-stone image; sung by Nammalvar. Tradition links the shrine to Sahadeva, one of five Pandava-associated temples of the region.
- Coordinates: Wikipedia 9°26'17"N 76°33'43"E (9.4381, 76.5620); Kerala Tourism 9.43812, 76.56202. Used: 9.438, 76.562 (the earlier draft's 9.453/76.557 was corrected).
- Location: Kottayam district, near Changanassery (nearest railway station).
- Age: sources conflict (7th c. per Kerala Tourism, ~9th c., c. 800 AD); 8th century used as an approximate Alvar-era attribution.
- Unverified: Vattezhuthu inscriptions, detailed festival dates (stated in the English entry from the earlier draft; treat as tradition).
