# Ulagalantha (Trivikrama) Perumal Temple, Tirukoilur (Tirukoyilur), Kallakurichi district

Region: Nadu Nadu. Master-list name/place confirmed.

## Deities
- Moolavar: Ulagalantha Perumal — Vishnu as Trivikrama, the form that measured the worlds.
- Thayar: Poongothai (Wikipedia); the government festival listing names Pushpavalli (same meaning, "flower") and also lists an Andal utsavam.

## Alvars
- Poigai, Bhoothath and Pey Alvar (the Mudhal Alvars) — tradition holds Vishnu appeared to them here and that they sang the first three Thiruvandhadhis of the Nalayira Divya Prabandham here.
- Thirumangai Alvar also praised the deity.
- Pasuram count: not confirmed this session.

## Location
- Coordinates: temple 11.96694 N, 79.20194 E (Wikipedia infobox); town 11.9675, 79.2056. Used 11.967, 79.202.
- Tirukoilur town, on the Thenpennai (Pennaiyar) river.

## History
- Believed built by the Medieval Cholas, with later contributions by the Vijayanagara kings and Madurai Nayaks (Wikipedia). (One listing claims Pallava beginnings — not in Wikipedia; omitted.)

## Architecture
- Rajagopuram 192 ft (59 m), described by Wikipedia as the third-tallest temple tower in Tamil Nadu. ("Second tallest" claims not supported.)
- Vimana and tank names: not confirmed this session.

## Legends
- Vamana–Trivikrama: Vishnu, asking Mahabali for three paces, grew to measure earth and sky (standard Puranic story of the form).
- The Mudhal Alvars: on a night of rain the three Alvars met in a narrow shelter and sensed a fourth presence pressing among them — the Lord. Tradition holds Poigai's verse lit a lamp with the world as its bowl and the sea as its oil, Bhoothath's with love as the lamp, and Pey Alvar then beheld the Lord (opening verses of the three Thiruvandhadhis — well-known tradition; not separately sourced this session).

## Festivals
- Panguni Brahmotsavam (utsav.gov.in listing).
- Andal utsavam (utsav.gov.in listing).
