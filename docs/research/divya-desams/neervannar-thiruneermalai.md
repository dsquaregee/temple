# Neervannar / Ranganatha Temple, Thiruneermalai — research notes

Region: Thondai Nadu. One of the 108 Divya Desams. Suburb of south-west Chennai
(Chengalpattu district), near Pallavaram.

Note: research via search-result text only.

## Deities — four postures
- Foot of the hill: Neervanna Perumal (Neervannar), standing; consort Animamalar Mangai
  Thayar; shrines of Kalyana Rama and Andal.
- Hilltop: Ranganatha reclining (Ranga Vimana over the sanctum), Ranganayaki Thayar; Shanta
  Narasimha seated; Ulagalanda Perumal (Trivikrama) striding/walking.
- Hilltop shrine faces east (one source).

## Alvars
- Thirumangai Alvar and Bhoothathalvar. Pasuram count not confirmed (one source mentions
  20 in total) — omitted from copy.

## Location
- Coordinates (Wikipedia, via search text): 12°57′49″N 80°06′53″E → 12.9636, 80.1147.

## History
- Founding date and dynasty not confirmed in search results; no inscription data found.
  App copy uses century 8 (Alvar hymns) and avoids dynastic claims.

## Architecture
- Two temples: base temple and hilltop temple, reached by a long flight of steps (count
  varies by source: ~200–260) — given as "a long flight of steps".
- Vimana (hilltop): Ranga Vimana.
- Tanks named on Wikipedia: Kshira, Karunya, Svarna and Siddha Pushkarini.

## Legend
- Tradition: Thirumangai Alvar came to worship but the hill was ringed by floodwater; he
  waited for months until the waters receded — hence "Thiru-neer-malai" (holy water-hill).

## Festivals
- Brahmotsavam (month not confirmed); Vaikunta Ekadashi.
