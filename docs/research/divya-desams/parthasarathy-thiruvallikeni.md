# Parthasarathy Temple, Thiruvallikeni (Triplicane, Chennai) — research notes

Region: Thondai Nadu. One of the 108 Divya Desams.

Note: research via search-result text only (direct fetches failed; search budget ran out
before a coordinate check). Coordinates below are the Wikipedia infobox values as known
before this session — **not re-verified by a search in this session**.

## Deity
- Presiding: Krishna as Parthasarathy, "charioteer of Partha (Arjuna)", depicted with a
  moustache; tradition: holds only the conch (no discus), and the utsavar's face is said to
  bear the scars of arrows from Kurukshetra.
- Consort: Vedavalli Thayar (shrine parallel to the sanctum; one source dates the present
  Thayar shrine to the later 19th century).
- Five forms of Vishnu enshrined: Parthasarathy (Krishna), Rama, Yoga Narasimha,
  Gajendra Varadaraja, Ranganatha.

## Alvars
- Peyalvar, Thirumazhisai Alvar, Thirumangai Alvar. Pasuram count not confirmed — omitted.

## Location
- Car Street, Triplicane, Chennai, near the Marina.
- Coordinates: 13.0539, 80.2770 (Wikipedia infobox 13°03′14″N 80°16′37″E — from prior
  knowledge, not re-verified).

## History
- Pallava foundation; dates differ (Wikipedia infobox 6th c.; text: present temple built by a
  Pallava king in the 8th c.).
- Inscription of the Pallava king Dantivarman (r. c. 796–847 CE) in the temple; Thirumangai
  Alvar's hymn describes the Pallava-era temple.

## Architecture
- Tank: Kairavini Pushkarini, named for the kairavini (lily/lotus); opposite the east entrance;
  said to contain five wells.
- Separate shrines for Vedavalli Thayar, Yoga Narasimha, Rama, Varadaraja, Ranganatha.

## Festivals
- Chithirai Brahmotsavam; the car runs twice — for Parthasarathy and for Yoga Narasimha.
- Masi Theppam (float festival) on the Kairavini tank.
- Vaikunta Ekadashi.
