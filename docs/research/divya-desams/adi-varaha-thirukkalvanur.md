# Thirukkalvanur — Adi Varaha (Kalvar) Perumal shrine, Kanchipuram — research note

- **Not a separate temple**: Thirukkalvanur is a Vishnu shrine inside the Kanchi Kamakshi Amman temple, on the right side of the Goddess's (Ambal's) sanctum. Region: Thondai Nadu; one of the 108 Divya Desams.
- Moolavar: Adi Varaha Perumal, locally the Kalvar / Kalvan Perumal; standing, facing west.
- Consort: Anjilai Valli Nachiyar (Anjilaivalli Thayar).
- Mangalasasanam: Thirumangai Alvar, 1 pasuram.
- Vimana: Vamana Vimanam; tank: Nithya Pushkarini (as listed by devotional references).
- Coordinates: shrine lies within the Kamakshi Amman temple; used the repo's kanchi-kamakshi coordinates 12.8400, 79.7036.
- Legend (versions differ): name from Tamil *kalvan*, "thief". (a) Lakshmi fondly called the lord "Kalva" when he came to her secretly; (b) Vishnu hid behind a pillar and overheard Kamakshi and Lakshmi, and Kamakshi noticed him.
- Not verified (omitted): festival calendar specific to the shrine; dating; some sources' "54th Divya Desam" numbering (orders vary); "Ashwattha Narayana" pratyaksham (unclear).
