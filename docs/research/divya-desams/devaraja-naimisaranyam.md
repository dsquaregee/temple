# Thiru Naimisaranyam — Devaraja Perumal (Naimisharanya, Sitapur, Uttar Pradesh)

Vada Nadu Divya Desam. Assigned id: `devaraja-naimisaranyam`. Master list correct.

## Facts
- Thirumangai Alvar sang Naimisaranyam in a decad of ten pasurams, Periya Thirumozhi 1.6, in
  mangalasasanam of Devarajan and his consort Sri Harilakshmi on the banks of the Gomudhi
  (Gomti) (ramanuja.org; sadagopan.org). The decad is noted for the Alvar's mood of nirvedham
  — remorse and self-surrender (sadagopan.org).
- In the tradition the forest itself is worshipped as the Lord (aranya form) (ramanuja.org).
- The temple at Naimisharanya: Shri Naimishnath Vishnu Temple; deity Naimishnath / Devaraja
  Perumal; consort Pundarikavalli Thayar (also Naimish-Lakshmi) per Wikipedia; Harilakshmi per
  Sri Vaishnava write-ups — text names Harilakshmi and notes Pundarikavalli.
- Counted among the eight Swayam Vyakta (self-manifested) kshetras of Vishnu (Wikipedia).
- Chakra Kund / Chakra Tirtha: circular tank, water from an underground source, revered as
  where Vishnu's Sudarshana chakra touched the earth; pilgrims bathe on festival days
  (Wikipedia; ISKCON Desire Tree).
- Traditions (general, phrased as tradition): the sages led by Shaunaka gathered here and heard
  the Puranas/Mahabharata recited by the bard Suta; the wheel (nemi) sent by Brahma wore out
  here, giving the name. Not confirmed in this session's search text.
- Festivals: chiefly bathing days at the Chakra Tirtha (Somavati Amavasya) and the Phalguna
  parikrama of the sacred area — general knowledge, phrased cautiously.
- Location: Sitapur district, on the Gomti; reached by road from Lucknow and Sitapur.
- Coordinates: approximate 27.347, 80.488 (Naimisharanya). **Approximate — verify.**
- century: 8.
