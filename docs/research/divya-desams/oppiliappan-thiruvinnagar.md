## Facts
- Divya Desam of Chola Nadu (60th in common listing), about 7 km from Kumbakonam near Thirunageswaram. [1][2]
- Presiding deity: Oppiliappan / Uppiliappan, also Venkatachalapathy, Srinivasan, Thiruvinnagarappan,
  Thanoppillaappan; consort Bhudevi (Bhoomi Devi). [1]
- Other names: Thiruvinnagar, Vaikunta Nagaram, Markandeya Kshetram, Tulasi Vanam. [1]
- Vimana: Suddhananda Vimanam. Tank: Ahoratra Pushkarini. [1]
- Alvars: Nammalvar, Peyalvar and Thirumangai Alvar (pasuram total not verified). [3][4]
- Legend: Bhumi Devi was raised as the daughter of the sage Markandeya, found near a tulasi plant;
  Vishnu came as an old man seeking her hand; Markandeya protested she was too young even to know how
  much salt to use in cooking; the Lord replied he would accept food without salt — hence offerings are
  prepared without salt, and the name Oppiliappan ("the peerless lord"). [1][4]
- History: origins traced to the Medieval Chola period; one source mentions gifts recorded from Chola
  kings (Rajaraja I, Rajendra I); dating otherwise uncertain; later Nayak additions. [4][5]
- Festivals: Sravanam (monthly star), the wedding festival (Thirukkalyanam) on Sravanam in Aippasi,
  Rama Navami, Vaikunta Ekadashi. [4][6]
- The Saiva Naganathar temple (Navagraha Rahu shrine) is in the same town. [2]
- Approximate coordinates used: 10.954, 79.430 (UNVERIFIED — town centre ~10.96, 79.43).
