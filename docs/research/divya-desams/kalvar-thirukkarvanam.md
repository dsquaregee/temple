# Thirukkarvanam — Kalvar Perumal (within the Ulagalantha Perumal temple, Kanchipuram)

Thondai Nadu Divya Desam. Assigned id: `kalvar-thirukkarvanam`. Master list correct
(master list also gives "Neelamega" as an alternate name — not confirmed in this session's
sources, so not used). One of the four Divya Desams in the Ulagalantha Perumal complex.

## Facts
- Presiding deity: Kalvar (also Karvanar / Karvanna Perumal), standing; consort Kamalavalli
  Nachiyar (Thamaraiyal) (acharya.org via search snippet; templefolks; incredibleindia).
- Facing: sources conflict (west vs north) — omitted.
- Shrine in the second precinct of the Ulagalantha temple (incredibleindia.gov.in).
- Tank: Gowri Thadagam (Gowri Theertham); Vimanam: Pushkala Vimanam (search snippet of
  acharya.org).
- A source notes details of the original temple are not known except, from Thirumangai Alvar's
  pasurams, that the Lord is Kalvar, standing, with Kamalavalli Nachiyar (temples-india/
  acharya snippet). Shrine said to have been worshipped by Parvati (Gowri).
- Legend (attested on a Kanchipuram temple page): Parvati saw Vishnu overhearing her
  conversation with Lakshmi and called him "Kalvan" (the stealthy one / thief) — phrased as
  tradition.
- Mangalasasanam: Thirumangai Alvar; count not confirmed.
- Coordinates: host temple, 12.8392, 79.7050.
