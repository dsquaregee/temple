# Koodal Azhagar Temple, Madurai (Thirukkoodal)

Divya Desam, Pandya Nadu. Master list confirmed.

Research note: WebFetch blocked and the shared search budget ran out; facts from search-result
text of the sources below. Coordinates from memory of the Wikipedia infobox — approximate.

## Deity
- Vishnu as Koodal Azhagar (seated in the sanctum); consort Mathuravalli Thayar.
- Ashtanga vimana in tiers: the lord seated at ground level, Surya Narayana standing above, and
  the reclining (Pallikonda) Perumal on an upper level, which can be viewed by climbing up.

## Alvars
- Periyalvar — tradition holds he composed the Thiruppallandu here. Other Alvars (Thirumangai
  etc.) commonly listed; not verified this session.

## Location
- In central Madurai, south-west of the Meenakshi Amman temple.
- Coordinates (approximate): 9.914 N, 78.114 E.

## Legends
- Vallabhadeva, a Pandya king who went about incognito to know his people, set a learned
  assembly to declare the supreme deity; Periyalvar (Vishnuchittar) proved Vishnu's supremacy
  and the lord appeared on Garuda; Periyalvar, fearing the evil eye on such beauty, sang
  Thiruppallandu (tradition; full story truncated in search text, completed from the widely
  told version).
- Other accounts: Vishnu slew the demon Somuka who stole the Vedas; four clouds gathered (koodal)
  to shield the city from a deluge — a folk etymology of Koodal (Madurai).

## Festivals
- Vaikunta Ekadashi and the Vaishnava calendar; specific festival months not verified.
