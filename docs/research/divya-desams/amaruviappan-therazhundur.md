# Devadirajan (Amaruviappan) Perumal Temple, Therazhundur

Divya Desam of Chola Nadu. Research note for `amaruviappan-therazhundur`.
(WebFetch unavailable; facts rely on WebSearch result text.)

- **Deities**: moolavar Devadirajan, standing (nindra thirukkolam), facing east; utsavar Amaruviappan, standing with a cow and calf beside him and his two consorts; Thayar Sengamalavalli. Garuda stands beside the lord within the sanctum.
- **Mangalasasanam**: Thirumangai Alvar — 45 pasurams per Dharmawiki infobox ("not less than 45"); single source, count stated cautiously.
- **Coordinates**: 11°02′47″N 79°34′46″E → **11.0464, 79.5794** (Wikipedia). Village near Kuthalam, Mayiladuthurai district.
- **History**: Wikipedia — believed (tradition) to have been built by Karikala Chola, with later additions. The village is honoured as the birthplace of Kambar, author of the Tamil Ramayana.
- **Architecture**: Garuda vimanam (tradition: given its name after Garuda, who received a vimana and diamond crown from Indra); tank Darshana Pushkarini, opposite the temple outside the main entrance. A separate Shiva temple (Vedapureeswarar) stands in the same village.
- **Legends**: Brahma hid the cows of Ayarpadi (the cowherd settlement); Krishna created new herds; Brahma repented and asked Krishna to appear here, which he did as Amaruviappan with cow and calf. The Kaveri did penance here to be freed from a curse of sage Agastya. Thirumangai Alvar, mistaking the shrine, began to leave, was halted and returned to sing the lord.
- **Festivals**: Brahmotsavam in Vaikasi; Andal festival in Aadi; Thirumangai Alvar's festival and Deepa utsavam in Karthigai; Garuda Sevai in Thai.
