# Sowmya Narayana Perumal Temple, Thirukoshtiyur

Divya Desam, Pandya Nadu. Master list confirmed (Thirukoshtiyur, Sivaganga district).

Research note: WebFetch blocked and the shared search budget ran out; facts from search-result
text. Coordinates approximate.

## Deity
- Vishnu as Sowmya Narayana Perumal; consort Thirumamagal (Lakshmi).
- Ashtanga vimana in three levels: reclining moolavar (ground), standing Devaloka Perumal (second),
  seated Vaikunta Perumal (third). The vimana rises taller than the five-tiered rajagopuram.

## Alvars
- Several Alvars (commonly Periyalvar, Bhoothathalvar, Peyalvar, Thirumazhisai and Thirumangai) —
  not verified this session; text says only "sung by the Alvars".

## Ramanuja
- Thirukachi Nambi sent Ramanuja to Thirukoshtiyur Nambi to learn the Ashtakshara mantra;
  Ramanuja was turned away seventeen times, was taught on his return and asked to keep it secret,
  then proclaimed it from the temple to all people regardless of caste. Shrines of Thirukoshtiyur
  Nambi and Ramanuja in the temple.

## Location
- Thirukoshtiyur, near Tiruppattur, Sivaganga district. Coordinates (approx.): 10.066 N, 78.555 E.

## Legend
- (Tradition) The name is told as the place where the devas gathered (koshti) to deliberate.
