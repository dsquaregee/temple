# Thirumeyyam — Sathyagirinathan / Sathyamurthi Perumal, Thirumayam (Pudukkottai)

## Facts
- Divya Desam of the Pandya Nadu group; glorified in the Nalayira Divya Prabandham; mangalasasanam by **Thirumangai Alvar** (pasuram count not confirmed).
- Deity: Vishnu as **Sathyamurthi Perumal**, also called **Sathyagiri Nathan**; consort **Uyyavandha Nachiyar** (Ujeevana Thayar).
- Cave sanctum: rock-cut cave on the **south face of Satyagiri hill** (Thirumayam hill). Relief of **Anantasayi Vishnu reclining on Adisesha**, Lakshmi on his chest; relief c. 15 ft long (travel blog). An eka-tala Dravidian vimana over the sanctum.
- A neighbouring rock-cut **Shiva cave** (Sathyagirisvarar) on the same hill, a Pandyan excavation of the 7th–8th c.
- Dating: the Vishnu cave temple dated to the closing decades of the 7th c.; attributed to the **Muttaraiyars**; an inscription records its renovation and an endowment by **Perumbidugu Perundevi**, mother of Sattan Maran (Videlvidugu Viluperadiaraiyan), a vassal of the Pallava king **Nandivarman II** (731–796 CE).
- Later, mostly **late-Pandya mandapas** were built in front, through which the cave sanctum is now reached.
- The hill also carries Thirumayam Fort; a large rock inscription on music lies at the site.
- Legend: the sage **Satya** did penance here; tradition says Vishnu turned the river into the pushkarani, the rock into the Meyyam hill and a banyan into the sacred fig (asvattha). Name derived from Thiru-Meyyam ("hill of truth").
- Coordinates (Wikipedia infobox): 10°14′49″N 78°45′07″E → **10.247, 78.752**.
- Festivals: not confirmed by sources this session; entry refers only to the general Vaishnava calendar (Vaikunta Ekadashi) and processional festivals.
