# Jagannatha Perumal Temple, Nathan Koil (Thiru Nandipura Vinnagaram)

Divya Desam of Chola Nadu. Research note for `jagannatha-nathankoil`.
(WebFetch unavailable; facts rely on WebSearch result text.)

- **Deities**: Jagannathan (Jagannatha Perumal), seated; consort Shenbagavalli Thayar. Facing: sources conflict (west vs east); one account says the lord turned west to watch King Sibi — content avoids stating direction as fact.
- **Mangalasasanam**: Thirumangai Alvar, ten hymns (Wikipedia: "revered ... by Thirumangai Azhwar in ten hymns").
- **Coordinates**: Wikipedia's infobox as returned by search reads 11°10′39″N 79°46′45″E, which places it ~45 km east of Kumbakonam and is inconsistent with every description ("near Kumbakonam"). **Used an approximate position 10.934, 79.348** (village near Kumbakonam, close to Pazhaiyarai/Muzhaiyur). NEEDS VERIFICATION.
- **Architecture**: five-tiered rajagopuram; Mandara vimanam; temple tank Nandi Theertham.
- **History**: attribution conflicts — one source credits the Pallava king Nandivarman, another tradition King Sibi. Content presents both as tradition only.
- **Legends**: Nandi, Shiva's mount, slighted Vishnu's dvarapalakas and was cursed to suffer burning heat; on Shiva's advice he did penance to Vishnu in the Shenbagaranyam (forest of shenbagam flowers), where Lakshmi too had done penance to win a place on Vishnu's chest (hence Shenbagavalli). Relieved here, Nandi gave the place its name, Nandipuram; the tank and shrines bear his name. Sibi legend (protecting a pigeon from a hawk) is linked to the temple.
- **Festivals**: Wikipedia notes festivals celebrated through the year; no specific calendar confirmed.
