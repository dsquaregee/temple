# Thiru Singavel Kundram (Ahobilam) — Ahobila Narasimha (Ahobilam, Nandyal, Andhra Pradesh)

Vada Nadu Divya Desam. Assigned id: `narasimha-ahobilam`. Master list correct.

## Facts
- Sung by Thirumangai Alvar as "Singavel Kundram" in a decad of ten pasurams, Periya
  Thirumozhi 1.7 (between 1.6 Naimisaranyam and 1.8 Thiruvenkatam) (sadagopan.org;
  divyaprabandham.koyil.org; ramanuja.org). The decad describes a wild hill of lions and
  hunters, and the Lord present there to protect devotees, recalling Prahlada.
- First mentioned in Thirumangai's 9th-c. Periya Thirumozhi (Wikipedia, per search text).
- Nava Narasimha kshetra: the Upper Ahobilam (Ahobila / Ugra Narasimha) shrine is the main
  one; the others in the hills — Bhargava, Yogananda, Chatravata, Karanja, Pavana, Malola,
  Varaha (Kroda) and Jvala Narasimha. Lower Ahobilam's Prahladavarada (Lakshmi Narasimha)
  temple is treated as a separate, later shrine (Wikipedia; Folia Orientalia article).
- Patronage: Vijayanagara rulers, from the Saluva dynasty (15th c.) and Tuluva dynasty
  (16th c.); Krishnadevaraya visited and patronised the shrines (Wikipedia).
- History before 16th c. described as obscure (Wikipedia); one source cites an 8th-c.
  Chalukya inscription — not used.
- Ahobila Matha, the Sri Vaishnava monastic institution, was founded here in the medieval
  period (Wikipedia).
- Late-16th-c. raid: sources conflict on attacker and date (1578/1579) — not used.
- Traditions (general, phrased as tradition): Narasimha emerged from a pillar to slay
  Hiranyakashipu and save Prahlada here; the gods cried "Aho balam!" (what strength!), giving
  the name; the Ugra Stambham crag is pointed out as the pillar; Narasimha wed Chenchu
  Lakshmi, a daughter of the forest-dwelling Chenchu people; Garuda's penance gives the hill
  the name Garudadri. Not confirmed in this session's search text — phrased as tradition.
- Festivals (general knowledge, phrased cautiously): annual Brahmotsavam; Narasimha Jayanti;
  the Paruveta festival in which the Lord is taken in procession to Chenchu villages after
  Sankranti. Not confirmed in search text.
- Location: Nandyal district (Kurnool district before the 2022 reorganisation); access via
  Allagadda; Nandyal nearest major railhead (general knowledge).
- Coordinates: approximate 15.130, 78.720 (Ahobilam village). **Approximate — verify.**
- century: 8 used (Thirumangai's hymns; Wikipedia dates him 9th c.) — 8 kept per brief default
  for Alvar-attested sites when nothing earlier is firm. Note: could be 9.
