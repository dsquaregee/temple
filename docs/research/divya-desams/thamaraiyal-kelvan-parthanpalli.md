# Thiruparthanpalli (Thamaraiyal Kelvan Perumal) — research notes

Master list entry confirmed (Wikipedia title "Thiruppaarththanpalli"; village Parthanpalli, near Thirunangur and Thiruvenkadu).

- Region: Chola Nadu; one of the 108 Divya Desams; one of the eleven Thirunangur Tirupatis.
- Moolavar: Thamaraiyal Kelvan Perumal ("consort of the lotus lady"). Utsavar: Parthasarathy.
- Thayar: Thamarai Nayagi (also given as Shenkamalavalli / Sengamalavalli).
- Mangalasasanam: Thirumangai Alvar; main festival the Thirumangai Alvar Mangalasasana Utsavam in Thai.
- Coordinates (Wikipedia infobox): 11.16972, 79.79722 (11°10′11″N 79°47′50″E).
- Vimanam: Narayana vimanam. Tank: Kadga (Katka) Pushkarini — "sword tank"; another source lists Sanka Saras / Ganga Theertham.
- Legend (Padma Purana per secondary sources): Arjuna, on pilgrimage, grew thirsty and asked the sage Agastya, who was in penance here, for water; Agastya had none; Krishna gave Arjuna a sword, with which he dug the earth and water gushed out (associated with the Ganga) — hence Kadga Pushkarini and the name Parthan-palli ("Partha's place"). Krishna is said to have shown himself as Parthasarathy here; Varuna and sages Agastya, Bharadvaja and Gautama are said to have done penance here.
- Location: about 8 km east of Sirkazhi (another source: ~12 km), on the way to Thiruvenkadu.
- Not verified: posture/facing, inscriptions, gopuram tiers, pasuram count.
