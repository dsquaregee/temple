# Thirukkadigai (Sholinghur) — Yoga Narasimha (Sholinghur, Ranipet, Tamil Nadu)

Thondai Nadu Divya Desam. Assigned id: `yoga-narasimha-sholinghur`. Master list correct.

## Facts
- One of the 108 Divya Desams; Vishnu worshipped as (Yoga) Narasimha, consort Amirthavalli
  (Amritavalli) Thayar (Wikipedia "Thirukkadigai"; greenmesg; divinetraveller).
- Also called Ghatikachalam: tradition holds Narasimha gave darshan to Prahlada and the
  Saptarishis here for the span of one ghatika (~24 minutes); hence the belief that worship
  for even a ghatika here bears fruit (Wikipedia; hindu-blog).
- Narasimha is seated in yogic posture, sanctum facing east (Wikipedia).
- Twin hills: Periya Malai (~750 ft / 230 m) with the Narasimha shrine; Chinna Malai
  (~350 ft) with the Yoga Anjaneya shrine. Legend: Narasimha asked Anjaneya (Hanuman) to help
  slay an asura, then to remain on the hill in yogic posture granting boons (Wikipedia).
- Steps: most sources give ~1,305 steps to the Periya Malai summit (some say 1,000–2,000);
  the text avoids a precise count.
- Mangalasasanam: Peyalvar and Thirumangai Alvar (Dinamalar cites Periya Tirumoli 8.9.4;
  also lists Nathamuni, Thirukkachi Nambi, Ramanuja, Manavala Mamunigal as associated).
  Pasuram count not confirmed — not used.
- Manavala Mamunigal is believed to have expounded the Thiruppavai here at the request of his
  disciple Erumbiappa (Wikipedia).
- Tank: sources differ (Thakkan Kulam per Dinamalar; Amirtha Theertham, Pandava Theertham also
  named). Text uses "Thakkan Kulam" only as a tank name at the foot of the hills — cautious.
- Vimana: one source names Hemakoti Vimanam — single source, not used.
- Karthigai month: widely held devotional tradition that the Lord, in yogic repose, opens his
  eyes in Karthigai; Karthigai Sundays draw large crowds — phrased as tradition (general
  knowledge; not confirmed in this session's search text).
- Town is in Ranipet district (earlier Vellore district — sources differ by date).
- Coordinates: search gave conflicting values (Bharatpedia 13.07, 79.25 appears wrong for the
  town). Used approximate Sholinghur location 13.118, 79.420 (town); the hill temple lies just
  outside the town. **Approximate — verify against the Wikipedia infobox.**
- Sholinghur also known for the 1781 Battle of Sholinghur (not used — not devotional).
- century: 8 (Alvar hymns; Peyalvar is among the earliest Alvars).
