# Devanatha Perumal Temple, Thiruvahindrapuram (Thiruvanthipuram), Cuddalore

Region: Nadu Nadu. Master-list name/place confirmed (spelling variants: Thiruvaheendrapuram, Thiruvanthipuram).

## Deities
- Moolavar: Devanathan (Deivanayakan), standing, facing east.
- Utsavar: Moovaragiya Oruvan — "the One who is the Three" (Brahma, Vishnu, Shiva); Thirumangai Alvar hails the lord as the godhead of the Trinity.
- Thayar: Hemambujavalli (Hemabhujavalli), also called Vaikuntanayaki; also Senkamala Thayar in some sources.

## Alvars
- Thirumangai Alvar — 10 pasurams, Periya Thirumozhi 3.1 (ramanuja.org; Wikipedia).

## Location
- 11°44′41″N 79°42′32″E → **11.7447, 79.7089** (Wikipedia infobox). Village infobox gives 11°44′49″N 79°42′40″E.
- On the Kedilam river (locally Uttaravahini), near Cuddalore.

## History
- Dravidian style; believed built under the Medieval Cholas, later expanded under the Pandyas (Wikipedia).
- Rajaraja I inscription on the ardhamandapa (damaged) names the place Thiru-ayindapuram; another of his reign records a village granted as devadana and brahmadeya; an 11th-c. record of a land gift by Trilokamadevi, chief queen of Rajaraja I; a Rajaraja III inscription in the western prakara records a village gift (Shanlax journal study, 2018).
- Vedanta Desika spent some of the most eventful years of his life here; the house where he lived is preserved as Desikan Tirumaligai; a well he dug by hand survives; the Desika image worshipped in the temple is held to have been cast by him (ramanuja.org).

## Architecture
- Five-tier rajagopuram about 60 ft (18 m); three prakarams.
- Vimana carries images of Vishnu (east), Dakshinamurthy (south), Narasimha (west), Brahma (north); compared to the Suddha Satva vimana of Vaikuntha.
- Tanks: Sesha Theertham (created by Adisesha); Garuda's stream (Garudanadhi / Vajra Theertham).
- Hayagriva shrine on the adjacent Aushadagiri hillock — said to be the only historical hilltop Hayagriva shrine in South India; Desika is believed to have worshipped Garuda there and received Hayagriva.

## Legends
- Adisesha is said to have planned and founded the town (Ahindra-puram, "city of the lord of serpents").
- When the Lord thirsted, Garuda ploughed the earth with his beak to bring the Garudanadhi; Adisesha cut a channel and returned first; the Lord drank Adisesha's water; to console Garuda he uses Garudanadhi water for thirumanjanam and Sesha Theertham water for naivedyam.

## Festivals
- Ten-day Brahmotsavam in Chittirai (April–May): Garuda Seva on day 5, Theerthavari on day 9 (crowds can exceed a lakh).
- Vedanta Desika's tirunakshatram utsavam in Purattasi.
- Vaikunta Ekadashi (30-day festival), Masi Magam sea bath at Devanampattinam, Adi Pooram, Narasimha Jayanthi, Aavani Pavitrotsavam, Thiru Karthigai.
