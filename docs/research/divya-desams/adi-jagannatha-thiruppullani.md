# Thiruppullani — Adi Jagannatha Perumal, Thiruppullani (Ramanathapuram)

## Facts
- Divya Desam of Pandya Nadu; village on the outskirts of Ramanathapuram.
- Mangalasasanam by **Thirumangai Alvar — 20 pasurams** in the Periya Thirumozhi.
- Deities: **Adi Jagannatha Perumal** (also "Dakshina Jagannathan", contrasted with Jagannatha of Puri) under the **Kalyana vimanam**; consort **Padmasini Thayar** (also given as Kalyanavalli). **Darbhasayana Rama** under the Swastika vimanam; **Pattabhi Rama** also enshrined.
- Tank: **Chakra Theertham** in front of the temple (link to Sudarshana); also Hema/Ratnakara theertham (the sea).
- History: believed built late 8th c.; contributions from Medieval Cholas, later Pandyas and the **Sethupathi** kings of Ramnad. A mutilated inscription in the 37th year of **Maravarman Sundara Pandya** (c. 1305) records an order of a minister named Arya Chakravarthy; some portions of the tower may have been built by the Jaffna kings (historians' view).
- Name: Tamil *pul* (grass) + *anai* (bed) → Thiruppullanai → Thiruppullani.
- Legends: sages **Pullar, Galavar (Kalavar), Kanvar** did penance in a darbha forest; Vishnu appeared as a sacred fig (asvattha) and then as Adi Jagannatha. **Rama** lay on darbha grass for three days (some say seven) beseeching the sea-king for passage to Lanka; when the sea did not answer he took up his bow, and the sea-king came and submitted. **Dasaratha** is said to have prayed here for offspring.
- Festivals: **Panguni Brahmotsavam**; Rama Navami / Rama's birth festival in **Chithirai**.
- Coordinates (Wikipedia infobox): 9°16′58″N 78°49′28″E → **9.283, 78.824**.
