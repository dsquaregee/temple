# Thiruppiridi — Paramapurushan, identified with Joshimath (Uttarakhand)

Assigned id: `paramapurusha-thiruppiridi`.

**Identification note:** Thiruppiridi is identified with Joshimath (Jyotirmath) in
Sri Vaishnava pilgrim guides and the sources found, and pilgrims worship at the
Narasimha (Narsingh) and Vasudeva shrines there. The identification is traditional
rather than settled: the deity described in Divya Desam lists (Paramapurushan) does
not match the seated Narasimha of the Joshimath temple, sources disagree on the
image (standing Vasudeva vs reclining Vishnu), and some sources mention Nandaprayag
ambiguously. The app copy presents Joshimath as the place "traditionally identified"
with Thiruppiridi.

## Identity
- Moolavar: Paramapurushan; Thayar: Parimalavalli. Vimanam: Govardhana Vimanam; theertham: Indra Theertham.
- Alvar: Thirumangai Alvar — Periya Thirumozhi 1.2 (verses 1.2.1 and 1.2.6 cited; a decad of ten).
- Region: Vada Nadu.
- Joshimath's Narasimha temple is credited by tradition to Adi Shankara; Joshimath is the seat of Jyotirmath, one of the four mathas attributed to him *(general reference)*, and the winter seat of Badrinarayan's worship *(source text ambiguous; general reference)*.

## Location
- Joshimath, Chamoli district, on the Rishikesh–Badrinath road, ~6,200 ft per one source (≈1,890 m).
- Coordinates used: 30.5550, 79.5650 *(unverified; town-level)*.

## History / architecture / festivals
