# Thiru Dwaraka — Dwarakadhish / Kalyana Narayanan (Dwarka, Gujarat)

Vada Nadu Divya Desam. Assigned id: `dwarakadhish-dwarka`. Master list correct.

## Facts
- Dwarkadhish Temple, also Jagat Mandir; main shrine (Jagat / Nija Mandir) is a five-storeyed
  building on 72 pillars (Wikipedia).
- Archaeological findings suggest an original structure no earlier than c. 200 BCE; the
  present temple was rebuilt and enlarged in the 15th–16th centuries (Wikipedia).
- Coordinates (Wikipedia infobox): 22°14′16.39″N 68°58′3.22″E → 22.238, 68.968.
- Presiding deity: Krishna as Dwarakadhish, "king of Dwarka"; Sri Vaishnava listings name the
  Divya Desam lord Kalyana Narayanan (from master list; not independently confirmed in search).
  Rukmini has her own temple outside the town (Wikipedia Dwarka article lists Rukmini Devi
  Temple, Gomti Ghat, Bet Dwarka).
- The temple stands above the Gomti creek; Sudama Setu crosses the Gomti (Gujarat Tourism).
- Divya Desam: listed as one of the 108 (number in lists varies). Alvars: Periyalvar, Andal,
  Thirumangai Alvar, Nammalvar per standard Sri Vaishnava lists — not re-confirmed this
  session; pasuram count not used.
- Dwarka: one of the Char Dham and of the seven Mukti Kshetras (Sapta Puri); seat of the
  western amnaya (Dwaraka Sharada Peetham) attributed to Adi Shankara — well-known, general.
- Gateways Swarga Dwar (entry) and Moksha Dwar (exit, steps to the Gomti) and the flag on the
  spire changed several times daily — widely described (Wikipedia article); phrased cautiously.
- Festivals: Krishna Janmashtami (main); Holi/Phuldol season; Vaikunta Ekadashi kept by Sri
  Vaishnava pilgrims — phrased generally.
- century: 8 used for the Alvar hymns (earliest structural dating in sources is not firm).
