# Deepaprakasa (Vilakkoli) Perumal Temple, Thiruthanka (Thoopul), Kanchipuram

Region: Thondai Nadu. Master-list name/place confirmed (Wikipedia article title: Tiruththanka).

## Deities
- Moolavar: Deepaprakasar — in Tamil, Vilakkoli Perumal, "the Lord of the lamp's radiance".
- Thayar: Maragathavalli.

## Vedanta Desika
- Born at this site (Thoopul) in 1268 CE (Hindupedia); a shrine to him stands within the temple (Wikipedia). Hence "Thoopul Vedanta Desikan". Hindupedia also calls the place Tiruttangaa (Himavanam).

## Alvars
- Divya Desam praised in the Nalayira Divya Prabandham. Standard Divya Desam lists credit Thirumangai Alvar — **not confirmed by a source this session**; the entry words this as tradition.

## Legends
- The name (Deepaprakasa / Vilakkoli) means light of the lamp. The commonly told sthala purana — Vishnu shining forth as a lamp when darkness was sent to hinder Brahma's sacrifice at Kanchi — is **not sourced this session**; worded as tradition in the entry.

## Festivals
- Desika's tirunakshatram falls in Purattasi (attested for Thiruvahindrapuram); its observance at his birthplace is natural but not separately sourced.
