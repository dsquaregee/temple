# Hara Saabha Vimochana Perumal, Thirukkandiyur

Master-list entry confirmed: Thirukkandiyur — Hara Saabha Vimochana Perumal, Kandiyur, Thanjavur, Tamil Nadu. Region: Chola Nadu.

- In Thirukkandiyur (Kandiyur), near Thiruvaiyaru, Thanjavur district.
- Moolavar: Hara Saabha Vimochana Perumal, standing, facing east.
- Thayar: Kamalavalli Nachiyar.
- Mangalasasanam: Thirumangai Alvar — 1 pasuram.
- Vimana: Kamalakruthi Vimanam. Tank: Kapala Moksha Pushkarini (also Kamala Theertham).
- Kshetram: called a Trimurthi kshetram; pratyaksham to Agastya.
- Legend: Shiva severed one of Brahma's heads and the skull clung to his hand; he worshipped Vishnu and Kamalavalli here and was freed — hence "Hara Saabha Vimochana" (the one who released Hara from the curse).
- The Shiva temple Brahmasirakandeeswarar (Kandeeswarar) stands a few hundred metres away.
- History: attributed to the Medieval Cholas (late 8th c.), later Vijayanagara and Madurai Nayak contributions (Wikipedia). No specific inscriptions found.
- Gopuram: sources disagree (three-tier vs five-tier) — tier count omitted.
- Festivals (Dinamalar): Panguni Brahmotsavam (Mar–Apr), Aippasi Pavitrotsavam, Vaikunta Ekadashi, Karthigai Deepam.
