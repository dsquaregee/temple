# Jagathrakshaka Perumal Temple, Thirukkoodalur (Aduthurai Perumal Koil)

Assigned id: `jagathrakshaka-thirukkoodalur`. Master list name/place confirmed (village is
Vadakurangaduthurai / Aduthurai, between Thiruvaiyaru and Kumbakonam, Thanjavur district).

Note: web research in this session was limited (WebFetch could not reach most domains;
search budget exhausted), so facts below rest on search-result text only.

## Facts
- Divya Desam in Chola Nadu, dedicated to Vishnu as Jagathrakshaka ("protector of the world"),
  in Tamil Vaiyam Katha Perumal. Locally called Aduthurai Perumal Koil. [1][2]
- Consort: sources differ — Padmasanavalli / Padmasinivalli and Pushpavalli are both given;
  the Thayar shrine faces south. [1][3]
- Alvar: sung by Thirumangai Alvar (Wikipedia: "in one hymn"; pasuram count not verified). Thirumangai
  calls the village "Pugunthaan Oor", the place where the Lord entered (went into the earth). [2]
- Also known as Sangama Kshetram; called "northern Koodalur" to distinguish it from Koodal (Madurai);
  name associated with Tamil "koodal", gathering. [1][2]
- Coordinates (Wikipedia infobox): 10°55′31″N 79°12′12″E → 10.925, 79.203. [3]
- History: believed built by the Medieval Cholas in the late 8th century CE, later contributions
  from Vijayanagara kings and Madurai Nayaks (Wikipedia). [1]
- Tradition/one source: the older temple was damaged by Kaveri floods and the present temple was
  raised by Rani Mangammal after the Lord appeared to her in a dream (single-source; tradition). [4][5]
- Architecture: brick compound wall enclosing shrines and water bodies; three-tiered rajagopuram;
  sanctum vimana is the Suddha Satva Vimana. [3]
- Legends: the Lord is believed to have appeared to the sage Nandaka and King Ambarisha (legend of
  Ambarisha and Durvasa tied to the shrine). The conch is believed to have appeared in the jackfruit
  tree behind the sanctum. [1][2]
- Maintained by the Tamil Nadu HR&CE Department. [1]
- Festivals: not verified in sources reached; only general Vaishnava observances to be mentioned.
