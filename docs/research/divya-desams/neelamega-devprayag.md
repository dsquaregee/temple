## Identity
- One of the 108 Divya Desams (Vada Nadu), Tamil name Thirukkandam enum Kadinagar ("the strong city called Kandam").
- Moolavar: Neelamegha Perumal; Thayar: Pundarikavalli. Locally worshipped as Raghunathji (Rama) with Sita. Vimanam: Mangala Vimanam; theertham: Mangala Theertham.
- Alvar: Periyalvar (decad "Kandam ennum kadinagar", Periyalvar Thirumozhi 4.7 — *decad number from reference memory; search could not confirm*). No count used in copy.

## Location
- At Devprayag, the confluence of the Bhagirathi and Alaknanda, where the river takes the name Ganga; Tehri Garhwal district.
- Coordinates used: 30.1456, 78.5981 *(unverified; town-level Wikipedia coordinates from reference memory)*.

## History
- Wikipedia credits the temple's founding to Adi Shankara (8th c.); other accounts credit the Maharaja of Tehri / Garhwal rulers; the present temple is said to have been reconstructed by Daulat Rao Scindia (after earthquake damage). Sources conflict — copy phrases these as tradition.
- Century used: 8 (Alvar hymns).

## Architecture
- Stone temple above the sangam with a tall towering superstructure in the north Indian (Nagara) manner *(general reference)*; reached by steps from the confluence.

## Legends
- Tradition holds Rama performed penance here (after the war in Lanka); the town is named after the sage Deva Sharma *(general reference)*.

## Festivals
- Ram Navami and other Rama festivals; bathing at the sangam *(general reference)*.

## Getting there
- On the Rishikesh–Badrinath highway (NH 7), about 70 km from Rishikesh; nearest rail Rishikesh; airport Jolly Grant, Dehradun.
