# Vaikunta Perumal Temple (Thiru Parameswara Vinnagaram), Kanchipuram — research note

- Region: Thondai Nadu; one of the 108 Divya Desams. Alvar name: Paramesvara Vinnagaram.
- Deity: Vaikunta Perumal; consort Vaikunthavalli Thayar (Wikipedia infobox).
- Built under Pallava king Nandivarman II Pallavamalla (reign c. 731–796 / 730–795 CE); stylistically dated c. 770–775 CE (Rhie, cited on Wikipedia). Sandstone.
- Mangalasasanam: Thirumangai Alvar, Periya Thirumozhi 2.9 (ten pasurams), likely composed c. 790 CE in Nandivarman II's reign. Refrain "pallavar kon paninda paramesura vinnagaram" (2.9.2, .3, .5, .7, .8). 2.9.2: the Pallava king, having defeated a Pandya, bowed to the lord. 2.9.10: those who learn the ten verses will rule as kings.
- Architecture: three sanctums stacked vertically within the vimana — seated (ground), reclining as Sheshashayee (first), standing (top; opened only on Ekadashi per Inditales). Vimana larger than that of the earlier Kailasanatha temple. Raised verandah/cloister around the vimana with sculpted panels of the reigns of several Pallava kings.
- Coordinates: 12°50′13″N 79°42′36″E → 12.8369, 79.7100 (Wikipedia infobox).
- Not verified (omitted): facing direction, tank, sthala purana beyond the Alvar's hymn, ASI status.
