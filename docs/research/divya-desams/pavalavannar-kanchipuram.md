# Pavalavannar Temple (Thiruppavalavannam), Kanchipuram — research note

- Wikipedia title: "Pavalavannam Temple". Region: Thondai Nadu; one of the 108 Divya Desams.
- Moolavar: Pavalavannar ("coral-hued"); consort Pavalavalli Thayar (also Piravalavalli).
- Alvars: Thirumangai Alvar did mangalasasanam; Wikipedia notes Peyalvar, Thirumalisai Alvar and Thirumangai Alvar sang the different forms of God in this temple.
- Legend: sage Bhrigu tests Brahma, Vishnu and Shiva; Vishnu appeared to the sage as Pavalavannar.
- Shrines: separate Alvar shrines north of the flagstaff; outer prakaram has shrines of Andal and Pavalavalli Thayar.
- Location: ~1.5 km from Kanchipuram railway station, ~2.5 km from the bus stand (devotional blog).
- Pachaivannar (emerald-hued) shrine nearby is customarily visited with it — from general knowledge, NOT verified by a source this session (phrased lightly in copy).
