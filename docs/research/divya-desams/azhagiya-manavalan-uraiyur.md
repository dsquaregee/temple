# Azhagiya Manavalan (Thirukkozhi / Nachiyar Koil), Uraiyur

Master-list entry confirmed: Thirukkozhi (Uraiyur) — Azhagiya Manavalan, Uraiyur, Tiruchirappalli, Tamil Nadu. Region: Chola Nadu.

- Also called Thirukkozhi and Nachiyar Koil; in Uraiyur, a suburb of Tiruchirappalli (the ancient Chola capital).
- Moolavar: Azhagiya Manavalan (Vishnu), standing, facing north, in kalyana (wedding) form.
- Thayar: Kamalavalli Nachiyar; the Goddess holds prominence here; no separate Thayar sannidhi — she is seen together with the Lord.
- Mangalasasanam: Kulasekhara Alvar (Perumal Thirumozhi, NDP 667) and Thirumangai Alvar (NDP 1762) — per SriPedia/Oppiliappan archive.
- Avatara sthalam (birthplace) of Thiruppan Alvar; a separate sannidhi for him on the northern side of the temple tank.
- Vimana: Kalyana Vimanam (majority of sources; one mirror says Kamala Vimanam).
- Tank: Kalyana Theertham / Surya Pushkarini (names vary; Dinamalar gives Kamala Pushkarini).
- Gopuram: five-tiered gateway tower; carved pillars and large mandapams.
- History: believed to date to the Medieval Cholas (late 8th c. CE), later contributions from Pandyas, Vijayanagara kings and Madurai Nayaks. Administered by the Tamil Nadu HR&CE Department.
- Legend: Azhagiya Manavalan (identified with Ranganatha of Srirangam) appeared here to wed Kamalavalli, daughter of the Chola king Nanda Chola; one version says she performed penance for him.
- Festivals: Chittirai (Mar–Apr) chariot festival is the most prominent; Kalyana Utsavam on Panguni Pooram, when the utsavar of Ranganatha is brought from Srirangam.
