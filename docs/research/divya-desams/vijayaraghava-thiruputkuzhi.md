# Vijayaraghava Perumal Temple, Thiruputkuzhi — research note

- Region: Thondai Nadu; one of the 108 Divya Desams; village in Kanchipuram district, west of Kanchipuram.
- Deity: Vijayaraghava Perumal (Vijayaraghavaswamy); consort Maragathavalli Thayar (Wikipedia: "Margathavalli").
- Legend: Jatayu, wounded fighting Ravana, died; Rama performed his last rites. Water body "Jatayu Tirtham". Name: Jatayu of the *pul* (bird) family buried in a *kuzhi* (pit).
- Thiruputkuzhi is believed to be the birthplace of Yadava Prakasa (Ramanuja's early teacher — the teacher relationship is standard Ramanuja biography).
- Dating: the Wikipedia village page says the temple is believed to have been built by the Pandya kings in the 13th century (phrased as local account in copy; dynasty field kept generic).
- Alvar: Thirumangai Alvar — from general knowledge; NOT confirmed by a source retrieved this session (search budget exhausted).
- Tradition of the two consorts having exchanged their usual sides because of the heat of Jatayu's pyre — from general knowledge, NOT source-verified; phrased as tradition.
- **Coordinates NOT verified**: used approximate 12.870, 79.625 (≈10 km west of Kanchipuram near the Chennai–Bengaluru highway) — needs confirmation.
- Not verified (omitted): vimana/tank names beyond Jatayu Tirtham, festival months, stone horse vahana.
