# Kallazhagar (Sundararaja Perumal) Temple, Alagar Koil — Thirumaliruncholai

Divya Desam, Pandya Nadu. Master list (Thirumaliruncholai / Alagar Koil, Madurai) confirmed.

Research note: WebFetch to Wikipedia was blocked in this session and the shared web-search
budget ran out mid-research; facts below come from search-result text of the sources listed.
Items marked (tradition) are presented as tradition only.

## Deity
- Presiding deity: Vishnu as Sundararaja Perumal / Kallazhagar (Azhagar), standing posture (one
  source names the standing moolavar Sundarabahu); consort Sundaravalli Thayar (sources also
  name Kalyana Sundaravalli and Andal/Sundaravalli Nachiyar in separate shrines).
- Vimana: Somachandra vimanam. Theertham: Noopura Gangai (on the hill above the temple).

## Alvars
- Sung by several Alvars; one source gives 128 pasurams in all. Thirumangai Alvar 33 pasurams;
  Bhoothathalvar and Peyalvar 3 between them; Nammalvar three decads of Tiruvaimozhi
  (2.10, 3.1, 10.7); Periyalvar ("thenthirumaliruncholai", "azhagan alankaran") and Andal also
  sang the hill. (Per-Alvar total not fully verified.)

## Location
- Alagar Kovil, foot of the Alagar hills, ~20 km north-east of Madurai, Madurai district.
- Coordinates (Wikipedia infobox via search): 10.074136 N, 78.214356 E; elevation ~266 m.

## History
- Pandya patronage: Jatavarman Sundara Pandya (1251–1270) is said to have plated the sanctum
  vimana with gold (secondary source); one account names Malayadhwaja Pandya as earliest patron.
- Tamil-Brahmi Jain inscription of c. 2nd century BCE on the hill (one source).
- Thirumalai Nayak (r. 1623–1659): in 1653 the Manduka Maharishi ritual of the Chithirai festival
  was shifted to Vandiyur, where he built a mandapam; Nayak-period art in the kalyana mandapam.

## Architecture
- Temple about 2 acres, five-tiered gopuram, Somachandra vimana; Nayak-era kalyana mandapam
  with sculpture.

## Legends
- Manduka Maharishi: a sage cursed to be a frog is freed when Azhagar descends from the hill to
  the Vaigai at Thenur (Wikipedia).
- (Tradition) Andal's vow of a hundred pots of butter and sweet rice to Azhagar, later fulfilled
  by Ramanuja — widely told; not verified in this session's searches.

## Festivals
- Chithirai festival (April–May): Kallazhagar travels from Alagar Kovil to Madurai, receives the
  Ethir Sevai welcome on the fourth day, and on the full moon enters the Vaigai on a golden
  horse vahana before lakhs of devotees; linked to the Meenakshi wedding celebrations.
