# Thirukkurungudi — Nambi Perumal (Vaishnava Nambi / Azhagiya Nambirayar), Thirukkurungudi (Tirunelveli)

## Facts
- Divya Desam of Pandya Nadu; locally called **Dakshina Vaikuntam**; Tenkalai worship.
- Deity: Vishnu as **Nambi** (Vaishnava Nambi / Azhagiya Nambirayar); consort **Kurungudivalli Nachiyar**.
- Mangalasasanam by **four Alvars: Thirumalisai Alvar, Nammalvar, Periyalvar, Thirumangai Alvar**.
- **Five Nambis**: Ninra (standing), Irundha (seated), Kidandha (reclining) Nambi in the main temple; **Thiruparkadal Nambi** by the Nambi river ~1 km away; **Thirumalai (Malaimel) Nambi** on the Mahendragiri hills ~8 km away.
- A separate **Shiva shrine** stands within the Vishnu temple complex (tradition: Shiva had darshan of the Lord here) — unusual.
- History: inscriptions refer to the **Chola** period from the **10th century**; later additions incl. **Vijayanagara**. Claims of 2,200 years are traditional, not archaeological.
- Tradition (blog-level, not corroborated): the Lord received Vaishnava initiation from Ramanuja here — omitted from the entry.
- **Kaisika Ekadasi** (Karthigai, the Ekadasi before Vaikunta Ekadasi): legend of **Nampaduvan**, a devotee-singer of low birth who sang outside; stopped by a brahmarakshasa on Ekadasi night, he promised to return after worship and kept his word; the demon was freed by the merit of his song. Based on an episode of the **Varaha Purana** (Kaisika Puranam). The **Kaisika Natakam** play is performed in the temple on that night; oldest extant manuscript c. 17th c. (Veerabadhra Nattuvanar).
- Coordinates (Wikipedia): 8°26′52″N 77°33′43″E → **8.448, 77.562**.
