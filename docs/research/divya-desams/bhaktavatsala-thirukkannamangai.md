# Bhaktavatsala Perumal Temple, Thirukannamangai

Divya Desam, Chola Nadu. Master list name/place confirmed (near Tiruvarur).

## Facts
- Deities (Wikipedia infobox): moolavar Perumpurakkadal with Kannamangai Nayagi; utsavar Bhaktavatsalan with Abhishekavalli.
- Coordinates (Wikipedia): 10°47′58″N 79°35′13″E → 10.7994, 79.5869. About 6 km from Tiruvarur on the Tiruvarur–Kumbakonam road.
- Alvar: Thirumangai Alvar (pasuram count not confirmed).
- One of the Pancha Krishnaranya (Panchakanna) Kshetrams.
- History: believed to have been begun by the Medieval Cholas in the late 9th century (Wikipedia; a blog says late 8th); three Chola-period inscriptions; later additions/renovation by the Thanjavur Nayaks (17th c.).
- Architecture: five-tiered rajagopuram; vimana Utpala (infobox); tank Darshana Pushkarini, described as one of the largest temple tanks in the state.
- Legends: Lakshmi is said to have wedded Vishnu here; the devas who witnessed the wedding took the form of bees and built a hive (by the Thayar shrine, on its northern side) so as to stay and worship daily; the bees are said never to harm devotees.
- Festivals: Brahmotsavam in Chithirai (April–May), ten days after Chitra Pournami, with chariot festival; Vaikunta Ekadashi; six daily rituals and three yearly festivals.
