# Gajendra Varadha Perumal Temple, Kapisthalam (Thirukkavithalam)

## Facts
- Divya Desam of Chola Nadu at Kabisthalam/Kapisthalam (Thirukkavithalam), about 3 km from Papanasam
  and about 20 km from Kumbakonam, Thanjavur district. [1][2]
- Moolavar: Gajendra Varadhar, reclining in Bhujanga sayanam, facing east. [3][4]
- Consort: Ramamanivalli (Potramaraiyal); some sources also give Shenbagavalli. [1][3]
- Alvar: Thirumazhisai Alvar, one pasuram; he called the Lord "Attrangarai Kidakkum Kannan" (Kannan
  who lies on the river bank), and the Lord is hence also called Kannan. [3][4]
- One of the Pancha Krishna (Pancha Kannan) Kshetrams. [1]
- Vimana: Gaganakruthi Vimanam (spellings vary). Tirthams: Gajendra Pushkarini and Kapila Theertham. [3][4]
- Name: "Kapi" = monkey — tradition that Hanuman worshipped / was granted darshan of the Lord (as Rama)
  here; alternative derivation "Kavisthalam" (place of poets). [2][4]
- History: Wikipedia attributes construction to Medieval Cholas of the late 9th century CE, with later
  contributions from Vijayanagara kings and Madurai Nayaks. [1]
- Legend: Gajendra Moksha — the elephant Gajendra seized by a crocodile, rescued by Vishnu; described as
  a shrine where the Lord blessed two non-human beings, the elephant and the crocodile. [3]
- Festivals: Gajendra Moksha Lila on Aadi Pournami (July–August), the most prominent; car festival on
  Vaikasi Visakam (May–June); Brahmotsavam. [1][5]
- Coordinates (Wikipedia): 10°56′43″N 79°16′31″E → 10.945, 79.275. [1]
- Not verified: rajagopuram tier count; specific inscriptions.
