# Saranathan Temple, Thirucherai

Divya Desam, Chola Nadu. Master list name/place confirmed.

## Facts
- Deities: Vishnu as Saranatha Perumal (Saranathan); consort Saranayaki Thayar. Tradition: the Lord is worshipped with five consorts — Sridevi, Bhudevi, Neeladevi, Mahalakshmi and Saranayaki (temple guides).
- "Pancha Sara Kshetram": Saranatha Perumal, Saranayaki, Sara Vimanam, Sara Pushkarini, and the place itself. Name linked to fertile soil (saram); also Tamil seru/cheru (mud).
- Coordinates (Wikipedia): 10°52′43″N 79°27′15″E → 10.8786, 79.4542. Between the Kudamurutti and Mudikondan rivers.
- Alvar: Thirumangai Alvar, 13 pasurams (one source; stated as "thirteen hymns").
- History: Chola inscription of Parantaka I / Parakesarivarman (10th c.; sources differ on reign dates); contributions of Medieval Cholas, Vijayanagara and Madurai Nayaks.
- Architecture: five-tiered rajagopuram (~120 ft per a tourism blog); a second three-tiered gopuram in the first precinct; Sara Vimanam; Sara Pushkarini.
- Legends: Kaveri, slighted when Ganga was held greater (Vishnu's foot touched Ganga in the Vamana avatar), did penance on the bank of the Sara Pushkarini; Vishnu appeared as a child on her lap; she has a shrine here showing her with the child — rare among Vishnu temples. Separately, Brahma is said to have fashioned a pot from Thirucherai's earth to hold the Vedas before the deluge — linking the shrine to the Kumbakonam origin story.
- Festivals: Thai Poosam — the Lord with his consorts gives darshan as a child in Kaveri's arms on a decorated chariot; bathing in the Sara Pushkarini on Thai Poosam in the Jupiter-year conjunction is held equal to the Mahamaham bath at Kumbakonam (Dinamalar).
- Part of the Kumbakonam Divya Desam yatra.
