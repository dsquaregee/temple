# Nithyakalyana Perumal Temple, Thiruvidanthai — research notes

Region: Thondai Nadu. One of the 108 Divya Desams. On the East Coast Road,
Chengalpattu district (~4 km from Kovalam/Covelong; ~19 km south of Thiruvanmiyur).

Note: research via search-result text only.

## Deity
- Presiding: Vishnu as Varaha (Lakshmi Varaha); the processional form is Nithyakalyana Perumal.
- Consort: Komalavalli Thayar (own shrine) — sources also name the goddess with the lord
  Akhilavalli (said to be the 360 daughters merged into one). Both names used carefully.

## Alvars
- Thirumangai Alvar — 13 pasurams.

## Location
- Coordinates (Wikipedia temple article): 12°45′47.9″N 80°14′31.2″E → 12.7633, 80.2420.

## History
- Pallava foundation (7th–8th c.) per secondary sources; an epigraphical plaque in the
  precincts (now worn) is said to date the temple to the 8th c.
- Renovated under the Cholas (11th c.); a Chola king (Vijaya Rajendra) is said to have granted
  the village to the temple.

## Architecture
- Vimana: Kalyana Vimanam.
- Tanks: Varaha Theertham and Kalyana Theertham.
- Sthala vriksha: Punnai tree.

## Legend
- Sage Kalava (Galava) had 360 daughters; Vishnu came as a young brahmachari and married one
  each day for a year — hence Nithya-kalyana ("daily wedding"). At the end the daughters
  became one, honoured as Akhilavalli (tradition).

## Practice
- Those praying for marriage take a garland, walk around the inner prakaram nine times and
  keep the garland at home; after marriage, couples return it (to the Punnai tree).

## Festivals
- Brahmotsavam (month not confirmed), Vaikunta Ekadashi.
