# Nanmathiya Perumal — Thalaichanga Nanmathiyam, Thalaichangadu

Master list confirmed: Thalaichangadu near Akkur, Mayiladuthurai district (older listings say Nagapattinam district — the district was bifurcated).

- One of the 108 Divya Desams, Chola Nadu; Dravidian style; praised by Thirumangai Alvar (mangalasasanam). Pasuram count not verified.
- Moolavar: Nanmathiya Perumal, standing, facing east; tradition describes a crescent moon on his head. Epithet Chandra Shaba Harar — the one who relieved the Moon of his curse.
- Utsavar: Vensudar (Venchudar) Perumal. Consort: Thalaichanga Nachiyar; utsava consort given as Senkamalavalli.
- Coordinates (Wikipedia): 11°7′47″N 79°47′7″E → 11.1297, 79.7853.
- Architecture: single-tiered gopuram; temple tank Chandra Pushkarani in front.
- Legend: Chandra (the Moon), cursed with a wasting disease by his guru Brihaspati, worshipped Vishnu here and was relieved; Chandra and the devas worshipped here.
- Name: Thalai (head) + Sangu (conch) — linked to conch; another tradition links it to the first Tamil Sangam.
- The village also has the Sangaranyeswarar (Shiva) temple sung in Thevaram by Sambandar — a separate temple.
- Not found: inscriptions or dated history; festivals not specified in sources (only general Vaishnava observances assumed).
