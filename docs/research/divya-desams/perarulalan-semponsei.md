# Thiru Semponsei Koil (Perarulalan Perumal), Thirunangur — research notes

Master list entry confirmed: Thirusemponsei Koil, Thirunangur, near Sirkazhi (now Mayiladuthurai district; older sources say Nagapattinam district).

- Region: Chola Nadu; one of the 108 Divya Desams and one of the eleven Thirunangur Tirupatis (Thirunangur Divya Desams).
- Moolavar: Perarulalan Perumal, standing, facing east. Also called Sempon Arangar / Semponsei Arangar.
- Thayar: Alli Mamalar Nachiyar (some accounts also mention Bhudevi with her).
- Mangalasasanam: Thirumangai Alvar (the only Alvar to sing the Thirunangur shrines). Pasuram count not confirmed from a reachable source (commonly a decad of the Periya Thirumozhi) — not stated in content.
- Coordinates (Wikipedia infobox "Thiruchsemponsey"): 11°10′42″N 79°46′46″E → 11.1783, 79.7794.
- Vimanam: Kanaka vimanam. Tank: Hema Pushkarini; Kanaka Theertham also listed.
- Architecture: Dravidian; no inscriptions or dated dynastic patrons found in available sources (left out).
- Sthala purana: after the war in Lanka, Rama stayed with the sage Drudhanetra (Thrudanethrar); on the sage's advice, to expiate the sin of slaying Ravana (a brahmin), he had a cow fashioned of gold and gave it to a brahmin; the brahmin used the gold to build this temple — hence "Sem-pon-sei koil", the temple made of red (pure) gold. Devotional sources call it the shrine where the Lord himself provided the wealth for his own temple.
- Shared Thirunangur legend: Shiva's fierce dance at Thirunangur after Sati's death at Daksha's sacrifice; at Shiva's request Vishnu appeared in eleven forms, at the eleven Thirunangur sites; Ekadasa Rudra shrines correspond.
- Festivals: Aippasi Brahmotsavam (Oct–Nov), culminating on Swati, the Perumal's birth star; the Thirunangur Garuda Sevai on the day after Thai Amavasai (Jan–Feb), when the eleven utsavars gather at Thirumanimadam (Manimadakoil) and Thirumangai Alvar's mangalasasanam is re-enacted.
- Location: about 8 km from Sirkazhi; Sirkazhi railway station nearest.
- Not verified: inscriptions, gopuram tiers, exact age (sources range 500–2000 years — left out).
