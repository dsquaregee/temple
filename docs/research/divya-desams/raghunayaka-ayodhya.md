# Thiru Ayodhi (Ayodhya) — Raghunayaka / Chakravarthi Thirumagan (Ammaji Mandir), Uttar Pradesh

## Facts
- Thiruvayodhi / Thiru Ayodhi is counted among the 108 Divya Desams (Vada Nadu); Rama as
  Chakravarthi Thirumagan / Raghunayaka, with Sita Piratti as Thayar (koyil.org; srivaishnavan).
- Vimana: Pushkala Vimanam; theertham: the Sarayu river (koyil.org); srivaishnavan also names
  a Paramapada Satya Pushkarini — not used.
- Mangalasasanam: 13 pasurams — Periyalvar 6, Kulasekara Alvar 4, Nammalvar 1,
  Thondaradippodi Alvar 1, Thirumangai Alvar 1 (srivaishnavan.com listing). Prabandhams:
  Periyalvar Thirumozhi, Perumal Thirumozhi, Thiruppalliyezhuchi, Periya Thirumozhi,
  Thiruvaimozhi (koyil.org).
- Ammaji Mandir: Sri Vaishnava temple where South Indian pilgrims offer worship to the Divya
  Desam lord. Founded in 1904 by Yogi Parthasarathy Iyengar (Saraswathi Bhandaram Committee,
  Chennai); after his death his wife Yogi Singammal, "Ammaji", completed it and organised daily
  worship and festivals, giving the temple its popular name (Deccan Herald; SriPedia/ramanuja.org).
- Images of Rama, Sita and Ranganatha; a processional (utsava) image linked to the Divya Desam
  of Darbhasayanam (Thiruppullani, Tamil Nadu); shrines of Nammalvar, Thirumangai Alvar,
  Ramanuja (Udayavar) and Manavala Mamunigal (ramanuja.org pilgrim accounts).
- Dravidian style; worship in the Ramanuja Sri Vaishnava tradition; Sri Rama Navami utsavam
  over five days (ramanuja.org).
- Coordinates: exact site of the Ammaji Mandir not confirmed; used Ayodhya city 26.799, 82.204.

- century: 8 (Alvar hymns).
