# Ashtabhuja Perumal Temple (Thiruvattabuyagaram / Ashtabujakaram), Kanchipuram

Region: Thondai Nadu. Master-list name/place confirmed.

## Deities
- Moolavar: Ashtabhuja Perumal — Vishnu with eight arms; also called Adikesava Perumal, Gajendra Varadhan and Chakradhar (Wikipedia).
- Thayar: Alarmelmangai / Padmasani (Dinamalar); oneindia gives Pushpakavalli — sources differ.

## Alvars
- Pey Alvar and Thirumangai Alvar (Dinamalar). Pasuram count not confirmed.
- One of the 14 Divya Desams in Kanchipuram (Wikipedia list).

## Legends
- Saraswati, angered when Brahma sided with Lakshmi, sent forces (demons, then the lion-bird Sharabha; other versions a serpent) to disrupt Brahma's sacrifice at Kanchi; Vishnu took an eight-armed form to defend it — hence the name.
- Some traditions link the shrine to Gajendra Moksha — the rescue of the elephant Gajendra from a crocodile — hence Gajendra Varadhan.
