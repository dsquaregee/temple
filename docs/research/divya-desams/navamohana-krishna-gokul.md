# Thiruvaipadi — Navamohana Krishnan (Gokul, Uttar Pradesh)

Vada Nadu Divya Desam. Assigned id: `navamohana-krishna-gokul`. Master list correct.

## Facts
- Wikipedia: Navamohana Krishna Temple, Gokul, Mathura district; called Tiruvaipadi in the
  Sri Vaishnava tradition; one of the 108 Divya Desams.
- Moolavar: Navamohana Krishna, standing, with consorts Rukmini and Satyabhama.
- Vimanam: Hemakoota Vimanam. Theertham: Yamuna; a tank lies west of the temple, parallel
  to the Yamuna. Pratyaksham (to whom the lord appeared): Nandagopan.
- Mangalasasanam: Periyalvar (10), Andal (5), Thirumangai Alvar (7) — 22 pasurams (Wikipedia
  summary + Sri Vaishnava database listing agree).
- The temple the Alvars praised and its images no longer exist; the present temple is of a
  later date (Wikipedia / bharatpedia).
- A separate shrine at Purana Gokul nearby is associated with Nandagopa, Yashoda and Balarama.
- Location ~15 km south-east of Mathura, on the Yamuna. Coordinates ≈ 27.450, 77.717.
- Legend: Gokul ("Ayarpadi", the cowherd settlement) is where Vasudeva carried the newborn
  Krishna across the Yamuna to Nanda and Yashoda; Krishna's childhood (butter, Putana etc.) —
  Periyalvar's verses of Krishna's infancy are set in Ayarpadi. One commentary links Thiruppavai
  verse 7 (the churning of the cowherd women) to this site.
- Festivals: Krishna Janmashtami and Nandotsav (the festival of Nanda's joy, the day after) —
  general Braj tradition, phrased generally.
- century: 8 (Alvar hymns).
