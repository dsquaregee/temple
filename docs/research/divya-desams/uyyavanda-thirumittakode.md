# Uyyavanda Perumal — Thiruvithuvakkodu (Thirumittacode), Malai Nadu

Research note. WebFetch failed (DNS/proxy); facts from WebSearch result text. The search budget ran out, so coordinates are unconfirmed.

- Temple: Thirumittakode Anchumoorthi Temple ("five deities"), Thirumittacode, Palakkad district, Kerala, by the Bharathapuzha; Divya Desam name Thiruvithuvakkodu.
- Region: Malai Nadu Divya Desam.
- Deity: Uyyavanda Perumal, also Abhaya Pradhan. Consort: Vithuvakkottu Valli (also Padmapani Nachiyar).
- Shiva shrine at the same site; the temple is associated with both Vishnu and Shiva.
- Alvar: Kulasekhara Alvar (Chera prince) — Perumal Thirumozhi. (Decad/pasuram count not confirmed here.) century = 9 (Kulasekhara is usually placed in the 9th c.).
- Legend: King Ambarisha; Durvasa repented and blessed him; Ambarisha asked the lord to appear in Vyuha form, visible in all four directions — hence four Vishnu sannidhis facing the four directions. One source: Ambarisha attained moksha here.
- Pandava tradition: sources differ (built by Dharmaputra / Pandavas installed images during exile) — phrased only as "the Pandavas are also remembered here".
- Festivals: Thiruvonam; Vaikunta Ekadashi.
- Administration: one search summary said Travancore Devaswom Board — doubtful for Palakkad (Malabar) — not used.
- Coordinates: NOT verified. Used approx. 10.788, 76.142 (Thirumittacode, south bank of the Bharathapuzha near Pattambi). Needs checking.
