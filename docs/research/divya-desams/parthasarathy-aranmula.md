# Parthasarathy — Thiruvaranvilai (Aranmula), Malai Nadu

Research note. WebFetch failed (DNS/proxy); facts from WebSearch result text. The search budget ran out, so coordinates are unconfirmed.

- Temple: Aranmula Parthasarathy Temple, Aranmula, Pathanamthitta district, Kerala, on the Pamba river. Divya Desam name Thiruvaranvilai.
- Deity: Krishna as Parthasarathy (charioteer of Arjuna). Nammalvar names the lord Thirukkuralappan; consort Padmasani Nachiyar.
- Alvar: Nammalvar (one of the 12 Alvars).
- Pancha Pandava tradition: attributed to Arjuna. Tradition: Arjuna first installed the image at Nilakkal near Sabarimala, but forest conditions made daily worship hard; the image was brought down the river on a raft of six bamboos — "Aranmula" = "six bamboo stems".
- Architecture: Kerala style; four gopurams over the entrances in the outer wall.
- Festivals: the Uthrattathi snake-boat regatta (Aranmula Vallamkali) in Onam season commemorates the installation; Valla Sadya feasts offered to the boat crews (Onmanorama); Onam celebrations (The News Minute).
- Village: known for Aranmula kannadi metal mirrors (The News Minute "sparkling gift").
