# Veeraraghava Perumal Temple, Thiruvallur (Thiruevvul) — research notes

Region: Thondai Nadu. One of the 108 Divya Desams.

Note: Web research for this batch was done via search-result text only (Wikipedia and
other sites could not be fetched directly); facts below are limited to what the sources
state. Unconfirmed items are marked or omitted.

## Deity
- Moolavar: Vishnu as Veeraraghava Perumal, reclining on Adishesha (bhujanga sayana); the
  image is described as large (about 15 ft per one source).
- Facing: sources disagree (east in temple/travel pages; a devotee post describes the gaze
  to the south) — not stated in the app copy.
- Consort: Kanakavalli Thayar.

## Alvars
- Mangalasasanam by Thirumangai Alvar and Thirumazhisai Alvar; also praised by Vedanta
  Desika. Pasuram count not confirmed — omitted.

## Location
- Tiruvallur town, Tiruvallur district, Tamil Nadu, ~45 km west of Chennai.
- Coordinates (Wikipedia infobox, via search text): 13°08′35.8″N 79°54′24.2″E → 13.1433, 79.9067.

## History
- Wikipedia: attributed to the Pallavas of the late 8th century CE, with later contributions
  from the Thanjavur Nayaks.

## Architecture
- Seven-tiered rajagopuram; enclosed by a granite wall.
- Vimana: Vijayakoti Vimanam.
- Tank: Hrithapapanasini (Hrudhapapanasini) — believed to relieve afflictions of the mind.

## Sthala purana
- Sage Salihotra kept a year-long fast/penance after bathing in the tank; Vishnu came as an
  aged Brahmin, received the sage's offering of food, and asked "evvul?" ("where may I lie?");
  he then lay down — hence the place-name Thiruevvul. (Tradition.)

## Festivals
- Two Brahmotsavams, in Chithirai (Apr–May) and Thai (Jan–Feb) (devotee source).
- Vaikunta Ekadashi; new-moon (Amavasai) days draw large crowds (Wikipedia lists Amavasai).
