# Ninra Narayana Perumal Temple, Thiruthangal

Divya Desam, Pandya Nadu. Master list place "Thiruthangal, Sivakasi" — correct (Thiruthangal is
beside Sivakasi, Virudhunagar district).

Research note: WebFetch blocked and the shared search budget ran out; facts from search-result
text. Coordinates approximate.

## Deity
- Vishnu as Ninra Narayana Perumal, standing (ninra kolam); consort named variously as Senkamala
  Thayar, Rakthapankajavalli, Arunakamala Mahadevi (names vary by source).
- Temple stands on the small Thangal hill; a cave shrine on the hill is also noted.

## Alvars
- Bhoothathalvar and Thirumangai Alvar.

## Location
- Thiruthangal, adjoining Sivakasi, Virudhunagar district. Coordinates (approx.): 9.482 N, 77.818 E.

## Legends
- The name Ninra Narayana is linked to the wedding of Usha and Aniruddha (grandson of Krishna,
  after Krishna defeated Banasura): at the couple's request the lord remained here standing.
- The goddess (Sri/Lakshmi) is said to have come to the Thangal hill and done penance here after a
  contest among the consorts; the place is named for her stay ("thangal" — staying). Versions vary.
