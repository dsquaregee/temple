# Thiruvellakkulam (Annan Koil, Srinivasa / Annan Perumal) — research notes

Master list entry confirmed (village: Annan Koil, near Sirkazhi; part of the Thirunangur group).

- Region: Chola Nadu; one of the 108 Divya Desams; one of the eleven Thirunangur Tirupatis.
- Moolavar: Srinivasa Perumal, called Annan Perumal — the "elder brother" of Thiruvenkatamudaiyan (Venkatachalapati) of Tirumala; the site is held equivalent to Tirupati for those unable to travel there.
- Thayar: Alarmelmangai; utsavar Thayar Padmavathi / Poovar Thirumagal. Separate shrine for Kumudavalli Nachiyar (Thanjavur Parampara).
- Mangalasasanam: Thirumangai Alvar, Periya Thirumozhi 4.7 — 10 pasurams. Dinamalar: he praises it as "Bhuloka Vaikuntam"; tradition says he addressed only two Perumals as "Anna" — this one and the lord of Tirupati.
- Coordinates (Wikipedia infobox "Thiruvellakkulam"): 11°11′24″N 79°45′54″E → 11.1900, 79.7650.
- Vimanam: Tattvadyotaka vimanam. Tank: Swetha Pushkarini (Vellai Kulam, "white tank").
- Architecture: Wikipedia classes it as Chola architecture; latest consecration (kumbhabhishekam) October 2016.
- Legends: (1) Prince Swetan, foretold to die young (at nine in one telling), on the sage Vasishtha's advice chanted the Mrityunjaya mantra for a month under a vilva tree by the tank and was granted long life — the tank takes his name (Swetha Pushkarini). (2) Kumudavalli: tradition holds that a celestial maiden who came to the tank for kumuda (water-lily) flowers stayed on earth as Kumudavalli; Neelan, the chieftain who became Thirumangai Alvar, sought her hand, and she asked him to embrace the Vaishnava way and serve devotees — the turning point of the Alvar's life.
- Festivals: Thirunangur Garuda Sevai in Thai; Thirumangai Alvar festivals. Popular for prayers for longevity and health.
- Location: Annan Koil village near Sirkazhi; Mayiladuthurai district (Wikipedia; Dinamalar says Nagapattinam — pre-2020 district).
- Not verified: posture/facing of moolavar, inscriptions, gopuram tiers.
