# Pambanaiyappan / Kamalanathan — Thiruvanvandoor, Malai Nadu

Research note. WebFetch failed (DNS/proxy); facts from WebSearch result text. The search budget ran out, so coordinates are unconfirmed.

- Temple: Thiruvanvandoor Mahavishnu Temple, Thiruvanvandoor, Alappuzha district (near Chengannur), Kerala.
- Region: Malai Nadu Divya Desam.
- Moolavar: Pambanaiyappan (Paambanaiappan, name linked to the Pamba river), also Kamalanathan. Consort: Kamalavalli Nachiyar (Dinamalar). A Gosala Krishna form is also worshipped.
- Alvar: Nammalvar — earliest reference, c. 800 CE (Wikipedia via search).
- Pancha Pandava tradition: attributed to Nakula.
- Administration: Travancore Devaswom Board.
- Festival: 10-day annual festival in Kumbham (Feb–Mar) ending in arattu; flag hoisting, annadanam, Kathakali.
- Coordinates: 9.34306, 76.57972 (Wikipedia infobox 9°20′35″N 76°34′47″E; verified by later search). Used 9.343, 76.580.
