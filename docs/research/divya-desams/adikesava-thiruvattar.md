# Adikesava Perumal Temple, Thiruvattar (adikesava-thiruvattar)

Brief 1-2 search verification of the existing English entry (resume mode).

## Facts
- One of the 108 Divya Desams, in Malai Nadu (Kerala-country group); Nammalvar sang the reclining lord (few verses).
- Deity: Adikesava Perumal, reclining on Adishesha; consort Maragathavalli (as in the English entry).
- Location: Thiruvattar, Kalkulam taluk, Kanyakumari district, Tamil Nadu (PIN 629177). Coordinates used: 8.3297 N, 77.2658 E (Wikipedia infobox value; search results did not restate them).
- An inscription relating to Rajendra Chola I (r. 1013-1045 CE) on a stone slab names the deity as the Thiruvattar god (wording of the source is garbled; treat exact reading as approximate).
- Front hall is the Ottakkal Mandapam; a source attributes it to Veera Ravi Varma Kulasekhara of Venad (date conversions in that source unreliable, so no date is given in the entry).
- Legend (sthala purana tradition): Kesan/Kesi, encircling rivers (Kothai and Parali) - kept as tradition in the entry.
- Not independently confirmed in this pass: the ~22 ft length of the image, the Moolam Thirunal flagstaff renewal, the Panguni sunset-ray observance. Kept in the entry as local report; re-check if precision is needed.
