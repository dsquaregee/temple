# Thirucheevaramangai (Vanamamalai, Nanguneri) — Thothadrinathan / Vanamamalai Perumal

## Facts
- Divya Desam of Pandya Nadu at **Nanguneri** (Thirucheeravaramangai / Sirivaramangai), Tirunelveli district.
- Deity: **Vanamamalai Perumal / Thothadrinathan**, **seated** under the canopy of Adisesha, with **Sridevi and Bhudevi** seated beside him — described as the Lord in royal durbar. Consort **Varamangai** (Sirivaramangai Nachiyar).
- Vimana: **Nandhavardhana vimanam** (Wikipedia infobox).
- Tank: **Chetruttamarai Theertham**.
- Oil: the daily **oil abhishekam**; the oil is collected in a well (**Ennai Kinaru**), and the oil prasadam is famed, compared by devotees to the Tirupati laddu.
- Legend (local tradition): a childless king (Kaarya) was directed by Vishnu to Nanguneri; digging, he found blood oozing from the earth; told to bathe the deity in oil daily to stop it; blessed with children, he built the temple.
- History: many inscriptions, **earliest 1236 CE**; **1794 CE** inscriptions record a land grant by the Nawab of the Carnatic to the mutt.
- **Vanamamalai Mutt** headquarters; founded by the first Vanamamalai Jeeyar, a foremost disciple of **Manavala Mamunigal** (age given variously as 600 / 750 years).
- Festivals: six daily rituals, about a dozen annual festivals; the **chariot festival in Chithirai** is the most prominent.
- Alvar: Nammalvar's Thiruvaimozhi hymns on Sirivaramangai — standard Sri Vaishnava attribution; **not confirmed by a source in this session**.
- Coordinates (Wikipedia): 8°29′30.9″N 77°39′26.5″E → **8.492, 77.657**.
