# Lakshmi Narasimha (Azhagiyasingar) — Thiruvali-Thirunagari

Master list confirmed. Thiruvali and Thirunagari are two villages/temples near Sirkazhi, Mayiladuthurai district, counted together as a single Divya Desam. Coordinates and primary entry use the Thiruvali (Azhagiyasingar) temple.

- One of the 108 Divya Desams, Chola Nadu; the pair is referred to together in all 41 pasurams (Thirumangai Alvar).
- Thiruvali: presiding deity Azhagiyasingar / Lakshmi Narasimha, with Lakshmi seated on his right side (unusual for Narasimha temples); consort Poornavalli / Amruthagadavalli. Tradition: Narasimha appeared to Thirumangai Alvar here. A south-facing Thirumangai Alvar shrine stands outside the temple walls. Tank: Ilakshmi (Ilatchani) Pushkarini.
- Thirunagari: Vedarajan temple; festival image Kalyana Ranganathar; north-facing Thirumangai Alvar shrine in the raised enclosure. Thirunagari is held as Thirumangai Alvar's home country (Thirukkuraiyalur nearby is given as his birthplace); his consort Kumudavalli is said to have been raised here.
- Legend: Lakshmi left Vaikuntha and hid in a lotus bud in the Thirunagari tank; the legend binds both temples.
- Coordinates (Wikipedia, Thiruvali): 11°12′13″N 79°46′28″E → 11.2036, 79.7744.
- Festivals: four daily rituals, three yearly festivals; Thirumangai Alvar Utsavam in Thai is the most prominent; Garuda Sevai in January (FindMyTemple).
- Not verified: vimana names, gopuram tiers, inscriptions, distance between the two temples.
