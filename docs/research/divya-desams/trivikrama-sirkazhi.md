# Trivikrama Perumal (Thadalan) — Kazhicheerama Vinnagaram, Sirkazhi

Master list confirmed. Sirkazhi, Mayiladuthurai district. Wikipedia: "Kazheesirama Vinnagaram"; also Thadalan Kovil.

- One of the 108 Divya Desams, Chola Nadu; praised by Thirumangai Alvar (Periya Thirumozhi). A blog gives 10 pasurams — not independently verified, left out of content.
- Moolavar: Trivikrama (Thadalan) — Vishnu as Vamana grown to measure the worlds, one foot raised (left per one source). Consort: Lokanayaki.
- Coordinates (Wikipedia): 11°14′26″N 79°43′53″E → 11.2406, 79.7314.
- Vimana: Pushkalavartha (Pushkala). Tanks: Shankha Pushkarini and Chakra Theertham.
- Historical names: Patalikavanam, Thirukazhiseera Vinnagaram.
- Administered by Tamil Nadu HR&CE.
- Legends: sage Romasa (Lomasa), whose hairs were tied to Brahma's lifespan, asked Vishnu to remain here as Trivikrama. Thirumangai Alvar met the Shaiva saint Sambandar at Sirkazhi; tradition says Sambandar honoured him with a vel (spear) after the Alvar's hymn.
- Festivals: Brahmotsavam in Vaikasi (May–June).
- Not found: inscriptions / dated history.
