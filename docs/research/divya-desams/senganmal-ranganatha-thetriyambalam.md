# Thiruthetriyambalam (Senganmal Ranganatha / Pallikonda Perumal), Thirunangur — research notes

Master list entry confirmed.

- Region: Chola Nadu; one of the 108 Divya Desams; one of the eleven Thirunangur Tirupatis.
- Moolavar: Pallikonda Perumal, also Senganmal ("the red-eyed Lord"), Ranganathar, Sri Lakshmi Rangar; reclining on Adisesha, four arms, facing east. Sridevi seated near his head, Bhudevi at his feet.
- Thayar: Sengamalavalli Thayar, in her own shrine (one source names Bhujangavalli — not used).
- Distinctive: the only shrine among the eleven Thirunangur temples where the Lord reclines (sayana thirukkolam); often noted as the only Perumal temple in Tamil Nadu bearing the title "ambalam".
- Mangalasasanam: Thirumangai Alvar.
- Coordinates (Wikipedia infobox): 11°10′35″N 79°46′38″E → 11.1764, 79.7772.
- Vimanam: Veda vimanam. Tank: Surya Pushkarini.
- Legends: tradition holds the Lord here is Ranganatha of Srirangam, who came at Shiva's request (part of the Thirunangur legend of eleven forms). Another telling (TN temples project): during the Varaha avatara, Sridevi and Bhudevi feared separation, so the Lord rested here with them while the Varaha form went to vanquish Hiranyaksha. Surya and Chandra are said to have hidden in two tanks — Surya Pushkarini here, Chandra Pushkarini at Thirumanikkoodam. (Wikipedia's mention of an Arjuna-dug tank appears to belong to Parthanpalli — omitted.)
- Festivals: Garuda Sevai the day after Thai Amavasai, the utsavars of all eleven carried on Garuda vahanams to Thirumanimadam; Thirumangai Alvar mangalasasana utsavam in Thai.
- Location: Thirunangur, ~8 km east of Sirkazhi on the way to Thiruvenkadu.
- Not verified: inscriptions, dynastic history, gopuram tiers, pasuram count.
