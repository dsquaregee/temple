# Thanjai Mamani Koil — Neelamega Perumal, Thanjavur

Divya Desam of Chola Nadu. Research note for `neelamega-thanjavur`.
(WebFetch unavailable; facts rely on WebSearch result text.)

- **Unique status**: a set of three adjacent temples on the bank of the Vennaru, on the outskirts of Thanjavur, counted together as ONE Divya Desam and referred to together in the pasurams: (1) Neelamegha Perumal; (2) Manikundra Perumal; (3) Narasimha — Thanjai Yali Koil (Veera Narasimha at Singaperumal Koil).
- **Deities**: Neelamegha Perumal, seated, facing east, with Sengamalavalli Thayar; Manikundra Perumal with Ambujavalli; Narasimha with Thanjai Nayagi (Wikipedia infobox list). Shrines to Lakshmi Narasimha and Lakshmi Hayagriva; Yoga Narasimha in the front mandapam.
- **Mangalasasanam**: 5 pasurams in all — Thirumangai Alvar 3, Nammalvar 1, Bhoothathalvar 1 (srivaishnavan.com, Google Groups listing).
- **Coordinates**: 10°48′57″N 79°08′19″E → **10.8158, 79.1386** (Wikipedia infobox).
- **Architecture**: vimana names vary between sources (Vedasundara / Soundarya vimanam for Neelamegha; Manikooda vimanam for Manikundra) — not stated in content. Theertham names vary (Amirtha theertham, Kanniga pushkarini, Vennaru, Surya pushkarini) — content mentions only the Vennaru.
- **Legends**: sage Parasara worshipped here and Neelamegha Perumal and Sengamalavalli Thayar appeared before him. The asura Thanjakan (with Gajamukhan/Dandakan in variants) troubled the sages; Vishnu slew him; repenting, he asked that the place bear his name — hence Thanjai/Thanjavur. Gajamukhan, as an elephant, was slain by Vishnu as Narasimha — the Thanjai Yali shrine.
- **History**: no firm dating/inscriptions found in this session; content relies on the Alvar hymns (century 8).
- **Festivals**: no temple-specific festival calendar confirmed; content mentions only Vaikunta Ekadashi and the Alvar days generally.
