# Thiruppadagam — Pandava Thoothar Perumal (Kanchipuram)

Thondai Nadu Divya Desam. Assigned id: `pandavadoothar-thiruppadagam`. Master list correct.

## Facts
- Presiding deity: Krishna as Pandava Thoothar ("envoy of the Pandavas"), seated; consort
  Rukmini (Wikipedia). Some sources add Satyabhama — not used.
- Image height: ~25 ft per two sources (Mahavishnu Info; templesofindia.org); one says 28 ft —
  entry says "some twenty-five feet tall, by common account".
- Faces east; vimana: Bhadra Vimanam; tank: Matsya Theertham (Wikipedia).
- History: believed built by the Pallavas in the late 8th century, with later Medieval Chola
  and Vijayanagara contributions (Wikipedia). Three wall inscriptions: two of Kulothunga
  Chola I (1070–1120), one of Rajadhiraja Chola (1018–54) (Wikipedia); south-wall Kulothunga I
  inscription of his 39th year (templepurohit); a 1109 CE record of curd offerings, a land grant
  for a flower garden (milliongods snippet).
- Front hall holds bronze festival images and the Alvars (Wikipedia snippet).
- Legend: Krishna went to Hastinapura as the envoy of the Pandavas; Duryodhana dug a pit,
  covered it with a carpet and a gem-studded seat to trap him; Krishna took his cosmic
  Vishwarupa form (Wikipedia). Janamejaya, a descendant of the Pandavas, hearing the story
  from the sage Vaisampayana, did penance here to see that form (Wikipedia; hindu-blog).
- Alvars: Divya Prabandham mention confirmed; specific Alvars NOT confirmed by search this
  session (budget exhausted). Standard Sri Vaishnava lists give Bhoothathalvar, Peyalvar,
  Thirumazhisai Alvar and Thirumangai Alvar — entry phrases generally ("sung by the Alvars")
  and does not name individual Alvars.
- Coordinates (Wikipedia): 12°50′33″N 79°41′49″E → 12.8425, 79.6969.
- century: 8.
