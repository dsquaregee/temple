# Andalakkum Aiyan Temple, Thiruaadhanur

Thanjavur district, between the Kaveri and Kollidam).

## Facts
- Divya Desam of Chola Nadu; Vishnu as Andalakkum Aiyan ("the lord who measures and rules"); consort
  Bhargavi / Ranganayaki. [1][2][3]
- Moolavar in reclining posture, facing east (one source); the Lord is shown with a marakkal (measuring
  vessel), read as a sign that he measures the qualities of his devotees. [2][4]
- Vimana: Pranava Vimanam. Tank: Surya Pushkarini. [2]
- Name: Thiru + Aa (cow) + Than (its) + Oor (place) — Kamadhenu performed penance here; Kamadhenu with
  her calf Nandini appears in the sanctum. [2]
- Alvar: Thirumangai Alvar (pasuram count not verified). [1]
- Legend: Thirumangai Alvar, short of funds to pay the workers on his Srirangam renovation, met a
  merchant on the Kollidam bank who had no money but a marakkal; he measured out sand which turned to
  gold only for those who had truly worked. Tradition holds the merchant was the Lord himself. [1][2][3]
- Location: between the Kaveri and Kollidam rivers. [5]
- Festivals: 10-day Brahmotsavam around May–June (single listing). [5]
- Approximate coordinates used: 10.962, 79.315 (UNVERIFIED).
