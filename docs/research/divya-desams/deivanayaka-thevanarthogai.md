# Thiruthevanarthogai — Deivanayaka Perumal (Keezhchalai, Thirunangur)

## Thirunangur group (shared context)
- One of the eleven Thirunangur Tirupathis in and around Thirunangur village, Sirkali taluk, Mayiladuthurai district (formerly Nagapattinam), ~10 km by road SE of Sirkazhi; Chola Nadu region of the 108 Divya Desams.
- All eleven sung by Thirumangai Alvar (Periya Thirumozhi, Nalayira Divya Prabandham, hymns placed 7th–9th c.).
- Group legend: Shiva's grief-stricken dance (after the Goddess's death) multiplied him into eleven Rudras; at his request Vishnu appeared in eleven forms, each enshrined in a separate temple.
- Festival: Thirumangai Alvar Mangalasasana Utsavam in Thai; Garuda Sevai on/around the day after Thai Amavasai — the eleven utsavars come on Garuda vahanas to Thirunangur (Manimadam); seeing all eleven together is held equal to visiting each.
- Nearest rail: Sirkazhi (Chennai–Mayiladuthurai line). Nearest major airport: Tiruchirappalli (~160 km).
- No inscription/dynasty data found for this shrine; century set to 8 (Alvar attestation).

## Facts
- Place: Keezhchalai, by Thirunangur (master list says "Thirunangur" — refined to the hamlet of Keezhchalai). Also called Madhava Perumal Temple.
- Moolavar: Deivanayakan, standing, facing east. Thayar: Kadalmagal Nachiyar. Utsavars: Madhava Perumal and Madhava Nayaki.
- Alvar: Thirumangai Alvar.
- Coordinates: NOT confirmed for the temple itself; 11.1775, 79.7792 (Thirunangur village point used by Wikipedia for neighbouring shrines) used as an approximation.
- Architecture: fairly small; three-tier entrance gopuram; Sopana vimana.
- Name: "gathering of the devas" — the Goddess is Kadalmagal, born of the ocean; all the devas were present at the churning, hence Devanar Thogai.
- Festival: Thirumangai Alvar Mangalasasana Utsavam (Thai) Garuda Sevai.
