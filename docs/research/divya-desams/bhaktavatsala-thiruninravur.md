# Thiruninravur — Bhaktavatsala Perumal (Thiruninravur, Tiruvallur, Tamil Nadu)

Thondai Nadu Divya Desam. Assigned id: `bhaktavatsala-thiruninravur`. Master list correct.

## Facts
- Vishnu as Bhaktavatsala Perumal, standing, facing east; consort Ennai Petra Thayar, also
  called Sudhavalli, in her own shrine (Wikipedia; Dinamalar; greenmesg).
- Town on Chennai's western outskirts (near Avadi), Tiruvallur district.
- History: believed built by the Pallavas in the late 8th century, with later contributions
  from the Medieval Cholas and Vijayanagara kings (Wikipedia). A blog claims ~1500 years —
  not used. Administered by the Tamil Nadu HR&CE; the Pedda Jeeyar of Tirupati noted as
  hereditary trustee in one source (not used).
- Gateway tower: Wikipedia says four tiers, another source five; main tower attributed to the
  Vijayanagara period. Text says "Vijayanagara-period gateway tower" without tier count.
- Tank: Varuna Pushkarini (Dinamalar); Varuna is said to have worshipped Vishnu here.
- Name: also called Srinivasa Kshetram, as Vishnu is said to have stayed here (search text).
- Legend of the Thayar: Lakshmi came to this place; Samudra Raja (the ocean king, her father)
  followed and addressed her as "Ennai petra thayar" — "the mother who bore me" — which became
  her name here (sources differ in detail; phrased as sthala purana).
- Thirumangai Alvar: tradition says he passed without singing here (versions differ on why);
  the Lord is said to have followed him; his two pasurams on this Lord are in Periya
  Thirumozhi 2.5.2 (sung at Thirukkadalmallai/Mamallapuram) and 7.10.5 (sung at
  Thirukkannamangai) (ramanuja.org; ibiblio SriPedia).
- Hridayaleeswarar (Shiva) temple in the same town, associated with Poosalar Nayanar who built
  a temple "in his heart" (search text) — mentioned only in passing.
- Festivals: Wikipedia notes six daily rituals and three yearly festivals; names not retrieved.
  Text mentions Brahmotsavam generally and Vaikunta Ekadashi only.
- Coordinates: not confirmed by search; approximate 13.114, 80.027 (Thiruninravur town / temple
  area). **Approximate — verify against the Wikipedia infobox.**
- Thiruninravur railway station on the Chennai Central–Arakkonam suburban line (general).
- century: 8.
