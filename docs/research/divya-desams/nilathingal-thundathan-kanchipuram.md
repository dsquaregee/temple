# Thiru Nilathingal Thundam — Nilathingal Thundathan Perumal (within the Ekambareswarar temple)

Thondai Nadu Divya Desam. Assigned id: `nilathingal-thundathan-kanchipuram`. Master list correct.

## Facts
- A Vishnu shrine inside the Shaiva Ekambareswarar temple, Kanchipuram, in the first precinct
  around the main sanctum, to the south-west (Wikipedia).
- Presiding deity: Nilathingal Thundathan (Nilathingal Thunda Perumal); consort named
  Nilathingal Thunda Nayagi (Wikipedia) or Ner Oruvar Illa Valli Thayar (greenmesg.org).
  Wikipedia: no separate consort shrine and no separate festival image.
- Name: "the Lord of the moonlight piece" — linked with the cool rays of the Moon.
- Legends (variants): (1) Parvati did penance at Kanchi to win Shiva; Vishnu took the crescent
  from Shiva's head and reflected its cool rays on the mango tree and Parvati (Wikipedia /
  holydham); variant: protecting her sand lingam from the Ganga. (2) In the churning of the
  ocean, when the Lord took the Vishwaroopa form, the Moon's rays fell on his throat/he was
  comforted by them (greenmesg; Google Groups series). Shiva is listed as pratyaksham.
- Vimanam: Purusha Sukta (Surya) Vimanam; tank: Chandra Pushkarini (greenmesg.org).
- Mangalasasanam: Thirumangai Alvar, 1 pasuram (greenmesg.org).
- Host temple (Ekambareswarar): Pancha Bhoota Sthalam (earth); Vijayanagara rayagopuram
  (Krishnadevaraya, 1509), 11 tiers; Panguni Uthiram Brahmotsavam (repo ekambareswarar.json;
  Wikipedia). Note: the "59 m gopuram" in the search snippet refers to the host temple.
- Coordinates: host temple, 12.8475, 79.6997 (repo ekambareswarar.json; Wikipedia
  12°50′51″N 79°42′00″E).
- century: 8.
