# Purushottama Perumal (Thirukkarambanur / Uthamar Koil), Uthamarkovil

Master-list entry confirmed: Thirukkarambanur (Uthamar Kovil) — Purushottaman, Uthamarkovil, Tiruchirappalli, Tamil Nadu. Region: Chola Nadu.

- Also called Thirukkarambanur and Bhikshandar Koil; on the outskirts of Tiruchirappalli, about 4 km from Srirangam.
- A Trimurti temple: shrines of Vishnu (Purushottama with Poornavalli Thayar), Shiva as Bhikshatana with Parvati, and Brahma with Saraswati — six sannidhis for the deities and consorts.
- Moolavar: Purushottama Perumal, reclining in Bhujanga sayanam, facing east.
- Thayar: Poornavalli Thayar.
- Mangalasasanam: Thirumangai Alvar — 1 pasuram; he calls the Lord "Karambanur Uthaman".
- Vimana: Udyoga Vimanam. Tank: Kadamba Theertham (legend: formed when Brahma bathed/worshipped a kadamba tree raised by Vishnu). Kapala Theertham also named in a Shiva legend.
- Name: tradition holds Vishnu appeared as a kadamba tree; Kadambanur became Karambanur. "Uthamar" from Purushottama, the highest (uttama) of beings.
- Legend: Shiva, after severing one of Brahma's heads, bore the skull as a begging bowl; at Karambanur Lakshmi (Poornavalli, "she who fills") filled his bowl and relieved him.
- Tradition: Thirumangai Alvar lived here for a time while supervising renovation works at Srirangam.
- History: Wikipedia lists the Cholas as builders; Dravidian style. Administered by HR&CE.
- Coordinates used: 10.877, 78.703 (Wikipedia infobox 10°52′38″N 78°42′11″E).
- Not verified: festival calendar specifics.
