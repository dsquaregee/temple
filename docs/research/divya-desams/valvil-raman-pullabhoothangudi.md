# Valvil Ramar Temple, Thirupullabhoothangudi

Assigned id: `valvil-raman-pullabhoothangudi`. Master list name/place confirmed (village near
Swamimalai / Kumbakonam, Thanjavur district).

Note: research limited to search-result text (WebFetch unavailable; search budget exhausted).

## Facts
- Divya Desam of Chola Nadu; Vishnu worshipped as Valvil Ramar (Rama "of the mighty bow"); consort
  Potramaraiyal (Lakshmi / Sita). (Hemambujavalli = Sanskrit form of "golden lotus" name — not
  confirmed by sources; not used.) [1][2]
- Moolavar reclining in Bhujanga sayanam facing east, under the Shobhana Vimanam; described as the
  only Perumal temple where Rama is shown in reclining (sayana) posture. [2][3]
- Alvar: Thirumangai Alvar, in his Periya Thirumozhi (pasuram count not verified). [2]
- Legend: Rama performed the last rites of Jatayu, the eagle-king, here — linked to the village's name
  ("pul" = bird). Lakshmi is said to have emerged from the golden-lotus temple tank during the rites. [2][4]
- Temple tank: Jatayu Theertham. Sacred tree: Punnai. [1][5]
- History: built by the Medieval Cholas, with later additions by other kings (Wikipedia). [1]
- Coordinates (Wikipedia): 10°58′17″N 79°18′12″E → 10.971, 79.303. [2]
- Not verified: festival calendar, gopuram tiers, inscriptions.
