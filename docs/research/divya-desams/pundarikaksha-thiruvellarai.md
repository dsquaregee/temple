# Pundarikakshan Perumal, Thiruvellarai

Master-list entry confirmed: Thiruvellarai — Pundarikakshan, Thiruvellarai, Tiruchirappalli, Tamil Nadu. Region: Chola Nadu.

- About 22 km from Tiruchirappalli, past Mannachanallur on the Thuraiyur road.
- Moolavar: Pundarikakshan, standing, facing east. Named "Mayavan" in Dakshinayana and "Thai Matha Nayagan" in Uttarayana (local usage).
- Thayar: Pankajavalli (also given as Shenbagavalli / Pankaya Selvi).
- Mangalasasanam: Periyalvar (NDP 71 and 192–201 — the "kappidal" decad; 11 verses by reference numbers) and Thirumangai Alvar (count not confirmed).
- Built on a ~15 m (50 ft) artificial rise of white rock.
- Two stepped entrances to the sanctum: Uttarayana Vasal (open mid-January to mid-July / Thai–Ani) and Dakshinayana Vasal (Aadi–Margazhi).
- Vimana: Vimalakruthi Vimanam. Rajagopuram unfinished.
- Two rock-cut caves with three inscriptions: two of Nandivarman II (732–796) and one of Dantivarman (796–847) — Pallava.
- Swastika-shaped tank (well) outside the complex, built c. 800 CE by Kamban Araiyan in Dantivarman's reign.
- Pallava sculptures of Narasimha and Varaha; Vijayanagara/Nayak wall paintings in the Pundarikakshan shrine, some surviving.
- Later contributions: Medieval Cholas, later Pandyas, Vijayanagara, Madurai Nayaks.
- Legend: tradition credits the founding to King Sibi Chakravarti; some local tradition holds it older than Srirangam (not scholarly).
- Coordinates used: 10.972, 78.668 (approximate — infobox not fetchable this session; VERIFY).
- Not verified: Thirumangai pasuram count; festival calendar.
