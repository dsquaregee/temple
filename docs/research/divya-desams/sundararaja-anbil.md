# Sundararaja Perumal (Vadivazhagiya Nambi), Anbil

Master-list entry confirmed: Thiru Anbil — Vadivazhagiya Nambi / Sundararaja, Anbil, Tiruchirappalli, Tamil Nadu. Region: Chola Nadu.

- On the banks of the Kollidam (Coleroon), near Lalgudi, Tiruchirappalli district. Also known as Mandukapuri, Tirumaalayanturai, Brahmapuri.
- Moolavar: Sundararaja Perumal (Vadivazhagiya Nambi — "the handsome one"), reclining (Bhujanga sayanam per Divya Desam lists); the two consorts hold his feet so that the feet are hidden from view.
- Thayar: Azhagiyavalli / Sundaravalli Thayar.
- Mangalasasanam: Thirumazhisai Alvar (table of his 20 temples includes Thiru Anbil). Pasuram count not confirmed here (traditionally a single verse in Nanmugan Thiruvandhadhi naming Anbil with Arangam, Kudanthai, Vehka, Evvul — from memory, not verified in this session).
- Vimana: Tharaka Vimanam. Tank: Manduka Theertham.
- Sthala purana: Brahma, proud of his power of creation, was cursed by Vishnu to be born on earth; repenting, he prayed here and was relieved.
- History: believed built by Cholas (late 8th c.); the Anbil copper plates record Chola generosity; Aniruddha Brahmarayar, prime minister of Sundara Chola, was from Anbil.
- Festivals: Theerthavari festival in Masi (Feb–Mar) is most prominent; Vaikunta Ekadashi. Administered by HR&CE.
- Coordinates used: 10.868, 78.882 (Wikipedia infobox 10°52′04″N 78°52′56″E).
- Not verified: facing direction; pasuram number.
