# Thiruvazhmarban Perumal Temple, Thiruppathisaram (thiruvazhmarban-thiruppathisaram)

Brief 1-2 search verification of the existing English entry (resume mode).

## Facts
- One of the 108 Divya Desams; ancient name Thiruvanparisaram; Malai Nadu group. Nammalvar's mangalasasanam.
- Deity: Thiruvazhmarban (also Kuralappa Perumal), seated; consort Kamalavalli (Lakshmi on his chest, so no separate Thayar shrine).
- Image reportedly about 9 ft tall, of katusarkara yogam (mustard and jaggery paste); no abhishekam is performed. Thenkalai worship tradition.
- Distance from Nagercoil varies by source: about 3.5 to 6 km north-east (the entry says about 5 km). Coordinates used: 8.2086 N, 77.4472 E (not restated by search; consistent with the village location east of Nagercoil).
- Festivals: Adi Swati (July-August) recalls the Lord taking Kulasekhara Alvar to Vaikuntha (per Dinamalar listing).
- Weakly sourced / unverified in this pass (Wikipedia tags the mother claim "citation needed"): that Thiruppathisaram is the home of Nammalvar's mother Udaya Nangai; the 1139 CE inscription; Thirumalai Nayak creating the tank; identification of the legendary king with Kulasekhara Alvar. Treat as tradition.
