# Azhagiya Singar (Mukunda Nayakan) Temple, Thiruvelukkai, Kanchipuram

Region: Thondai Nadu. Master-list name/place confirmed. No dedicated Wikipedia article (disambiguation only).

## Deities
- Moolavar: Azhagiya Singar — Narasimha; also called Mukunda Nayakan.
- Posture/facing: sources differ — one gives yoga (seated) posture facing east; another seated facing west; one notes the deity was originally standing and east-facing and later became west-facing and seated, as he appeared before sage Bhrigu as Azhagiya Singar. Entry says "seated" and avoids facing.
- Thayar: Amruthavalli, also Velukkaivalli.

## Alvars
- Pey Alvar and Thirumangai Alvar. Pasuram count not confirmed.

## Location
- Kanchipuram, near the Ashtabujakaram temple.
- Coordinates: Wikipedia (Tiruvelukkai) 12°49'20"N 79°42'24"E (12.8222, 79.7067). Used 12.822, 79.707. Smallest Divya Desam in Kanchipuram; very near Ashtabhuja temple.

## Architecture
- Vimana: Kanaka Vimanam. Tank: Kanaka Saras.
- One source notes the temple is in modest/worn condition.

## Legends
- Saraswati sent a Kapalika to destroy Brahma's yagna; Vishnu as Narasimha (Azhagiya Singar) vanquished him.
- Name: Vel (desire) + irukkai (dwelling) — the Lord came to stay here of his own wish: Thiru Vel-irukkai → Thiruvelukkai.

## Worship / festivals
- Thenkalai tradition; Vaikhanasa agama.
- Annual festival in Margazhi.
