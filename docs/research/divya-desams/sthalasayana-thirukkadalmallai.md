# Sthalasayana Perumal Temple, Thirukkadalmallai (Mamallapuram) — research notes

Region: Thondai Nadu. One of the 108 Divya Desams. A living temple in Mamallapuram town —
distinct from the UNESCO-inscribed Group of Monuments (app: unesco=false).

Note: research via search-result text only.

## Deity
- Presiding: Sthalasayana Perumal — Vishnu reclining on the bare ground (sthala sayana),
  not on the serpent Adishesha; the sage Pundarika is depicted standing in the sanctum.
- Consort: Nilamangai Thayar.

## Alvars
- Thirumangai Alvar (26 pasurams) and Bhoothathalvar (1) — 27 in total.
- Believed to be the birthplace of Bhoothathalvar; his shrine stands near the tank opposite
  the temple.

## Location
- Coordinates (Wikipedia): 12°37′0″N 80°11′55″E → 12.6167, 80.1986.

## History
- Believed built by the Pallavas; later contributions from the Medieval Cholas, Vijayanagara
  kings and Madurai Nayaks. (No specific inscription confirmed.)

## Architecture
- Tank: Pundarika Pushkarini.

## Sthala purana (tradition)
- Pundarika Maharishi gathered lotuses to offer to Vishnu on the Milk Ocean and set about
  emptying the sea to reach him; Vishnu came as an old man, sent the sage for food, and on
  his return was found reclining on the ground — the Sthalasayana form.

## Festivals
- Vaikunta Ekadashi; the birth-star festival of Bhoothathalvar.
