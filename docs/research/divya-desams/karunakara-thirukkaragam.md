# Thirukkaragam — Karunakara Perumal (within the Ulagalantha Perumal temple, Kanchipuram)

Thondai Nadu Divya Desam. Assigned id: `karunakara-thirukkaragam`. Master list correct.
One of the four Divya Desams housed in the Ulagalantha Perumal temple complex.

## Facts
- Presiding deity: Karunakara Perumal (Karunakaran), seated on Adisesha; consort Padmamani
  Nachiyar, also called Ramamani Thayar (greenmesg.org; incredibleindia.gov.in; Google Groups
  108 Divya Desam series).
- Facing direction: sources conflict (south vs north) — omitted.
- Location in complex: third precinct (prakaram) of the Ulagalantha temple (greenmesg.org).
- Legend: the sage Garga performed penance here and obtained knowledge; the place was named
  Garagaham after him, later Kaaragam (greenmesg.org; astroved; templepurohit). One variant
  names the sage Kaaraha.
- Tank: Agraya (Akraya) Theertham. Vimanam: Vamana Vimanam (some say Ramya Vimanam)
  (greenmesg.org; temples-india blog).
- Mangalasasanam: Thirumangai Alvar (incredibleindia; greenmesg) — pasuram count not confirmed.
- Shrines believed once independent temples (incredibleindia.gov.in).
- Coordinates: host temple, 12.8392, 79.7050.
