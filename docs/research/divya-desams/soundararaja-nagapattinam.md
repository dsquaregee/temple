# Soundararaja Perumal Temple, Nagapattinam (Thirunagai)

Divya Desam of Chola Nadu. Research note for `soundararaja-nagapattinam`.
(WebFetch unavailable in this session; facts below rely on WebSearch result text.)

- **Deities**: moolavar Neelamega Perumal, standing, facing east, holding a mace (gada); utsavar Soundararajan (Soundarya Rajan). Thayar: Soundaryavalli (Soundaravalli); utsava Thayar Gajalakshmi.
- **Mangalasasanam**: Thirumangai Alvar (Periya Thirumozhi). His verses here are in the nayaki mood, admiring the beauty of the lord and comparing it with the lords of other Divya Desams. Pasuram count: sources disagree (one says 10) — not stated in content.
- **Coordinates**: 10°45′38″N 79°50′24″E → **10.7606, 79.8400** (Wikipedia infobox).
- **History**: Wikipedia — traditionally credited to the Medieval Cholas in the late 8th century, with later contributions of the Thanjavur Nayaks.
- **Architecture**: five-tiered rajagopuram; granite compound wall; vimanam named Soundarya Vimanam (blog/Google Groups listing); tank Sara Pushkarini.
- **Legends**: sages Markandeya and Dhruva, and Bhudevi, are said to have received darshan here. Local tradition links the town's name (Nagai / Nagapattinam) to Adisesha, king of the Nagas, who worshipped here.
- **Festivals**: chariot festival in Chittirai — most prominent (Wikipedia). Panguni Uthiram marks the celestial wedding of Soundararaja Perumal and Soundaryavalli Thayar. In Aadi a festival brings the lord to a hall to hear the hymns of Thirumangai Alvar.
- **Not verified**: exact pasuram count; Adisesha penance details beyond the naming tradition.
