# Navamukundan — Thirunavai (Thirunavaya), Malai Nadu

Research note. WebFetch failed (DNS/proxy); facts from WebSearch result text. The search budget ran out, so coordinates are unconfirmed.

- Temple: Thirunavaya Navamukunda Temple, Tirunavaya, Malappuram district, Kerala, on the north bank of the Bharathapuzha.
- Region: Malai Nadu Divya Desam.
- Deity: Navamukunda (Vishnu). Consort: Malarmangai Nachiyar; Lakshmi has a separate sreekovil — unusual among Kerala Vishnu temples.
- Alvars: Nammalvar and Thirumangai Alvar (Grokipedia; Divine Traveller mentions Thirumangai).
- Mamankam: medieval festival held every 12 years on the riverbank by the temple, of great political significance (Zamorin era).
- Karkidaka Vavu: bali tarpanam (ancestral rites) on the riverbank draws pilgrims from northern Kerala.
