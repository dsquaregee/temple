# Imayavarappan — Thiruchenkundrur (Thrichittatt, Chengannur), Malai Nadu

Research note. WebFetch to Wikipedia/temple sites failed in this session (DNS/proxy); facts below come from
WebSearch result text. The shared web-search budget ran out mid-research, so several details stayed unconfirmed (marked).

- Temple: Thrichittatt (Thrichittattu) Maha Vishnu Temple, Chengannur, Alappuzha district, Kerala; Divya Desam name Thiruchenkundrur (the river name Chitraru / Thiruchittaru is the source of "Thrichittatt" — tradition; not separately confirmed).
- Region: Malai Nadu (Kerala) Divya Desam.
- Moolavar: Imayavarappan (Imayavarappa Perumal), standing. Consort: Sengamalavalli Thayar (Dinamalar listing).
- Facing: Dinamalar says west — single source; not used in content.
- Wikipedia notes the image holds the conch in the right hand (an unusual feature) — single source; not used.
- Vimana: Jagajjyoti (Jega Jyoti) Vimana (Dinamalar). Tank: Sankha (Sangu) Theertham, to the right past the entrance arch.
- Alvar: Nammalvar (Thiruvaimozhi). Exact decad/pasuram count NOT confirmed in this session (commonly given as one decad of Thiruvaimozhi) — left out of content.
- Pancha Pandava tradition: one of five Chengannur-area Vishnu temples each attributed to a Pandava — Thrichittatt (Yudhishthira), Puliyur (Bhima), Aranmula (Arjuna), Thiruvanvandoor (Nakula), Thrikodithanam (Sahadeva).
- Legend: Yudhishthira did penance here for the half-truth about "Ashwatthama" that brought down Drona (tradition).
- Dating: inscriptions said to place the structure between the 8th and 12th centuries CE (Wikipedia, per search text). century = 8.
- Administration: Travancore Devaswom Board.
- Coordinates: 9°19′36″N 76°36′15″E = 9.32667, 76.60417 (Wikipedia infobox, via search). About 2 km NW of Chengannur town (hindu-blog).
- Access: Chengannur railway station nearest.
- Festivals: not confirmed specifically — content mentions only the annual utsavam generically and Vaikunta Ekadashi as a Vaishnava observance.
