# Yathothkari Perumal Temple, Thiruvekka (Kanchipuram) — research note

- Region: Thondai Nadu; one of the 108 Divya Desams. In Chinna Kanchipuram (eastern Kanchipuram).
- Moolavar: Yathothkari Perumal, also Sonna Vannam Seidha Perumal ("the lord who did as he was told"); reclining (bhujanga sayanam) on Adisesha, facing west. Unusual reversed posture: head resting on his left hand ("maaru sayanam").
- Consort: Komalavalli Thayar; her shrine faces the central shrine, on the eastern side.
- Mangalasasanam: Poigai Alvar, Peyalvar, Thirumazhisai Alvar, Nammalvar, Thirumangai Alvar (greenmesg gives 15 pasurams — single source, count not used in copy).
- Poigai Alvar is traditionally held to have been born in the temple's lotus tank (Poigai Pushkarini), outside the temple complex.
- Coordinates: 12°49′27″N 79°42′44″E → 12.8242, 79.7122 (Wikipedia infobox).
- History: mentioned in the Sangam text Perumpanatruppadai; credited to the Pallavas, with later contributions by the Medieval Cholas and Vijayanagara kings. Three wall inscriptions: two of Kulothunga Chola I, one of Rajadhiraja Chola (endowments).
- Architecture: three-tiered rajagopuram; Vedasara Vimanam over the sanctum.
- Legends: (1) Saraswati, slighted at Brahma's sacrifice (Ashwamedha) at Kanchi, rushed as the Vegavati river; Vishnu lay across the river as a dam — name Vekka/Thiruvekka traced to this. (2) The Pallava king banished Kanikannan, disciple of Thirumazhisai Alvar; the Alvar left with him and asked the lord to roll up his serpent bed and follow; the lord did; when the king repented the lord returned at the Alvar's word — hence Yathothkari / Sonna Vannam Seidha Perumal.
- Festivals: Brahmotsavam in Panguni (Wikipedia gives April–May; Vedadhara Panguni); Poigai Alvar's birth star, Aippasi Thiruvonam.
- Not verified (omitted): exact pasuram count; tank dimensions.
