# Thirumanikkoodam (Varadaraja Perumal), Thirunangur — research notes

Master list entry confirmed.

- Region: Chola Nadu; one of the 108 Divya Desams; one of the eleven Thirunangur Tirupatis.
- Moolavar: Varadaraja Perumal, also Manikkooda Nayakan; standing (nindra thirukkolam), facing east.
- Thayar: Thirumamagal Nachiyar (Sridevi) with Bhudevi (TN temples project lists Bhoonayagi); one source notes there is no separate Thayar shrine — the consorts are in the sanctum with the Lord.
- Mangalasasanam: Thirumangai Alvar. (One web source claims another Alvar also sang it — not confirmed; omitted.)
- Coordinates (Wikipedia infobox "Thirumanikkoodam"): 11°10′46″N 79°47′17″E → 11.1794, 79.7881.
- Vimanam: Kanaka (Wikipedia infobox "tower: Kanaka"). Tank: Chandra Pushkarini.
- Legends: tradition holds the Moon (Chandra) was blessed/relieved here, giving the tank its name; a related telling says Surya and Chandra, frightened (during Shiva's dance at Nangur), hid in two tanks they made — the Surya Pushkarini (Thiruthetriyambalam) and the Chandra Pushkarini. Shared Thirunangur legend of Vishnu's eleven forms at Shiva's request. (Variant claims about Arjuna conflict between sources — omitted.)
- Festivals: Thirunangur Garuda Sevai in Thai (day after Thai Amavasai) at Thirumanimadam; Thirumangai Alvar mangalasasana utsavam.
- Location: Thirunangur, about 8 km east of Sirkazhi.
- Not verified: inscriptions, dynasty-specific history, gopuram tiers, pasuram count.
