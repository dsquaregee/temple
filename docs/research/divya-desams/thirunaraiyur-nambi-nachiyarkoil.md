# Thirunaraiyur Nambi Temple (Nachiyar Koil), Thirunaraiyur

Divya Desam, Chola Nadu. Master list name/place confirmed (Nachiyar Koil / Natchiarkoil near Kumbakonam).

## Facts
- Deities: Vishnu as Thirunaraiyur Nambi / Srinivasa Perumal; the Goddess as Nachiyar — Vanjulavalli (Lakshmi; Wikipedia: Nachiyar as Niladevi). The Goddess is treated as the main deity, hence the name Nachiyar Koil.
- Coordinates (Wikipedia infobox): 10°54′57″N 79°26′44″E → 10.9157, 79.4456.
- Alvar: Thirumangai Alvar (mangalasasanam); he calls the shrine "Mani Mada Koil" and the Lord "Naraiyur Nindra Nambi". Pasuram index lists verses from 1078, 1329, 1470 onward (exact count not confirmed — not stated in content).
- Tradition (travel blog, single source): Thirumangai Alvar received the Pancha Samskaram (Vaishnava initiation) from Thirunaraiyur Nambi here. Phrased as tradition.
- Builder: Wikipedia lists Kochengat Cholan; described as the only Vishnu temple attributed to him (his other foundations are Shiva temples). A blog says Thirumangai devotes a decad of the Periya Thirumozhi to Kochengan's building of the temple. Dating to "3rd century" by one devotional source — not used.
- Vimana: Srinivasa Vimanam. Tank: Manimukta Pushkarini.
- Rajagopuram: five tiers (single blog source; used cautiously).
- Kal Garuda: stone Garuda in its own shrine; carried in procession twice a year (4th day of the Margazhi and Panguni festivals); tradition holds the image grows heavier — four bearers at the shrine, 64 (some say 128) by the time it leaves the temple. Believed to relieve Navagraha dosha.
- Legend: Lakshmi appeared as Vanjulavalli, daughter of the sage Medhavi, a devotee of Vishnu who wished the Lord as his son-in-law; Vishnu came and wedded her.
- Location: Nachiyar Koil, roughly 8–10 km south-east of Kumbakonam.

## Not verified
- Exact pasuram count; posture/facing direction; Brahmotsavam month (only the Margazhi and Panguni Kal Garuda days confirmed).
