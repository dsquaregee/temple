# Kripasamudra Perumal (Arulmakadal) — Thirusirupuliyur

Master list confirmed: Thirusirupuliyur (Sirupuliyur), Nannilam taluk, Tiruvarur district, near Kollumangudi. Wikipedia titles it "Sthalasayana Perumal Temple, Tirusirupuliyur".

- One of the 108 Divya Desams, Chola Nadu; praised in the Nalayira Divya Prabandham.
- Moolavar: Sthalasayana / Arulmakadal / Kripasamudra ("ocean of grace") Perumal, in a small reclining form (Bala Sayanam), facing south.
- Consort: Thirumamagal Nachiyar.
- Mangalasasanam: Thirumangai Alvar. (Pasuram count not verified — left out.)
- Coordinates (Wikipedia): 10°59′26″N 79°40′10″E → 10.9906, 79.6694.
- About 2 km from Kollumangudi, ~17 km from Mayiladuthurai, ~10 km from Karaikal.
- Architecture: four-tiered rajagopuram; vimana named Nanda Vardhana Vimanam. Sthala vriksha: vilvam.
- Legends: sage Vyaghrapada (tiger-footed) worshipped here; because the aged sages could not take in a vast form, Vishnu showed himself in a small reclining form — hence Siru (small) + Puli (tiger) + Ur (place). Images of Vyaghrapada and Kanva are in the sanctum. Tradition: the Lord here gave protection to Adisesha from Garuda. Tradition: Thirumangai Alvar remarked on the small image and was told to see the Lord in his larger form at Thirukkannamangai.
- Festivals: three daily rituals and three yearly festivals per Wikipedia (not named in available text). Not verified in detail.
- Not found: inscriptions, tank name, dated history.
