# Kola Valvill Ramar Temple, Thiruvelliyangudi

Divya Desam of Chola Nadu. Research note for `kolavilli-raman-thiruvelliyangudi`.
(WebFetch unavailable; facts rely on WebSearch result text.)

- **Deities**: moolavar Kola Valvilli Raman, reclining (bhujanga sayanam), facing east; utsavar Sringara Sundaran; Thayar Maragathavalli.
- **Mangalasasanam**: Thirumangai Alvar, 10 pasurams.
- **Coordinates**: 11°3′25″N 79°26′36″E → **11.0569, 79.4433** (Wikipedia). About 19 km from Kumbakonam, on the north bank of the Pazhavaru.
- **Architecture**: Dravidian; Pushkalavartaka vimanam; four tirthas — Sukra, Brahma, Indra, Parasara (Parasara the main tank). Garuda shown with four hands holding discus and conch (single-source, presented as local tradition). Administered by HR&CE.
- **History**: one source credits the Medieval Cholas with later Vijayanagara expansions (not presented as firm).
- **Legends**: Shukra (Velli = Venus), who as an insect blocked Mahabali's kamandalu spout and lost an eye when Vamana cleared it with a blade of darbha grass, worshipped here and regained sight; Vishnu promised to remain — hence Velliyangudi; one of the Vaishnava Navagraha sthalams (Venus). Mayan, architect of the asuras, asked to see Rama without conch and discus; Rama appeared as Kola Valvilli Raman, giving them to Garuda. Sage Parasara worshipped here. Historic name Bhargava kshetra.
- **Festivals**: not confirmed; content keeps to Vaikunta Ekadashi / Rama Navami-type general phrasing as tradition.
