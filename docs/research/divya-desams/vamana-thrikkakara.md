# Vamanamoorthy Temple, Thrikkakara (vamana-thrikkakara)

Brief verification of the existing English entry (resume mode).

## Facts
- One of the 108 Divya Desams in Kerala (Malai Nadu), near Ernakulam/Kochi; presiding deity Vamana, standing, facing south; moolavar called Katkkarai Appan / Thrikkakara Appan (Onathappan).
- Name explained as "Thiru Kaal Kara", the place of the holy foot.
- Onam: ten-day festival beginning with Kodiyettu (flag hoisting); historically a month-long festival here.
- Present structure largely rebuilt in the early 20th century; managed by the Travancore Devaswom Board; said to be about two millennia old by tradition.
- Coordinates: 10.035 N, 76.329 E (Wikipedia, Thrikkakara town entry); entry uses 10.034, 76.329.
- Nammalvar's mangalasasanam: listed by a travel source and the Divya Desam tradition; the pasuram count was not found.
- Not independently confirmed in this pass: Perunchelvanayaki as consort name, the roughly eighteen Chera-era inscriptions, the Shiva shrine attributed to Mahabali.
