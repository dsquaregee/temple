# Paadal Petra Sthalams — canonical reference list (276)

Companion to `paadal-petra-sthalams.md` (the Wave C plan). This is the region-by-region
membership list, the target for content generation.

## Provenance & reliability

Compiled via **WebSearch** against **shivatemples.com** (adopted as the canonical source
for membership and ordering — its per-region index pages, `/scnaadu/`, `/pnaadu/`,
`/nnaadu/`, `/knaadu/`, `/tnaadu/`), cross-checked against koyil.com, templenet.com,
Wikipedia, veludharan and aanmeegam. Direct page fetch is blocked in this environment, so
the lists were rebuilt from many search summaries of those pages.

**Reliability by column:**
- **Temple name, region, town/village — reliable** (follow shivatemples.com's numbering).
- **District — mostly reliable** (current boundaries; a few best-effort post-2019-split).
- **Presiding deity — reliable where named; some alternates in parentheses.**
- **Goddess name & native Tamil name — PARTIALLY VERIFIED.** Entries the searches could
  not confirm are marked `?`. These get verified per temple at content-generation time
  (the pipeline requires research notes before content), so treat them as a starting
  point, not final.

**Counts & the 275/276 question.** The regional counts below sum to **275**; the
canonical total is usually given as **276** (Wikipedia itself says 275 in one place). The
variance comes from boundary cases — Chola-south 128 vs 129 (tntemplesproject), Nadu Nadu
22 vs 23 (shivatemples adds Kiliyanur), Thondai 31 vs 32 (± Srikalahasti), Vada Nadu 5 vs
7. shivatemples.com catalogues the **266 in Tamil Nadu + Puducherry**; the ~10 outside
(Srikalahasti, Srisailam, Gokarna, Thiruvanchikulam, the 2 in Sri Lanka, and the 4–5 in
the far north) bring the total to 275–276. **Settle the exact total against shivatemples
before the circuit is finalised.**

## In-catalog status

**42 of these are already in the catalog**, verified from repo content;
each region's shipped members are listed under its table. The rest are the content target.

> **Two corrections found during this audit (NOT Paadal Petra Sthalams):**
> `keezhaperumpallam-naganathaswamy` (Ketu) and `thingalur-kailasanathar` (Chandran) are
> **Vaippu Sthalams** — mentioned in Appar's hymns but the deity is not named, so they are
> *excluded* from the 276. Both catalog pages currently state they are Paadal Petra
> Sthalams, which is **incorrect** and should be fixed (text → "Vaippu Sthalam", then
> re-run narration). See `paadal-petra-sthalams.md` → "Content corrections".
