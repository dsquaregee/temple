# Full 42-temple catalog (reconstruction, 2026-07-18)

The original Phase-1 long-form catalog was lost to container reclamation; the
[research synthesis](00-research-synthesis.md) preserves its anchors: UNESCO
clusters (Great Living Chola Temples; Hampi; Pattadakal; Mahabalipuram; plus
Hoysala ensembles and Ramappa, inscribed since), named circuits (Pancha Bhoota
Sthalams, Divya Desams, Pancharama Kshetras), the Dravidian architecture
taxonomy (Pallava, Chola, Pandya, Vijayanagara/Nayaka, Hoysala, Chalukya,
Kerala style), and the South-India-first positioning. This file reconstructs
the full 42 from those anchors. Owner expanded content scope from the v1 ten
to the full catalog on 2026-07-18.

## New circuits (3)

- **arupadai-veedu** — The Six Abodes of Murugan: thiruparankundram,
  thiruchendur, palani, swamimalai, thiruthani, pazhamudircholai.
- **pancharama-kshetras** — five Shiva temples of the split Amrita lingam:
  amararama, draksharama, kumararama, ksheerarama, somarama.
- **divya-desams** — "the grand five" of the 108: srirangam, tirumala,
  srivilliputhur, varadaraja (Kanchi), padmanabhaswamy.
