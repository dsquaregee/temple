# Completing the researched 42-temple catalog

The research synthesis anchored a **42-temple launch catalog** on UNESCO clusters,
the Pancha Bhoota Sthalams, Divya Desams, the Hoysala ensembles, and the major
pan-South pilgrimage centres. The synthesis did not enumerate all 42 by name, so
this note records the **16 temples selected to complete the catalog** from those
stated anchors (26 were already shipped). Selection favours canonical,
well-documented, historically or architecturally significant temples of South
India, avoiding sites with access restrictions or contested sensitivities.

## Selected remaining 16

**Arupadai Veedu — the six abodes of Murugan** (new circuit `arupadai-veedu`):
1. `palani-murugan` — Dhandayuthapani, Palani
2. `thiruchendur-murugan` — Subramanya Swamy, Thiruchendur (seashore)
3. `swamimalai-murugan` — Swaminatha Swamy, Swamimalai
4. `thiruparankundram-murugan` — Subramanya Swamy, Thiruparankundram (Madurai)
5. `thiruttani-murugan` — Subramanya Swamy, Thiruttani
6. `pazhamudircholai-murugan` — Solaimalai, near Madurai

**Chalukya & Kakatiya heritage:**
7. `badami-cave-temples` — Chalukya rock-cut, Badami
8. `aihole-durga-temple` — Chalukya, Aihole ("cradle of temple architecture")
   (with the existing Pattadakal → new circuit `badami-chalukya-monuments`)
9. `ramappa-palampet` — Rudreshwara (Ramappa), Kakatiya, **UNESCO 2021**
10. `thousand-pillar-warangal` — Rudreshwara, Hanamkonda, Kakatiya
    (9 + 10 → new circuit `kakatiya-temples`)

**Major pilgrimage centres:**
11. `srisailam-mallikarjuna` — Jyotirlinga + Shakti Peetha, Andhra Pradesh
12. `simhachalam-narasimha` — Varaha Lakshmi Narasimha, Visakhapatnam
13. `lepakshi-veerabhadra` — Vijayanagara, Andhra Pradesh (murals, hanging pillar)
14. `kollur-mookambika` — Shakti/Saraswati, Karnataka
15. `gokarna-mahabaleshwara` — Atmalinga Shiva, Karnataka
16. `chamundeshwari-mysuru` — Shakti Peetha, Chamundi Hill, Mysuru

## Standard (same as the shipped 26)

Each temple ships in all six locales (`en ta te kn ml hi`), with original,
reverent editorial prose (summary + history, architecture, legends, festivals,
experience; visit guidance), facts grounded in well-established history, a
generated hero placeholder, and must pass the content validator, translation QA
(target-script prose), the unit suite, the web build + performance budget, and a
headless-browser render check. Non-English prose is a careful draft under the
owner's standing translation sign-off.
