# Mahalakshmi (Ambabai) Temple, Kolhapur (mahalakshmi-kolhapur)

Circuit: none yet (Ashtadasha Maha Shakti Peetha; one of the three and a half Shakti Peethas of Maharashtra).
District/State: Kolhapur, Maharashtra. Tradition: Shaktism. Old name: Karaveera / Karavira (Karaveerapura); Goddess also called Karaveera Mahalakshmi.

- Goddess + Bhairava: Mahalakshmi, locally **Ambabai**. In the Peetha Nirnaya Tantra tradition quoted by
  greenmesg.org the Devi at Karavira is named *Mahishamardini* and the Bhairava *Krodhisa* (Krodhisha);
  used "Krodhisha" in the content. (Ashtadasha Shakti Peetha Stotram, attributed to Adi Shankaracharya:
  "Kolhapure Mahalakshmi", the sixth/fifth-listed seat depending on the verse numbering; greenmesg cites verse 5.)
- Sati body-part tradition: **the (three) eyes** of Sati fell here (Wikipedia, "Three and a half Shakta pithas":
  "3 eyes"; daanyam.in: "eyes"). Content says "eyes" (no "three") as the safe common denominator.
- Coordinates: **16.699, 74.222** (city-centre/old-city precision). Sources disagree: Wikipedia infobox
  16.70, 74.2333 (rounded, ~1 km east); ambabai.com 16.6949, 74.2229; latitude.to ~16.70, 74.2333.
  Chosen value sits between them and agrees with ambabai.com longitude; accurate to roughly +/-0.5 km.
- Defining legend: Kolhasura / Karveer Mahatmya (a later text narrated through Agastya; Wikipedia dates the
  Kolhapur Mahatmya to c. 1730 CE). Goddess Amba/Mahalakshmi fights demon Kolhasura; his dying wish that the
  place bear his name (hence Kolhapur); alternate telling: he asked her to stay and guard the city.
  Popular Bhrigu-kicks-Vishnu / Lakshmi-leaves-Vaikuntha story (Tirupati link) -- one source says it is of
  recent origin and absent from religious texts; content flags it as "not found in the older texts".
  General Sati/Daksha-yajna / 51-52 pitha narrative retained as framing.
- History: Wikipedia: built by Chalukya king Karnadeva, 634 CE (7th c.). Maharashtra tourism: originally 7th c.,
  present structure 9th c. templenet: deity attested epigraphically in 7th c., temple 10th c.; worship restored
  in 1715 after the rise of the Marathas. One incredibleindia-type guide: renovated by Shilahara and Marathas.
  Royal patronage by Karveer Sansthan (Dasara under royal patronage -- ambabai.com festivals page).
  Contested: historian Paul Dundas (citing a 12th-c. Kannada poet) says the site was originally a Jain temple of
  Chandraprabha -- deliberately **not** put in the app prose (contested, single scholarly claim).
- Architecture: Hemadpanti (Wikipedia infobox); dark stone. Idol: swayambhu black stone, ~3 ft, crowned
  (templenet; tirthayatra.org "monolithic black stone"). Three sanctums with brick-and-mortar shikharas of the
  19th c. (templenet). Stone Ganesha mandapa on a raised platform faces the sanctum; Garuda mandapa
  18th c. (templenet) / built under Daji Pandit 1838-43 (blog) -> content says "18th or 19th century".
  Address: Bhavani Mandap, Mahadwar Road. (Gem idol of 40 kg claim in one source contradicts -- not used.)
- Festivals: Sharadiya Navaratri (Ashwin Shuddha Pratipada to Khandenavami) with Palakhi (palanquin) procession
  of the utsav murti at night (ambabai.com: every Friday 9:30 pm, and the nine Navaratri days); Lalita Panchami
  visit to Tryambuli temple on town outskirts; Vijayadashami palakhi to Dasara Chowk and Panchaganga river under
  Karveer royal patronage; Rathotsav on Chaitra Vadya Pratipada (silver chariot, nagar pradakshina);
  **Kiranotsav** -- setting-sun rays on the idol, twice a year: ambabai.com 31 Jan-2 Feb and 9-11 Nov;
  UNI 30 Jan-3 Feb and 9-13 Nov; (punekarnews says March/September -- outlier). Rays reach the feet first and
  move upward over the following days (hindupad.com).
- Access: Mahadwar Road, old Kolhapur. Railway (Chhatrapati Shahu Maharaj Terminus) 3 km (hotel listing) to 5 km
  (Maharashtra tourism); Kolhapur Airport ~9-10 km; Pune ~240 km, Mumbai ~380 km (Maharashtra tourism).
  Timings reported (third-party): 4:00/4:30 AM to ~10 PM, darshan 4:30 AM-12:30 PM and 1:00-8:30 PM, evening
  aarti ~7:30-8:30 PM (incredibleindia); 4:30 AM-11 PM (myoksha).
- **Not verified:** exact temple coordinate (see above); founding date/patron (634 CE Karnadeva vs 9th/10th c.);
  Shilahara/Yadava involvement; Shesha/Sri Yantra associations; the identities of the three sanctums
  (commonly Mahalakshmi/Mahakali/Mahasaraswati -- not confirmed in searches, so not named in prose);
  sanctum orientation; official timings; official photography rule (only "generally restricted in sanctum"
  from one source); Tripurari Purnima; whether the Stotram verse numbering is 5th or 6th; body-part count
  ("three eyes" vs "eyes"). WebFetch was blocked; all facts are from search-result summaries.
