# Koodalmanikyam Temple, Irinjalakuda (koodalmanikyam-irinjalakuda)

- Deity: Bharata, brother of Rama (Koodalmanikyaswamy / Sangameshwara); described as the only major temple in India dedicated to Bharata; moolavar also understood as Maha Vishnu / Sangameshwara.
- Only one deity: no sub-shrines, not even Ganapati. No deepa-aradhana; three pujas daily and no daily siveli (sheeveli only during the festival). Only flowers in worship (no cosmetic items).
- Coordinates: Wikipedia infobox 10°20′48″N 76°12′04″E = 10.3467, 76.2011. Used 10.3467, 76.2011.
- History: earliest record is a stone inscription of the Chera Perumal king Sthanu Ravi Kulasekhara dated 854/55 CE leasing lands to the temple (two more inscriptions of his regnal years); temple held great lands; associated with the Thachudaya Kaimals until 1971 (land reform). Now administered by the Koodalmanikyam Devaswom.
- Legend: sage Kulipini and rishis performed a yagna; Ganga springs welled up and the rishis merged with the Lord; the Ganga is held present in Kulipini Theertham (tank within compound; four ponds around the temple). Name: a gem (manikyam) from the Kayamkulam raja brought for comparison with the glow on the idol merged into it — "koodal" (merging) + manikyam. Four images legend (Vakkay Kaimal) as Nalambalam.
- Arts: great Koothambalam; centre of Koodiyattam, Koothu, Kathakali, Thullal; Ammannur Chakyars performed here.
- Festival: 10-day Utsavam in Medam (April–May), flag hoisted on Uthram asterism, the day after Thrissur Pooram; 17 elephants in seeveli with panchari melam, two calf elephants beside the lead, seven with gold nettipattam; final day arattu at a river. Nalambalam (Karkidakam), 2nd stop.
- Unverified: lotus-garland (thamaramala) offering — not used.
