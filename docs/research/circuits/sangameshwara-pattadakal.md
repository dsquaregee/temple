# Sangameshwara (Vijayeshvara) Temple, Pattadakal (sangameshwara-pattadakal)

- Coordinates used: 15.950, 75.816. Not individually published; taken from Pleiades/OSM point of the Pattadakal temple area (15.9504, 75.8160) and UNESCO nomination "Temple Area" (15°57'00.7"N 75°48'56.2"E). Approximate to ~100 m.
- Built by Vijayaditya Satyashraya (r. 696-733); linga named Vijayeshvara after him. Oldest temple at Pattadakal (ASI). Left unfinished at his death (734 per one source); work continued later.
- Dravida; square sanctum, antechamber, large pillared hall (rangamandapa, ~20 pillars, partly ruined); ruined Nandi mandapa in front; ASI: nearer Pallava form, no sukanasika. Stands between Galaganatha and Virupaksha.
- Kannada inscription tablet of 1162 CE at the temple (Wikimedia file description).
- UNESCO 1987 (Group of Monuments at Pattadakal). Pattadakal = place of coronation (Wikipedia Pattadakal), on the Malaprabha.
- Ticket covers the Pattadakal complex (existing catalog file for Virupaksha).
