# Triprayar Sree Rama Temple (triprayar-rama)

- Deity: Rama (Sree Ramaswamy, "Thriprayar Thevar"), four-armed, holding conch, discus, bow and garland; traditionally understood as Rama after the slaying of the demon Khara (fierce aspect). Sanctum faces east.
- Location: east bank of the Theevra (Thriprayar puzha / a channel of the Karuvannur river system; the canal later became part of the inland waterway, late 18th c.), Triprayar, Thrissur district, Kerala; ~25 km SW of Thrissur.
- Coordinates: Wikipedia (Thriprayar Temple infobox) 10°24′51″N 76°06′56″E = 10.4142, 76.1155; AroundUs 10.41414, 76.11541 (agrees). Used 10.4142, 76.1155.
- Legend: the four images (Rama, Bharata, Lakshmana, Shatrughna) once worshipped by Krishna at Dwaraka, washed ashore near Chettuva after Dwaraka sank, found by fishermen; local chieftain Vakkayil Kaimal installed them after a prasnam: Rama at Triprayar, Bharata at Irinjalakuda, Lakshmana at Moozhikkulam, Shatrughna at Payammal.
- Hanuman: believed present though without an image; Kadhina Vedi linked to Hanuman's arrival with news of Sita.
- Offerings: Meenoottu (feeding fish in the river), Chathan Bhandaram (to remove evil influences; Chathan / Chathan Swamy associated with the temple).
- Architecture: circular srikovil with copper-covered conical roof; rich wood carvings; sanctum carvings of Ramayana scenes; copper-plated namaskara mandapam with 24 wood-carved panels incl. navagrahas.
- History: originally under the Zamorins of Calicut, later Dutch, Mysore and the rulers of Cochin; owned earlier by Nambudiri families; now Cochin Devaswom Board. No firm founding date (century 17 used: earliest firm historical context = Dutch/Zamorin era; tradition is far older).
- Festivals: Ekadasi in Vrishchikam (Nov–Dec) with procession; Thriprayar Thevar presides over the Devamela of the Arattupuzha Pooram (Meenam, March–April); Nalambalam yatra in Karkidakam (Jul–Aug) begins here; Rama Navami.
- Unverified: exact age of carvings; specific dates of ownership changes.
