# Swarga Brahma Temple, Alampur (swarga-brahma-alampur)

- Coordinates used: 15.8778, 78.1348 (Wikipedia Alampur Navabrahma Temples infobox point 15°52′40.1″N 78°08′05.4″E, whose infobox illustrates Svarga Brahma; Commons camera geotags close by). Behind (west of) Bala Brahma (trawell).
- Group: nine Early (Badami) Chalukya Shiva temples at Alampur, Jogulamba Gadwal dist., Telangana, left bank of the Tungabhadra near the Krishna confluence; ASI-protected; named Bala, Kumara, Arka, Vira, Vishwa, Garuda, Swarga, Padma, Taraka Brahma(eshwara). Tradition: Brahma did penance here and worshipped Shiva; the nine names are the forms in which he is remembered. Standing temples datable from mid-7th c. (ASI); earliest inscription (Vikramaditya I, 654–681) records a linga installed in the Arka Brahma temple. Most sikharas are northern curvilinear (rekha-nagara, 3–4 bhumis, karna-amalakas). Damage in post-14th-c. raids; Islamic dargah compound abuts the NW of the group.
- Coordinates: WebFetch/curl to Commons/OSM/Wikidata blocked in this environment; no per-temple published coordinate found. Point ESTIMATED from Commons camera geotags (Svarga 15.877367,78.135536 & 15.877231,78.135361; Garuda 15.877014,78.135583 heading N; Bala via PICRYL mirror 15.87751,78.13579), the Wikipedia group point (15.877806,78.134833) and relative positions in trawell (Garuda south of Bala; Swarga behind Bala; Padma behind Swarga; Taraka behind the museum; Vishwa/Vira/Arka/Kumara row to the north, Kumara opposite museum). Accuracy perhaps ±30–50 m; worth checking against the ASI site plan.

- Inscription: engraved over a dvarapala figure; records that Lokaditya Ela Arasa built the temple (devakula) in honour of the queen of the Chalukya king Vinayaditya (r. 681–696). Lokaditya described variously as a feudatory or prince. Commons captions date consecration to c. 704 CE — conflict; written as "late 7th c., reign of Vinayaditya, completed around 700".
- Considered the most richly ornamented temple of the group. East-facing porch of six pillars with amalaka ornament; Nandi on a raised platform facing it; sanctum with circumambulatory passage; curvilinear Nagara tower largely intact.
- Exterior walls: niches flanked by pilasters, 4 each on N and S, 2 on E and W (Commons plan); inside walls also have niches. Sculpture: Shiva as Nataraja, Lingodbhava, Tripurantaka, Gangadhara, Bhikshatana, Dakshinamurti; Vishnu avatars (Vamana); Ganga and Yamuna and a Garuda-naga motif on the doorway; Indra on Airavata. Many figures defaced in later raids.
