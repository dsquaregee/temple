# Veerateeswarar Temple, Vazhuvur (veerateeswarar-vazhuvur)

Circuit: ashta-veerattanam. Tradition: Shaivism. Veerattanam deed: Gaja samhara (flaying of the elephant demon).

- Deity: Shiva as Veerateeswarar (Krittivasar); consort Bala Gujambigai (Wikipedia) / Ilankili (Ilankizhai) Nayaki (TN Temples Project).
- Famous bronze of Gajasamharamurti, generally dated to Rajaraja I's reign — the centre of worship here.
- Legend: Darukavanam — the sages, devoted only to rites and penance, were humbled by Shiva as Bhikshatana (with Vishnu as Mohini); they sent forth a tiger, serpent and a rogue elephant; Shiva vanquished the elephant and wore its hide (Krittivasar). Historical name Darukavanam.
- Present masonry Chola, 9th c.; later Thanjavur Nayak expansions (Wikipedia). Village named in the Tevaram.
- Location: ~9 km south of Mayiladuthurai (TN Temples Project). Separate Veerabhadrar (Vazhikaraiyan) temple in the village — not this one.
- Coordinates: Wikipedia infobox 11°02′42″N 79°38′16″E -> 11.0450, 79.6378.
