# Trimbakeshwar Temple, Trimbak (trimbakeshwar-nashik)

- Deity: Shiva as Tryambakeshwar (the three-eyed lord); one of the twelve Jyotirlingas (stotram: "Tryambakam Gautamitate" — on the bank of the Gautami/Godavari).
- Linga: in a hollow in the pedestal, three small lingas/faces honoured as Brahma, Vishnu and Shiva (ASI notes a hollow; tradition reads it as three lingas). Covered at times with a jewelled crown/mask held by tradition to be of great antiquity.
- Location: Trimbak town, Nashik district, Maharashtra, c. 28 km from Nashik, at the foot of Brahmagiri hill.
- Coordinates: 19.9322, 73.5308 (Wikipedia infobox 19°55′56″N 73°31′51″E).
- Godavari: physical source on Brahmagiri; ritual origin at Kushavarta tank in Trimbak; Simhastha Kumbh (every 12 years, Nashik–Trimbak; Shaiva akhadas bathe at Kushavarta).
- History: present black-stone temple built by Peshwa Balaji Bajirao (Nanasaheb, Peshwa 1740–61); one source: begun 1755, completed 1786.
- Style: Nagara, black basalt, carved walls, high enclosure.
- Legend: Sage Gautama and Ahalya on Brahmagiri; a cow dies in his field; to atone he worships Shiva, who releases the Ganga here as the Godavari (Gautami); Shiva remains as Tryambaka.
- Rituals: Narayan Nagbali, Kalsarpa Shanti, Tripindi Shraddha performed by Trimbak priests.
- Admission: non-Hindus are not admitted inside the temple (notice at entrance).
- Nearby: samadhi of Sant Nivruttinath (elder brother of Sant Dnyaneshwar) in Trimbak; annual yatra.
- Festivals: Maha Shivaratri, Shravan Mondays, Kartik (Tripuri) Purnima, Simhastha Kumbh — confirm dates locally.
- Getting there: road from Nashik (c. 28 km); Nashik Road railway station; Nashik (Ozar) airport; Mumbai c. 180 km.
