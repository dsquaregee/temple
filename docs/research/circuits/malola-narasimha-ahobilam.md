# Malola Narasimha, Ahobilam
- On Vedadri (Vedachala) hill ~2 km from Upper Ahobilam temple; also called Markanda Lakshmi Kshetra (vedadhara.com).
- Image: Lord seated, left leg folded, right leg hanging; Lakshmi seated on folded leg. "Ma" (Lakshmi) + "lola" (beloved).
- Ahobila Matha tradition: Adivan Satakopa Jeeyar (c.1379–1458 per one source) received the Malola utsava murti; it has accompanied every jeeyar (Azhagiya Singar) since (hindu-blog.com; greenmesg.org).
- Planet: Venus (Shukra) per most lists (one source: Moon).
- Coordinates: NOT FOUND in a published source. Used approximate 15.1330, 78.7420 (estimate ~1 km N/NE of Upper Ahobilam on the Vedadri hill, consistent with "2 km by path") — needs verification.
