# Apathsahayar Temple, Thirupazhanam (apatsahayar-thirupazhanam)

Circuit: saptha-sthanam (2nd stop after Thiruvaiyaru). Tradition: Shaivism.

- Deity: Shiva as Apathsahayar (Apatsahayeswarar, "helper in distress"); consort Periyanayaki, shrine in the outer prakaram (east-facing Amman shrine linked to Kulothunga III's time).
- Sthala purana: Parvati is held to be present with Shiva in the sanctum in invisible form.
- Coordinates: Wikipedia infobox 10°53′34″N 79°8′19″E → 10.893, 79.139.
- Paadal Petra Sthalam (Tevaram). Close to Thingalur; Periya Puranam story of Appar and Appoothi Adigal (son revived after snakebite) is associated with the neighbourhood and told locally of Apathsahayar.
- History: VMIS (Varalaru/heritage archive) places the temple in the reign of Aditya I (871–907 CE); unusual vimana and ardhamandapa plan; niche figures heavily stuccoed. SII: Aditya I inscription on south wall of central shrine – 20 kalanju gold for a perpetual lamp, donor a lady of Pallava family related to the king's queen; land gift to the "Mahadeva" of Tiruppalanam (SII 13.299). Later records of Parantaka I, Rajaraja I, Kulothunga III; Nayak and Maratha contributions.
- Saptha Sthanam: Thirupazhanam ("pazham" = fruit) is said to have supplied fruits for Nandi's wedding.
- Not verified: tank names, gopuram tiers.
