# Lad Khan Temple, Aihole (lad-khan-aihole)

Correction to brief: approximate coordinates 15.9503, 75.8789 were off in latitude; Wikipedia gives 16°1'11.68"N 75°52'52.46"E.

- Coordinates used: 16.0199, 75.8812 (Wikipedia "Chalukya Shiva Temple", a.k.a. Lad Khan Temple). Sits in the Durga temple enclosure at Aihole.
- Dating: formerly 7th-8th c.; Wikipedia now ~5th c. (mid-5th c. in some scholarship; porch possibly added soon after, c. 550). Older books (e.g. New World Encyclopedia) say late 7th/early 8th. Presented as "disputed, 5th-8th century".
- Dedication: Reported as originally Vishnu (or sun god Suryanarayana); now houses a Shiva linga; a small roof shrine has Vishnu, Surya and Devi images at its sides.
- Name: from a 19th-century occupant ("Lad Khan"; accounts differ: a prince or a Muslim hermit who lived in it).
- Plan: large pillared porch with heavy square pillars; sabha-mandapa with pillars in two concentric squares; mukha-mandapa with 12 carved pillars; small rectangular sanctum at rear; large lattice (jali) windows; sloping stone roofs around a small roof-top shrine; flat/low-pitched roofs recall timber village assembly halls (tradition among architectural historians).
- Visit: ASI-protected, ticketed enclosure (Aihole Durga complex); ticket prices unconfirmed (blog 2020 only).
- Region: Bagalkot district, ~35 km from Badami, ~15 km from Pattadakal (existing catalog file).
