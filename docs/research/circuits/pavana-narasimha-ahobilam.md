# Pavana Narasimha, Ahobilam
- On the bank of the Pavana stream; ~6–7 km trek from Upper Ahobilam (path behind the Upper temple) or ~18–20 km forest jeep track from Lower Ahobilam; forest officials restrict vehicle entry after ~3 pm; private vehicles not allowed.
- Legend: sage Bharadwaja freed of brahma-hatya sin here (single source: srinarasimhakutumbam.org).
- Chenchu Lakshmi shrine nearby; Chenchu tradition that Lakshmi was born among them and wed Narasimha.
- Planet: Mercury.
- Coordinates: NOT FOUND in a published source. Used approximate 15.0950, 78.7600 (~4 km S/SE of Upper Ahobilam, deep forest). Needs verification.
