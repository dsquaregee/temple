# Kothaparameswarar Temple, Kunnathur (kothaparameswarar-kunnathur)

Circuit: nava-kailasam (4th, Rahu). Tradition: Shaivism.

- Deity: Shiva as Kotha Parameswarar (Gotha Parameswarar); consort Sivakami (Sivagami Amman).
- Place: Kunnathur, also called Chenkani / Sengani; older inscriptional name Keezh-vembu-nattu Chenkani alias Navaninarayana chaturvedimangalam. Set on a small hillock, close to Keezha/Mela Thiruvengadanathapuram, NW edge of Tirunelveli town; ~11 km from Tirunelveli Junction (district site).
- Coordinates: no temple-specific coordinate retrievable; used 8.748, 77.676 (APPROXIMATE, near Thiruvengadanathapuram).
- History: inscriptions show the temple built by the 13th century; a King Veera Pandya endowed money for daily worship.
- Features: a serpent figure on the linga noted as rare — basis of the Rahu association; worshipped for relief from Rahu and naga dosham. With the nearby Vishnu (Venkatachalapathi) temples of Thiruvengadanathapuram the locality is likened to Kalahasti and Tirupati.
- Festivals: Shivaratri, pradosham, Rahu kalam worship (customary) — confirm locally.
