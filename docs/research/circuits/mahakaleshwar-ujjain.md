# Mahakaleshwar Temple, Ujjain (mahakaleshwar-ujjain)

- Deity: Shiva as Mahakala; swayambhu linga, dakshinamukhi (south-facing) — the only south-facing Jyotirlinga. Stotram: "Ujjayinyam Mahakalam".
- Location: Ujjain, Madhya Pradesh, beside the Rudra Sagar lake near the Shipra river.
- Coordinates: 23.1828, 75.7683 (Wikipedia infobox 23°10′58″N 75°46′6″E).
- History: Ujjain (Avantika) one of the seven moksha-puris; Mahakala shrine praised by Kalidasa (Meghaduta). Temple sacked in Iltutmish's raid 1234–35; linga traditionally said to have been kept safe in the Koti Tirtha kund. Present structure built c. 1734 under the Maratha commander Ranoji Shinde (by his diwan Ramchandra Baba Shenvi Sukhtankar), Maratha/Bhumija-influenced style; Scindias continued patronage.
- Structure: three levels — Mahakaleshwar (lowest, sanctum), Omkareshwar (middle), Nagchandreshwar (top; opened only on Nag Panchami). Koti Tirtha kund in the precinct. Shri Mahakal Lok corridor opened 2022.
- Rituals: Bhasma Aarti before dawn (advance booking; dress rules: dhoti for men, saree for women).
- Legend (Shiva Purana): demon Dushana troubled the Brahmin Vedapriya's family in Avantika; Shiva burst from the earth as Mahakala and destroyed him, then stayed at their prayer. Also the story of King Chandrasena and the cowherd boy Shrikar.
- Festivals: Maha Shivaratri (Shiv Navratri), Mahakal Sawari processions on Mondays of Shravan–Bhadrapada, Nag Panchami, Simhastha Kumbh every 12 years on the Shipra (next 2028).
- Access: Ujjain Junction railway; Indore airport ~55 km.
