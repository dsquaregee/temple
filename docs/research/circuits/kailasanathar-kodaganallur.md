# Kailasanathar Temple, Kodaganallur (kailasanathar-kodaganallur)

Circuit: nava-kailasam (3rd, Mars / Sevvai). Tradition: Shaivism.

- Deity: Shiva as Kailasanathar; consort Sivakami (Sivagami Amman; also called Ananthagowri / Avudai Nayaki per TN Temples Project).
- Sthala vriksham: vilvam. Tirtham: Tamiraparani.
- Coordinates: no temple-specific coordinate retrievable. Kodaganallur BO post office 8.6992, 77.5883 (postal directory) conflicts with the stated 15–16 km road distance from Tirunelveli. Temple is 1 km south of Nadukallur on the Tirunelveli–Mukkudal road, ~16 km from Tirunelveli Junction, on the Tamiraparani. Used 8.706, 77.626 (APPROXIMATE, derived).
- History: inscriptions of the 12th–13th c. (Pandya period); village earlier called Kodanur and a chaturvedimangalam (Brahmin settlement). Village also has the west-facing Abhimukteeswarar temple on the river bank and the Brihan Madhavan Vishnu temple.
- Legend: older name Karkotaka-nallur — the naga Karkotaka, who bit King Nala, was later advised (by Vishnu) to worship Shiva here and attained release through penance.
- Festivals (district site): monthly pradosham, Margazhi Thiruvathirai, Maha Shivaratri.
- Kodaganallur is also known for its Vedic tradition (Kodaganallur Sundara Swamigal) — not used.
