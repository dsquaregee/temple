# Sri Annapoorneshwari Temple, Horanadu (horanadu-annapoorneshwari)

- Coordinates used: 13.2706, 75.3414 — Wikipedia "Hornadu" village infobox (13°16′14″N 75°20′29″E). Village point (the temple is the village's centre); no separate temple point found — approximate.
- Location: Horanadu, Kalasa taluk, Chikkamagaluru district, Karnataka; Malnad, Western Ghats, on the bank of the Bhadra river amid forest near Kudremukh National Park. ~10 km from Kalasa (Karnataka Tourism; another account says 8 km); ~95 km from Chikkamagaluru town by road (travel blog).
- Deity: Annapoorneshwari, the Goddess as giver of food; the image is of gold (Karnataka Tourism).
- History / tradition: tradition says the original consecration (prathishtapana) was by sage Agastya, who installed a Sri Chakra; Adi Shankaracharya is traditionally associated with re-energising the shrine (accounts differ). A new image was consecrated in 1973 on Akshaya Tritiya, with the renovation led by the hereditary dharmakarta D. B. Venkatasubba Jois. The dharmakarta (Jois) family has conducted the worship for about 400 years (hence century 17 = earliest firm-ish attestation; flag for lead).
- Annadana: every visitor is fed by the temple, meals served three times a day; free food is the defining practice.
- Festivals: Akshaya Tritiya (regarded as the Goddess's birthday; anniversary of the 1973 consecration), Rathotsava (about five days, Feb–March), Navaratri (Sept–Oct).
- Nearby: Kalaseshwara temple at Kalasa; Sringeri lists Horanadu among nearby temples.
- Not verified: posture/attributes of the image; gopuram details; exact dress rules (reported strict); exact distances.
