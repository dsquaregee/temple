# Veerateeswarar Temple, Korukkai (Thirukkurukkai) (veerateeswarar-korukkai)

Circuit: ashta-veerattanam. Tradition: Shaivism. Veerattanam deed: Kama dahana (burning of Kamadeva/Manmatha).

NAMING: Korukkai is the modern village name of the Tevaram-era Thirukkurukkai (Tiru-Kurukkai) — same temple (shivatemples: "Tirukurukkai sivasthalam, now called Korukkai"; Wikipedia article "Veerateeswarar Temple, Korukkai"). It is distinct from Vazhuvur (Gajasamhara, ~12 km south). The traditional venba reads "maa Vazhuvur / Kaaman Kurukkai" — elephant at Vazhuvur, Kama at Kurukkai.

- Deity: Shiva as Veerateeswarar (Yogeswarar/Yoganathar form), consort Gnanambigai.
- Mahamandapa: metal Kamadahanamurthy with six arms holding weapons; metal images of Kamadeva and Rati. Kama-dahana-murthy sabha shows Shiva in yogic posture with one leg folded, with the Sanakadi rishis.
- Lingam's avudaiyar carries a lotus design (Dinamalar).
- "Vibhoothi Kuttai" pit ~200 m from the temple: tradition that Manmatha was burnt here.
- Tank: Soola Gangai (Trishula Gangai) opposite the entrance; also Pasupathi theertham. Sthala vriksha Kadukkai (Dinamalar).
- 26th Tevaram shrine on the north bank of the Kaveri; Appar sang it.
- Location: ~12 km from Mayiladuthurai, Mayiladuthurai–Manalmedu bus route.
- Coordinates: Wikipedia infobox 11°09′19″N 79°36′43″E -> 11.1553, 79.6119.
- Festivals: Masi Magam; 10-day Kamadahana utsavam / Brahmotsavam with Pancha Murthy procession.
