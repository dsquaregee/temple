# Puruhutika Devi (Kukkuteswara Swamy Temple), Pithapuram (puruhutika-pithapuram)

- Coordinates used: 17.1068, 82.2433 — Wikipedia "Kukkuteswara Temple" infobox (17°06′24.46″N 82°14′36″E). The Puruhutika shrine is inside this complex; no separate point published.
- Deities: Shiva as Kukkuteswara Swamy — swayambhu linga said to resemble a rooster (one source: white stone, ~2 ft); consort Rajarajeswari; the Shakti Peetha goddess Puruhutika Devi has her own shrine in the complex.
- Shakti Peetha: one of the 18 Maha Shakti Peethas (Ashtadasha list: "Pithikayam Puruhutika"). Tradition: Sati's seat / lower body fell here (sources vary: "seat", "back part").
- Pada Gaya: temple tank Pada Gaya Sarovar; Gayasura legend — his head at Gaya (Bihar), navel at Jajpur (Odisha), feet at Pithapuram; shraddha rites here held equal to Kashi/Gaya. Large footprints (of Gayasura) shown near the flagstaff. Folk tale: Shiva as a rooster crowed early to end Gayasura's penance/sacrifice, and remained as Kukkuteswara.
- Name "Puruhutika": Indra-epithet (Puruhuta) link NOT confirmed by sources — omitted.
- History: Pithapuram = Pishtapura of Samudragupta's Prayaga pillar inscription (c. 350 CE); early Eastern Chalukya capital (7th c.) before Vengi; Pithapuram Chalukyas 12th–13th c. Builder of present temple not established.
- Festivals: Maha Shivaratri, Navaratri (Devi Navaratri utsavalu listed on utsav.gov.in), Karthika month.
- Town also associated with Sripada Srivallabha (Dattatreya tradition) — well known; mentioned only in experience.
- Access: Pithapuram station on the Vijayawada–Visakhapatnam main line; ~20 km from Kakinada; airports Rajahmundry, Visakhapatnam.
