# Kampahareswarar (Sarabeswara) Temple, Thirubuvanam (kampahareswarar-thirubuvanam)

Circuit: kaveri-delta-temple-towns. Tradition: Shaivism.

- Deity: Shiva as Kampahareswarar ("remover of trembling"); inscription name Tirubhuvana Viresvarar; separate Sarabeswarar shrine.
- Coordinates: Wikipedia infobox 10°59'24"N 79°26'00"E = 10.990, 79.433 (audit value 10.981/79.440 superseded).
- Builder: Kulottunga III (late 12th-early 13th c.), last of the four great medieval Chola temples (with Thanjavur, Gangaikonda Cholapuram, Darasuram). A Sanskrit inscription records construction on the advice of the preceptor Someshvara, commemorating a Pandya victory (VMIS).
- Distinguished by a tall vimana; main shrine and Sarabeswarar shrine on a raised platform.
- Legends: king afflicted with trembling/haunting after killing a Brahmin by mistake, relieved by Shiva (kampa = quaking). Sharabha form of Shiva pacified Narasimha after the slaying of Hiranyakashipu.
- On Kumbakonam-Mayiladuthurai road, Thanjavur district.
- Not verified: ASI status, goddess name, festivals specifics.
