# Kedarnath Temple (kedarnath)

- Deity: Shiva as Kedarnath (Kedareshwara); linga is a conical/irregular natural rock, identified in tradition with the hump of Shiva in bull form. Stotram: "Kedaram Himavatprishthe".
- Location: Kedarnath, Rudraprayag district, Uttarakhand; ~3,583 m, near the source of the Mandakini; highest of the twelve Jyotirlingas.
- Coordinates: 30.7352, 79.0669 (Wikipedia infobox 30°44′6.7″N 79°4′0.9″E; Bhakti Bharat 30.735203, 79.067096).
- Also: first of the Panch Kedar (Kedarnath hump; Tungnath arms; Rudranath face; Madhyamaheshwar navel; Kalpeshwar hair); one of the Chota Char Dham (Yamunotri, Gangotri, Kedarnath, Badrinath).
- History: tradition credits the Pandavas with the first shrine and Adi Shankara (8th c.) with its revival; Adi Shankara samadhi memorial behind the temple. Built of large grey stone slabs on a raised platform; Nandi faces the sanctum. Head priest (Rawal) from the Veerashaiva (Jangama) community of Karnataka.
- Season: opens in spring (around Akshaya Tritiya, April–May; dates announced on Maha Shivaratri), closes on Bhai Dooj (Oct–Nov); during winter the Panchmukhi utsava image is carried by doli to the Omkareshwar temple at Ukhimath, where worship continues for six months.
- 2013: on 16–17 June 2013 flash floods from the Chorabari lake area devastated Kedarnath town, killing thousands of pilgrims; the temple survived; a large boulder lodged behind it diverted the flow and is now venerated as Bhim Shila. ASI found the foundation sound.
- Access: road to Gaurikund (Sonprayag), then trek of ~16–18 km (post-2013 route); ponies, palkis, helicopter services from Phata/Guptkashi/Sitapur; nearest rail Rishikesh; airport Jolly Grant (Dehradun). Registration required for Char Dham yatra.
- century: 8 (traditional Shankara revival; firm early attestation not established).
