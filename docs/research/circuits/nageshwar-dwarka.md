# Nageshwar Jyotirlinga Temple, near Dwarka (nageshwar-dwarka)

- Deity: Shiva as Nageshwara ("lord of serpents"); Jyotirlinga of the stotram verse "Nagesham Darukavane" (Darukavana).
- Location: on the road between Dwarka (Gomti Dwarka) and Okha/Bet Dwarka, ~15–17 km from Dwarka, Devbhumi Dwarka district, Gujarat.
- Coordinates: 22.3360, 69.0870 (Wikipedia infobox ~22.3359, 69.0869; booking POI 22.335985, 69.08701).
- Linga of "Dwarka shila" stone with small chakra marks (Art of Living page).
- Large seated Shiva statue in the grounds, ~25 m (82 ft).
- Present building is modern (rebuilt in the 1990s; Gulshan Kumar association known from memory, not confirmed — omitted from content).
- Other claimants: Aundha Nagnath (Hingoli, Maharashtra) and Jageshwar (Almora, Uttarakhand) are also revered as the Nageshwar Jyotirlinga.
- Legend (Shiva Purana, Koti Rudra Samhita): demon Daruka and wife Daruki in Darukavana; devotee Supriya imprisoned with others, led chanting of Shiva's name; Shiva appeared, destroyed the demon, remained as Nageshwara (Parvati as Nageshwari).
- Festivals: Maha Shivaratri, Shravan month — confirm locally.
- Access: Dwarka railway station; airports Jamnagar (~130 km) and Porbandar (~100 km); usually visited with Dwarkadhish and Bet Dwarka.
- century: set to 20 (present structure); earliest firm attestation of the Dwarka shrine not established.
