# Neyyadiappar Temple, Thillaisthanam (neyyadiappar-thillaisthanam)

Circuit: saptha-sthanam (7th and last stop before the return to Thiruvaiyaru). Tradition: Shaivism.

- Deity: Shiva as Neyyadiappar (swayambhu lingam); consort Balambigai (Ilamangai Ammai).
- Coordinates: Wikipedia only to arcminute (10°53′N 79°06′E). Thillaisthanam post office listed at 10.8783, 79.0898; temple is ~2 km west of Aiyarappar on the Thiruvaiyaru–Thirukattupalli road, north bank of Kaveri. Used 10.880, 79.088 (APPROXIMATE).
- Name: Thiruneithanam; Thillaisthanam is read as a corruption of Thiru-nei-sthanam.
- Legend: a cow's milk turned to ghee at a spot; a king had it dug and found a swayambhu lingam; ghee abhishekam (nei aadal) → Neyyadiappar. Daily abhishekam with ghee followed by hot water.
- History: earliest inscription dated AD 862 (Wikipedia); core attributed to Aditya I (9th c.); Pallava Nandivarman III record of 60 kalanju gold for lamps; contributions from Pandya, Pallava, Chola, Chera, Irukkuvel rulers. East-facing rajagopuram, spacious first prakaram. Theertham: Kaveri.
- Tevaram: Appar and Sambandar; 52nd of the north-bank Kaveri temples.
- Festivals: Maha Shivaratri (Masi), Thiruvadhirai (Margazhi), Saptha Sthanam.
