# Pushpavananathar Temple, Thirupoonthuruthi (pushpavananathar-thirupoonthuruthi)

Circuit: saptha-sthanam (6th stop). Tradition: Shaivism.

- Deity: Shiva as Pushpavananathar (Pushpavaneswarar); consort Soundaryanayaki (Soundaranayagi).
- Coordinates: Wikipedia infobox 10°51′38″N 79°4′46″E → 10.861, 79.079. (Village: Mela Thirupoonthuruthi.)
- Paadal Petra Sthalam; Appar and Sambandar.
- Appar lived here and set up a mutt (Dinamalar) and did uzhavarappani (temple cleaning service).
- Legend: Sambandar came to meet Appar; Appar, unrecognised, joined the bearers of Sambandar's palanquin; when Sambandar asked where Appar was, he answered he was beneath; the two saints bowed to each other. Sambandar is said to have worshipped from outside because Appar had sanctified the temple with his service.
- Indra, under a curse, worshipped Shiva with flowers → Pushpavananathar.
- Saptha Sthanam: flowers sent for Nandi's wedding; flowers still sent to the Nandi Kalyanam festival.
