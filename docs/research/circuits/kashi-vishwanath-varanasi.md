# Kashi Vishwanath Temple, Varanasi (kashi-vishwanath-varanasi)

(Content kept strictly devotional and historical per brief — no disputes discussed.)

- Deity: Shiva as Vishwanatha / Vishveshwara, Lord of the Universe; one of the twelve Jyotirlingas (stotram: "Varanasyam tu Vishvesham").
- Location: Varanasi (Kashi), Uttar Pradesh, near the western bank of the Ganga (Lalita / Manikarnika / Dashashwamedh ghats area).
- Coordinates: 25.3108, 83.0106 (Wikipedia infobox 25°18′38.79″N 83°0′38.21″E).
- Kashi: Avimukta ("never forsaken" by Shiva); one of the seven Moksha-puris; tradition that Shiva grants liberation to those who die in Kashi. Kashi Khanda (Skanda Purana) glorifies Vishveshwara.
- History: ancient shrine, rebuilt more than once over the centuries; present temple built 1780 by Ahilyabai Holkar of Indore; Maharaja Ranjit Singh gave about a tonne of gold to plate the spire and dome (1830s; one source 1835).
- Kashi Vishwanath Dham corridor: first phase inaugurated 13 December 2021; links temple to the Ganga ghats with a broad stone-paved precinct.
- Architecture: Nagara spire; gilded spire and dome; small sanctum with the linga in a silver-lined base.
- Rituals: Mangala aarti (pre-dawn), Bhog, Saptarishi aarti (evening), Shringar, Shayana aarti.
- Festivals: Maha Shivaratri, Shravan month, Rangbhari Ekadashi, Dev Deepawali (Kartik Purnima on the ghats) — confirm dates locally.
- Nearby: Annapurna Devi temple beside it; Vishalakshi temple at Mir Ghat (a Shakti Peetha); Kala Bhairava (guardian of Kashi).
- Visit: phones/bags deposited under security rules; Varanasi Junction (Cantt) and Banaras stations; Lal Bahadur Shastri International Airport at Babatpur (c. 25 km).
