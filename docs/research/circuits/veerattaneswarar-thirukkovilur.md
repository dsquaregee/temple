# Veerattaneswarar Temple, Thirukkovilur (veerattaneswarar-thirukkovilur)

Circuit: ashta-veerattanam. Tradition: Shaivism. Veerattanam deed: Andhakasura samhara. NOT the Vishnu Divya Desam (trivikrama-thirukkovilur) in the same town.

- Deity: Shiva as Veerateeswarar; consort Periyanayagi (Wikipedia) / Sivanandavalli (shivatemples, Dinamalar). Utsava: Andhakasura Samhara Murthy; metal images of Nataraja, Somaskanda and Andhakasuramurthy in the first precinct.
- Historical names: Anthakapuram, Thirukkaivelur (Kallakurichi district site).
- Location: Thirukkovilur town, Kallakurichi district, on the southern bank of the Then Pennai (Pennaiyar). Holy water Thenpennai.
- Coordinates: Wikipedia infobox 11°58′17″N 79°12′38″E -> 11.9714, 79.2106 (Bharatpedia identical).
- Paadal Petra Sthalam: sung by Sambandar, Appar, Sundarar; 11th of the Nadu Nadu temples (district site).
- Secondary (Grokipedia, unverified): Rajaraja I 27th-year inscription with endowments; Virarajendra reconstruction in granite — omitted from content or phrased generally ("Chola inscriptions").
- Town associations: Malayaman chiefs (Sangam age) ruled from Thirukkovilur — weak source; mentioned only lightly as an ancient town on the Pennai.
- Not verified: Avvaiyar/Periya Yanai Ganapathi link (omitted).
