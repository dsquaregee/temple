# Odhanavaneswarar Temple, Thiruchotruthurai (odhanavaneswarar-thiruchotruthurai)

Circuit: saptha-sthanam (3rd stop). Tradition: Shaivism.

- Deity: Shiva as Odhanavaneswarar (Chotruthurainathar; also Oppillaselvar); consort Annapoorani (Oppilambikai).
- Coordinates: Wikipedia infobox 10°52′32″N 79°8′16″E → 10.876, 79.138.
- About 7 km from Thiruvaiyaru, 14 km from Thanjavur.
- Paadal Petra Sthalam; Tevaram hymns by Sambandar, Appar, Sundarar (TN Temples Project).
- History: oldest masonry 9th-century Chola; additions to 16th-century Thanjavur Nayaks; four gopurams. Renovation/restoration under way in 2025.
- Legend: in a famine, a devotee Arulalan (Arulalar) sought to give up his life at the temple; Shiva sent rain and an akshaya patram (inexhaustible vessel) that fed all; hence Chotruthurainathar (choru = cooked rice/food); Odhanam = cooked rice (Sanskrit).
- Saptha Sthanam: food sent from here for Nandi's wedding at Thiruvaiyaru; the temple still supplies food for the Nandi Kalyanam festival.
