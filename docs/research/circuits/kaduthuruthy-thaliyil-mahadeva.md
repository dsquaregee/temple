# Kaduthuruthy Thaliyil Mahadeva Temple (kaduthuruthy-thaliyil-mahadeva)

- Deity: Shiva (Mahadeva), a "thali" (Thaliyil) temple. Third of the Vaikom-Ettumanoor-Kaduthuruthy trio.
- Legend: the demon Khara (Kharasura) obtained three Shiva lingas by penance; he carried them in his right hand, left hand and mouth/neck (accounts vary); they were installed at Vaikom, Ettumanoor and Kaduthuruthy, with Kaduthuruthy the one carried at the mouth/neck. Pilgrims on Vaikathashtami are said to worship all three before noon (see Vaikom file).
- History: Kaduthuruthy was the headquarters of the Vadakkumkoor dynasty; a Vadakkumkoor ruler is said to have worshipped at all three on the same day and arranged Kaduthuruthy so that Ettumanoorappan and Vaikathappan are placed at its southern and northern gates (per Vaikom temple site). Dating: one source says 10th century; another only "over a thousand years" - unverified. century field set to 10 on that basis only.
- Festival: 10-day annual festival in Medam (April-May): Kodiyettu, cultural programmes, elephant procession, traditional music. Less famous than Vaikom or Ettumanoor.
- Location: Kaduthuruthy, Kottayam district (PIN 686604), between Vaikom and Ettumanoor. Coordinates: no temple-specific value found; town centre Wikipedia 9.75N 76.50E, OSM 9.7655, 76.4922. Used 9.766, 76.492 (town centre). Audit's 76.4333 longitude was wrong. Do NOT confuse with Thaliyil/Talikotta temple at Kottayam (9.596, 76.507).
- Unverified: architecture, deity iconography, exact dates.
