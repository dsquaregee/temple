# Payammal Shatrughna Temple (payammal-shatrughna)

- Deity: Shatrughna (Shatrughnaswamy), regarded as an incarnation of Vishnu's Sudarshana chakra. Original panchaloha image hidden in the temple pond during Tipu Sultan's invasion and never recovered; a granite image now in the sanctum. Sanctum (garbhagriha) square.
- Location: Payammal, ~5 km south of Irinjalakuda, reached by a turn at Arippalam on the Irinjalakuda–Kodungallur road (~2 km inside), Thrissur district.
- Coordinates: Wikipedia infobox 10°18′43″N 76°11′21″E = 10.3119, 76.1892. Used.
- History: declined after local power struggles; damaged during the Mysore (Tipu) invasion, renovated by local people (Shatrughna Seva Samiti). No dated founding record.
- Legend: four images (Vakkayil Kaimal dream / sea find) — Shatrughna installed at Payammal.
- Offerings: Sudarshana Pushpanjali (overcoming enemies, prosperity); offering of a Sudarshana chakra. Attending the night puja believed important.
- Festivals: annual festival from Pooyam in Kumbham, 5 days; Nalambalam yatra in Karkidakam — fourth and final temple.
