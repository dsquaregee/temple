# Kailasanathar Temple, Murappanadu (kailasanathar-murappanadu)

Circuit: nava-kailasam (5th, Jupiter / Guru). Tradition: Shaivism.

- Deity: Shiva as Kailasanathar; consort Sivakami Amman (TN Temples Project table).
- District: Thoothukudi (Srivaikuntam taluk); on the Tirunelveli–Thoothukudi national highway, ~17 km from Tirunelveli and ~40–46 km from Thoothukudi; temple on the Tamiraparani bank, east-facing.
- Coordinates: no reliable temple point retrievable (indiamapia's 9.075, 78.090 is clearly wrong). Used 8.723, 77.833 (APPROXIMATE, where the highway crosses the river at Murappanadu).
- History: origins unclear; tradition of a local chieftain (vallal) as builder; renovated under Vijayanagara. Local tradition: Vittala Raya of Vijayanagara worshipped Guru here before a campaign against Travancore.
- Name: (1) a woman of the village drove off a tiger with a muram (winnowing fan); (2) ground full of flat stones (murambu).
- Guru (Jupiter) sthalam — Thursday worship customary.
- Not verified: "Dakshina Kasi"/north-flowing river claim, horse-faced Nandi — not used.
