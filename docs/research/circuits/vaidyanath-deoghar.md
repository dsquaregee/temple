# Baidyanath (Vaidyanath) Temple, Deoghar (vaidyanath-deoghar)

- Deity: Shiva as Baidyanath / Vaidyanath ("Lord of Physicians"), Baba Baidyanath; one of the twelve Jyotirlingas named in the Dvadasha Jyotirlinga Stotram. Parli Vaijnath (Maharashtra) is also revered by many as the Vaidyanath Jyotirlinga.
- Also counted among the Shakti Peethas (Jaya Durga; tradition: where Sati's heart fell — "Hardapeeth"); Jyotirlinga and Shakti Peetha side by side.
- Location: Deoghar ("abode of the gods"), Deoghar district, Jharkhand.
- Coordinates: 24.4925, 86.7000 (Wikipedia infobox 24°29′33″N 86°42′00″E).
- History: builder unknown; front portion attributed to Puran Mal, ancestor of the Maharaja of Gidhaur, 1596 (Sanskrit inscription). Wikipedia infobox names Raja Beiju Bhil in tradition.
- Complex: 22 temples — central Baba Baidyanath shrine + 21 others (incl. Parvati temple). Panchashula (five-pointed trident) crowns the main spire; red cloth/threads link the Shiva and Parvati shrines (gathbandhan) — devotional custom.
- Linga: small, its top slightly broken — tradition: pressed by Ravana's thumb.
- Legend: Ravana's penance; Shiva healed him (Vaidya); Shiva gave him the linga on condition he would not set it down; at Deoghar he handed it to a cowherd (Baiju; Vishnu in disguise in tradition) who set it down; it remained.
- Shravani Mela: Shravan (July–August); kanwariyas carry Ganga water from Sultanganj (Ajgaibinath), Bihar, about 105 km on foot, chanting "Bol Bam".
- Festivals: Shravani Mela, Maha Shivaratri — confirm dates locally.
- Getting there: Baidyanath Dham station (branch from Jasidih Junction, c. 7 km, Howrah–Delhi main line); Deoghar airport; Basukinath (c. 45 km) often visited afterwards.
