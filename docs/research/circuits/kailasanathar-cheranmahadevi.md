# Ammainathar (Kailasanathar) Temple, Cheranmahadevi (kailasanathar-cheranmahadevi)

Correction / confirmation: the Nava Kailasam shrine at Cheranmahadevi is the **Ammainathar
(Ammanathar) Swamy temple** (Tirunelveli district site, TN Temples Project, town panchayat site).
Wikipedia's Nava Kailasam table names its lord generically "Kailasanathar". Id kept.

Circuit: nava-kailasam (2nd, Moon / Chandran). Tradition: Shaivism.

- Deity: Shiva as Ammainathar; consort Avudaiammal (Avudai Nayaki; some list Gomathi Amman), in a separate shrine to the right of the lord — unusual placement.
- Sthala vriksham: banyan. Tirtham: Tamiraparani.
- Coordinates: no temple-specific coordinate retrievable (Wikipedia/OSM unreachable). Town infobox 8°41′N 77°34′E (8.683, 77.567); temple is on the riverbank at the town; used 8.688, 77.566 (APPROXIMATE).
- Location: ~22–24 km west of Tirunelveli on the Tirunelveli–Ambasamudram road; Cheranmahadevi railway station on the Tirunelveli–Tenkasi line.
- History: renovation in the time of Kulothunga Chola I (late 11th–early 12th c.); local panchayat page cites inscriptions; dates otherwise conflicting.
- Legend: two devout, poor sisters who pounded paddy for a living saved to build a shelter for the linga; Shiva came as a hungry brahmin, a lamp failed during his meal and they lit a lamp in a coconut shell; granted their wish that he help build the temple; wealth came to them. A pillar carries a carving of the sisters at the paddy. Coconut lamp and rice offerings continue. Name "Ammainathar" = lord of the (two) mothers/ladies.
- Festivals: Aippasi Thirukalyanam, Maha Shivaratri, monthly pradosham.
