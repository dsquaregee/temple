# Kailasanathar Temple, Senthapoomangalam (kailasanathar-senthapoomangalam)

Circuit: nava-kailasam (9th and last, Venus / Sukran). Tradition: Shaivism.
Also spelt Serndhapoomangalam / Sernthapoomangalam (சேர்ந்தபூமங்கலம்).

- Deity: Shiva as Kailasanathar. Consort: Soundara Nayaki per TN Temples Project and the Tirunelveli district site ("Sounthara Nayaki"); Dinamalar gives Azhagia Ponnammai. Used Soundara Nayaki (two sources).
- Planet: Sukran (Venus) (TNTP; Dinamalar). Navagraha mandapam where devotees offer white cloth and white lotuses to Sukra (Dinamalar).
- Location: ~2 km east of Athur (Authoor) on the Thoothukudi–Tiruchendur road; PIN 628 151 (district site). Near where the Tamiraparani meets the sea at Punnakayal (Dinamalar).
- Sacred tank: Thamira Pushkarani; sthala vriksham: vilvam (district site; TNTP).
- History: builder uncertain — possibly Kulasekhara Pandya or Maravarman Sundara Pandya I, inferred from separate shrines for Madurai's Chokkanathar and Meenakshi (Dinamalar). Two entrances (Dinamalar). century=13 (Maravarman Sundara Pandya I, r. 1216–1238; attribution tentative).
- Consecrated by Romasa/Romaharshana (TNTP).
- Festivals: Aadi and Thai new-moon days, monthly pradosham, Maha Shivaratri (Dinamalar); Fridays customary for Sukra.
- Name: popularly read as serndha poo — "where the flower arrived/joined" (the last lotus). Folk etymology, presented as such.
- Coordinates: no published temple point. Athur (Wikipedia) 8°36′38″N 78°04′57″E = 8.6106, 78.0825; temple 2 km east → used 8.611, 78.101 (APPROXIMATE).
