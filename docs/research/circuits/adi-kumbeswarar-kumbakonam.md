# Adi Kumbeswarar Temple, Kumbakonam (adi-kumbeswarar-kumbakonam)

Circuit: kaveri-delta-temple-towns. Tradition: Shaivism. Tamil Nadu (Thanjavur district).

- Deity: Shiva as Adi Kumbeswarar (lingam), consort Mangalambigai Amman.
- Coordinates: Wikipedia infobox 10°57'30"N 79°22'16"E = 10.958, 79.371 (approximate value in audit, 10.9587/79.3797, superseded).
- Paadal Petra Sthalam: hymns of Appar and Sambandar (Tevaram).
- One of the 12 Shaiva temples linked with the Mahamaham festival (every 12 years).
- History: Chola foundation per most sources (dating varies, 7th-9th c.; firm attestation via Tevaram, 7th c.); Vijayanagara and Thanjavur Nayak expansions (15th-17th c.).
- Architecture: complex about 2,800 sq m, four gopurams, tallest the eastern, 11 storeys, about 128 ft (Wikipedia); sixteen-pillared hall of the Vijayanagara period with 27 nakshatras and 12 rashis carved on a single stone.
- Legend: at the pralaya a pot (kumbha) holding amrita and seeds of creation floated; Shiva, as a hunter, broke it with an arrow; nectar spread, one place becoming the Mahamaham tank; Shiva made a lingam from the pot's remains and entered it: Adi Kumbeswarar ("the one who entered the pot"). Tradition holds the lingam is not of hewn granite.
- Not verified: Potramarai tank name, exact timings.
