# Veerateeswarar Temple, Thirupariyalur (veerateeswarar-thirupariyalur)

Circuit: ashta-veerattanam. Tradition: Shaivism. Veerattanam deed: Daksha samhara (destruction of Daksha's sacrifice, via Veerabhadra).

- Deity: Shiva as Veerateeswarar, also Dakshapureeswarar; consort Ilam Kombanayal (Ilangombanaiyaal). Daksha Samhara Moorthy worshipped; separate shrine of Veerabhadra (TN Temples Project).
- Location: Keezhaparasalur (Keelaparasalur), formerly Thirupariyalur, Mayiladuthurai district (formerly Nagapattinam), a few km west of Thirukkadaiyur.
- Coordinates: Wikipedia infobox 11°05′27″N 79°43′33″E -> 11.0908, 79.7258.
- Paadal Petra Sthalam: one pathigam of Sambandar (shivatemples).
- Present masonry Chola (9th c. per Wikipedia), later Thanjavur Nayak additions; TN Temples Project suggests Pandya-period core (conflict; content says Chola).
- Four daily pujas.
- Not verified: tank name, specific festivals.
