# Kaleshwara Mukteswara Swamy Temple, Kaleshwaram (kaleshwara-mukteswara-kaleshwaram)

- Coordinates used: 18.8119, 79.9039 — Wikipedia "Kaleshwara Mukteswara Swamy Temple" infobox (18°48′43″N 79°54′14″E).
- Location: Kaleshwaram village, Mahadevpur mandal, Jayashankar Bhupalpally district, Telangana (older sources: Karimnagar dist.). ~277 km from Hyderabad, ~125 km from Karimnagar, ~60 km from Manthani.
- Deity: two Shiva lingas on a single pedestal (panavattam) — Mukteswara (Shiva) and Kaleshwara (Yama); together "Kaleshwara Mukteswara Swamy".
- Ritual order: pilgrims bathe at the confluence, then worship Ganesha, then Yama (Kaleshwara), then Shiva (Mukteswara).
- Mukteswara linga has an opening that is said never to fill with water; local belief in an underground passage to the Godavari (unconfirmed — presented as belief).
- Legend: a merchant bathed the linga with many pots of milk, and the milk is said to have appeared at the Godavari–Pranahita confluence; place called Dakshina Gangotri / Dakshina Triveni Sangamam (Godavari, Pranahita, and the unseen Antarvahini Saraswati).
- Trilinga Desham: one of three Shiva shrines (with Srisailam and Draksharamam) said to bound the "land of three lingas" (Telugu country).
- Architecture: simple, ancient stone shrine in Kakatiya-style masonry (one source); a Vishnu (Madhava) shrine is also reported in the complex. Kakatiya fortifications at Kaleshwaram (12th–14th c.) per a regional blog. No firm construction date found.
- Festivals: Maha Shivaratri; Karthika month; Pushkaralu of the Godavari / Pranahita / Saraswati at the confluence (12-yearly cycles; Saraswati Pushkaralu 2025).
- Unconfirmed: exact date of the shrine, Yama-penance legend, gopuram details.
