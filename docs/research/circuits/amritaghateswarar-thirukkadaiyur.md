# Amritaghateswarar-Abirami Temple, Thirukkadaiyur (amritaghateswarar-thirukkadaiyur)

Circuit: ashta-veerattanam. Tradition: Shaivism. Veerattanam deed: Kala samhara (Shiva subdues Yama to save Markandeya).

- Deity: Shiva as Amritaghateswarar (Amirthakadeswarar), consort Abirami. Bronze Kala Samhara Murthy.
- Legend: boy-sage Markandeya, destined to die at sixteen, clung to the lingam; Yama's noose fell on the lingam; Shiva emerged and kicked Yama (Kalasamhara), granting Markandeya eternal youth — Shiva as Mrityunjaya. Name: pot (ghata) of amrita said to have become the lingam (tradition).
- Abhirami Bhattar: devotee whose devotion to Abirami led the goddess to make the moon appear on a new-moon day (Thai Amavasai), the occasion of the Abhirami Anthadhi.
- Customary: Shashtiabdapoorthi (60th), Bhimaratha Shanti (70th), Sathabhishekam (80th) celebrated here by couples — widely known; source search didn't surface but is standard (phrased generally).
- Inscriptions: Rajaraja I 13th regnal year (merchant's paddy endowment); Rajendra Chola (paddy for an eight-day festival); Rajadhiraja 1054 CE (land to feed 17 devotees); under Kulothunga I brick walls replaced with stone and front mandapa built.
- Administered by Dharmapuram Adheenam.
- Paadal Petra Sthalam (Appar, Sambandar, Sundarar per tradition lists).
- Coordinates: temple article 11°4′39″N 79°49′6″E -> 11.0775, 79.8183 (village article 11°4′27″N 79°48′31″E differs slightly; temple point used).
- Location: Thirukkadaiyur, Mayiladuthurai district, ~20 km east of Mayiladuthurai, near Tarangambadi coast.
