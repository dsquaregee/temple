# Veerateeswarar Temple, Thiruvirkudi (veerateeswarar-thiruvirkudi)

Circuit: ashta-veerattanam. Tradition: Shaivism. Veerattanam deed: Jalandhara samhara.

- Deity: Shiva as Veerateeswarar; consort Elavar Kuzhali, also Parimala Nayaki (Dinamalar).
- Legend: Shiva destroyed Jalandhara with a discus (chakra) which he later gave to Vishnu; in the outer prakara a shrine of Shiva worshipped by Vishnu, and the spot where Shiva restored Brinda (Jalandhara's wife) to life as Tulasi (Dinamalar).
- Paadal Petra Sthalam: sung by Sambandar (TN Temples Project, Dinamalar).
- Present masonry Chola, 9th c.; later Thanjavur Nayak expansions (Wikipedia).
- Location: Virkudi, ~10 km north of Tiruvarur; Tiruvarur district (Wikipedia says Mayiladuthurai — Tiruvarur used per two sources and assignment).
- Coordinates: Wikipedia infobox 10°49′53″N 79°39′49″E -> 10.8314, 79.6636.
