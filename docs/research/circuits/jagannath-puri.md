# Jagannath Temple, Puri (jagannath-puri)

- Coordinates used: 19.8047, 85.8183 — Wikipedia "Jagannath Temple, Puri" infobox (19°48′17″N 85°49′6″E), via mirror. Puri city point (19.8106, 85.8314) is ~1 km off; not used.
- Deities: Jagannath with Balabhadra (elder brother), Subhadra (sister) and the Sudarshana pillar, on the Ratnavedi (jewelled platform); wooden (daru) images renewed in Nabakalebara (most recently 2015).
- Tradition: Vaishnavism; one of the four Char Dham of Adi Shankara's scheme (eastern seat; Govardhana Matha in Puri). Vimala shrine within the complex is revered as a Shakti Peetha.
- History: present temple begun under Anantavarman Chodaganga Deva (Eastern Ganga, r. 1078–1150; c. 12th c.), developed under Anangabhima Deva III; later Gajapati kings (Suryavamsi) added halls. Natamandapa and Bhogamandapa added later (Ganga/Gajapati period — sources differ).
- Architecture: Kalinga style; four aligned structures — vimana (rekha deula, ~65 m), jagamohana (pidha deula), natamandapa, bhogamandapa. Nila Chakra (eight-spoked wheel) on the summit; flag changed daily. Outer wall Meghanada Pacheri (~6 m), inner wall Kurma Bheda. Four gates: Singhadwara (east, lion gate), Ashwadwara, Vyaghradwara, Hastidwara. Aruna Stambha before the Singhadwara (tradition: brought from Konark).
- Kitchen: one of the largest temple kitchens; Mahaprasad cooked in earthen pots on wood fires, sold at Ananda Bazar.
- Legend: King Indradyumna seeks Nila Madhava, worshipped secretly by Sabara chief Vishwavasu; priest Vidyapati finds him; deity vanishes; sacred log (daru) floats ashore; divine carpenter (Vishwakarma / Vishnu as old artisan) carves images behind closed doors; queen opens doors early; images left "unfinished".
- Festivals: Rath Yatra (Ashadha; chariots Nandighosa – Jagannath, Taladhwaja – Balabhadra, Darpadalana – Subhadra; to Gundicha temple; Bahuda return; Suna Besha), Snana Yatra (Jyeshtha full moon) followed by Anavasara, Chandan Yatra, Nabakalebara in certain years.
- Entry: only Hindus admitted (long-standing temple rule). Cameras/mobile phones not allowed inside.
- Access: Puri railway station; Biju Patnaik airport, Bhubaneswar (~60 km).
