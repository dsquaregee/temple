# Grishneshwar Temple, Verul (grishneshwar-ellora)

- Deity: Shiva as Ghrishneshwar / Ghushmeshwar; the twelfth Jyotirlinga named in the Dvadasha Jyotirlinga Stotram ("Ghushmesham ca Shivalaye").
- Location: Verul village, Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar (formerly Aurangabad) district, Maharashtra; c. 1–1.5 km from the Ellora Caves (UNESCO); c. 30 km from Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar.
- Coordinates: 20.0250, 75.1699 (Wikipedia infobox 20°1′29.9″N 75°10′11.7″E).
- History: restored by Maloji Bhosale (grandfather of Chhatrapati Shivaji); temple inscription names Maloji and Ekoji Bhosale; present structure attributed to Ahilyabai Holkar of Indore, 18th century. Listed by ASI Aurangabad circle (Ellora sub-circle) — living temple.
- Architecture: rectangular platform; red basalt up to the entablature, brick and stucco above (shikhara); Nagara with Maratha leanings; carved figures and friezes. Shivalaya tank nearby.
- Legend: Kusuma/Ghushma, younger wife of the Brahmin Sudharma, made 101 clay lingas daily and immersed them in a tank; her son was killed by the jealous elder wife Sudeha and thrown into the tank; Ghushma kept her worship; the boy returned alive; Shiva appeared, and at her plea forgave Sudeha and remained here as Ghushmeshwar.
- Custom: men remove shirts/vests before entering the sanctum.
- Festivals: Maha Shivaratri, Shravan Mondays, Kartik — confirm dates locally.
- Getting there: road from Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar (rail + airport); often combined with Ellora Caves and Daulatabad.
