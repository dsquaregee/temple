# Kota Gullu (Ghanpur Temples) (kota-gullu-ghanpur)

- Correction: audit coordinates (18.3150, 79.9000) were wrong. Wikipedia: 18°11'02"N 79°31'19"E = 18.1840, 79.5220. Used 18.184, 79.522.
- Location: north-east corner of Ghanpur village, Mulugu mandal, Jayashankar Bhupalpally district, Telangana; about 9 km north-west of Ramappa Temple, Palampet.
- Deity: Shiva as Ganapeswara (Ganapeswaralayam / Ganapateshwara); main shrine faces east.
- History: Kakatiya reign of Ganapatideva. An inscription slab (Jaya samvatsara, Vaisakha Sudha Trayodasi, = 1234-35 CE) records Ganapati Reddy installing Ganapateshwara and granting land; Deccan Chronicle identifies him as third son of Recherla Rudra (Ramappa's builder). Wikipedia says 12th c./Ganapatideva; Deccan Heritage Foundation dates by similarity to Ramappa.
- Layout: about 20-22 shrines (DHF counts 19 subsidiary shrines); stone enclosure, later mud fortification gave name "Kota gullu" (fort temples). 60-pillared natya mandapa to the south of the main temple believed to be the entrance. Asthana mandapa and ranga mandapa; eaves brackets with composite yalis and dancing women; Gaja Kesari and mythical animal carvings on east/south porticos; Nandi. Two east-facing Shiva shrines in the ruins.
- Condition: badly damaged / ruined; Deccan Heritage Foundation prepared a conservation plan. Protection status not confirmed (a blog says ASI) - visit text: protected monument, confirm access.

## Coordinate correction (2026-10-13)
Wikipedia's Kota Gullu infobox gives 18°11′02″N 79°31′19″E (18.184, 79.522), but the same
article places the temples 9 km north-west of Ramappa (18.259, 79.943) — ~45 km off.
The Ghanpur, Mulugu district village point (18°18′40″N 79°52′20″E = 18.311, 79.872) is
~9.4 km NW of Ramappa, matching the text and the Deccan Heritage Foundation conservation
project; the app uses it (village-level). Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kota_Gullu ,
https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghanpur,_Mulugu_district , https://www.deccan-heritage-foundation.org/?p=360
