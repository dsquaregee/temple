# Mahakuta Temples (mahakuta-temples)

Correction: approximate 15.9347, 75.7336 replaced by Wikipedia value 15°55'58"N 75°43'18"E.

- Coordinates used: 15.9328, 75.7217. Mahakuta village, Bagalkot district, ~15 km from Badami (02-circuit-audit).
- Group dated 6th/7th c. (early Badami Chalukyas) by style (like Aihole) and two inscriptions.
- Mahakuteshvara (Dravida) and Mallikarjuna are the largest shrines; many smaller shrines.
- Vishnu Pushkarini (also Kashi Tirtha): large tank fed by a natural spring flowing within the complex; small shrine in centre with a five-faced Shiva linga. Tradition attributes the pond to sage Agastya.
- Mahakuta Pillar inscription: c. 595-602 CE, Sanskrit in Kannada script; pillar now in Vijayapura (Wikipedia Mahakuta Pillar; original position uncertain).
- Vinapoti inscription in the porch of Mahakuteshvara (c. 696-733 per one source).
- Unconfirmed: timings, festivals (Mahashivaratri customary; confirm locally).
