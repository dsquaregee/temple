# Krishna Temple (Balakrishna), Hampi (krishna-hampi)

- Coordinates used: 15.3303, 76.4605 — decoded from Google plus code 8FJ6+454 Hampi (7J7R8FJ6+454) given by the Mindtrip listing ("Hampi Shri Krishna Temple", Krishnapura, 583239). Sits south of Hemakuta Hill, on the road from Kamalapura to Virupaksha; Virupaksha is at 15.335, 76.4599 (catalog).
- Deity: Krishna as Balakrishna (child Krishna). The original image was brought from Udayagiri (Nellore region, AP) as a trophy of Krishnadevaraya's Udayagiri campaign; that image is now in the Government Museum, Chennai (Egmore). Not in active worship (monument).
- Date: inscription dated 1513 CE in the complex (Halegannada stone inscription before the temple) records the foundation celebrating the Udayagiri victory. Some sources: built 1514, image installed Feb 1515. One source gives 1531 (outlier, ignored).
- Dynasty: Tuluva dynasty of Vijayanagara, Krishnadevaraya.
- Layout: faces east; monumental eastern gopuram (brick/plaster upper tiers ruined) with carved bands incl. scenes of warriors; enclosed court with main shrine and subsidiary shrines (incl. goddess shrine), pillared mandapas. Sculpture programme: Dashavatara, Ramayana and Krishna-lila scenes. Some sources claim "musical pillars" — not adopted (that is Vittala's fame).
- Surroundings (Krishnapura township): long colonnaded bazaar / car street (~0.5 km) in front of the temple; large Krishna pushkarani (stepped tank) with a central pavilion beside the bazaar; a small pavilion with a stone grain trough in front (tradition: offerings of grain); a granary building nearby. Kadalekalu Ganesha on Hemakuta slope and the Ugra (Lakshmi) Narasimha monolith and Badavilinga lie close by.
- Status: part of UNESCO "Group of Monuments at Hampi" (1986); UNESCO's description names the Krishna complex. World Monuments Fund conservation listing.
- Visit: ASI-protected monument; no worship; free walk-in area per common practice (confirm locally).
