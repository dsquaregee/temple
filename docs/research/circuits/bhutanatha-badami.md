# Bhutanatha Temples, Badami (bhutanatha-badami)

- Coordinates used: 15.9208, 75.6878 (Wikipedia: 15°55'15"N 75°41'16"E), east shore of Agastya Lake.
- Two subgroups: East Bhutanatha (main group), 7th-8th c., mostly Dravida; North Bhutanatha / Mallikarjuna group on the north bank, 11th-12th c., mostly Nagara.
- Main temple: Dravida three-tier (tritala) superstructure over sanctum; walls with kinnara and gandharva heads. Inner shrine and mandapa late 7th c. (Badami Chalukya); outer mandapa facing the lake completed 11th c. (Kalyani Chalukya).
- Agastya Lake (Agastya Tirtha): large man-made tank with ghats; locals believe the water has healing powers; tradition names sage Agastya (Badami/Vatapi legend).
- Deity: Shiva as Bhutanatha ("lord of beings"). Not independently confirmed how regular worship is; kept general.
- Unconfirmed: timings, tickets (open lakeside site).
