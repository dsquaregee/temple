# Kailasanathar Temple, Srivaikuntam (kailasanathar-srivaikuntam)

Circuit: nava-kailasam (6th, Saturn / Sani). Tradition: Shaivism.
Not to be confused with the Vaikuntanatha Perumal Divya Desam in the same town (`srivaikuntanathan-srivaikuntam`).

- Deity: Shiva as Kailasanathar; consort Sivakami (Wikipedia; TN Temples Project).
- Planet: Saturn (Sani); separate shrine for Saneeswaran; devotees come for relief from Sani dosham (TN Temples Project).
- Location: Srivaikuntam, Thoothukudi district, on the Tamiraparani; ~30 km from Tirunelveli (Wikipedia).
- Coordinates: 8°38′09″N 77°54′54″E = 8.636, 77.915 (Wikipedia infobox).
- History: originally built by Chandrakula Pandya (c. 13th century per TN Temples Project); pillared halls and major shrines added by Vijayanagara and Nayak rulers in the 16th century (Wikipedia).
- Architecture: Dravidian; three precincts; flat gateway tower rather than a pyramidal gopuram (Wikipedia); interior shows Pandya and Nayak styles (vgopalan portfolio).
- Bhootanathar: a manifestation of Sastha, wooden image; given first honours at the Chithirai Brahmotsavam; believed to guard the temple at night — in earlier times the staff handed him the keys at day's end (Wikipedia).
- Legend: sixth lotus of Romasa (Nava Kailasam story).
- Festivals: Chithirai Brahmotsavam (Apr–May); Saturdays / Sani transit customary at a Sani sthalam; Shivaratri, pradosham.
- Not used: aroundus claim of a reclining Vishnu (conflation with the Perumal temple); Oneindia's west-facing Sivakami shrine (single source).
