# Sankaranarayanar Temple, Sankarankovil (sankaranarayanar-sankarankovil)

Circuit: temples-of-the-deep-south. Tradition: Shaivism (with Vaishnava Hari-Hara worship; Shaktism for Gomathi Amman shrine) -> catalogued as Shaivism.

- Deity: Sankara Linga Swami (Shiva), Gomathi Amman, and Sankaranarayanar (half Shiva, half Vishnu).
- Coordinates: Wikipedia infobox 9°10'N 77°33'E; used 9.172, 77.542 (audit value, consistent). Tenkasi district.
- Builder: Pandya king Ukkira Pandiyan, 10th century CE per temple sources (Wikipedia's "900 BC" treated as an error). Later Nayak additions.
- Legend: a dispute over Shiva versus Vishnu; serpent kings Sankan (Shiva devotee) and Padman (Vishnu devotee); Gomathi's penance in the punnai forest, Shiva appearing as Sankaranarayanar on Aadi Uthiradam; Aadi Thapasu July-August.
- Nine-tier rajagopuram; Nagasunai tank said to be dug by the serpent kings; anthill sand given as prasad; Sri Chakra peetham before Gomathi Amman.
- Not verified: gopuram height, timings.
