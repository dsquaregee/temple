# Jwala Narasimha, Ahobilam
- Highest of the Nava Narasimha; on Achalachaya Meru hill, ~4 km climb beyond Upper Ahobilam temple, near the Bhavanasini falls; Ugra Stambham crag above (trek starts after the falls near Jwala).
- Image: eight-armed Narasimha tearing Hiranyakashipu on his lap (ISKCON Desire Tree: "eight armed form... flame like mane").
- Tradition: a small pool (Raktakundam) below where the Lord washed his hands; water said reddish. (tradition, widely told)
- Planet: Mars (Kuja) — most lists.
- Coordinates: 15.1255, 78.7447 — Commons "Jwala Narasimha Temple at Ahobilam -1.jpg" camera location 15°07′31.77″N 78°44′41″E (camera point, approximate).
- https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Jwala_Narasimha_Temple_at_Ahobilam_-1.jpg
- https://www.trawell.in/andhra/ahobilam/jwala-narasimha-temple

- Not a Divya Desam in its own right; part of the Ahobilam (Singavel Kundram) pilgrimage. Circuit: ahobilam-nava-narasimha.
