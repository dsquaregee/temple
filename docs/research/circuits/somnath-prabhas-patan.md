# Somnath Temple, Prabhas Patan (somnath-prabhas-patan)

- Deity: Shiva as Somanatha ("lord of Soma, the Moon"); counted first of the twelve Jyotirlingas (Dvadasha Jyotirlinga Stotram opens "Saurashtre Somanatham ca").
- Location: Prabhas Patan, near Veraval, Gir Somnath district, Gujarat; Arabian Sea shore.
- Coordinates: 20.8880, 70.4014 (Wikipedia infobox 20°53′16.9″N 70°24′5.0″E).
- Prabhasa tirtha: Triveni Sangam of Kapila, Hiran and (underground) Sarasvati rivers nearby; Bhalka Tirtha / Dehotsarga associated with Krishna's departure (tradition).
- History: ancient pilgrimage; temple raided by Mahmud of Ghazni 1025–26 CE; rebuilt repeatedly (Chaulukya/Solanki kings, incl. Kumarapala, 12th c.); damaged again in later centuries; tradition counts it destroyed and rebuilt several times. Ahilyabai Holkar built a separate Shiva temple nearby (1783; "Ahilyabai/old Somnath temple").
- Present temple: rebuilding initiated after Independence (Somnath Trust; Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel, K. M. Munshi); pran-pratishtha by President Rajendra Prasad on 11 May 1951; architect Prabhashankar Sompura; completed mid-1950s (shikhara works).
- Architecture: Maru-Gurjara (Chalukya/Solanki) style, "Kailash Mahameru Prasad" plan — garbhagriha, sabhamandapa, nrityamandapa; shikhara c. 150 ft with a 10-tonne kalasha; Baan Stambh (arrow pillar) on the sea wall with inscription that no land lies between it and the South Pole along that line (traditional claim).
- Legend: Chandra (Soma), cursed by his father-in-law Daksha to wane for favouring Rohini, worshipped Shiva at Prabhasa and was relieved — waxing and waning thereafter; hence Somanatha.
- Festivals: Maha Shivaratri (peak), Shravan month (Mondays), Kartik Purnima fair (Kartiki Purnima Mela) — confirm dates locally; evening light-and-sound show.
- Visit: Trust-managed; mobiles/cameras deposited at entry (security); nearest rail Somnath/Veraval; airports Diu (~85 km), Rajkot (~190 km); Ahmedabad ~400 km.
