# Banashankari Temple, Cholachagudda (banashankari-badami)

Correction: approximate 15.8797, 75.7060 replaced by Wikipedia temple page 15°53'14"N 75°42'18"E (Cholachagudda village article gives 15°52'20"N 75°43'12"E, ~1 km off).

- Coordinates used: 15.8872, 75.7050. ~5 km from Badami (02-circuit-audit).
- Goddess: Banashankari / Shakambari, a form of Parvati/Durga; black stone image, eight-armed, seated on a lion trampling a demon. Tilakaranya forest ("Vana-shankari" = forest consort of Shiva).
- History: popular belief credits Badami Chalukyas (7th c.) / origin possibly older; one source says Rashtrakuta-period old temple; present structure 1750 by Maratha chieftain Parashuram Agale (historian Sheelkant Pattar, via The Hindu).
- Tank: Haridra Tirtha (Harishchandra Tirtha), stone-walled, ~360 ft square, mantapas on three sides, pillared cloister.
- Legend: Skanda and Padma Puranas: goddess as Shakambari slew demon Durgamasura; Shakambari name from famine legend (green vegetables from her body).
- Festivals: Banashankari Jatre from Pushya full moon (Jan-Feb), 10-12 days, rathotsava; Navaratri decoration for nine days.
- Unconfirmed: timings, photography rules.
