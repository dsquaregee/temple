# Bhargava Narasimha, Ahobilam
- ~2 km from Lower Ahobilam (Ahobila Matha site), on a hill near Bhargava Tirtha / Akshaya Tirtha pond; ~100–150 steep steps from the pond; rough unpaved track, jeep/auto usual.
- Parashurama (Bhargava) did penance here (ISKCON Desire Tree; srinarasimhakutumbam.org).
- Image: Narasimha slaying Hiranyakashipu, Prahlada with folded hands (widely described).
- Planet: Sun.
- Coordinates: NOT FOUND in a published source; direction from Lower Ahobilam not stated by sources. Used approximate 15.1400, 78.6900 (~2 km NE of Lower Ahobilam). Needs verification.
- https://www.trawell.in/andhra/ahobilam/bhargava-narasimha-temple
- https://mindtrip.ai/attraction/ahobilam-andhra-pradesh/bhargava-narasimha-swamy-temple/at-XkKRX0Jn

- Not a Divya Desam in its own right; part of the Ahobilam (Singavel Kundram) pilgrimage. Circuit: ahobilam-nava-narasimha.
