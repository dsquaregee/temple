# Omkareshwar Temple, Mandhata island (omkareshwar-mandhata)

- Deity: Shiva as Omkareshwara; with Mamleshwar/Amareshwar (Amaleshwara) on the south bank, worshipped together as the Jyotirlinga. Stotram: "Omkaram Amaleshvaram" (Omkara with Amaleshvara).
- Location: Mandhata island (Shivpuri) in the Narmada, Khandwa district, Madhya Pradesh; island said to be shaped like the sacred syllable Om; Narmada and its branch the Kaveri encircle it.
- Coordinates: 22.2456, 76.1511 (Wikipedia infobox 22°14′44.1″N 76°09′03.8″E).
- History: earliest Paramara record at Mandhata: Jayasimha I (c. 1055–70), which mentions the Amareshvara linga (Puratattva article); Omkareshwar rose to prominence under the Paramaras; later patronage (Holkars). Adi Shankara traditionally met his guru Govinda Bhagavatpada in a cave at Omkareshwar; 108-ft "Statue of Oneness" of Adi Shankara on Mandhata hill (2023).
- Legends: Vindhya mountain's penance with a sand-and-clay linga and yantra — Shiva appeared in two forms, Omkareshwara and Amaleshwara; King Mandhata of the Ikshvaku line worshipped here (island named after him); also sons Ambarisha and Muchukunda.
- Festivals: Maha Shivaratri, Shravan Mondays, Kartik Purnima; Narmada parikrama pilgrims — confirm locally.
- Access: bridges and boats to the island; Omkareshwar Road (Mortakka) railway station ~12 km; Indore airport ~80 km; Khandwa ~70 km.
