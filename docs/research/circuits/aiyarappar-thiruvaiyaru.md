# Aiyarappar (Panchanadeeswarar) Temple, Thiruvaiyaru (aiyarappar-thiruvaiyaru)

Circuit: kaveri-delta-temple-towns. Tradition: Shaivism.

- Deity: Shiva as Aiyarappar (Panchanadeeswarar); consort Aram Valartha Nayaki (Dharmasamvardhini).
- Coordinates: Wikipedia infobox 10°53'N 79°06'E (arcminute precision); used 10.879, 79.104 (audit value, consistent with it).
- Paadal Petra Sthalam sung by Appar, Sambandar, Sundarar.
- Appar is believed to have received a vision of Kailasa here ("Dakshina Kailasam").
- Nandi legend: Nandi born here to sage Salankayana (Silada); Sapta Sthanam festival (seven temples, palanquins converge at Thiruvaiyaru), April-May.
- Thiruvaiyaru is the home of Saint Thyagaraja (Carnatic trinity); the Aradhana is held on the Kaveri bank each year.
- Architecture (one source): about 15 acres, five prakarams, seven-tiered rajagopuram; tirthams Kaveri, Surya, Nandi, Samudra, Devamritha.
- About 13 km from Thanjavur on the Kaveri's bank.
- Not verified: dynasty specifics (Chola, 9th c. or earlier per audit; later Nayak/Maratha work generic), timings.
