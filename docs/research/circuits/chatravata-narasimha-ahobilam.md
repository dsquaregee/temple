# Chatravata (Kshatravata) Narasimha, Ahobilam
- Near Lower Ahobilam (sources say 2–4 km), on the same road as Yogananda.
- Legend: gandharvas Haha and Huhu sang before the Lord; he keeps tala with left hand (tala mudra), smiling; blessed them as best singers in three worlds.
- Name: tree canopy (chatra = umbrella).
- Planet: Ketu.
- Coordinates: 15.1137, 78.6735 — Commons "Chatravata Narasimha Temple, Ahobilam in February 2024 (2).jpg" camera location 15°06′49.43″N 78°40′24.44″E (≈2.2 km S of Lower Ahobilam temple).
- https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Chatravata_Narasimha_Temple,_Ahobilam_in_February_2024_(2).jpg
- https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Chatravata_Narasimha_Temple,_Ahobilam
- https://www.awanderingmind.in/post/the-nava-narasimha-temples-of-ahobilam

- Not a Divya Desam in its own right; part of the Ahobilam (Singavel Kundram) pilgrimage. Circuit: ahobilam-nava-narasimha.
