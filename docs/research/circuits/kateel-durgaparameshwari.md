# Sri Durgaparameshwari Temple, Kateel (kateel-durgaparameshwari)

- Coordinates used: 13.0438, 74.8701 — Wikipedia "Kateel" town infobox (13°02′38″N 74°52′13″E). No temple-specific point found (OSM/Nominatim blocked from this environment); the town point is in the small village around the islet temple — flag as approximate.
- Setting: islet in the middle of the Nandini river; connected by a small footbridge; ~29 km from Mangaluru city (Karnataka Tourism). Mangaluru airport at Bajpe is nearby.
- Deity: Durgaparameshwari, self-manifested (udbhava) linga form in the river; the sanctum is said to be always damp, and the kumkum prasada stays moist (belief: Nandini's blessing).
- Legend (Kshetra Purana): drought; sage Jabali seeks Nandini (daughter of Kamadhenu) for a yajna; she refuses and is cursed to flow as a river; the Goddess promises to be born from her as her daughter. Demon Arunasura (boon: not to be killed by two- or four-legged beings) — goddess becomes a rock / swarm of bees (Bhramari) and the bees sting him to death; she then appears as a linga in the Nandini. Sources differ in details (whether Jabali went to Indra/Brahma).
- Architecture: large mantapa, antarala, garbhagriha; garbhagriha in Kerala style with sloping roofs (mangaloreheritage). Main entrance built 2020.
- Yakshagana: temple melas (troupes) since mid-19th c.; 5–6 troupes (sources differ) perform as seva.
- Festivals: Navaratri (temple open late); annual jatre in April (Mesha) — eight days, includes Thoothedhara / Agni Keli (devotees throw burning palm-frond bundles at each other); Brahmakalashotsava (Karnataka Tourism lists Jan–Feb — unclear; may be a periodic event).
- No inscriptional/dynastic dating found; century field set to 19 (earliest firm attestation = the mid-19th c. Yakshagana mela). Flag for lead.
