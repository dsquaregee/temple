# Vedapuriswarar Temple, Thiruvedhikudi (vedapureeswarar-thiruvedhikudi)

Circuit: saptha-sthanam (4th stop). Tradition: Shaivism.

- Deity: Shiva as Vedapuriswarar (Vazhaimadunathar); consort Mangayarkarasi, separate shrine in outer courtyard preceded by a Vasantha mandapam.
- Coordinates: Wikipedia infobox 10°51′23″N 79°8′14″E → 10.856, 79.137.
- ~7 km from Thiruvaiyaru. Paadal Petra Sthalam (Tevaram: Appar, Sambandar).
- History: oldest masonry 9th-century Chola; gopurams added up to Thanjavur Nayak period; HR&CE administered. Sources disagree on gopurams (four vs three-tier rajagopuram).
- Features: Veda Vinayakar in the maha mandapam, head tilted as if listening to Vedic recitation ("Sevi saitha Vinayakar"); four Nandis on the outer wall and on the vimana facing the four directions, read as the four Vedas.
- Legend: Brahma, Surya and Kubera worshipped here; TN Temples Project links the place to the recovery of the Vedas from the demon (Hayagriva story). Regarded as a Thirumana prarthana sthalam (prayers for marriage).
- Saptha Sthanam: said to have sent Vedic priests for Nandi's wedding.
