# Karanja Narasimha, Ahobilam
- Under a karanja (Indian beech/Pongamia) tree, ~1 km before Upper Ahobilam, near the road from Lower Ahobilam.
- Legend: Hanuman meditating on Rama would not accept Narasimha; Lord appeared holding Rama's bow — also called Saaranga Narasimha. Alternative: sage Gobhila, cursed by Durvasa, freed of the curse here.
- Image (widely described): seated Narasimha with third eye, under Adisesha's hood, with bow.
- Planet: Moon.
- Coordinates: 15.1274, 78.7278 — Commons "Karanja Narasimha Temple, Ahobilam, January 2020 (2).jpg" camera location 15°07′38.68″N 78°43′40.18″E.
