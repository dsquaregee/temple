# Jogulamba Temple, Alampur (jogulamba-alampur)

- Coordinates used: 15.8770, 78.1320 — Wikipedia "Jogulamba Temple, Alampur" infobox (15°52′37.2″N 78°07′55.2″E). Wikidata Q101314175 gives 15°52′37″N 78°8′2″E (~200 m east). Navabrahma group point (15.8778, 78.1348) is distinct.
- Location: Alampur, Jogulamba Gadwal district, Telangana; left bank of the Tungabhadra near its confluence with the Krishna; SE corner of the village, in the same precinct as the Navabrahma temples. ~220 km south of Hyderabad; ~25 km from Kurnool.
- Deity: Jogulamba (Yogulamba / Yogamba, "mother of yogis"), a fierce form of the Goddess; consort Shiva as Bala Brahmeshwara (Bala Brahma temple, the main living Navabrahma shrine).
- Iconography: seated on a corpse (Shava); hair adorned with a scorpion, lizard and skull (sources vary: also frog, bat, owl).
- Shakti Peetha: counted 5th among the 18 Maha Shakti Peethas of the Ashtadasha Shakti Peetha stotram attributed to Adi Shankara; tradition: Sati's upper teeth/jaw fell here.
- History: original shrine destroyed in 1390 CE (Bahmani period per sources); the image was safeguarded in the Bala Brahmeshwara temple; present temple reconstructed in 2005 (~615 years later).
- Festivals: Dasara / Navaratri, Maha Shivaratri; annual Brahmotsavam (hindupad). Fridays/full moons popular.
- Unconfirmed: exact architecture of the 2005 temple.
