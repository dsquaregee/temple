# Kachabeswarar (Kachapeshwarar) Temple, Kanchipuram (kachabeswarar-kanchipuram)

Circuit: temples-of-kanchipuram. Tradition: Shaivism. (Distinct from Kachabeswarar, Thirukachur.)

- Deity: Shiva as Kachabeswarar (self-manifested lingam); consort Sundarambikai (Sundarambika).
- Coordinates: Wikipedia infobox (Karchapeswarar Temple) 12°50'17"N 79°42'04"E = 12.838, 79.701.
- Paadal Petra Sthalam, sung by Sundarar (Tevaram).
- Legend: Vishnu in tortoise (kurma, kachapa) form worshipped Shiva here.
- Origins Pallava; Chola and later Vijayanagara/Nayak work; present masonry largely 16th c. (sources vary).
- Located on West Raja Street, a short distance from the Kamakshi temple.
- Shrines: Thyagarajar, Sundarar, hall of the 63 Nayanmars, Durga, Dattatreya, Adi Sankara (one source).
- Associated with Kachiyappa Sivachariyar, temple priest remembered as author of the Tamil Kanda Puranam (composed with Kumarakottam Murugan temple).
- Festivals: Chithirai Brahmotsavam and Chitra Pournami (April-May).
- Not verified: tank name, timings.
