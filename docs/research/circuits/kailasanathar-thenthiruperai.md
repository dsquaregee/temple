# Kailasanathar Temple, Thenthiruperai (kailasanathar-thenthiruperai)

Circuit: nava-kailasam (7th, Mercury / Budhan). Tradition: Shaivism.
Not the Makara Nedunkuzhaikathar Vishnu Divya Desam in the same town (`makaranedunkuzhaikkathar-thenthiruperai`).

- Deity: Shiva as Kailasanathar; consort Azhagiya Ponnammai (TN Temples Project; Tirunelveli district site "Arulmigu Azhakiya Ponnammai").
- Planet: Budhan (Mercury) — devotees seek command of speech and intellect; worship here held equal to worshipping Shiva at Thiruvenkadu (Budhan's Navagraha shrine) (TNTP / district site).
- Budhan and Varuna both worshipped here → "Varuna Kshetram".
- History: origins unknown; inscriptions of Maravarman Sundara Pandya call the place Sundarapandya Chaturvedimangalam (TNTP). century=13.
- Consecrated by Sage Romaharshana/Romasa (Nava Kailasam tradition).
- Name: "Then" (southern) Tirupperai; "perai" read as fort (TNTP).
- Location: ~38 km from Tirunelveli on the Tirunelveli–Tiruchendur road; nearest railway station Alwarthirunagari; Thoothukudi airport (district site).
- Coordinates: no temple-specific point found. Town: Wikipedia 8°36′38″N 78°00′05″E (older) / Makara Nedunkuzhaikathar temple 8.6032, 77.9860 (current Wikipedia); indiamapia 8.6033, 77.9879. Used 8.606, 77.988 (APPROXIMATE, town centre near the river; distinct from the Perumal temple point).
