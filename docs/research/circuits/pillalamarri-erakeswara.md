# Erakeswara Temple, Pillalamarri (pillalamarri-erakeswara)

- Correction: audit coordinates (17.1396, 79.6234) were off. Wikipedia infobox 17°10'11"N 79°34'56"E = 17.1697, 79.5822. Used 17.1697, 79.5822.
- Location: Pillalamarri, just outside Suryapet, Suryapet district, Telangana. Nearby Nameswara Temple 17.169, 79.585.
- Deity: Shiva (Erakeswara). Saivite temple.
- History: c. 1208 CE, Kakatiya style; commissioned by Erakasani, wife of Beti Reddi of the Recherla family, feudatories of the Kakatiyas (Wikipedia). A Telugu inscription of 1208 mentioning Ganapatideva is reported (secondary tourism source). One of four stone temples in the village (12th-early 13th c.): Nameswara (c. 1202, Namireddi, on the Musi bank), Trikuteswara, Erakeswara and others. Pillalamarri was a seat of the Recherla chiefs.
- Unverified: architectural details (no firm sources found; do not claim Nandi/platform specifics). Famous for fine carving (audit).
