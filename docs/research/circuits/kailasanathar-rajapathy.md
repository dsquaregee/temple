# Kailasanathar Temple, Rajapathy (kailasanathar-rajapathy)

Circuit: nava-kailasam (8th, Ketu). Tradition: Shaivism.

- Deity: Shiva as Kailasanathar; consort Soundara Nayaki / Soundaryanayaki (TN Temples Project; Tirunelveli district site "Sounthara Nayaki").
- Planet: Ketu — tradition that Ketu worshipped Shiva here; devotees with Ketu dosham or in Rahu's 7-year period worship for relief (TNTP). Ketu parihara pujas customary on Sundays and Tuesdays (hindu-blog). Sometimes called "Dakshina Kalahasti" (hindu-blog snippet; single source — phrased as "sometimes called").
- Architecture: the Shiva linga is depicted with four wheels, representing a chariot (TNTP); separate Navagraha shrine in which all nine planets are represented as Shiva lingas, Shiva being adhipati of the grahas (TNTP); Nandi faces the main linga (hindu-blog). Temple recently repainted (TNTP, 2016).
- Waters: Tamiraparani and Palazhi theertham (TNTP).
- Location: Tiruchendur block, Thoothukudi district; ~45 km from Tirunelveli on the Tiruchendur road near Kurumbur; ~32 km from Thoothukudi; nearest railway station Kurumbur, mini-buses from Kurumbur; nearest airport Thoothukudi (district site; TNTP).
- History: no inscriptions/dates found. century=13 used as the Pandya-era placement of the Nava Kailasam group — NOT firmly attested for this shrine.
- Coordinates: no published temple point found. Used 8.605, 78.025 (APPROXIMATE — between Thenthiruperai (Rajapathy panchayat includes Thenthiruperai (Rajapathi) per villageinfo) and Kurumbur, near the river). Needs map confirmation.
