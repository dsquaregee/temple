# Papanasanathar Temple, Papanasam (papanasanathar-papanasam)

Circuit: nava-kailasam (1st, Sun / Suriyan). Tradition: Shaivism.

- Deity: Shiva as Papanasanathar (also Papavinasar); consort Ulagammai (Lokanayaki).
- Coordinates: Wikipedia "Papanasanathar Temple" infobox 8°42′48″N 77°24′21″E -> 8.7133, 77.4058 (town of Papanasam 8.712, 77.393).
- Setting: Tirunelveli district, at the foot of the Western Ghats where the Tamiraparani leaves the hills; Agasthiyar Falls and Papanasam dam upstream. About 50–60 km west of Tirunelveli; Ambasamudram ~10 km; Vikramasingapuram adjoining.
- Nava Kailasam: first of the nine; tradition: Romasa (Romaharshana / Uromasa), a disciple of Agastya, floated nine flowers (lotuses) on the river and installed a linga wherever each came ashore; Papanasam = Sun.
- History: Wikipedia attributes the original building to the Pandyas (Chandrakula Pandyas per temple accounts); additions by Vijayanagara and Nayak rulers in the 16th century.
- Architecture: seven-tiered rajagopuram; granite Dravidian temple on the river bank; bathing ghat on the Tamiraparani in front.
- Legends: (1) Agastya, sent south to balance the earth during Shiva–Parvati's wedding at Kailasa, was granted the vision of the divine wedding here, with Lopamudra (kalyana darshanam celebrated on the first day of Chithirai). (2) Name "Papanasam" = destruction of sins; one account ties it to Indra's release from sin/curse after bathing and worship here.
- Festivals: Chithirai festival (first day of Chithirai, Agastya's wedding vision; 10–13 days in different accounts), Thaipoosam (Jan–Feb) with river bathing; Shivaratri; Aadi Amavasai bathing at the river is customary at Papanasam.
- Not verified: specific inscriptions; gopuram height; exact founding date.
