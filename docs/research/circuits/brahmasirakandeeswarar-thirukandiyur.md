# Brahma Sirakandeeswarar (Veerattaneswarar) Temple, Thirukandiyur (brahmasirakandeeswarar-thirukandiyur)

Circuits: saptha-sthanam (5th stop), ashta-veerattanam. Tradition: Shaivism.
NOT the Vishnu Divya Desam (harasaba-vimochana-thirukkandiyur), which stands a few hundred metres away.

- Deity: Shiva as Brahma Sirakandeeswarar (Kandeeswarar, Veerattaneswarar); consort Mangalanayaki (Mangalambikai).
- Coordinates: Wikipedia infobox 10°51′36″N 79°6′30″E → 10.860, 79.108 (NB: identical to the value already used for the Divya Desam; the two temples are a few hundred metres apart).
- ~3 km south of Thiruvaiyaru on the Thanjavur road; ~10–12 km from Thanjavur.
- Paadal Petra Sthalam; sung by Appar and Sambandar.
- Ashta Veerattanam: the site where Shiva removed one of Brahma's five heads (Brahma's pride). The sin (brahmahatti) was removed by bathing at Thirukandiyur (Vishnu temple tank, Kamala Pushkarani / Kapala theertham, in the Vaishnava version).
- Brahma and Saraswati images stand outside the sanctum (said to be moved from a ruined Brahma temple nearby); Brahma seated, hands folded, holding flower and rudraksha mala.
- Inscription: SII 3.134, Parakesarivarman Uttama Chola, north wall of central shrine of "Viratteswara temple at Kandiyur". Contributions from Cholas, Thanjavur Nayaks, Marathas.
