# Neelakandeswarar Temple, Rajendrapattinam (Thiru Erukkathampuliyur) — research notes

Master-list row 219 (Nadu Nadu). Mostly confirmed. Note: the temple is also listed as Thirukumaresar (Wikipedia) and Swetharanyeswarar (Dinamalar, tntemplesproject); "Swetharkavaneswarar" in the master list matches the erukku (white arka) forest name. Consort confirmed as Neelamalarkanni; "Veeramulaiyammai" not confirmed — not used.

- Place: Rajendrapattinam, Vriddhachalam taluk, Cuddalore district, just off the Vriddhachalam–Jayankondam road, ~13 km from Vriddhachalam. Tevaram name Thiru Erukkathampuliyur; also Kumaresa Pattinam.
- One of the 276 Paadal Petra Sthalams; 4th in Nadu Nadu (shivatemples nn04). Sung by Sambandar.
- Birthplace of Thiruneelakanda Yazhpanar, the Nayanar who played the yazh to accompany Sambandar's hymns; the temple has a shrine/images of him and his wife Mathanga Choolamani.
- Moolavar: Neelakandeswarar (Thiruneelakandeswarar); also Swetharanyeswarar / Thirukumaresar (Thirukumaranathar). Ambal: Neelamalarkanni.
- Legends: (1) Murugan, cursed by Shiva to be born mute after a quarrel, worshipped at many shrines and regained speech here; struck his vel into the ground to make the tank in front of the temple, the Neelotpala (Nilurpala) theertham — hence Kumaresar. (2) Name Erukkathampuliyur: hunters turned into erukku (arka) plants (Dinamalar). (3) Rajaraja Chola prayed here for a son, and the son became Rajendra Chola — hence Rajendrapattinam (Dinamalar; tntemplesproject says the place is named after Rajendra).
- History: Chola temple; tradition/sources tie it to Rajaraja I and Rajendra I period; Wikipedia: construction style suggests Rajaraja's time.
- Century: 7 (Sambandar).
- Festivals: not specifically documented — keep to customary Shaiva observances; Yazhpanar's guru puja (Vaikasi Moolam per Periya Puranam tradition) — not specifically confirmed for this temple, not used.
- Coordinates: 11.4239, 79.3478 — Wikipedia "Thirukumaresar temple" infobox (11°25′26″N 79°20′52″E). Village point 11.4248, 79.3434 consistent. No other catalog entry here.
