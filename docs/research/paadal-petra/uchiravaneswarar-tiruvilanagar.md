# Tiruvilanagar — Uchiravaneswarar Temple

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Uchiravaneswarar** (Tamil **Vizharkaattu Nathar**, "lord of the reed forest"; also **Thurai Kattum Vallalar**); goddess **Veyuru Tholi Ammai**.
- **Location:** Tiruvila Nagar (Arupathi post, PIN 609309), ~7–11 km from Mayiladuthurai on the Tarangambadi road; now Mayiladuthurai district (older listings say Nagapattinam), Tamil Nadu. Approx 11.0800 N, 79.7200 E (approximate).
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam — hymned by Tirugnana Sambandar; Kaveri south bank. Grouped among the Mayiladuthurai Pancha Dakshinamurti sthalams.
- **Name/legend:** sthala vriksham **vizhal** (reed/spear-grass); Sanskrit *uchira* = vizhal → Uchiravaneswarar; lingam found amid the reeds.
- **Festivals:** Maha Shivaratri (Masi); Navaratri (Purattasi); Karthikai Mondays; Pradosham.
