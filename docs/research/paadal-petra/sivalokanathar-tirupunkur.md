# Sivalokanathar Temple, Tirupunkur (sivalokanathar-tirupunkur)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Chola Nadu (north of the Kaveri). District: Mayiladuthurai
(older sources: Nagapattinam), ~3 miles (~5 km) west of Vaitheeswaran Koil. Tradition: Shaivism.
Tevaram name: **Tirupunkur** (திருப்புன்கூர்). Sung by **all three**: Sambandar, Appar and
Sundarar (Appar's hymn shares a patikam with Tiruneedur). One blog counts it the 20th shrine on
the northern bank of the Kaveri.

- Deity: Shiva as **Sivalokanathar**; swayambhu linga said to have arisen from an anthill.
  Consort: **Sokkanayaki** (Chokka Nayaki; also Soundaranayaki) — shrine on the left side of the
  temple with its own pradakshina path.
- Coordinates: **approximate ~11.205, 79.655** — derived from "3 miles west of Vaitheeswaran
  Koil" (Wikipedia); no sourced geo-coordinate found via search. Refine against a mapping source.
- Defining legend (Nandanar, Tirunalaippovar): barred as a devotee of humble birth from entering,
  he worshipped from the outer gate; the stone Nandi blocked his view of the linga; Shiva told
  Nandi to move aside; Nandi shifted and Nandanar saw the Lord. The Nandi (large outer one and
  the small inner one) remains off-axis to the sanctum today. Nandanar cleaned the grounds and
  dug the tank; alternate tradition: Vinayaka dug it overnight (Kinaru Vettiya Vinayakar, "the
  Vinayaka who dug the well").
- Secondary legend: Sundarar and a king/drought — rain, then flood, king asks the saint to stop
  it, 24 veli land granted to the temple (TN Temples project/templenet-type guides). One heritage
  site names the king as Rajendra Chola I, which conflicts chronologically with Sundarar
  (earlier); prose leaves the king unnamed and hedges as "local tradition".
- Architecture: five-tiered rajagopuram; shrines to Vinayaka, Subramanya, Surya; panchamukha
  linga; Nandi shown with tongue held inside the mouth (one source); theertham Rishabha Theertham
  (Templenet) — TN Temples calls it Ganapati Theertham; a heritage site lists Vrushaba,
  Devendra and Nandanar theerthams. Content uses Rishabha Theertham.
- Festivals: ten-day Vaikasi Visakam Brahmotsavam (May–June) per one heritage site; Maha
  Shivaratri and Pradosham (generic).
- Access: Vaitheeswaran Koil (railway) ~5 km east; Sirkazhi/Mayiladuthurai nearby; airports
  Tiruchirappalli/Chennai (general knowledge).
- **Not verified:** coordinates; timings (none found; generic wording used); founding
  dynasty/structural date (century 7 for the Tevaram era; "Chola-era renovation under Rajendra
  Chola I" reported by one site only); direction the temple faces; festival dates beyond Vaikasi
  Visakam; Nandanar's birthplace (Adanur) and the meaning of "Tirunalaippovar" ("he who will go
  tomorrow") from the Periya Puranam tradition — general knowledge, not confirmed by pages
  fetched, so the prose avoids them; the exact claim that the Nandi's tongue is within its mouth
  (single source, hedged "said to"); the exact 5 km distance from Vaitheeswaran Koil ("3 miles"
  from Wikipedia).
