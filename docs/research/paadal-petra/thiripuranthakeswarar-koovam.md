# Thiripuranthakeswarar (Tripuranthaka Swami) Temple, Koovam (Thiruvirkolam) — research notes

Master-list row 252 (Thondai Nadu). Row facts confirmed: deity Thiripuranthakar / Tripuranthaka Swami; consort Thiripuranthaki (Tripuranthaka Nayagi / Tripurasundari); singer Sambandar (one pathigam). No correction.

- Place: Koovam village (Kadambathur post), Tiruvallur district, near Perambakkam; the Koovam (Cooum) river is held to rise here. Tevaram name Thiruvirkolam ("the sacred form with the bow"). Counted 14th Thondai Nadu sthalam (shivatemples.com tn14).
- Distance from Chennai given variously as 50 / 70 km — content avoids an exact figure ("west of Chennai").
- Lingam: swayambhu; not touched even by the priests — "Theenda Thirumeni" (dinamalar); devotees hold its colour changes with the seasons (stated as belief). Abhishekam water fetched from the Koovam; tender-coconut water as alternative (shivatemples).
- Worship custom: devotees pray to the goddess before the Lord (dinamalar); couples pray for harmony in marriage.
- Legend: Tripura samhara — the gods' chariot set out against the three cities; the axle broke because Vinayaka had not been worshipped first; after the gods honoured him, Shiva appeared here with his bow — hence Thiruvirkolam. Vinayaka shrine "Achirutha / Achumuri Vinayakar" (the one who broke the axle); worship before new ventures.
- Tanks: Agni Theertham (priests bathe before daily rituals; held never to dry) and Kopagni Theertham.
- History: Kamakoti Peetam article — the Tripurantakesvara temple largely rebuilt in the 17th c. reusing stones/carvings from an early 10th-c. structure. Madras Musings — Chola-period records of the village tank: renamed in 1057 CE; an arrangement of 1112 under which the temple collected dues to maintain it.
- century: 7 (Sambandar).
- Coordinates: 13.0122, 79.8149 — Wikipedia "Tiruvirkolam" infobox (13°00′44″N 79°48′54″E); the Wikipedia "Tripuranthaka Swamy Temple" infobox gives 13.02, 79.82 (2 dp), consistent. Village point; no other catalog temple in Koovam.
