# Thiripuranthakeswarar (Tripuranthaka Swami) Temple, Koovam (thiripuranthakeswarar-koovam)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Thondai Nadu (Tevaram name: Tiruvirkolam). Tradition: Shaivism.

- Deity: Shiva as Thiripuranthakeswarar / Thripuranthakar (Tripuranthaka Swami), the form
  of the destroyer of the three cities (Tripura). The sanctum linga is held to be a
  **swayambhu** (self-manifested) stone, worshipped without being touched by the priests'
  hands, earning the Lord the title **Theenda Tirumeni Nathar** ("Lord of the form not to
  be touched").
- Consort: given here as **Thiripurasundari** (per task brief; one source notes devotees
  pray to Thripurasundari before the Lord). **Note the goddess-name variants in sources:**
  Dinamalar lists her as **Tripuranthaka Nayagi**, shivatemples.com as **Thripuranthaki** —
  flag below.
- Counted as the **14th temple of the Thondaimandalam (Thondai Nadu) region** praised in
  the Tevaram. Hymned by **Tirugnanasambandar** (Sambandar); Madras Musings places the
  reference in the **third Tirumurai**. Sambandar is traditionally dated to the 7th century
  CE — **used century 7**.
- Legend (the temple's defining story): the **Tripura Samhara** — Shiva destroyed the three
  flying cities (gold, silver, iron) of the asuras with a single arrow, using the earth as
  chariot, sun and moon as wheels, Mount Meru as his bow. At Tiruvirkolam he is remembered
  as revealing this bow-bearing form, which gives the Tevaram name Tiruvirkolam ("the sacred
  form with the bow"; *vil* = bow).
- Architecture: **south-facing five-tiered rajagopuram** as the main entrance; sanctum
  crowned by a **Gajaprishta vimana** (apsidal, "elephant's-back" tower; Dinamalar:
  "Gajabrushta Vimana"). Separate shrine for the goddess. **Agni Theertham** tank said never
  to dry even in famine; the **Koovam (Cooum) river** is said to rise at this spot.
- Festivals: ten-day **Chithirai Brahmotsavam** (Apr–May); **Maha Shivaratri** (Feb–Mar);
  **Arudra Darshan / Margazhi Tiruvadhirai** (Dec–Jan); monthly Pradosham.
- Location / access: **Koovam village, Kadambattur post, Tiruvallur district**, near
  Perambakkam; roughly 50–70 km west of Chennai (sources disagree); temple ~1 km from the
  Koovam bus stop. Tiruvallur (Chennai suburban railway) is the convenient rail point.
- Coordinates: **town-level ~13.012, 79.815** (Wikipedia gives ~13.0122, 79.8149 for
  Tiruvirkolam). Found on only one source — refine against a mapping source when possible.

- **Not verified:** exact temple coordinate (town-level only, one source); consort's proper
  name (task brief gives Thiripurasundari; sources give Tripuranthaka Nayagi / Thripuranthaki
  for the shrine goddess — treated as the same Devi, name left as Thiripurasundari per brief);
  founding dynasty/century of the structural temple (Dinamalar says "500 years old," which
  conflicts with the 7th-c. Sambandar hymn — used century 7 for the Tevaram era; Chola-period
  tank reference, 1057 CE "Tribhuvanmahdevi Pereri," is the firmest medieval anchor but not a
  construction date); distance from Chennai (50 vs 70 km across sources); current timings;
  the claim that the Koovam river originates here (traditional, repeated in sources).
