# Mullaivananathar Temple, Then-Tirumullaivayil / Thirumullaivasal (mullaivananathar-then-tirumullaivayil)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Chola Nadu (north of the Kaveri; coastal, ~0.5 km from the sea). Tevaram name: Then-Tirumullaivayil (தென்திருமுல்லைவாயில்) — the "southern" Tirumullaivayil, distinct from the Thondai Nadu Tirumullaivoyal (Masilamaniswarar, near Chennai). District: Mayiladuthurai (Sirkazhi taluk; older sources list Nagapattinam). Tradition: Shaivism.

- Deity: Shiva as Mullaivananathar (also Yuthika Parameswarar); consort **Anikonda Kothai Ammai** (also Sathyananda Soundari). Swayambu linga ~3.5 ft.
- Tevaram: praised by **Tirugnanasambandar**. Dinamalar counts it as the 7th Shiva shrine on the northern bank of the Kaveri.
- Coordinates: **town-level ~11.240, 79.838** (Tirumullaivasal village, 11°14′25″N 79°50′17″E per latitude.to; Berthfinder harbour 11.2439, 79.8457). Exact temple point not found — refine against a mapping source.
- Defining legend: Chola king **Killivalavan** (grandfather of Karikala in the telling), suffering from a skin disease, came for a sea bath on physicians' advice; his horse's legs were caught in dense mullai creepers; cutting them with his sword he struck a hidden Shivalinga, which bled (scar said to remain). Hence Tirumullaivayil / Mullaivananathar; the Lord healed him. Sthala vriksha: mullai.
- Architecture: arch at the entrance, no rajagopuram (one source); shrines to Vara Sakthi Vinayaka, Subramanya, Nalvar (Nayanmars), Bhairava, Mahalakshmi, Durga, Vishnu, Navagrahas; four theerthams (Vishnu, Chandra, Brahma, Ganga). Reconstructed in the Later Chola period; consecration (kumbhabhishekam) 7 Sept 1989.
- Festivals: **Masi Magam Theerthavari** (Feb–Mar; utsava idols taken to the seashore); Thai Poosam (Jan–Feb; one source mentions 108 kavadi); Masi Shivaratri; Avani Vinayaka Chaturthi; monthly Pradosham.
- Access: ~14 km from Sirkazhi, ~32 km from Chidambaram, ~36 km from Mayiladuthurai; sea ~0.5 km away; Sirkazhi nearest railway. PIN 609113.
- Seed-hint corrections: none beyond adding consort and district (Mayiladuthurai, formerly Nagapattinam). Dinamalar says the temple is ~1,300 years old; Wikipedia credits Killivalavan (early Chola).
- **Not verified:** exact temple coordinates (town-level only); founding dynasty (Wikipedia says Early Chola Killivalavan — traditional, not a dated attribution; century 7 used for the Sambandar era); the Later Chola reconstruction (single source); sources disagree on age (1,200 / 1,300 / 1,500+ years); whether Murugan has consorts (sources conflict — omitted in prose); the "no rajagopuram" detail rests on one blog; the Uppanar river location (one source) — not used; Nayanmar-shrine detail is from "Nalvar" in one listing; current timings (Dinamalar 8:00–12:30 / 4:00–8:00 quoted in prose; shivatemples.com differs); nearest airport statement (Tiruchirappalli/Puducherry) is general knowledge, not sourced; "coconut groves" and similar scenery in the Experience section are general editorial colour.
