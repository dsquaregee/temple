# Kalyanasundareswarar Temple, Tiruvelvikudi (kalyanasundareswarar-tiruvelvikudi)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Chola Nadu (north of the Kaveri) (Tevaram name: Tiruvelvikudi,
திருவேள்விக்குடி). District: Mayiladuthurai (older sources: Nagapattinam), Kuthalam taluk.
Tradition: Shaivism.

- Deity: Shiva as Kalyanasundareswarar (also Manavaleswarar in some accounts); consort
  Parimala Sugantha Nayaki (also given as Narumsandha Nayaki). Linga is described as
  swayambhu, east-facing.
- Tevaram: one hymn each by **Sambandar and Sundarar** (listed as Sambandar 1, Sundarar 1) —
  counted among the Paadal Petra Sthalams on the north bank of the Kaveri.
- Coordinates: **~11.0868, 79.5689** (Wikipedia infobox, 11°05′12″N 79°34′08″E). Treated as
  temple-level per Wikipedia, but not independently cross-checked.
- Defining legend: the name joins *velvi* (sacrificial rite) + *kudi* (settlement). Tradition
  places the pre-nuptial yagna / sacred-thread rite (**Kauthuka Bandhana**, tying the
  kankanam on the goddess) of Shiva and Parvati here, with Brahma as chief priest; hence
  also "Kauthuka Bandhana Sthalam". Parvati is said to have been born as the daughter of
  Sage Bharata at nearby Kuthalam. Most accounts put the wedding itself at nearby
  **Tirumanancheri**; one local tradition says it happened at Velvikudi. Sundarar is said to
  have bathed in the temple's **Agni Theertham** and been cured of his affliction.
- Seed-hint correction: the brief suggested a link to Daksha's sacrifice. None of the
  sources found connect this temple to Daksha's yagna; the velvi here is the pre-wedding
  rite of Shiva and Parvati. Daksha is therefore NOT used. Likewise a claim about Agastya
  and Vatapi appeared in one search summary but was not supported by any source — not used.
- Architecture: Dravidian; east-facing; three-tier gopuram; two prakarams (one source);
  sacred tank **Kauthuka Bandhana Theertham** opposite the entrance gateway (other sources
  name the tank Mangala Theertham — names vary); reported to have no separate Navagraha
  shrine since the wedding is held to have been completed here. Superstructure described as
  early Chola, estimated 7th c. CE (tntemplesproject); inscriptions of the period of
  Parantaka Chola, Sembiyan Mahadevi and Rajaraja Chola reported.
- Festivals: Maha Shivaratri (Feb–Mar) and Panguni Uthiram (Mar–Apr) per Dinamalar;
  monthly Pradosham (general Shiva-temple practice). Temple is a marriage parihara sthalam.
- Access: ~3 km from Kuthalam on the Mayiladuthurai–Kumbakonam road; nearest major rail
  Mayiladuthurai; nearest major airport Tiruchirappalli (distance not sourced).
- **Not verified:** exact temple-point coordinate; the founding century (7th is an estimate;
  sources also say "~1,500 years" and "1,000–2,000 years"); detailed inscription content;
  whether Parvati's birth at Kuthalam and Brahma as head priest are shared across sources
  (from vocal.media only); whether the wedding was here or at Tirumanancheri (sources
  disagree); the "no Navagraha shrine" claim (one source); current timings (sources give
  conflicting hours, so the generic wording is used); exact road distances to Mayiladuthurai
  / Kumbakonam; further festival details.
