# Tirukkozhambam — Kokileswarar Temple

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Kokileswarar** ("the lord worshipped by the cuckoo"); goddess **Soundara Nayaki**. Old name Kokilapuram.
- **Location:** Tirukkozhambam, near Kumbakonam, Thanjavur district, Tamil Nadu. Approx 10.9500 N, 79.5000 E (approximate).
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam (Tevaram); administered by the **Thiruvaduthurai Adheenam**; Chola patron **Sembiyan Mahadevi**.
- **Legends:** cursed being (a Vidyadhara, Chandhan) as a **kokila (cuckoo)** worshipped here and regained his form → Kokileswarar; **Parvati cursed to a cow** (after a dice-game ruling) worshipped here, hoofprints shown on the lingam. Brahma Theertham tank opposite.
