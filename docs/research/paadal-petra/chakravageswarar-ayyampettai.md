# Chakravageswarar Temple, Tiruchakkarappalli / Ayyampettai (chakravageswarar-ayyampettai)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Chola Nadu south of the Kaveri. Tradition: Shaivism.
- Deity: Shiva as Chakravageswarar (swayambhu linga); consort Devanayaki. Sung by Appar & Thirugnana Sambandar.
- Legend: Vishnu received his discus (Sudarshana) here → "Chakkarapalli"; also the chakravaka bird's worship. One of the Chakkarapalli Sapta Sthanam (Saptamatrika) temples.
- Early Chola (Aditya I) origin; expansions under Rajaraja I & Rajendra I.
- Coordinates: ~10.90, 79.25 (approximate, Ayyampettai/Chakkarapalli area — refine against a map).
- Not verified: exact founding date (sources give 9th–11th c.; used 9th / Aditya Chola); precise temple coordinate.
