# Koteeswarar (Thirukoteeswarar) Temple, Tirukkodikka / Thirukodikaval (koteeswarar-tirukkodikka)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Chola Nadu (north of the Kaveri) (Tevaram name: Tirukkodikka, திருக்கொடிக்கா).
District: Thanjavur (Thiruvidaimarudur taluk). Tradition: Shaivism.

- Deity: Shiva as Koteeswarar / Thirukoteeswarar; consort **Tripurasundari, also called
  Vadivambikai** (seed hint gave only Thiripurasundari; the second name is in sources).
- Tevaram: Appar (3 decads) and Sambandar (1 decad) per shivatemples.com. Counted among the five
  "-kaa" Tevaram shrines: Tiruvanaikka, Tirunellikka, Tirukolakka, Tirukurakka, Tirukkodikka.
  Listed as 137th of the 275 Paadal Petra Sthalams in one source.
- Coordinates: **11.0495, 79.5128** (Wikipedia, Thirukoteeswarar Temple: 11°2′58″N 79°30′46″E).
  Sourced from Wikipedia; not independently checked.
- Defining legend: 3 crore (30 million) devas were cursed by the sage Durvasa (variants: pride in
  mantra power / faulty intonation of mantras); told to seek Shiva on the Kaveri bank, they
  worshipped here and were freed, hence "Koti-eswarar" (tri-koti-eswarar). Second legend: Yama and
  Chitragupta incurred Shiva's displeasure, did penance and bathed in Sringodbhava Theertham on
  Chitra Pournami and were relieved; worship here said to relieve "Yama bhayam" (fear of death).
  Prose keeps the Yama story general (the detailed Lokakanta episode is from a single source).
- History: earliest inscriptions c. 750 CE under Pallava Nandivarman; Sembiyan Mahadevi (mother
  of Uttama Chola) credited with the stone rebuilding c. 979 CE and re-engraving of older records
  (about 26 inscriptions preserved there, per one source; the 979 attribution carries a
  "citation needed" on Wikipedia). Century set to 7 for the Tevaram era.
- Architecture: east-facing; main tower reported as five-tiered (blogs; sub-gopuram tier counts
  conflict); tank Sringodbhava Theertham (also Kaveri, Mukkodi theertham listed); sacred tree
  pirambu (cane). Shrines: Karaiyerum Vinayaka, Subramanya, several lingas, Gajalakshmi,
  Jyeshtha Devi, Kala Bhairava, Surya, Chandra, Sani, Durvasa, Nataraja, Saptakanniyar; separate
  Yama and Chitragupta shrines.
- Festivals: Chitra Pournami (Yama/Chitragupta), Pradosham, Skanda Sashti, Tuesdays, Aippasi
  Brahmotsavam (one tourism source), Maha Shivaratri (general).
- Access: Thirukodikaval Post, via Narasinganpettai, PIN 609802; ~19 km from Kumbakonam on the
  Kumbakonam–Mayiladuthurai road via Kuthalam; alternative via Aduthurai. Timings listed as
  7:00–12:30 and 4:00–8:30.
