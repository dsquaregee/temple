# Mundeeswarar Temple (mundeeswarar-tirumundeeswaram)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Nadu Nadu. Tradition: Shaivism.
(Note backfilled by the lead session; the drafting agent was interrupted by a rate limit

- Deity: Shivaloka Nathar (Mundeeswarar); Soundaranayaki.
- Tirumundeeswaram (village of Gramam), Villupuram district. Coords 11.85, 79.404. Praised by Appar; rebuilt in stone in the early Chola age.
- Legends: the name from Shiva's guardians Tindi and Mundi; and Appar remembered here for his humble labour of tending the temple.
