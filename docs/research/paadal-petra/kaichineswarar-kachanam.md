# Tirukkaichinam (Kachanam) — Kaichineswarar Temple

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Kaichineswarar** (Kaichina Nathar), a **swayambhu** lingam; goddess **Palvalai Nayaki** (TN Temples gives Swetavana Nayaki). (Worklist goddess "Palvalai Nayaki" — confirmed.)
- **Location:** Kachanam (Tevaram **Tirukkaichinam**), ~18 km from Tiruvarur, ~9 km from Thiruthuraipoondi, Tiruvarur district, Tamil Nadu. ~10.6245 N, 79.6677 E (Wikipedia).
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam — hymned by Tirugnana Sambandar; Kaveri south bank (~122nd). Sthala vriksham **kongu ilavam**; tank **Indira / Vajra Theertham** (Indra's worship).
- **Festivals:** Maha Shivaratri (Masi); Navaratri (Purattasi); Karthikai Mondays; Pradosham.
