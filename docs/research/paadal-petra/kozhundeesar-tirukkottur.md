# Tirukkottur — Kozhundeeswarar Temple

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Kozhundeeswarar**; goddess **Madhuravasanaambikai** (also Thenmozhippaavai). (Master-list goddess "Madhuravasanambikai" — confirmed.)
- **Location:** **Kottur (Tirukkottur)**, hist. Indirapuram, ~15 km from Mannargudi, ~27 km from Tiruvarur; Tiruvarur district. ~10.62 N, 79.50 E (approximate).
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam (111th, Kaveri south) — hymned by **Tirugnana Sambandar**. A **guru-sthalam** (Shiva & Parvati face each other). Legend: Vritrasura and Dadichi's vajra. Inscriptions of Raja Raja I, Rajadhiraja I, Kulothunga III.
- **Festivals:** Maha Shivaratri (Masi); Navaratri (Purattasi); Karthikai Mondays; Pradosham.
