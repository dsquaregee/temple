# Tirunedungalam — Nithyasundareswarar Temple

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Nithyasundareswarar** (Nedungalanathar / Thirunedungalanathar); goddess **Oppila Nayaki** (Mangalanayaki). (Worklist goddess "Oppila Nayaki" — confirmed.)
- **Location:** **Tirunedungalam**, near Thuvakudi, ~21 km from Tiruchirappalli off the Trichy–Thanjavur highway; Tiruchirappalli district. ~10.78 N, 78.80 E (approximate — no Wikipedia coord; derived from Thuvakudi).
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam — hymned by **Tirugnana Sambandar**; upper Kaveri. Legend: the goddess did penance to wed Shiva, who came as a thief and took her hand; Agastya worshipped here.
- **Festivals:** Maha Shivaratri (Masi); Navaratri (Purattasi); Karthikai Mondays; Pradosham.
