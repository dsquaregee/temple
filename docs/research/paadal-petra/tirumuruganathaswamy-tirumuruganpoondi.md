# Tirumuruganathaswamy (Thirumuruganatheeswarar) Temple, Tirumuruganpoondi (tirumuruganathaswamy-tirumuruganpoondi)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Kongu Nadu (Tiruppur district). Tradition: Shaivism.

- Deity: Shiva as Tirumuruganathaswamy (Thirumuruganatheeswarar); consort Avudainayaki
  (spelling varies: Avudainayagi; Tevaram name Muyangu Poon Mulai Ammai).
- Counted as the second Kongu Nadu shrine praised in the Tevaram; hymns by Sundarar.
- Coordinates: **~11.165, 77.311** — Wikipedia lists the temple at 11°09′54″N 77°18′41″E
  (town centre 11°10′44″N 77°18′52″E). Treat as approximate, not surveyed; refine against
  a mapping source.
- Sthala legend (Sundarar): returning with gifts from the Chera king Cheraman Perumal, he
  rested before the Vinayaka shrine without going in to worship Shiva (one version). Shiva
  sent his ganas (bhuta-ganas) disguised as hunters, who robbed him of everything.
  Sundarar prayed to Vinayaka, who directed him eastward; he sang a hymn blaming the Lord,
  and Shiva restored the goods. Commemorated in the **Vedupari Utsavam**. Sources differ on
  whether it was a test or Shiva wishing to hear Sundarar's verse.
- Murugan legend: after slaying Surapadma, Murugan dug a spring with his vel and worshipped
  Shiva here, hence Muruganathaswamy; a square stone under a neem tree nearby is said to be
  the Brahmahathi (sin) that left him.
- Architecture: **no rajagopuram**; wall sculptures of Sundarar in three moods (anger,
  humiliation, joy); Vinayaka shrine associated with the legend.
- Date: present structure attributed to Kongu Chola period, ~10th century (Wikipedia);
  century 8 used for the Tevaram/Sundarar era (sources differ on Sundarar 7th vs 8th).
- Festivals: Vedupari Utsavam; annual Brahmotsavam in Maasi (Feb–Mar), Sundarar honoured;
  Maha Shivaratri most prominent; monthly Pradosham (general practice).
- Access: ~4–5 km from Avinashi, ~7–9 km from Tiruppur (nearest rail; sources vary);
  Coimbatore airport ~40 km (estimated from Avinashi–Coimbatore ~35 km).
- **Not verified:** exact temple coordinate; founding patrons/date; whether a separate
  Sundarar shrine exists (not claimed); current timings; precise Vedupari date; exact
  road/bus approach; the "Brahmahathi stone" and spring details are traditional accounts.
