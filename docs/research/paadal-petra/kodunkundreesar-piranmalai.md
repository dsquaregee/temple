# Kodunkundreesar (Kodunkundranathar) Temple, Piranmalai (kodunkundreesar-piranmalai)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Pandya Nadu (Tevaram name: Tirukkodunkundram). Tradition: Shaivism.

- Deity: Shiva as Kodunkundreesar / Kodunkundranathar; consort Kuyilamudanayaki (sources also
  give Kuyilamirtha Nayaki, Amuthambikai, Amriteswari, Thaenambigai).
- Hill temple at Piranmalai, Singampunari taluk, Sivagangai district (PIN 624503 per one
  listing; another gives 630502). Counted among the 14 Pandya Nadu Paadal Petra Sthalams;
  hymn (1 pathigam) by Sambandar; Arunagiriar also praises the Lord of this hill.
- Coordinates: **temple ~10.2386, 78.4369** (Wikipedia temple point 10°14′19″N 78°26′13″E;
  village 10°14′09″N 78°26′17″E).
- Legend (sthala purana): Adisesha coiled around Mount Meru; Vayu blew with full force to
  dislodge him; fragments of Meru broke off in the wind, one being this hill.
- Local memory: area once Parambu Nadu / hill Parambu Malai (→ Piranmalai), linked with Paari,
  one of the seven great patrons (Wikipedia).
- Features: hill reported at over 2,000 ft; sacred tree Uranga Puli (tamarind); tank
  Madhu Pushkarini / Thaenaazhi Theertham; Vaduga Bhairavar and Mangaibagar shrines; rock
  Pandava Therthams; a dargah (Waliullah Sheikh Abdullah Shaheb) on the summit.
- Festivals: Brahmotsavam in Chithirai (Apr–May); Maha Shivaratri; monthly Pradosham.
- Timings (older listing): morning 6:00–12:00; evening 4:00–8:00 (Kodunkundreesar);
  Mangaibagar and Vaduga Bhairavar evening 4:00–6:30.
- Access: ~7 km from Singampunari; ~20–25 km from Tiruppathur; ~40–52 km from Karaikudi
  (sources differ); Madurai–Ponnamaravathi buses pass through; Madurai nearest rail and airport.
- **Not verified:** age and builders of the present structures (no firm Pandya/other dynasty
  sourced; used century 7 for the Sambandar era and kept dynasty general); gopuram, mandapa and
  sanctum measurements or architecture (not found — prose kept general and says so); exact
  hill height (2,000+ ft is one source's figure); the goddess's name spelling variants; current
  timings; festival day counts. Etymology of "Kuyilamudanayaki" (koel + nectar) is an
  interpretation of the name, not a sourced claim.
