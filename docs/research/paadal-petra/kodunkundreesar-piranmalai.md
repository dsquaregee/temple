# Kodunkundreesar Temple, Piranmalai (Thirukodunkundram) — research notes

Master-list row 198 (Pandya Nadu 5). Row facts confirmed; no corrections (consort spelled Kuyilamudhanayagi / Kuyilamritanayaki).

- Place: Piranmalai, hill village in Singampunari taluk, Sivaganga district. Tevaram name Thirukodunkundram ("the steep hill").
- Paadal Petra Sthalam, 5th in Pandya Nadu (shivatemples pn05). Sung by Thirugnana Sambandar; also praised by Arunagirinathar (Thiruppugazh, Murugan shrine).
- Moolavar: Kodunkundreesar / Kodunkundranathar (base level). Ambal: Kuyilamudhanayagi (Kuyilamritanayaki). Other goddess shrines listed: Visalakshi, Thenambal (Dinamalar); Amuthambikai.
- Three levels on the hill (said to stand for pathalam, bhoolokam, Kailasam):
  - base: Kodunkundreesar with Kuyilamudhanayagi;
  - middle: Viswanathar with Visalakshi, associated with Bhairava; Ugra Bhairavar shrine (special festival in Thai — Wikipedia village article);
  - top: Mangai Bagar (Umamaheswarar) in wedding form (kalyana kolam) with Parvati. Murti said to be of herbal composition, so no abhishekam; only punugu (civet) oil offered on Fridays. No Nandi — said to have been playing the maddalam at the wedding.
- Legend: Vayu–Adisesha contest at Meru; fragments broke off; one became this hill. Agastya, sent south to balance the earth during Shiva's wedding, granted the wedding vision — given here at the summit (Mangai Bagar).
- Sangam: tradition identifies the hill with Parambu malai of the chieftain Pari (one of the seven great patrons, kadai ezhu vallalgal), Parambu Nadu → Piranmalai.
- Rock-cut: five peedas/tanks called Pandava theerthams carved in the rock of the hill (Wikipedia/one source).
- Elevation ~610 m (2,000 ft) (Wikipedia).
- Coordinates: 10.2386, 78.4369 — Wikipedia "Piranmalai Kodunkundreeswar Temple" infobox (10°14′19″N 78°26′13″E). Village article: 10.2359, 78.4380 (consistent).
