# Tirukkollikadu — Agneeswarar Temple

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Agneeswarar**; goddess **Panjin Melladi Ammai** (Mridupada Nayaki — matches the worklist draft "Mrudupadanayaki").
- **Location:** **Tirukkollikadu**, ~18 km from Thiruthuraipoondi (~18–25 km from Tiruvarur); Tiruvarur district (NOT Nagapattinam, despite a distance-mix-up in some directories). ~10.62 N, 79.72 E (approximate — no Wikipedia coord).
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam (No. **115**, Kaveri south bank) — hymned by **Tirugnana Sambandar**. Legend: Agni (fire-god) worshipped here → Agneeswarar.
- **Note:** distinct from the catalog's **Agneeswarar at Melaithirukkattupalli** (Chola south) and **Kanjanur Agneeswarar** (Chola north).
- **Festivals:** Maha Shivaratri (Masi); Navaratri (Purattasi); Karthikai Mondays; Pradosham.
