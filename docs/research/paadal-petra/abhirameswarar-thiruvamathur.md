# Abhirameswarar (Abirameswarar) Temple, Thiruvamathur — research notes

Master-list row 236 (Nadu Nadu). Row confirmed: Abhirameswarar (Azhagiyanathar) / Muthambigai (also Azhagiya Nayaki, Kuktambikai per tntemplesproject; Wikipedia says Manonmani — minority, not used); Villupuram; Appar, Sambandar, Sundarar.

- Place: Thiruvamathur (old name Gomathupuram / Thiru-aa-mathur), ~7 km north-west of Villupuram on the Villupuram–Tiruvannamalai/Thirukkovilur road (shivatemples).
- Paadal Petra Sthalam, Nadu Nadu (shivatemples nn21). Sung by all three — Sambandar 2, Appar 2, Sundarar 1 pathigams (shivatemples). tntemplesproject: Sundarar sang here on the way to Thiruvarur (tradition).
- Legend: cows originally had no horns; Kamadhenu and the cattle sought horns (via Nandi) and worshipped Shiva here, receiving horns — "aa" = cow, hence Thiru-aa-mathur. Another local tradition: Rama and Sugriva made their alliance at this spot (Dinamalar — tradition).
- Layout: two separate temples — Shiva's faces east, the Goddess Muthambigai's own temple opposite faces west; an opening between lets the deities "see" each other (tourism listing). Wikipedia: consort temple opposite the main complex.
- History: original complex believed Chola; main temple with Vijayanagara patronage (Wikipedia). Inscriptions: Parantaka I (Parakesarivarman yr 3, gift of gold — SII vol 3), Rajaraja I, Kulothunga I.
- Coordinates: 11.9676, 79.4657 — Wikipedia infobox 11°58′3.5″N 79°27′56.5″E via search text. No other catalog temple there.
