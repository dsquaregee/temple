# Avinashilingeswarar Temple, Avinashi (Thiruppukkoliyur) — research notes

Master-list row 214 (Kongu Nadu 6; shivatemples kn6). Row facts confirmed; no corrections.

- Place: Avinashi, Tiruppur district, on the Coimbatore–Erode (Salem) road; about 13 km from Tiruppur. Tevaram name Thiruppukkoliyur (Tiruppukkozhiyur).
- One of the 276 Paadal Petra Sthalams, Kongu Nadu; temple's own tradition calls it the first of the seven Kongu Tevaram shrines. Sung by Sundarar. Century 8.
- Moolavar: Avinashilingeswarar / Avinashiappar. Ambal: Karunambigai (temple also called Karunaiyaaththaal temple).
- Inscriptions call the town Dakshina Varanasi (southern Kashi). Kasi Kinaru (well) is one of the three sacred waters.
- Inscriptions record endowments by Cholas, Pandyas and Hoysalas (Wikipedia / district sources).
- Legend: Sundarar passing through saw two houses — one celebrating the upanayanam of an 8-year-old, the other grieving a son taken
  by a crocodile in the tank years before (who would have had his upanayanam then). Sundarar sang a pathigam at the tank; the crocodile
  returned the boy, grown to the age he would have been. The tank: Thamarai Kulam (lotus pond), ~0.5 km SW of the temple, with a
  shrine to Sundarar on its shore. Sculpture of a crocodile with a boy emerging from its mouth (muthalai vaai pillai).
  Festival re-enacting it linked to Panguni Uthiram.
- Pathiri tree (sthala vriksham per one source) said to blossom only during the brahmotsavam.
- Festivals: Chithirai brahmotsavam (April–May), flag-hoisting and great car festival; the temple car is among the largest in
  South India (one source: 72 ft total height), finely carved; the old car burned (1990s) and was rebuilt. 63 Nayanmars darshan, Shiva on Rishabha vahana.
- Coordinates: 11.1883, 77.2686 — Wikipedia "Avinasilingeshwarar Temple (Tiruppukkozhiyur)" infobox (11°11′18″N 77°16′07″E),
  cross-checked with findlatitudeandlongitude.com (11.188205, 77.268383). ~9 km from Thirumuruganpoondi entry (distinct).
- Getting there: Tiruppur railway station (~13 km); Coimbatore airport (~40 km) on the NH 544 (Salem–Coimbatore highway).
