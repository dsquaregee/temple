# Ambar Perunthirukoil — Brahmapureeswarar Temple

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Brahmapureeswarar**; goddess **Poonguzhalammai** (also Sugandhakunthalambikai). (Master-list draft goddess "Soundaranayaki?" was wrong — corrected.)
- **Location:** **Ambar (Ambal) Perunthirukoil**, ~2 km west of the Ambar Makaalam temple; Tiruvarur district. ~10.88 N, 79.55 E (approximate).
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam (Kaveri south bank) — hymned by **Tirugnana Sambandar**. A Maadakoil attributed to Kochengat Chola; legend of Brahma (as a swan) freed of a curse at the sacred well.
- **Festivals:** Maha Shivaratri (Masi); Navaratri (Purattasi); Karthikai Mondays; Pradosham.

**Notes:** Coordinates approximate. Genuinely-missing PPS temple found during strict circuit-reconciliation (town-matcher had masked it).
