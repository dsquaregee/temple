# Haridwaramangalam (Tiruaradaiperumpazhi) — Paathaleswarar Temple

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Paathaleswarar** (Padaleswarar / Pathaaleeswarar, "lord of Patala / the netherworld"); goddess **Alankaravalli**. (Worklist goddess "Alankaravalli" — confirmed.)
- **Location:** Haridwaramangalam (Tevaram **Tiruaradaiperumpazhi**), ~10 km west of Alangudi, Valangaiman taluk, Tiruvarur district, Tamil Nadu, PIN 612802. ~10.8317 N, 79.3508 E (Wikipedia).
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam — hymned by Tirugnana Sambandar; one of the **Pancha Aranya Kshetrams** (Vanni vanam); a **rina-vimochana sthalam** (relief from debts). Sthala vriksham **vanni** (shami).
- **Legend:** Shiva hid in **Patala** before his wedding; **Vishnu (Hari)** opened a door (**dwara**) to the underworld to find him → name Haridwaramangalam; lord Paathaleswarar.
- **Festivals:** Maha Shivaratri (Masi); Navaratri (Purattasi); Karthikai Mondays; Pradosham.
