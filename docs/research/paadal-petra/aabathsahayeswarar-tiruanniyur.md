# Aabathsahayeswarar (Agnipureeswarar) Temple, Tiruanniyur / Ponnur (aabathsahayeswarar-tiruanniyur)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Chola Nadu (north of the Kaveri). District: Mayiladuthurai
(formerly listed under Nagapattinam). Tevaram name: Tiruanniyur (திருஅன்னியூர்; today the village of
Ponnur). Tradition: Shaivism.

- Deity: Shiva as Aabathsahayeswarar (Apatsahayar), self-manifested (swayambhu) linga; also praised
  as Agnipureeswarar in some listings. Consort: **Periya Nayaki** (Perianayaki).
- **Do not confuse** with the Agnipureeswarar temple at Anniyur/Vanniyur in Tiruvarur district
  (consort Gowri/Parvati; Wikipedia "Anniyur Agnipureeswarar Temple" — a different shrine), nor with
  Abathsahayeswarar temples at Alangudi, Thirupazhanam or Aduthurai. Wikipedia's article for this
  temple is "Anniyur Abathsahayeswarar Temple".
- Tevaram: sung by Sambandar and Appar (Tirunavukkarasar); Dinamalar lists it as the **22nd Shiva
  temple on the northern bank of the Kaveri** praised in the Tevaram. (Needur — Tiruneedur — is the
  neighbouring shrine, no. 21 in the shivatemples.com sequence.)
- Older names recorded: Bhaskara Kshetram (also "Vikusaranyam" in one Tamil source — spelling not
  confirmed, omitted from the content).
- Coordinates: **approximate, village-level ~11.130, 79.585** (Ponnur, about 5–8 km from
  Mayiladuthurai; sources differ on distance and direction — "5 km north-west" vs "8 km"). No
  sourced temple point found — refine against a mapping source.
- Legends (seed hint "Agni worship" confirmed as the principal tradition):
  1. **Agni** was punished by Veerabhadra for joining Daksha's yagna; created the **Agni
     Theertham** here and worshipped Shiva (basis for the Agnipureeswarar epithet).
  2. **King Harishchandra** cured of leprosy by bathing in the tank and offering curd rice
     (neivedyam); one source derives the name Aabathsahayeswarar from Shiva blessing him.
  3. **Rati** pleaded here for her husband Manmatha (Kama), whom Shiva restored.
  (A "Surya regains his lost hand" variant appears in one source; not used.)
- Architecture: small east-facing temple on the north side of the village; **no gopuram** — the
  entrance has a sculpture of Shiva and Parvati in its place (one source: main tower "not tiered");
  **Nandi mandapa topped by a small decorated vimana**; sanctum with the swayambhu linga; tank
  opposite the entrance (**Agni Theertham / Varuna Theertham**). Sthala vriksham recorded as lemon
  (elumichai) — sources differ in wording.
- Festivals: **Vaikasi Vishakam** curd-rice offering (May–June) to remove obstacles; **sun rays on
  the linga for five days in Panguni** (dates disputed: 24–28, 25–29 or 26–30 Panguni; rising vs
  setting sun) — content says "several days… confirm locally"; Maha Shivaratri; Pradosham.
- Access: Ponnur (Paandur Post, via Needur, Mayiladuthurai Taluk, PIN 609203); town buses toward
  Namashivayapuram and Paandur pass through; walk from the stop. Needur is the neighbouring
  Tevaram shrine.
- **Not verified:** exact temple coordinate; Ponnur's distance/direction from Mayiladuthurai;
  founding dynasty and structural date (medieval, unsourced — century 7 used for the Tevaram era);
  current timings (listings give ~7:00–10:00 / 5:30–19:00 and ~7:00–11:30 / 17:00–20:30); the
  precise sun-ray dates and direction; the sacred tree (lemon/elumichai, wording varies); whether
  Periya Nayaki has a separate shrine (content says honoured in the temple); the "Vikusaranyam"
  older name; Harishchandra legend details (sources differ); airport distances (described
  generally).
