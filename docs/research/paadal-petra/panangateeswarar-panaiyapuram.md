# Panangateeswarar (Panankatteesar / Netroddharakaswami) Temple, Panaiyapuram — research notes

Master-list row 235 (Nadu Nadu). Row confirmed: Panangateeswarar / Sathyambigai (Puravammai; also Meyyambal); Villupuram district; Sambandar.

- Place: Panaiyapuram village, Vikravandi taluk, Villupuram district; Tevaram name Puravar Panangattur (Thirupuravar Panankattur). About 2 km from Vikravandi (Wikipedia); Dinamalar: about 13 km from Villupuram on the Villupuram–Thirukkanur–Puducherry road; Templenet: near Mundiyampakkam. Village also called Paravaipuram.
- Paadal Petra Sthalam, Nadu Nadu (shivatemples nn20). Sung by Thirugnana Sambandar (Nagaswamy quoted; Dinamalar). Only singer confirmed.
- Names: Panangateeswarar (palm-grove Lord) — palm (panai) is the sthala vriksham; Netroddharakaswami ("restorer of eyes") in Rajendra I inscription; Tamil "Kan Parithu Aruliya Nathar". Theertham: Padma Theertham.
- Legend: Surya lost his radiance/sight in the episode of Daksha's sacrifice; he worshipped Shiva here and was restored; hence prayers for eye ailments. Annually, first seven days of Chithirai, sun's rays fall on the lingam and then on the Goddess.
- History: Pallava origin is local belief. Rajendra Chola I built/rebuilt the temple early 11th c. (Nagaswamy: 1025–1040 CE); village named Paravaipuram after Paravai (Wikipedia calls her his "assistant" — content says "Paravai, of Rajendra's court"). Inscriptions: Rajendra I (deity Netroddharaka Swami; land/money gift; mentions conquest of Kadaram/Kedah), Rajendra II, Athirajendra, Kulothunga I, Jatavarman Sundara Pandya I, Vikrama Pandya. NHAI highway widening threatened compound (outcome unknown — omitted).
- Coordinates: 12.0036, 79.5344 — Wikidata Q12986434 (12°0′13″N 79°32′4″E) via search text. ~4 km SSW of Vikravandi town (12.037, 79.544) — consistent. No other catalog temple there.
