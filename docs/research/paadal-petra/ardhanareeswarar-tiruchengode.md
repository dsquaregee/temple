# Ardhanareeswarar Temple, Tiruchengode (Thirukkodimadachengundrur) — ardhanareeswarar-tiruchengode

Master-list row 210 (Kongu Nadu 2). Facts confirmed; no corrections.

- Place: hilltop temple at Tiruchengode, Namakkal district. Tevaram name Thirukkodimadachengundrur (Kodimadachengunrur); hill also called Nagagiri; "Sengodu" — red hill.
- Deity: Shiva as Ardhanareeswarar (half Shiva, half Parvati) — the united form; consort named Bhagampiriyal (Bhagampriyal) in listings.
- Tevaram: Sambandar (one of the 276; Arunagirinathar also sang the Murugan here). Periya Puranam tradition: Sambandar sang the Thiruneelakanta pathigam here to relieve his companions of the Kongu fever (well-known tradition, phrased as tradition).
- Hill ~650 ft high; ~1,200 steps (sources vary 1,156 / 1,206) and a ghat road; a 60-ft snake carved on the rock along the steps (Nagagiri); mandapams along the path; old walls, mandapams, sculptured pillars at the summit.
- Sengottu Velavar (Murugan) shrine on the same hill; Vaikasi Visakam car festival, known as the Sengottuvelavar car festival, with Ardhanareeswarar and Murugan.
- Spring at the foot of the image (Wikipedia snippet, truncated) — mentioned as tradition.
- Legends (tradition): Parvati's penance (Kedara Gowri vratam) to become half of Shiva's body; the hill as a fragment of Meru from the contest of Vayu and Adisesha, red with the serpent's blood.
- Coordinates: the Wikipedia temple infobox (11°15′N 77°56′E) is only arcminute precision and falls ~14 km SE of the town. Used the Tiruchengode town point from Wikipedia (11°22′44″N 77°53′42″E → 11.3789, 77.8950); the hill rises at the edge of town. **Needs a map check for the hilltop point.**
- Access: ~20 km NE of Erode; Erode Junction nearest major railhead; Salem ~45 km.
