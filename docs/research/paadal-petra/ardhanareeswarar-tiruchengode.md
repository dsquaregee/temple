# Ardhanareeswarar Temple, Tiruchengode (ardhanareeswarar-tiruchengode)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Kongu Nadu (Tevaram name: Kodimada Chengunrur / Tirukkodimada Chenkundrur). Tradition: Shaivism.

- Deity: Shiva as Ardhanareeswarar (Ardhanari, half-male half-female; goddess as the left half).
  Temple on the red hill (Chengodu) of Tiruchengode, Namakkal district. Counted as the fourth of
  the seven Tevaram shrines of Kongu Nadu. Hymns attributed to Sambandar.
- Hill names: Nagagiri (in the hymns), Chemmalai, Panimalai, Nandimalai, Uragaverpu. Silappadikaram
  mentions the hill as Neduvelkunru.
- Coordinates: **town-level ~11.38, 77.894** (Tiruchengode, 11.38 N 77.89 E on Wikipedia).
  Exact summit-temple point not found — refine against a mapping source.
- Architecture: Dravidian; hill-top temple reached by ~1,156 steps (some sources say ~1,200) plus
  a motor road; ~60-ft serpent carved in the rock on the ascent; Senkottu Velavar (Murugan,
  sung by Arunagirinathar) and a Vishnu shrine (Adhikesava Perumal) on the same hill; one
  source credits the 84.5-ft northern (Thirumalai) gopuram to Krishnadevaraya, 1512.
- Legend: Adisesha–Vayu contest, serpent fragments falling in three places and reddening the hill
  (hence Chengodu / Nagagiri); Ardhanari legend (goddess united with Shiva as one body).
  Couples pray for harmony and children.
- Festivals: Vaikasi Visakam (May–June); Margazhi early-morning emerald (marakata) lingam darshan
  (~3:30–7 a.m. in one source) and during Uchikala puja; Maha Shivaratri; Pradosham.
- Access: Tiruchengode (Namakkal district); buses from Erode, Salem, Namakkal; railway Erode Jn
  ~20–25 km, Namakkal ~30 km, Salem Jn ~36 km; Salem airport ~50 km.
- **Not verified:** founding dynasty/century of the structural temple (one source says

  step count (1,156 vs ~1,200); which Tevaram saints besides Sambandar sang here (one source
  dates Sambandar to "5th century" — disregarded; century 7 used); Adhikesava Perumal shrine

  Tamil name (not found, omitted); Aadi Pooram / Vaikasi car-festival details not confirmed;
