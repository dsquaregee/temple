# Vajrathampanathar (Vaidyanathaswamy) Temple, Tirumazhapadi (vajrathampanathar-tirumazhapadi)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Chola Nadu (north of the Kaveri). Town: Thirumazhapadi, Ariyalur district, Tamil Nadu, on the northern bank of the Kollidam, close to Tiruvaiyaru. Tevaram name: Tirumazhapadi (திருமழபாடி). Tradition: Shaivism.

- Deity: Shiva as Vajrathampanathar / Vajrasthambanathar / Vajrasthambamoorthy / Vayirathoon Nathar / Vaidyanathaswamy; consort Sundarambigai (also Azhagammai); a separate shrine to Balambigai is also reported.
- Tevaram: sung by Sambandar, Appar and Sundarar (per brief and shivatemples.com's listing). Sundarar's decad **"Ponnar Meniyane"** is the hymn associated with this shrine (tntemplesproject.in; a tradition there says Sundarar passed by without stopping and was rebuked by Shiva, after which he composed it — not used in the prose).
- Coordinates: **10.8994, 79.0583** (Wikipedia: 10°53′58″N 79°03′30″E). Sourced to the temple article; not independently cross-checked.
- Defining legends:
  - **Nandi's wedding** (Nandi Kalyanam): Nandi, son of sage Shilada, is married to Suyasa (spellings vary: Suyasambikai / Swayambikai), daughter of sage Vyaghrapada, with Shiva arranging the match. Held at Panguni Punarpoosam; Aiyarappar of Tiruvaiyaru and the Saptasthanam deities come in procession to Thirumazhapadi. Praying here is held to help those facing delays in marriage.
  - **Name Mazhapadi**: Shiva danced with the *mazhu* (axe/battle-axe) for sage Markandeya (tntemplesproject Tamil text: "மழு ஆடி").
  - **Vajrasthambam**: sage Purushamriga built the shrine for the self-manifested linga; Brahma tried to remove it and could not (tntemplesproject.in). shivatemples.com gives a variant: the linga was brought from Brahma's realm by Purushamriga.
- Architecture: east-facing; flagstaff, balipeedam and Nandi just inside the entrance; separate shrines for Sundarambigai and Balambigai; Dravidian. The Kollidam is called Uttaravahini (northward-flowing) here (aanmeegam.org). **Gopuram tiers were NOT stated in the prose** — one source says seven tiers, none confirmed it.
- Epigraphy (heritage blog; unconfirmed in a primary source): inscriptions of Parantaka I, Rajaraja I, Rajendra I, Kulottunga I etc., recording lamp endowments and land grants. A Flickr post citing an Archaeology Department official claims ~140 inscriptions from Chola to Maratha and Vijayanagara times — not used for a number.
- Festivals: Nandi Kalyanam (Panguni Punarpoosam, Mar–Apr); ten-day Brahmotsavam ending on Masi Magam (Feb–Mar) — sources disagree on month, Masi is used; Maha Shivaratri; monthly Pradosham.
- Access: Kollidam bank near Tiruvaiyaru; Tiruchirappalli the nearest large rail/air hub.
- **Seed hint corrections:** Seed gave goddess "Sundarambigai?" — confirmed (Sundarambikai / Azhagammai). Town "Ariyalur district on the Kollidam" confirmed.
- **Not verified:** founding dynasty / structural date (Chola-era inscriptions from Parantaka I onward per a blog; century 7 used for the Tevaram era); gopuram height and tier count; temple area; whether Appar and Sundarar both have full decads here independent of the brief; distance to Tiruvaiyaru / Tiruchirappalli (given qualitatively); current timings and festival dates; the temple's "Jwarahareswarar" fever-cure epithet seen as a tag on tntemplesproject (not used); exact temple point (the coordinate is Wikipedia's).
