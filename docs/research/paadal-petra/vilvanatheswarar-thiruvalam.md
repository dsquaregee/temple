# Vilvanatheswarar Temple, Thiruvalam — research notes

Master-list row 248 (Thondai Nadu). Deity Vilvanatheswarar (Vilwanatheswarar) confirmed. Consort Vallambigai (Wikipedia); temple site gives Dhanu Madhyambal / Vallambikai — master list's "Thanumathyambal" is a spelling variant. Singer Sambandar confirmed (Wikipedia; place called Thiruvallam in his verses). Century 7 (Tevaram; also inscriptions of Nandivarman II era, 8th c.).

- Place: Thiruvalam (Thiruvallam), village in Vellore district (now Katpadi taluk area), on the Niva / Ponnai river. Administered by HR&CE.
- Paadal Petra Sthalam of Thondai Nadu (shivatemples tn10).
- Name legend: the contest for the fruit — Vinayaka circled his parents Shiva and Parvati (valam = going around) instead of the world, claiming they were his world; it happened here, hence Thiruvalam → Thiruvallam.
- River legend: Shiva called the river "Nee va" ("you come"), and it changed course — hence the name Niva (Neeva); also called Ponnai.
- Inscriptions: many, from the time of Pallava Nandivarman II (8th c.) onward; Chola records of Rajaraja I, Rajendra I, Kulottunga I and III. The Bana king Vikramaditya I appears as a vassal; Tiruvallam is thought to have been a Bana centre (one of its names Vanapuram) — interpretation (SII vol. 3).
- Architecture: five-tiered gopuram; shrines enclosed in concentric rectangular granite walls; three-tiered vimana over the sanctum (Vellore nic). Many parts attributed to the Cholas; present masonry structure largely Nayak (16th c.) per Wikipedia. Shrines of Ganesha, Murugan, Vishwanatha, Gajalakshmi in the complex. Several Nandis (four per shivatemples) — orientation details conflict; not used.
- Festivals: ten-day Brahmotsavam in Masi (Feb–Mar) — Bharatpedia mirror of Wikipedia; month not in the current Wikipedia snippet. Stated with "confirm locally".
- Timings listed 6–12 and 4–8 (not used — no clock times in content).
- Coordinates: 12.9839, 79.2667 — Wikipedia "Vilwanatheswarar temple" infobox (12°59′02″N 79°16′0″E), Bharatpedia mirror agrees. No other catalog temple in Thiruvalam.
