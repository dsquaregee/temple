# Vilvanatheswarar Temple, Thiruvalam (vilvanatheswarar-thiruvalam)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Thondai Nadu (Tevaram name: Thiruvallam). Tradition: Shaivism.

- Deity: Shiva as Vilvanatheswarar (Bilvanathesvara); consort Valli Nayaki per caller.
  **Sources give the consort differently** — Wikipedia: Vallambigai; shivatemples.com:
  Dhanumadhyambal / Vallaambikai (temple town is named for Sri Dhanurmadhyambigai Sametha
  Sri Vilvanadeeswarar). "Valli Nayaki" (caller) not found verbatim in sources — kept as given,
  flagged here.
- Praised in the Tevaram by Sambandar (Pathigam) — Paadal Petra Sthalam; Vellore district
  government lists it among Thondai Nadu Devaram shrines.
- Coordinates: **town-level ~12.974, 79.194** (Thiruvalam village, Wikipedia). Exact temple
  point not found — refine against a mapping source. (A postal listing gives a slightly
  different point ~13.006, 79.208 for the post office.)
- **River:** caller says **Palar**; sources place the temple on the **Ponnai / Niva river**
  (a Wikipedia caption: "Thiruvalam bridge across river Ponnai"). The Ponnai is part of the
  Palar system; prose kept general ("the plain watered by the Ponnai"). Flagged.
- Legends (local tradition, not firm history): (1) the place was a **bilva forest**; a cow
  shed its milk daily over an **anthill (termite mound)** that proved to hold a linga, around
  which the temple grew; (2) a devotee troubled by a giant (Kanja/Kanjan) who seized his water
  was guarded by **Nandi**, sent by Shiva.
- Inscriptions: the **north wall carries one of the earliest inscriptions to name the Tevaram
  singers**; records from 8th-century Pallava **Nandivarman II** and later. (Used century 8.)
- Architecture: Dravidian; entered through a gopuram; sanctum linga (self-formed from an
  anthill); koshta murtis (Vinayaka, Dakshinamurti, Vishnu, Brahma) on the sanctum walls;
  sthala vruksham bilva. **Sources disagree on gopuram tiers (four vs five) and complex size
  (~1 acre vs ~5 acres)** — kept general.
- Access: Thiruvalam village, Vellore district; ~2 km from Tiruvalam railway station
  (Chennai–Katpadi line); ~10 km from Ranipet on the Chennai–Bangalore highway; Vellore /
  Katpadi nearest large centres.

- **Not verified:** consort name (caller: Valli Nayaki; sources: Vallambigai / Dhanumadhyambal);
  river (caller: Palar; sources: Ponnai/Niva); exact temple coordinate (town-level only);
  gopuram tiers and complex size (sources conflict); current timings; precise century of first
  structure (Tevaram 7th c., firm Pallava inscriptions 8th c. — used 8).
