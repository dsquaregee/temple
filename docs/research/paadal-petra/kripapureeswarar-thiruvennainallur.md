# Kripapureeswarar (Thaduthatkonda Nathar) Temple, Thiruvennainallur — research notes

Master-list row 229 (Nadu Nadu). Row facts confirmed: Kripapureeswarar, also Thaduthatkonda Nathar / Arutkonda Nathar / Aatkonda Nathar / Venupureeswarar; consort Mangalambigai. The alternative "Veyuru Tholiammai" not found in search results — omitted.

- Place: Thiruvennainallur (T. V. Nallur), town in Viluppuram district, ~19 km from Villupuram on the Panruti–Thirukkovilur road; on the southern bank of the Pennai.
- Paadal Petra Sthalam, Nadu Nadu (shivatemples nn14). Sung by Sundarar — the site of his first hymn, "Pitha pirai soodi" (opening of the 7th Tirumurai). Century 8.
- Legend (Periya Puranam, widely told): on the day of Sundarar's wedding at Puthur, Shiva came as an aged Brahmin with a palm-leaf deed claiming Sundarar and his descendants as his bondsmen; Sundarar called him "pittha" (madman). The dispute was taken before the elders of Thiruvennainallur, who upheld the deed; the old man led them to the temple and vanished into the sanctum, revealing himself as the Lord. He bade Sundarar sing, beginning with the very word he had used — "Pittha". Hence Thaduthatkonda Nathar, "the Lord who stopped (the wedding) and claimed (him)".
- Meykandar (Meykanda Devar, author of Sivagnana Bodham, 13th c.) is associated with Thiruvennainallur; one source says at age 5 he was initiated by the Vinayaka (Polla Pillaiyar) of this temple.
- History: exact construction date not ascertained; structure Chola (10th c.), possibly renovated in Uttama Chola's time (971–988); Sembiyan Mahadevi credited with endowments; inscriptions mention temple gardens.
- Architecture: Dravidian; granite wall encloses the shrines; lingam in sanctum; ardha mandapa and mukha mandapa. Gopuram five- or seven-tiered (sources conflict — tier count not stated).
- Festivals: Sundarar's day in Aadi (Jul–Aug; "Aadi star day"), Panguni Uthiram (Mar–Apr) with float festival — the most prominent.
- Coordinates: 11.8603, 79.3664 — Wikipedia temple article (11°51′37″N 79°21′59″E) via search text. Town article: 11°52′45″N 79°22′44″E (town point). Distinct from T. Edaiyar (~5 km W).
