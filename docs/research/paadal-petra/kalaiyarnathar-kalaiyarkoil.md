# Kalaiyappar (Swarnakaleeswarar) Temple, Kalaiyarkoil (kalaiyarnathar-kalaiyarkoil)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Pandya Nadu (Tevaram name: Tirukkanapper; also Tirukkanapper in
the Sangam anthology Purananuru). Tradition: Shaivism.

- Deity: Shiva as Kalaiyappar / Kaaleesar / Swarnakaleeswarar; consort Sornavalli
  (Swarnavalli), also called Soundaranayaki (one source also lists Meenakshi — not used).
  Spellings vary (Kalaiyarkoil / Kalayar Kovil / Kaalayar Kovil).
- Location: Kalaiyarkoil, Sivagangai district.
- Coordinates: **town-level ~9.846, 78.632** (Wikipedia 9°50′45″N 78°37′53″E; other geocoders give
  9.847, 78.645 — a few km of spread). Exact temple point not found — refine against a mapping
  source when possible.
- Architecture: two east-facing gopurams — a nine-tiered tower of ~150–157 ft (sources say 150 or
  157) and a smaller five-tiered one; builders disputed across sources (Marudhu Pandiyar
  brothers / Pandya king Sundara Pandiyan / both), so prose calls it traditional. Two
  prakarams; three sanctums — Swarnakaleeswarar (centre), Someswarar (right), Sundareswarar
  (left); goddess shrine. Tradition: Madurai's Meenakshi gopuram was once visible from the top
  of the tower.
- History: Marudhu Pandiyar brothers (Periya and Chinna Marudhu), late 18th c. rulers of the
  Sivagangai country who resisted the British; renewed the temple inside their fort and raised
  a lofty gopuram; memorial on the street opposite the main gopuram. Sundarar is said to have
  visited (one source places this in the 9th century; the Tevaram-era century 8 is used here).
- Legend (as tradition): a childless Pandya king's queen made a golden child image; the king
  later built the temple around the spot — origin of Swarnakaleeswarar. Single-source,
  wiki-style; told as tradition only.
- Festivals: Thai (Jan–Feb) car festival on Poosam for Kaaleesar; Someswarar Brahmotsavam in
  Vaikasi; Aadi Pooram for Swarnavalli amman; Marudhu Pandiyar Guru Pooja every 27 October;
  Maha Shivaratri and Pradosham assumed as at all Shiva temples.
- **Not verified:** which Tevaram saints sang Tirukkanapper (only Sundarar's visit is attested in
  the sources found; Sambandar/Appar not confirmed, so prose says only "the Tevaram"); gopuram
  builders and exact height; the golden-child legend (single source); exact temple coordinate;
  current timings; distances from Madurai/Sivagangai/Karaikudi (not found — prose is general);
  PIN codes conflict across sites (not used).
