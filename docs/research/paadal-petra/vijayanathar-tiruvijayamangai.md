# Vijayanathar (Vijayanatheswarar) Temple, Tiruvijayamangai (vijayanathar-tiruvijayamangai)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Chola Nadu (north of the Kaveri) (Tevaram name: Tiruvijayamangai). District: Thanjavur (Kumbakonam taluk, PIN 612301 per temple listings). Tradition: Shaivism.

- Deity: Shiva as Vijayanathar / Vijayanatheswarar (swayambhu linga); consort Mangalanayaki
  (also given as Mangalambikai / Mangainayaki). Sacred tank: Arjuna Teertham.
- Tevaram: praised by Sambandar and Appar (Wikipedia / Dinamalar / shivatemples). Counted as the
  47th Shiva temple on the northern bank of the Kaveri.
- Coordinates: **approximate ~10.99, 79.27 — NOT sourced.** No source in the searches gave
  lat/lng. Estimated from the sources placing the temple ~11 km (Wikipedia) to 12.9 km
  (shivatemples) from Kumbakonam, in the Thiruvaikavur/Swamimalai belt. Refine against a mapping
  source before relying on it (map pin may be off by several km).
- Defining legend: on Krishna's counsel Arjuna (Vijaya) did penance here for the Pashupatastra;
  the demon Mukasura disturbed him; Shiva appeared as a hunter, a contest followed, and Shiva,
  pleased, revealed himself with Parvati and gave the weapon. Local belief: a scar from
  Arjuna's blow is still visible on the linga. Shiva's name Vijayanathar = lord of Arjuna's
  victory (one source derives the name from Arjuna's archery pleasing Shiva).
- Architecture: sources agree it is a Chola-style temple built by the medieval Cholas
  (Wikipedia; tntemplesproject). Gopuram details conflict: Dinamalar says the rajagopuram is
  smaller than the sanctum vimana; a Tamil guide says the small temple has no gopuram. Content
  therefore says only "compact temple in the Chola manner" and makes no gateway-tower claim.
- Festivals: Masi Maha Shivaratri (Feb–Mar) celebrated grandly (Dinamalar / Wikipedia).
  Monthly Pradosham is generic Shaiva practice, not specifically sourced here.
- Access: alight at Govindaputhur (= Tiruvisayamangai), temple a short walk away (Dinamalar,
  shivatemples); ~11–13 km from Kumbakonam (nearest railway). Timings reported ~8:00 a.m.–12:00
  and 5:00–7:00 p.m. Tiruchirappalli as nearest major airport is general knowledge, distance not
  sourced. Pairing with Thiruvaikavur is a suggestion based on proximity (both north of the Kaveri
  near Kumbakonam); distance between the two not verified.
- **Not verified:** founding dynasty/century of the structural temple (medieval Chola per
  Wikipedia; Dinamalar gives only "1000–2000 years"; century 7 used for the Tevaram era); exact
  coordinates; presence and form of a gopuram; whether Appar's and Sambandar's hymn counts are as
  stated; the detail of the Mukasura episode (single-source, repeated by blogs); whether the Mangalanayaki
  shrine is a separate shrine (the content says only she is worshipped as consort); Pradosham
  observance at this temple; current timings. Note: one source flags confusion between this
  Govindaputhur and another place of the same name (Wikipedia places a Govindaputhur in Ariyalur
  district), which tntemplesproject rejects for this temple — keep the Thanjavur/Kumbakonam
  identification.
- Seed-hint corrections: none beyond the above.
