# Vaikal — Vaigalnathar (Shenbaga Aranyeswarar) Temple

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Vaigalnathar / Shenbaga Aranyeswarar** ("lord of the champak forest"); goddess **Kombial Kothai**.
- **Location:** Vaikal, near Kumbakonam (~12 km; sources also list Nagapattinam district), Tamil Nadu. Approx 10.9500 N, 79.4833 E (approximate).
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam (Tevaram; Appar and others). Historical name **Tiruvaikal Madakoil** — a **Maada Kovil** (traditionally Kochengat Chola). Revered as a **mukkan kshetram**.
- **Name/legend:** sthala vriksham shenbagam (champak); "Vaikal" ~ "vai-kurugal" (a small mound). A small, lesser-documented shrine, in recent times in need of care.
