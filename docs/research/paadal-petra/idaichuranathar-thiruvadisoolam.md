# Idaichuranathar (Gnanapureeswarar) Temple, Thiruvadisoolam (idaichuranathar-thiruvadisoolam)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Thondai Nadu (Tevaram name: Tiruvidaichuram / திருவிடைச்சுரம்). Tradition: Shaivism.

- Deity: Shiva as Idaichuranathar / Gnanapureeswarar; consort Govardhanambigai (also listed as
  Imayamadakodi in Dinamalar).
- Sung by Tirugnanasambandar (one padigam, per shivatemples.com); counted among the Thondai Nadu
  Paadal Petra shrines (sources number it 26th or 27th — numbering varies, not used in content).
- Coordinates: Wikipedia infobox 12°41′54″N 80°01′49″E = **12.698, 80.030** (the brief's seed
  hint of lng 79.98 was off by ~5 km; used the sourced value). Village-level; exact gate not
  independently confirmed.
- Defining legends:
  1. Cow legend: the Goddess took the form of a cow; villagers saw her pour milk over an anthill,
     dug it up, found a Shiva linga, and built the temple. Hence Govardhanambigai ("go" = cow) and
     the place-name Idaichuram.
  2. Sambandar legend: tired and hungry, Sambandar was offered curd by a cowherd under a tree; the
     cowherd led him towards the shrine and vanished near a pond; Sambandar realised it was Shiva
     and sang the padigam addressing Him as Idaichuranathar.
  - Alternative name explanation: village among hills; "churam" = tract between hills.
- Architecture: sources give almost no structural detail (no tier count/height for gopuram found).
  Content therefore stays general: modest Dravidian shrine; sanctum of Idaichuranathar; separate
  shrine for the goddess; Vinayaka shrines reported (Varasiddhi Vinayaka near the entrance,
  Valampuri Vinayaka in the outer prakaram); three trees (bilva, neem, banyan) growing together,
  read as Shiva, Ambica and Vinayaka; tank Katchi Kulam (also listed as Madhura Teertham),
  said to be where the cowherd vanished.
- Inscriptions: ~15 inscriptions of Chola and Vijayanagara kings (land gifts, lamp endowments).
- Festivals (Dinamalar): wedding festival with Panguni Uthiram (Mar–Apr); Chitra Pournami
  (Apr–May); Valaikappu (bangle ceremony) for the goddess on Aadi Pooram (Jul–Aug).
- Timings reported: 7:00–11:00 and 4:30–18:30 (Dinamalar; phone +91-44-2742 0485).
- Access: ~9 km from Chengalpattu on the Chengalpattu–Thirupporur road (Thirupporur ~18 km),
  ~60 km from Chennai. Chengalpattu Junction = nearest major railway; Chennai airport.
- **Not verified:** gopuram tiers/height, structure date or patron of the present building
  (7th century per temple accounts; "2000 years" claims disregarded); sanctum/prakaram
  orientation; Vinayaka shrine locations (one directory only); Maha Shivaratri/Pradosham at this
  temple specifically (stated as general Shiva-temple practice); precise tank name (Katchi Kulam
  vs Madhura Teertham); exact temple coordinate; Chennai airport and rail distances are
  approximate; current timings; whether "Pallava-era" applies (not stated in content).
- Note: a shivatemples.com page describing a three-tiered gopuram for "Idaiyatrunathar" is a
  different temple and was not used. Pallava/Kulothunga Chola details found
  in searches belong to Tirusoolanathar Temple, Tirusulam, and were not used.
