# Tirunellikka — Nellivananathar Temple

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Nellivananathar** (Sanskrit **Amlavaneswarar**, "lord of the gooseberry/nelli forest"); goddess **Mangalanayaki** (also **Amlakeswari**). (Worklist goddess "Mangalanayaki?" — confirmed.)
- **Location:** Tirunellikka, ~17 km from Tiruvarur, ~23 km from Mannargudi, Tiruvarur district, Tamil Nadu, PIN 610205. ~10.6900 N, 79.7000 E (approximate).
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam — hymned by Tirugnana Sambandar; Kaveri south bank (~117th). Sthala vriksham **nelli** (amla/gooseberry); tanks **Brahma** and **Surya** theerthams.
- **Legend:** celestial trees cursed by **Durvasa** to be born as nelli trees here; Shiva stayed among them as a **swayambhu** lingam; Durvasa blessed to overcome his anger.
- **Festivals:** Maha Shivaratri (Masi); Navaratri (Purattasi); Karthikai Mondays; Pradosham.
