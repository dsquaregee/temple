# Ambar Makalam — Mahakalanathar Temple

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Mahakalanathar** (Maakaalanathar); goddess **Pachaiyambikai** (also recorded **Bhakshayambikai**, **Rajamathangi**). (Worklist drafted goddess "Madhurabhashini?"; sources give the above — corrected, variants noted.)
- **Location:** Ambar Makalam / Thirumakalam, via Poonthottam (~3 km), Tiruvarur district, Tamil Nadu; ~21 km from Tiruvarur, ~25 km from Mayiladuthurai. Kaveri south bank. ~10.9700 N, 79.5600 E (approximate).
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam — hymned by Tirugnana Sambandar. One of only **three "Makalam" temples** (Ujjain Mahakaleshwar, Irumbai near Pondicherry, Ambar Makalam). Ambar village also holds the Brahmapureeswarar Paadal Petra temple.
- **Theme:** Shiva as **Mahakala**, conqueror of time and death; Devi in fierce Shakti form.
- **Festivals:** Maha Shivaratri (Masi, special); Navaratri (Purattasi); Karthikai Mondays; Pradosham.
