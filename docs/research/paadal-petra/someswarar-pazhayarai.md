# Pazhayarai Vadathali — Someswarar Temple

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Someswarar**; goddess **Somakamalambikai** (worklist draft "Somakalambikai?" — corrected to Somakamalambikai per shivatemples.com; the Someswarar-Temple Wikipedia article also gives "Somasundari" for a shrine there).
- **Location:** **Pazhayarai Vadathali**, near **Patteeswaram**, ~8 km from Kumbakonam, Thanjavur district — an early-Chola **capital**. ~10.94 N, 79.34 E (approximate).
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam — revered in the Tevaram by **Appar, Sambandar and Sundarar**; believed built in the Chola period (8th–9th c.).
- **Vaippu vs Paadal Petra (resolved):** earlier deferred for ambiguity. The Wikipedia article "Pazhayarai Vadathali" classifies the **Vadathali** (northern) shrine as a Paadal Petra Sthalam revered by all three samayacharyas — so written as a Paadal Petra Sthalam.
- **Two shrines at Pazhayarai:** the **Someswarar** shrine (goddess Somakamalambikai) and the neighbouring **Vadathali Dharmapureeswarar** shrine (goddess Vimalai Nayaki); sources sometimes conflate the names. Body notes this.
- **Festivals:** Maha Shivaratri (Masi); Navaratri (Purattasi); Karthikai Mondays; Pradosham.
