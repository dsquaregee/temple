# Srivanchiyam — Vanchinathar Temple

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Vanchinathar** (swayambhu lingam); goddess **Mangalambikai**.
- **Location:** Srivanchiyam, near Nannilam, Tiruvarur district, Tamil Nadu (~18 km from Tiruvarur). Approx 10.7328 N, 79.6322 E (approximate).
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam — sung in the Tevaram.
- **Distinctive:** One of the rare temples with a **separate shrine for Yama**, given prominence in worship; associated with freedom from fear of death and rebirth for those who die here. Revered for rites for the departed.
- **Tirtha:** **Gupta Ganga** tank (hidden Ganga). Sthala-purana also: Surya regained his radiance here after Daksha's yagna.
- **Festival:** **Masi Magam** — processional deity carried on Yama's mount.
