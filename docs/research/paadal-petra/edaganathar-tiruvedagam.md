# Edaganathar (Edaganatheswarar) Temple, Tiruvedagam (edaganathar-tiruvedagam)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Pandya Nadu (Tevaram name: Thiruvedagam). Tradition: Shaivism.

- Deity: Shiva as Edaganathar / Edaganatheswarar; consort **Elavarkuzhali** (shivatemples spells
  Edavarkuzhali; her shrine is separate, to the right of the main sanctum). Note: the Wikipedia
  infobox names the goddess Visalakshi — conflicts with the temple sources; Elavarkuzhali used.
- Sung by Tirugnana Sambandar; pathigam in the 3rd Tirumurai; listed as the 4th Pandya-country
  Tevaram shrine in one source.
- Location: village on the north bank of the Vaigai, Vadipatti taluk, Madurai district; ~18–20 km
  from Madurai, 3–5 km from Sholavandan (nearest railway). Coordinates: **village-level
  ~9.986, 77.993** (Wikipedia village infobox 9°59′09″N 77°59′35″E). The Wikipedia temple page's
  9°35′N 77°35′E is ~25 km off and was rejected. Exact temple point not found.
- Legend (the temple's defining story): in Sambandar's contest with the Jains at Madurai
  (reign of Pandya king Arikesari Nedumaran), each side set a palm leaf (olai/edu) bearing its
  verses on the flooded Vaigai. The Jains' leaf was carried off; Sambandar's hymn-leaf moved
  **against the current** and halted here. Tradition (shivatemples) has the minister
  Kulachiraiyar follow it; another source has **Vinayaka, in the form of a fish, stop the leaf**
  (shrine of "Vaaduvenra Vinayagar" — rendered "the Vinayaka who won the debate"). Name read as
  Thiru-Edagam, "the place of the leaf". A fire-ordeal variant (Sambandar's Tirunallaru leaf
  unburnt) also exists; sources differ on the format of the contest — prose says "tellings
  differ".
- Flow direction: one temple guide says the Vaigai flows south→north here (Kasi comparison);
  Wikipedia says north→south. Not used in the JSON.
- Architecture: Dravidian; ~5 acres; **two gopurams**, each leading to a sanctum (Wikipedia);
  spacious corridors; Pandya original, present masonry attributed to the 16th-century Madurai
  Nayaks; inscriptions linked to Jatavarman Kulasekhara Pandya and Krishnadevaraya.
- Festivals: **Muthu Pandal** festival on Magam (Feb–Mar), the most prominent of four yearly
  festivals (Wikipedia mirror); Maha Shivaratri; monthly Pradosham.
- Access: city buses from Periyar (routes 29A, 54A, 63, 65, 68) and Anna bus stands (28, 28A, 93),
  and from Tirumangalam (route 4); nearest railway Sholavandan; Madurai airport.
- Timings as listed (third-party): 6:30–12:00 / 5:00–8:00.
- **Not verified:** the goddess's name (Elavarkuzhali vs Visalakshi); exact contest format and the
  Kulachiraiyar/fish-Vinayaka details (each rests on one source — hedged as "tradition says");
  "Vaaduvenra" gloss is the author's reading; exact temple coordinate; current timings and
  route numbers; festival dates.
