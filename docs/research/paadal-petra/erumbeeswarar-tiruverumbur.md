# Erumbeeswarar Temple, Tiruverumbur (erumbeeswarar-tiruverumbur)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Chola Nadu (south of the Kaveri) (Tevaram name: Tiruverumbur, திருவெறும்பூர்). District: Tiruchirappalli. Tradition: Shaivism.

- Deity: Shiva as Erumbeeswarar ("Lord of the Ants"); consort Narunkuzhal Nayaki
  (நறுங்குழல் நாயகி). Some listings also give Soundaranayaki / Madhuvaneswari /
  Rathnambikai as the goddess's names. (The brief's seed hint did not name the goddess.)
- Local name: **Malai Kovil** (hill temple). Counted among the Tevaram shrines on the
  southern bank of the Kaveri. Protected monument (ASI).
- Coordinates: **10.79278, 78.76750** (Wikipedia infobox, 10°47′34″N 78°46′3″E) — a
  temple-level point from Wikipedia; not independently checked against a map.
- Legend (defining story): the demon Tharukasura oppressed the worlds; Indra and the devas
  were told to worship at Tiruverumbur. They took the form of ants to evade the demon but
  could not climb the smooth, slippery linga; Shiva became an ant-hill and **tilted his
  head** so they could climb and worship. The linga is earthen, sandy, rough like an
  ant-hill, and slightly tilted; tradition says it must be shielded from water. Secondary
  tradition (Dinamalar): the hill is a piece of Mount Meru split off by Vayu in a contest
  among the gods. Others said to have worshipped: Muruga, Brahma, Agni, Agastya, sage
  Naimisa.
- Architecture: temple on a ~60 ft (18 m) rocky hill, reached by a flight of steps;
  sanctum with the tilted earthen linga; goddess in a separate shrine.
- Dynasty: Wikipedia describes the temple among those attributed to Chola king Aditya I
  along the Kaveri after his victory at Tirupurambiyam; treated as local tradition, not
  documented founding. Century 7 used for the Tevaram era.
- Festivals: Vaikasi Brahmotsavam (May–June, the major annual festival); Maha Shivaratri
  (Feb–Mar); Aipasi Annabishekam (Oct–Nov); Tirukarthikai (Nov–Dec); monthly Pradosham
  (general practice).
- Access: on the Tiruchirappalli–Thanjavur road, ~11–15 km east of Tiruchirappalli
  (sources differ: 11, 12, 15 km); Tiruverumbur railway station on the
  Tiruchirappalli–Thanjavur line; Tiruchirappalli Junction main railhead;
  Tiruchirappalli International Airport nearest airport. Timings per listings: roughly
  early morning (6:30–8:00) to noon and 4:00/4:30 to 8:30 pm.
