# Dharmapuram (near Karaikal) — Yazhmoorinathar Temple

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Yazhmoorinathar** ("lord of the yazh", the ancient Tamil lute).
- **Location:** Dharmapuram, near Karaikal (Puducherry enclave), close to the Nagapattinam/Mayiladuthurai delta. Approx 10.9167 N, 79.8333 E (approximate).
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam — sung by Sambandar.
- **Signature legend (Yazh Muri Pathigam):** home region of **Tiru Neelakanta Yazhpanar** (one of the 63 Nayanmars, the great yazh-player who set Sambandar's hymns to music); when the yazh could not capture a hymn's depth, he resolved to break his lute, and Sambandar stopped him. Also: Yama worshipped here (Markandeya aftermath); Yudhishtira in the Pandavas' exile.
