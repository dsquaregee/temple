# Thirumeniazhagar (Tirumeni Azhagar) Temple, Tirumayendirapalli / Mayendrapalli (thirumeniazhagar-tirumayendirapalli)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Chola Nadu (north of the Kaveri; southern bank of the Kollidam). Tevaram name: Tirumayendirapalli (திருமயேந்திரப்பள்ளி). District: Mayiladuthurai (Sirkazhi taluk; older sources list Nagapattinam). Tradition: Shaivism.

- Deity: Shiva as Thirumeniazhagar / Tirumeni Azhagar ("the Lord beautiful of form"); consort **Vadivambikai** (Vadivambigai).
- Tevaram: praised by **Tirugnanasambandar** (one pathikam). Verses suggest Surya, Chandra, Brahma and Indra worshipped here.
- Coordinates: **approximate ~11.32, 79.82** (village of Mayendrapalli / Mahendrapalli, south bank of the Kollidam, ~2 km from the sea). Not found in any source; inferred from "~25 km from Sirkali (11.24, 79.73)" and the Kollidam position. Refine against a mapping source.
- Place-name legend: Mahendra (Indra) worshipped Shiva here → Mahendrapalli. Tradition (as reported) that Indra came to be freed of a curse; Mahendra Theertham is the temple tank in front of the temple. Devotees believe bathing and worshipping for **48 consecutive days** cures long-standing ailments.
- Architecture: east-facing; **three-tiered rajagopuram**; Mahendra Theertham in front; shrines to Vinayaka (Rahu and Ketu placed beside him — **no separate Navagraha shrine**), Kasi Viswanathar, Mahavishnu, Bhairava, Surya, Chandra; Nataraja shrine with Sivakami and Manikkavasagar.
- Sun phenomenon: sun's rays fall on the linga for ~7 days in **Panguni** (Mar–Apr) — Surya Puja.
- Festivals: Panguni Surya Puja; Maha Shivaratri; Panguni Uthiram; Margazhi Tiruvadirai; Tirukarthikai; monthly Pradosham.
- Access: on the Chidambaram–Sirkazhi route cross the Kollidam bridge, take the branch road to Aachalpuram (Tirunallur Perumanam) and Mayendrapalli; ~7 km from Aachalpuram; villageinfo.in gives ~25 km from Sirkali. Buses from Chidambaram and Sirkazhi. Postal: Mayendirapalli via Aachalpuram, PIN 609101.
- Seed-hint corrections: none needed beyond adding consort and district (Mayiladuthurai, formerly Nagapattinam).
- **Not verified:** exact temple coordinate (approximate above); the Gautama/Ahalya-curse detail for Indra (reported in one search summary, not corroborated in others — prose says only "a grievous curse"); founding dynasty and century of the structural temple (century 7 used for the Sambandar era); the exact number and dates of Panguni sun-ray days (sources say ~7 days); current timings (sources disagree — Dinamalar 9–12 and 6–7:30; prose kept general); the sentence that the sanctum is "aligned" for the sun is an inference from the phenomenon; nearest-railway statement (Sirkazhi) is general knowledge; a "Masi Magam sea-bath" festival seen on a shivatemples.com page appeared to refer to a nearby temple and was not used.
