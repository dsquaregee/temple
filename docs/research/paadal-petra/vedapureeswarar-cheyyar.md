# Vedapureeswarar Temple, Cheyyar (Thiruvothur) — research notes

Master-list row 246 (Thondai Nadu). Deity Vedapureeswarar confirmed. Consort: sources give Balakujambigai (Bala Kusambikai) with the Tamil name **Ilamulai Nayagi** — the master list's "Elavarkuzhali" was not found for this temple; used Balakujambigai / Ilamulai Nayagi. Singer Sambandar confirmed (palm legend).

- Place: Thiruvothur, now part of Cheyyar town (locally Thiruvathipuram / Tiruvaththipuram), Cheyyar taluk, Tiruvannamalai district, PIN 604 407; on the northern bank of the Cheyyar (Seyyaru) river. Shivatemples: about 33 km south-west of Kanchipuram. Temple is close to the highway (devotee blog).
- Paadal Petra Sthalam of Thondai Nadu (shivatemples tn08). Sung by Thirugnana Sambandar. Century 7.
- Name/legend: Wikipedia — the Lord as Vedapureeswarar came here and imparted the Vedas (taught their meaning). "Othur" connected by tradition with ōthu, to recite/teach.
- Palm legend: a devotee who tended palms in the temple grounds lamented that all were male (barren); Sambandar sang a pathigam and the male palms became fruit-bearing female palms. A sculpture in the inner prakaram shows a palm beside a Shiva lingam with Sambandar standing near. The palm (panai) is the sthala vriksham.
- Dynasty/inscriptions: none found in search results — not stated. Tamil Digital Library has a book "Thiruvothur Varalaru" referring to the temple's inscriptions (content not read).
- Festivals: not found specifically — customary Shaiva observances only (Pradosham, Maha Shivaratri, Karthigai Deepam, Arudra Darshan), "confirm locally".
- Coordinates: Wikipedia temple infobox gives 12°23′N 79°19′E (12.39, 79.32) — arcminute-only and ~30 km SW of Cheyyar town, inconsistent with the temple being in Cheyyar on the river's north bank; rejected. Used the Cheyyar town point from Wikipedia "Cheyyar" (12.662°N, 79.5435°E; older revision 12°39′29″N 79°32′33″E). **Town point — needs a map check for the temple itself.** No other catalog temple in Cheyyar.
