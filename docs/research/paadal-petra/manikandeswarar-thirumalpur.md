# Manikandeswarar Temple, Thirumalpur (Thirumarperu) — research notes

Master-list row 249 (Thondai Nadu). Deity Manikandeswarar confirmed; consort Anjanakshi (Dinamalar), also Karunai Nayagi (Wikipedia). Singers Appar and Sambandar confirmed — six pathigams: two by Sambandar, four by Appar (Wikipedia). Periya Puranam records that Sundarar also visited and worshipped (no hymn). Century 7.

- Place: Thirumalpur, Ranipet district (formerly Vellore), on the Ocheri–Thirumalpur road; Tevaram name Thirumarperu. Thirumalpur railway station on the Chengalpattu–Arakkonam line (general knowledge; station name exists).
- Paadal Petra Sthalam of Thondai Nadu (shivatemples tn11).
- Name: Tirumal (Vishnu) worshipped Shiva here — Thirumal-pur.
- Legend: Vishnu's discus was damaged/lost power against the body of sage Dadhichi (versions: battle on behalf of King Kshuva/Kuban); to regain a discus he worshipped Shiva with a thousand flowers daily; Shiva hid one flower; Vishnu offered his own eye in its place; Shiva, pleased, granted him the Sudarshana chakra. (Tradition — variants.)
- History: Wikipedia — believed built under the Cholas in the 9th century. Inscriptions: a Parthivendravarman (Pallava-period) record about the building of the temple and enclosing verandah by Paramandaladitya (SII 22 — transcript partly missing); Uttama Chola 14th-year record on the north wall of the central shrine (SII 3.142); Chola patronage from Aditya I to Rajaraja I; Rajaraja I gold flower gift 988 CE (reported).
- Gopuram: five-tier vs four-tier conflict — tier count not stated.
- Festivals: six daily rituals, three yearly festivals; Maasi Magam, Aadi Pooram, Aani Thirumanjanam, Navarathri; Garuda Seva for Vishnu during the Brahmotsavam (recalling Vishnu's worship here).
- Coordinates: 12.9567, 79.6736 — Wikipedia "Thirumarperu" infobox (12°57′24″N 79°40′25″E). No other catalog temple in Thirumalpur.
