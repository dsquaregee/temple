# Bhaktajaneswarar Temple, Thirunavalur — research notes

Master-list row 223 (Nadu Nadu). Confirmed: Bhaktajaneswarar (Thirunavaleswarar), consort Manonmani (also Sundaranayaki / Sundarambikai), singer Sundarar. District: Wikipedia/Wikidata say Viluppuram; Thirunavalur block now lies in Kallakurichi district (carved out of Viluppuram in 2019) — master list's "Kallakurichi" kept; content just says the village is on the Viluppuram–Kedilam road.

- One of the 276 Paadal Petra Sthalams; 8th in Nadu Nadu (shivatemples nn08). Sung by Sundarar.
- Birthplace of Sundarar (Nambi Aruran, 8th c.); a newly consecrated Sundarar temple stands a little down the street on the site of his birth-house. Inside the main temple, a Sundarar shrine in the NE corner shows him standing with his two consorts (Paravai Nachiyar of Tiruvarur and Sangili Nachiyar of Thiruvottriyur).
- Epigraphy (Balasubrahmanyam, Early Chola Temples; Wikipedia): an inscription of Parantaka I's 28th year says the temple was built in stone by his eldest son Rajadityadevar; from Parantaka's 29th year it is called Rajadityeswaram; other inscriptions indicate conversion from an earlier brick structure. Rashtrakuta Krishna III (Kannaradeva) records mention his defeat of the Cholas and the death of Rajaditya at Takkolam. Later inscriptions of Rajaraja I, Kulothunga I and Rajendra III.
- Festivals: Sundarar's guru puja (Aadi Swathi — customary Nayanar guru puja day; tntemplesproject tags guru puja) — present as customary + confirm locally; Shivaratri, Pradosham.
- Century: 8 (Sundarar only; stone temple 10th c.).
- Coordinates: 11.7588, 79.3988 — English Wikipedia infobox (11°45′31.5″N 79°23′55.5″E), matching Wikimedia Commons. Wikidata Q16313601 gives 11°45′48″N 79°23′51″E (~500 m off) — not used. No other catalog entry here (the Varadaraja Perumal temple in the village is not in the catalog).
