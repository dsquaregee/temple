# Athmanatheswarar Temple, Tiruvalampozhil (athmanatheswarar-tiruvalampozhil)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Chola Nadu south of the Kaveri. Tradition: Shaivism.
- Deity: Shiva as Athmanatheswarar; consort Vadivudai Nayaki. Sung by Appar (place = Tiruvalampozhil).
- Legend: the Ashta Vasus, cursed to human birth for stealing Kamadhenu, worshipped here to shorten it.
- Near Pushpavananathar, Tiruppoonthuruthi; delta village temple, Chola-maintained; inscriptions confirm the literary references.
- Coordinates: ~10.9167, 79.2667 (approximate, village-level — refine against a map).
- Not verified: exact founding century/dynasty (used 7, Tevaram era); precise temple coordinate; consort name given per Dinamalar/tradition.
