# Tirumeninathar (Bhoominathar) Temple, Tiruchuli (tirumeninathar-tiruchuli)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Pandya Nadu (Tevaram name: Thiruchuzhiyal). Tradition: Shaivism.

- Deity: Shiva as Tirumeninathar / Bhoominathar; consort **Thunaimalai Nayaki** (Thunaimalai
  Ammai), also given as Sahayavalli.
- Sung by Sundarar (7th Tirumurai); regarded as the 12th Tevaram shrine of the Pandya country.
- Location: Tiruchuli, Virudhunagar district, on the Virudhunagar–Manamadurai road; ~15 km from
  Aruppukottai, ~55–60 km from Madurai. Coordinates: **town-level ~9.533, 78.200** (Tiruchuli
  town 9°32′N 78°12′E). Wikipedia's temple infobox (9°42′N 78°29′E) is inconsistent with the
  town and was not used. Exact temple point not found.
- Legend: a world-ending deluge (in one telling, the Dwapara Yuga); a Shiva-devoted Pandya king
  prayed; Shiva struck the earth with his trident, and the floodwaters whirled into the cavity
  around the trident — Bhoomi (earth) saved; hence Bhoominathar and "Chuzhiyal" (swirl). A
  variant (shivatemples) has Shiva relieving the Pandya king Bruhat Balan of a sin and
  controlling the deluge.
- Architecture: Dravidian; ~2.5 acres; shrines within concentric rectangular walls; an elegant
  gopuram leads to the Sahayavalli shrine; high boundary walls attributed to Muthuramalinga
  Setupati's reign; original complex believed Pandya, later additions by other dynasties;
  sthala vriksham punnai; temple tank with a central mandapam (Kavvaikadal in one source);
  theertham given variously (Kundaru / Bhagavari river).
- Festivals: **Thirukalyanam** in Panguni (Wikipedia says Feb–Mar; Panguni is Mar–Apr, the JSON
  says Mar–Apr); Chitra Pournami; Maha Shivaratri; monthly Pradosham.
- Ramana Maharshi: born 30 Dec 1879 at Tiruchuli, in a house adjacent to (north-east of) the
  temple; parents Sundaram Iyer and Alagammal; the house, now **Sundara Mandiram**, is a
  memorial (acquired 1944 by Niranjananda Swami; Tiruchuzhi Nadar Sangha held it from 1934).
  Sources do not say "opposite" the temple — JSON says "beside/adjacent".
- **Not verified / deliberately omitted:** the "Sivaganga tank that never dries" in the brief —
  **no source found**; the tank is named Kavvaikadal in one source and the river Kundaru/
  Bhagavari elsewhere, so the JSON does not claim a Sivaganga tank or that any water never dries.
  Also not verified: century/patron of the structural temple (Pandya foundation; century 8 used
  for Sundarar's era); the "two gopurams" claim (only one gopuram is sourced); exact temple
  coordinate; current timings; festival dates.
