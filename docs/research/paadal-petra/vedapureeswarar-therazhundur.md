# Therazhundur — Vedapureeswarar Temple

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Vedapureeswarar**; goddess **Soundaraambigai**.
- **Location:** **Therazhundur** (Tevaram Tiruvazhundur), ~15 km from Mayiladuthurai; Mayiladuthurai district (some sources Nagapattinam). ~11.00 N, 79.63 E (approximate).
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam — hymned by **Tirugnana Sambandar**. Legend: Ganesha pointed out this Shiva shrine to the child-saint Sambandar (vs the neighbouring Vishnu temple, the Divya Desam Amaruviappan). Marriage/reunion prarthana sthalam. Kulottunga Chola I inscriptions.
- **Note:** distinct from the catalog's other Vedapureeswarar shrines (Tiruvedikudi, Tiruvothur, Thiruverkadu).
- **Festivals:** Maha Shivaratri (Masi); Navaratri (Purattasi); Karthikai Mondays; Pradosham.
