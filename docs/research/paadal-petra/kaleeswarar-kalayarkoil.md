# Swarna Kaleeswarar Temple, Kalayarkoil (Thirukkanapper) — kaleeswarar-kalayarkoil

Master-list row 203 (Pandya Nadu 10). Facts confirmed; no corrections.

- Place: Kalayarkoil (Kalaiyar Kovil), Sivaganga district, Tamil Nadu. Sangam name Kaanapper / Kaanappeyil (Purananuru 21, Aiyur Mulankizhar); Tevaram name Thirukkanapper.
- Paadal Petra Sthalam of Pandya Nadu; Tevaram pathigams by Sambandar and Sundarar (one each per shivatemples listing).
- Three main shrines in one complex: Swarna Kaleeswarar with Swarnavalli; Someswarar with Soundaravalli (Soundaranayaki); Sundareswarar with Meenakshi.
- Gopurams: a tall 9-tier gopuram (~150–157 ft) and a smaller 5-tier gopuram; attribution varies between sources (Marudhu brothers vs. Pandya king Sundara Pandya) — content says the tall tower is associated with the Marudhu brothers' renovation and leaves the smaller tower's builder out.
- Marudhu brothers (Periya and Chinna Marudhu), rulers of Sivaganga in the late 18th century, renovated the temple; tradition: Meenakshi temple gopuram at Madurai visible from the top; their memorial stands opposite the temple. Temple managed by the Sivaganga Devasthanam (royal family trust).
- Tank: large Teppakulam named Aanai Madu (elephant tank), with a central mandapam; tradition says dug by Airavata, Indra's elephant.
- Legend: Sundarar, seeing the ground full of lingams, would not tread on it; Shiva in the form of a bull (kaalai) showed the path — linked to the name Kalaiyar Kovil ("temple of the Lord of the bull").
- Coordinates: 9.8457, 78.6314 (Wikipedia infobox, 9°50′45″N 78°37′53″E, via search result text).
- Access: ~18 km from Sivaganga, ~65 km from Madurai; nearest railhead Sivaganga; airport Madurai.
