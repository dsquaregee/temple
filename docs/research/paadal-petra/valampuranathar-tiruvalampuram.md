# Tiruvalampuram (Melaperumpallam) — Valampuranathar Temple

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Valampuranathar** (a **prithvi lingam of sand**); goddess **Vaduvakirkanni** (Swarna Padmambigai / Sankari / Gnana Soundari). (Worklist draft goddess "Vadivambikai?" was wrong — corrected to Vaduvakirkanni.)
- **Location:** **Melaperumpallam** (Tevaram Tiruvalampuram), ~2 km off the Poompuhar branch road, ~18 km from Sirkazhi, ~20 km from Mayiladuthurai, near the Kaveri's mouth. ~11.13816 N, 79.80934 E (Wikipedia exact — distinct from Keelaperumpallam village coords).
- **District:** Wikipedia **Mayiladuthurai**; TN Temples Project & Dinamalar **Nagapattinam**. Noted in body.
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam — **Sambandar, Appar, Sundarar**. Legends: conch oriented right (valam); sage **Herandar** restored the vanished Kaveri (jeeva samadhi here); birthplace of **Sati**.
- **Festivals:** Maha Shivaratri (Masi); Navaratri (Purattasi); Karthikai Mondays; Pradosham.
