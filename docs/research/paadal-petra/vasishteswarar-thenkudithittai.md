# Vasishteswarar Temple, Thenkudithittai (vasishteswarar-thenkudithittai)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Chola Nadu south of the Kaveri. Tradition: Shaivism.
- Deity: Shiva as Vasishteswarar (worshipped by sage Vasishta); consort Loganayaki.
- Guru (Jupiter) sthalam — notable separate Guru shrine with its own vimana; also a south-facing Dakshinamurthy shrine.
- On a mound between the Vennar and Vettaru; pralaya legend for the village name; a water-drop said to fall on the linga each naazhigai.
- Coordinates: ~10.86, 79.13 (approximate, village-level near Thanjavur — refine against a map).
- Not verified: exact founding century/dynasty (used 7, Tevaram era); precise temple coordinate; no confirmed navagraha-set shrine.
