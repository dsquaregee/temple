# Thenkurangaduthurai — Apathsahayeswarar Temple

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Apathsahayeswarar** (Abathsahayeswarar), 'the help in adversity'; goddess **Pavalakkodi Ammai** (Wikipedia: Prabhavalli).
- **Location:** **Thenkurangaduthurai**, at **Aduthurai**; Thanjavur district. ~11.02 N, 79.48 E (approximate).
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam (Kaveri south) — hymned by **Sambandar and Appar**. Tall 5-tier western gopuram (~72 ft). Chief festival **Aippasi Annabishekam**.
- **Note:** "Then" (south) Kurangaduthurai — distinct from the catalog's **Dayanidheeswarar at (Vada) Tiruvadakurangaduthurai**.
