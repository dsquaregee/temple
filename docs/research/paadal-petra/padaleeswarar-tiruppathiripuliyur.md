# Padaleeswarar Temple (padaleeswarar-tiruppathiripuliyur)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Nadu Nadu. Tradition: Shaivism.

- Deity: Padaleeswarar (Karaiyetreeswarar); Periyanayaki.
- Tiruppathiripuliyur, in Cuddalore town, Cuddalore district (a large temple). Coords 11.7456, 79.7534.
- Legend: Appar, bound to a stone and cast into the sea by opponents, was borne safely ashore here by the Lord's grace (Karaiyetreeswarar, "who brought [him] to shore").
