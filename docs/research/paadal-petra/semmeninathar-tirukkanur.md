# Semmeninathar (Karumbeswarar) Temple, Tirukkanur (semmeninathar-tirukkanur)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Chola Nadu (north of the Kaveri) (Tevaram name: Tirukkanur / Tirukkaanoor). District: Thanjavur (near Tirukkattupalli, on the Kollidam; locality also called Manalmedu). Tradition: Shaivism.

- Deity: Shiva as Semmeninathar ("the red-bodied Lord"); the processional (utsava) form is
  called Karumbeswarar. Consort: **Sivayoga Nayaki** (Sivayoganayaki; Dinamalar also lists
  "Soundaryanayaki" — not used).
- Tevaram: hymns by Sambandar and Appar (Tirunavukkarasar) — shivatemples.com lists one
  pathikam each. Counted as the 56th Shiva temple on the northern bank of the Kaveri
  (Dinamalar); Wikipedia places it among the 275 Paadal Petra Sthalams (counts differ by source).
- Coordinates: **approximate ~10.89, 78.96** — no sourced coordinate found. Derived from
  the temple being ~6 km from Tirukkattupalli (town ~10.85, 78.96) toward the Kollidam.
  The only mapping hit (11.99, 79.64) is a different Thirukkanur. Refine against a mapping
  source before relying on it.
- Legends (three local traditions, presented as tradition): (1) Parvati did penance here and
  Shiva appeared as a red fire — origin of "Semmeninathar" (blog source); (2) Parasurama
  worshipped Shiva to be rid of the sin of killing kshatriyas (Wikipedia); (3) the shrine lay
  buried under Kollidam sand and was found by a shepherd with one sugarcane growing at the
  spot — origin of "Karumbeswarar" (karumbu = sugarcane). Wikipedia also associates the place
  with the Chola king Karikala.
- Architecture (blog/Wikipedia-level sources): east-facing; two-tiered rajagopuram with solar
  and lunar eclipse reliefs on its ceiling; swayambhu linga; goddess in a separate
  south-facing shrine with a vesara-style vimana.
- Festivals: sun's rays fall on the linga in Panguni, reported as 2–4 April (Dinamalar,
  "Surya Puja"); Maha Shivaratri and Pradosham are generic (not source-specific).
- Access: Buses run only to Tirukkattupalli (~6 km); auto/car from there; last stretch
  unpaved. Priest lives in Tirukkattupalli — visits best arranged in advance. Nearest
  railway listed: Budhalur; airport: Tiruchirappalli. Dinamalar timings (dated): ~10:00–11:30
  and ~15:30–17:00.
- Correction to seed hints: the seed called the place simply "Tirukkanur"; research adds that
  Karumbeswarar is an alternate name and that the locality is also Manalmedu. The "red-bodied
  Lord" gloss is confirmed (Semmeni = red body).
- **Not verified:** founding dynasty/century of the structure (century 7 used for the Tevaram
  era; Karikala link is tradition only); exact coordinates; the date/cause of the sand burial
  (one blog gives the 1924 flood and a 20th-century revival — conflicting, not used); name of
  the sacred tank (Kollidam vs Vedha Theertham — sources differ); sun-ray dates (vary by
  year); current timings; Tevaram verse numbers; the Parvati-penance legend rests on a
  non-scholarly blog.
