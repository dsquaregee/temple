# Thirumuruganatheswarar Temple, Thirumuruganpoondi — research notes

Master-list row 212 (Kongu Nadu 4; shivatemples kn4). **Correction:** the consort is Avudainayagi
(also called Muyangu Poonmulaiyammai in the Tamil form); "Mangalambigai" was not found in any source and is not used.

- Place: Thirumuruganpoondi, Tiruppur district, on the Tiruppur–Avinashi road (about 9 km from
  Tiruppur; about 5 km from Avinashi — sources vary slightly).
- One of the 276 Paadal Petra Sthalams, Kongu Nadu. Sung by Sundarar only (the "Kodugu Venchilai" pathigam,
  sung after the robbery). Century 8 (Sundarar only).
- Moolavar: Thirumuruganatheswarar / Thirumuruganathaswamy (lingam). Ambal: Avudainayagi.
- Present structure believed to date to the Kongu Cholas, 10th century (Wikipedia). No rajagopuram — unusual.
- Legend 1 (name): Murugan, after slaying Surapadman, incurred the sin of killing (brahmahatya) and installed and
  worshipped a lingam here to be freed of it; hence the Lord's name. The brahmahatya is said to remain as a square
  stone under the neem tree outside the temple.
- Legend 2: Sundarar, returning from Chera country with gifts from the Chera king (Cheraman Perumal), was waylaid by
  Shiva in the guise of a hunter (with his ganas) and robbed; he sang a pathigam of protest and the Lord restored the goods.
  One version: he had rested before the Ganesha shrine without entering to worship. Local tradition: Ganesha showed him
  where the goods were hidden.
- Two images of Sundarar: one sorrowful (after losing the gifts), one joyful (after recovery). Inner mandapam has an
  image of Shiva as hunter.
- Three theerthams: Shanmuga theertham (centre), Gnana theertham (left), Brahma theertham (right). Pandya king
  Maharadhan, childless, bathed in Shanmuga theertham and offered payasam made with its water; was blessed with children (twins in one version).
- Festivals: Vedupari festival re-enacts the robbery annually; customary Shaiva observances (Maha Shivaratri, Pradosham) — confirm locally.
- Coordinates: 11.1650, 77.3114 — Wikipedia "Thirumuruganatheeswar Temple" infobox (11°09′54″N 77°18′41″E) via search text.
  The town article gives 11°10′44″N 77°18′52″E (town centre, not used).
- Getting there: Tiruppur railway station (~9 km); Coimbatore airport (~45 km by road).
