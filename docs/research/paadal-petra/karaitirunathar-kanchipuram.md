# Karaitirunathar (Sathyanatheswarar) Temple, Thirukalimedu, Kanchipuram (Kachi Nerikaraikadu) — research notes

Master-list row 243 (Thondai Nadu). Row facts confirmed: Karaitirunathar / Sathyanatheswarar (also Karaivananathar); consort Pramarambikai (festival image in a south-facing shrine); singer Sambandar (one pathigam).

- Place: Thirukalimedu locality on the eastern side of Kanchipuram, near the Indra Theertham tank.
- 5th Thondai Nadu sthalam (Dinamalar; shivatemples tn05 "Kaarai Tirunatheswarar, Kachchineri Kaaraikkadu").
- Name: "Karai" trees once surrounded the site (Karaivanam forest) → Thiru Neri Karaikadu (old name cited from South Indian Inscriptions Vol. VIII by casualwalker); Dinamalar gives Karai Tirunagar.
- Main lingam swayambhu; central shrine faces east.
- Legends: Indra, cursed by sage Gautama after the Ahalya episode, did penance here to regain his form; Indra Theertham linked to his ablutions. Indra and Budhan (Mercury) worshipped Shiva here — temple sought by devotees for Budhan-related afflictions in astrology.
- Festivals (Dinamalar): Margazhi Thiruvadhirai, Maha Shivaratri, Aippasi Annabhishekam.
- Coordinates: 12.8292, 79.7169 — Wikipedia "Sathyanatheswarar temple" infobox (12°49′45″N 79°43′01″E). Distinct from other Kanchipuram entries (Varadaraja Perumal area is further south-east; checked catalog).
