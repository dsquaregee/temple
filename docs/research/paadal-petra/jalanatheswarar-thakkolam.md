# Jalanatheswarar Temple, Thakkolam (Thiruooral) — research notes

Master-list row 250 (Thondai Nadu). Deity Jalanatheswarar (Jalantheeswarar / Jalanatheeswarar) confirmed; master list's "Umapatheeswarar" alias not confirmed in search — omitted. Consort Giriraja Kannikambal confirmed. Singer Sambandar (one pathigam) confirmed. Century 7.

- Place: Thakkolam (Takkolam), Ranipet district (formerly Vellore), near Arakkonam; on the Kosasthalaiyar (one source: Kallaru). Tevaram/inscription name Thiruooral (Tiruvural); epigraphic name Aparajita-chaturvedimangalam.
- Paadal Petra Sthalam of Thondai Nadu (shivatemples tn12).
- Lingam: of sand (prithvi lingam), called Theenda Thirumeni — not touched by hand; tradition: Parvati embraced it during a flood, leaving her imprint.
- Legend: Nandi, guided by sage Uthathi, channelled the waters of the Ganga through his mouth — the Sathya Ganga theertham west of the Gangadharar shrine; water flows from the Nandi's mouth in times of high water (one account).
- Name legend: Daksha's yagna destroyed by Veerabhadra here; Daksha's dying cry "O" → Thakkolam (tradition).
- History/dating conflicts: Pallava origin (6th c. per one source; Aparajita Varman, 876, per another; Pallava iconography). Chola inscriptions: Aditya I; Parantaka I (7th to 35th regnal year); Rajendra I gifts; Kulottunga III, Rajaraja III. Battle of Takkolam (c. 949) between Cholas and Rashtrakuta Krishna III was fought nearby; Chola prince Rajaditya killed. Temple in need of restoration (2025 news items) — not used.
- Shrines: Nataraja sabha, Dakshinamurthy, Ambal shrine, Gangadharar shrine.
- Festivals: not found — customary observances, confirm locally.
- Coordinates: 13.0158, 79.7369 — Wikipedia "Jalantheeswarar Temple" infobox (13°00′57″N 79°44′13″E). No other catalog temple in Thakkolam.
