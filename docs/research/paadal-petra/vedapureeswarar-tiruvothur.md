# Vedapureeswarar Temple, Tiruvothur (vedapureeswarar-tiruvothur)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Thondai Nadu (Tevaram name: Thiruvothur). Tradition: Shaivism.

- Deity: Shiva as Vedapureeswarar; consort Balambikai, also called Ilamulai Ammai
  (sources also give the consort as Bala Kusambikai / Elamulai Nayagi — variant spellings).
- Counted among the 275/276 Paadal Petra Sthalams; praised in the Tevaram by the child-saint
  Tirugnana Sambandar.
- Coordinates: **town-level ~12.662, 79.543** (Cheyyar / Tiruvothur town centre, Wikipedia).
  Exact temple point not found in sources — refine against a mapping source when possible.
- Legend (the temple's defining story): a devotee/caretaker had raised palmyra palms on the
  temple grounds but grieved they were all **male (aan) and bore no fruit**; the child-saint
  Sambandar sang a decad (padigam) to the Lord of Tiruvothur, and by Shiva's grace the male
  palms were transformed into **female (pena), fruit-bearing** trees — the "Othur" / aan-pena
  miracle. Commemorated by a sculpture in the inner prakaram (palm + linga + Sambandar).
- Sthala vruksham: the **palmyra palm** — Tiruvothur is one of a small handful of Shiva temples
  whose sacred tree is the palm.
- "Vedapureeswarar" glossed as the master/lord of the (sacred) Vedas; town name linked to this.
- Architecture: Dravidian; sanctum linga as Vedapureeswarar; goddess Balambikai in a separate
  shrine. (Gopuram tiers / complex size / exact orientation not firmly sourced — kept general.)
- Festivals: ten-day **Brahmotsavam** with street processions; **seventh-day car festival
  (ther)** the highlight; Maha Shivaratri; monthly Pradosham.
- Access: Tiruvothur adjoins **Cheyyar** town, Tiruvannamalai district, on the Kanchipuram–
  Vandavasi road; ~40 km from Kanchipuram; nearest major railheads Kanchipuram / Arakkonam;
  Chennai nearest airport.
- **Location note:** sources treat "Thiruvothur" (Tevaram name) and the present **Cheyyar**
  town as the same place; the temple is on the north bank of the Cheyyar river. District given
  as Tiruvannamalai. The caller's "near Cheyyar" is consistent.

- **Not verified:** founding dynasty/century of the structural temple (medieval; patrons not
  firmly sourced — used century 7 for the Tevaram/Sambandar era); exact temple coordinate
  (town-level only); gopuram tiers and complex size; current timings; precise consort spelling
  (Balambikai per caller; sources vary — Bala Kusambikai / Elamulai Nayagi).
