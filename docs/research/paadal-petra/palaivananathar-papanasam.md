# Papanasam — Palaivananathar Temple (Tiruppalaithurai)

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Palaivananathar** ("lord of the palai / iron-wood forest"); goddess **Dhavala Vennagai Ammai** (Thavala Vennagai Ammai).
- **Location:** Papanasam town (historical name Tiruppalaithurai), Thanjavur district, Tamil Nadu (~18 km from Kumbakonam). Approx 10.9272 N, 79.2706 E (approximate).
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam — sung in the Tevaram (Appar; also Sambandar).
- **Legends:** town name **Papanasam** ("washing-away of sin") from **Rama** worshipping here (made lingams at nearby Ramalingaswami) to be freed of Ravana's slaying; also the Tharukavanam sages' tiger subdued by Shiva. Worshipped by Rama, Lakshmana, Sita, Gautama, Arjuna.
- **Fabric:** five-tiered gopuram; notable **Vijayanagara-era brick granary** (protected monument).
