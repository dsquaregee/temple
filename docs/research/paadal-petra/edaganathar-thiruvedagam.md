# Edaganathar Temple, Thiruvedagam — research notes

Master-list row 197 (Pandya Nadu 4). Row facts confirmed; no corrections.

- Place: Thiruvedagam (Tiruvedakam), village on the bank of the Vaigai, ~18 km NW of Madurai, ~3 km from Sholavandan (Madurai district). Nearest railway station Sholavandan.
- Paadal Petra Sthalam (Pandya Nadu, pn04). Sung by Thirugnana Sambandar (only singer confirmed).
- Moolavar: Edaganathar (Edunathar / Edaganathar — "Lord of the palm leaf"). Ambal: Elavarkuzhali (Elavaarkuzhali, "she of cardamom-fragrant tresses"); Wikipedia infobox also gives Visalakshi; another source Maadeviyambikai.
- Sthala vriksham: vilvam. Theertham: Brahma Theertham; also the Vaigai.
- Legend: Sambandar came to Madurai at the call of the Pandya queen Mangaiyarkkarasi and minister Kulachirai to win the king (Koon Pandyan / Arikesari Nedumaran) back to Shaivism. Contests with the Jain scholars: fire test (Sambandar's leaf, the Thirunallaru pathigam, untouched) and the water test — leaves cast into the Vaigai; Sambandar's leaf (with his pathigam "Vazhga Anthanar...") moved against the current and came to rest here — "edu" = palm leaf, hence Thiru-edu-agam. The minister Kulachirai followed it; a lingam found where it stopped. Vaadhu Venra Vinayagar ("Ganesha who won the debate") is said to have stopped the leaf in the form of a fish (temple tradition). Omitted: the tradition of the impalement of Jains (sensitive; not needed).
- History: original shrine attributed to the Pandyas; present masonry Nayak period (16th c.). Inscriptions of Jatavarman Kulasekhara Pandya and of Krishnadevaraya (Vijayanagara) reported (one source, indianetzone — keep general: "Pandya and Vijayanagara-era inscriptions").
- Architecture: two gopurams (one before each of the two shrines, of the Lord and the goddess). Tier count unknown.
- Festivals: customary — Aavani Moolam? not confirmed. Keep general; a re-enactment of the edu (leaf) festival is commonly mentioned? not confirmed — not used.
- Coordinates: 9.9858, 77.9931 — Wikipedia "Thiruvedagam" village infobox (9°59′09″N 77°59′35″E); village point, temple lies in the village by the river. The Edaganathar temple article's infobox (9°35′N 77°35′E) is ~45 km off and was rejected. PIN-code listing 9.996, 77.988 (post office) is nearby and consistent.
