# Avinashiappar (Avinasilingeswarar) Temple, Avinashi (avinashiappar-avinashi)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Kongu Nadu (Tevaram name: Thiruppukkoliyur). Tradition: Shaivism.

- Deity: Shiva as Avinashiappar / Avinasilingeswarar; consort Karunambikai.
- Counted as the foremost Kongu Nadu shrine praised in the Tevaram; associated with Sundarar.
- Coordinates: **town-level ~11.192, 77.268** (Avinashi town centre). Exact temple point
  not found in sources — refine against a mapping source when possible.
- Legend (the temple's defining story): Sundarar, en route to Thiruvanchikulam, heard a
  wedding in one house and mourning in another. Two boys had gone to the temple tank years
  before; a crocodile seized one. Sundarar sang a decad (padigam) to Avinashiappar; as the
  hymn reached its 4th verse the crocodile surrendered the boy, grown to the age he would
  have been, and the two boys were wed together. Shrine to Sundarar on the tank bank; the
  flagstaff (kodimaram) base carries the scene in relief. Festival **Mudalai Vaai Pillai
  Utsavam** ("the child from the crocodile's mouth") re-enacts it, at Panguni Uthiram.
- "Avinashi" glossed as "the imperishable."
- Architecture: east-facing **seven-tiered rajagopuram (~100 ft)**; a **single-stone
  deepasthambam (lamp pillar, ~70 ft)**, unusual in Kongu Nadu; goddess Karunambikai in a
  separate east-facing sanctum under a five-tiered gopuram; complex ~1.5 acres.
- Festivals: Mudalai Vaai Pillai Utsavam (Panguni Uthiram, Mar–Apr); Chithirai car
  festival; procession of the 63 Nayanmars; Maha Shivaratri; monthly Pradosham.
- Access: Avinashi on the Coimbatore–Tiruppur–Erode road; ~13 km from Tiruppur (nearest
  railway); Coimbatore International Airport (~35 km) nearest airport.
- **Not verified:** founding dynasty/century of the structural temple (medieval; patrons
  not firmly sourced — used century 8 for the Tevaram/Sundarar era); exact temple
  coordinate; current timings.
