# Magudeswarar Temple, Kodumudi (magudeswarar-kodumudi)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Kongu Nadu (Tevaram name: Kodumudi / Tiruppandikodumudi). Tradition: Shaivism.

- Deity: Shiva as Magudeswarar / Kodumudinathar; consort Vadivudai Nayaki (also Soundaranayaki;
  Wikipedia gives Panmozhi Nayaki / Soundarambika — consort names vary by source).
- Tevaram: Sambandar, Appar and Sundarar are listed as having sung it (Dharisanam, Wikipedia
  snippets); counted the 6th Kongu Nadu temple in the Tevaram (blogs also number it 264th
  overall; not used in the content). Sundarar's "Namachivaya padigam" reportedly recited here.
- Name: Tiruppandikodumudi from Pandya patronage. Wikipedia notes epigraphs from the reign of
  Sundarapandiyan Kesari.
- Inscriptions (blogs, via snippets): 12th-century Kerala ruler Veera Narayanaravivarman;
  13th-century Kongu Cholas; Kongu Pandya records of 1288 and 1291 CE; 16th-century Vijayanagara
  — mostly lamp/offering endowments.
- Trimurti sthala: shrines of Shiva, Parvati (goddess), Vishnu (Veeranarayana Perumal) and
  Brahma — "Trimurthy Kovil". Brahma has three faces here; the vanni (sthala vriksham) is said
  to be his fourth. Naga stones are installed beneath the vanni; temple sought for serpent
  (naga) dosha relief.
- Architecture: east-facing; enclosure ~640 ft x 484 ft (tntemplesproject/Dinamalar); a Pandya
  king said to have built three gopurams. On the Kaveri bank; rock in midstream opposite.
  Rajagopuram height/style not found.
- Legends: Agastya — a crow overturned his water pot on the rock in the Kaveri; he caught the
  disappearing lingam, finger marks visible. Adisesha–Vayu contest around Mount Meru (single
  Tamil secondary source, treat with caution); a gem/diamond that fell became the swayambhu
  linga; "magudam" (crown) -> Magudeswarar.
- Festivals: 11-day Chithirai Thiruvizha (Apr–May) — main festival; Aadi Perukku (Jul–Aug);
  Arudra Darisanam (Margazhi) per hindu-blog; Pradosham, new/full moon poojas.
- Coordinates: **~11.076, 77.889** — Wikipedia temple coordinates 11°04′34″N 77°53′20″E (town
  11°04′44″N 77°53′12″E; postal listing 11.078, 77.887). One travel site gave a point ~6 km
  southwest (rejected as an outlier).
- Access: Tiruchirappalli–Karur–Kodumudi–Erode road; ~40 km from Erode (one source), ~25 km from
  Karur; Kodumudi railway station (Erode–Tiruchirappalli line) ~2 km; buses from Erode, Karur,
  Coimbatore; Coimbatore airport ~100–105 km.
- Timings: one listing 6:00 AM–12:00 noon and 4:00–9:00 PM ("12.00 a.m." in the source taken
  as a typo for noon); content says "confirm locally".
- **Not verified:** founding date (devotional listings claim 2,000–3,000 years and a pre-7th
  century Chera founding — rejected as unsourced); consort name variants; exact Tevaram hymn
  attribution by saint (taken from secondary listings); rajagopuram description and acreage;
  the Adisesha–Meru legend details; Arudra Darisanam observance (single blog); distance
  Erode–Kodumudi (one source says 40 km, others differ); exact timings. Sourcing is moderate:
  history/inscriptions rest on blog summaries of inscription lists — recommend human review.
