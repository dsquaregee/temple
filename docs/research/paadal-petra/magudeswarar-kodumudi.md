# Magudeswarar Temple, Kodumudi (Thiruppandikodumudi) — research notes

Master-list row 213 (Kongu Nadu 5; shivatemples kn5). Row facts confirmed; no corrections.

- Place: Kodumudi, Erode district, on the west bank of the Kaveri, about 40 km from Erode. Tevaram name Thiruppandikodumudi.
- One of the 276 Paadal Petra Sthalams, Kongu Nadu. "Moovar" sthalam: Appar, Sambandar and Sundarar each sang one pathigam
  (Sambandar 1, Sundarar 1, Thirunavukkarasar 1 — tntemplesproject). Sundarar's here is his Namachivaya pathigam
  (Dinamalar: even if he forgets the Lord, his tongue will keep saying "Namasivaya").
- Moolavar: Magudeswarar, also Kodumudinathar, Malai Kozhundeeswarar; swayambhu lingam (small). Ambal: Vadivudai Nayaki / Soundaryanayaki.
- Trimurthy Kovil: shrines of Shiva, Vishnu (Veeranarayana Perumal) and Brahma in one complex.
- Brahma shown with three faces; the vanni tree (sthala vriksham) is held to be his fourth face. The tree's age is said to be beyond estimate.
- Legend: Adisesha and Vayu contest strength over Mount Meru; Adisesha clasps the peak, Vayu blows; the peak breaks into
  pieces (five or seven — sources differ), gems falling at different places; the piece at Kodumudi became the swayambhu lingam. Name "Kodumudi" = summit of the peak.
- History: Pandya patronage (name Thiru-PANDI-kodumudi); epigraphs of a Pandya king (Sundara Pandya) mentioned in one source. No firm Chola-specific inscription found — not mentioned.
- Festivals: Chithirai brahmotsavam (11-day Chithirai Thirunal, April–May); Aadi Perukku (July–Aug) — lamps floated in the Kaveri, huge crowds. Kodumudi is also a place where pilgrims carry Kaveri water (tradition) — not used.
- Coordinates: 11.0761, 77.8889 — Wikipedia "Kodumudi Magudeswarar Temple" infobox (11°04′34″N 77°53′20″E) via search text.
- Getting there: Kodumudi railway station on the Erode–Karur–Tiruchirappalli line; ~40 km from Erode by road.
