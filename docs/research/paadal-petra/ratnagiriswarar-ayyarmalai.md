# Ratnagiriswarar (Vatpokkinathar) Temple, Ayyarmalai (ratnagiriswarar-ayyarmalai)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Chola Nadu (south of the Kaveri) (Tevaram name: Thiruvatpokki / திருவாட்போக்கி). District: Karur (hill temple at Ayyarmalai / Iyermalai, near Kulithalai). Tradition: Shaivism.

- Deity: Shiva as Ratnagiriswarar (Vatpokkinathar; also Malaikozhundeesar in one listing); consort **Surumbar Kuzhali** (alternate spellings seen: Kurambarkuzhali, Aralakesi). Lingam is self-manifested (swayambhu), said to have appeared on the ninth of nine rocks.
- Tevaram: hymns by Appar (Thirunavukkarasar); Wikipedia and one other source also credit Sundarar. **Sambandar not confirmed** — search results that mention Sambandar and "Ratnagiriswarar" concern the unrelated Tirumarugal temple (Nagapattinam).
- Coordinates: **10.878, 78.383** (Wikipedia infobox 10°52′42″N 78°22′57″E, rounded). Sourced from Wikipedia; not independently checked against a map.
- Defining legend (Vatpokki, "sword-turner"): a king came for gems; Shiva as a Brahmin asked him to fill a vessel with Kaveri water; when the king struck at him with his sword the Lord stayed the blow and gave him a gem. Tamil vaal = sword, pokki = one who turns it aside. Secondary tradition: the hill is a fragment of Mount Meru flung here when Vayu and Adishesha contended.
- Architecture: summit temple reached by a stair of ~952 steps with four-pillared resting mandapas (one source dates the stair to 1783 CE; counts vary by source); sanctum faces **west**, goddess shrine faces **east** (uncommon); pillared hall incorporating natural rock; sacred tree neem; theertham Kaveri.
- Festivals: Maha Shivaratri (a notable event at the temple per one listing; details cut off); monthly Pradosham. Prose kept general for the rest.
- Pilgrimage circuit: traditionally paired in a day with Kadambavaneswarar, Kulithalai (morning), this temple (afternoon), and Thiruvengoimalai / Eengoimalai (evening).
- Access: ~10 km from Kulithalai bus stand; Kulithalai station on Tiruchirappalli–Karur line; Tiruchirappalli (airport) ~40 km (approximate, derived from Kulithalai–Tiruchirappalli 34 km).
- **Seed hint corrections:** none contradicted (goddess Surumbar Kuzhali confirmed).
- **Not verified:** construction dynasty and century (sources vary: Tevaram-era worship, "12th–13th century" build, an Aryar community tradition from the 7th century); the Vayu–Adishesha legend (seen only in a truncated snippet, from a secondary source); the 952-step count and 1783 date (secondary/promotional sources); the daily Kaveri-water custom details; festival list beyond Maha Shivaratri and Pradosham; temple timings; the "footwear left at foot of hill" and bus/auto access details (generic, not sourced); Sundarar's hymn.
