# Mayuranathaswami Temple, Mayiladuthurai (mayuranathar-mayiladuthurai)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Chola Nadu south of the Kaveri. Tradition: Shaivism.
- Deity: Shiva as Mayuranathar; consort Abhayambikai (Parvati). Sung by Appar & Thirugnana Sambandar; also Arunagirinathar's Tirupugazh.
- Name legend: Parvati worshipped as a peahen (mayil) on the Kaveri bank and danced → Mayuranathar / Mayiladuthurai; Shiva's Gowri Thandava.
- Famous for Tula Snanam (Aippasi / Tula month Kaveri bathing). Administered by Thiruvavaduthurai Adheenam.
- Coordinates: 11.0956, 79.6561 (Wikipedia, Mayuranathaswami Temple).
- Not verified: exact founding century (sources 7th–10th; used 7, Tevaram era); consort name given as Abhayambikai (sources vary: also Anjanakshi/Abhayapradambika).
