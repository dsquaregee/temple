# Sivanandeswarar Temple, Tirukkandalam (sivanandeswarar-tirukkandalam)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Thondai Nadu (Tevaram name: Tirukkallil; also Tirukkalleeswarar). District: Tiruvallur. Tradition: Shaivism.

- Deity: Shiva as Sivanandeswarar (Tirukkalleeswarar), swayambhu linga; consort Anandavalli
  Ammai.
- Tevaram: one decad by Tirugnana Sambandar; listed as the 18th of the 32 Thondai Nadu Tevaram
  shrines (Dinamalar).
- Coordinates: **village-level ~13.143, 79.909** (Tirukkandalam). Exact temple point not found
  in sources — refine against a mapping source when possible.
- Legend (the temple's defining story): Sambandar left his worship articles on the riverbank
  before bathing; they vanished, and he found them beside the swayambhu linga in the temple —
  the Lord had drawn them there to bring the saint — whereupon he sang his decad. Also: the sage
  Bhrigu worshipped with a thousand *kalli* (spurge) flowers and Shiva appeared to him as
  Dakshinamurthy. The name Tirukkallil is read from the kalli shrubs.
- Architecture: east-facing, five-tiered rajagopuram; tank Nandi Theertham outside the gateway
  (said to be formed by Nandi); flagstaff, balipeetam and Nandi in one line; Anandavalli and
  Murugan shrines beside the sanctum, both east-facing (Somaskanda-style alignment).
- Festivals: Tirukarthikai / Karthigai Deepam (Nov–Dec); Maha Shivaratri (Masi); Skanda Sashti
  (Aippasi); Aippasi full-moon Annabhishekam; Margazhi Tiruvadhirai; Vinayaka Chaturthi;
  Navaratri; monthly Pradosham.
- Access: ~40 km north-west of Chennai, near Kannigaipper (~4 km, branch road) on the
  Chennai–Periyapalayam route, Tiruvallur district; ~10 km from Periyapalayam; MTC route 58-D
  (Koyambedu–Vengal) passes through. Chennai is the nearest airport and railhead (Tiruvallur
  also nearby).
- Do not confuse with the Sivanandeswarar Temple at Tirupandurai (Thanjavur district), a separate
  Paadal Petra Sthalam.
- **Not verified:** founding dynasty/century of the structural temple (century 7 used for the
  Tevaram era); exact coordinate; the Bhrigu/Dakshinamurthy legend and Nandi Theertham origin
  (carried from existing en entry); bus route number; current timings.
