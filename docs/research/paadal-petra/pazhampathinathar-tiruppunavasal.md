# Pazhampathinathar (Viruthapureeswarar) Temple, Tiruppunavasal (pazhampathinathar-tiruppunavasal)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Pandya Nadu (Tevaram name: Tiruppunavayil). Tradition: Shaivism.

- Deity: Shiva as Pazhampathinathar (Tamil) / Vriddhapureeswarar (Sanskrit; spelled
  Viruthapureeswarar in the catalog); consort Periyanayaki (one listing also gives
  Karunai Nayaki / Kachchanimulai Ammai). Periyanayaki has a separate east-facing shrine
  (Dinamalar).
- Coastal village in Avudaiyarkoil taluk, Pudukkottai district; ~25 km south of Avudaiyarkoil.
  One of the 14 Pandya Nadu Paadal Petra Sthalams; hymns by Sambandar (1) and Sundarar (1).
  Name read as "entrance" of river and sea (tntemplesproject). Tiruvadanai ~21 km away.
- Coordinates: **approximate ~9.90, 79.07** — NOT sourced. Inferred from Tiruvadanai
  (9.783, 78.919; ~21 km) and Avudaiyarkoil (10.07, 79.04; ~25 km). Refine against a map.
- Names by age (sthala purana): Vajravanam / Indrapuram (Krita), Brahmapuram (Treta),
  Vriddha Kasi (Dwapara), Vriddhapuri / Pazhampathi (Kali). Yama and his messengers cannot
  enter the boundary.
- Legend: Brahma lost his office as Creator (could not explain the Pranava mantra); on
  Parvati's advice installed a linga here and worshipped; tank = Brahma Theertham. Alternate:
  Indra worshipped here to be freed from Gautama's curse.
- Features: linga and avudaiyar said to have the largest circumference in Tamil Nadu;
  rajagopuram ~65 ft (Dinamalar); later construction credited to a Sundara Pandya, a blend of
  Chola and Pandya styles; Pandya inscription, Vikrama Pandya 15th regnal year (ARE 612 of 1902,
  SII VIII 209), land gift for worship and upkeep.
- Festivals: 11-day Vaikasi Visakam (May–Jun); Maha Shivaratri; monthly Pradosham.
- Timings (listings differ): 6:00–11:30 and 4:00/5:00–7:30 (one lists to 8:30); temple said to
  close early.
- **Not verified:** which Sundara Pandya and the date of the later construction (period
  worded generally; century 7 used for Sambandar, 7th–8th in the period line for Sundarar);
  precise coordinates (see above); rail/air access (not found — prose says so); whether Appar
  also sang here (one source says Appar and Sambandar; others say Sambandar and Sundarar —
  the latter used); the Cheraman Perumal visit with Sundarar (single source, omitted);
  mandapa/tier/sculpture detail (not found); the Raja Raja Chola inspiration claim (single
  source, omitted); consort name variants; current timings. Note: a Wikipedia-sourced snippet
  confuses this temple with a Ramanathapuram-district namesake; Pudukkottai is better supported.
