# Mangalapureeswarar Temple, Thiruchopuram (Thiyagavalli) — research notes

Master-list row 221 (Nadu Nadu). Confirmed: Mangalapureeswarar (Chopuranathar), consort Thyagavalli Ammai (also Velnedunkanni, Sathyayadakshi; Chopuranayaki), singer Sambandar (pathigam in the First Thirumurai). No corrections.

- Place: Thiruchopuram, Thiyagavalli post, Cuddalore taluk (PIN 608801), ~19–20 km south of Cuddalore, near the coast.
- One of the 276 Paadal Petra Sthalams; 6th in Nadu Nadu (shivatemples nn06). Sambandar — pathigam in the 1st Thirumurai.
- Lingam: of sand, on a square pedestal; a small ball-shaped form on top said to represent the Ganga; bears palm prints of Agastya, who is said to have installed it. Tradition: Agastya's sea-sand lingam would not hold shape until mixed with herb juice.
- Tradition: Parvati merged into the lingam — kumkumam and turmeric given as prasadam instead of vibhuti; name Mangalapureeswarar.
- Tradition: the temple was once buried by sand dunes; a devotee saw only the gopuram's kalasam; villagers dug it out.
- Name Thiyagavalli: after Thyagavalli, chief queen of Kulothunga Chola I, believed to have renovated the temple.
- Festivals (Dinamalar): Maha Shivaratri (Feb–Mar), Thirukarthigai (Nov–Dec), Annabhishekam, Panguni Uthiram (Mar–Apr).
- Century: 7 (Sambandar).
- Coordinates: 11.6158, 79.7417 — Wikidata Q12984096 / Commons category (11°36′57″N 79°44′30″E). ~8 km from Theerthanagiri's temple (P05 neighbour) — distinct.
