# Tiruthelicheri (Koilpatthu, Karaikal) — Parvatheeswarar Temple

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Parvatheeswarar**; goddess **Parvathi Ammai** (also **Sakthi Nayaki**, **Swayamvara Tapasvini**). (Worklist drafted goddess "Soundaranayaki?" — corrected to the sourced names.)
- **Location:** Koilpatthu (ancient **Tiruthelicheri**), ~3 km from Karaikal bus stand, **Karaikal, Puducherry UT**, PIN 609602. ~10.9200 N, 79.8300 E (approximate).
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam — hymned by Tirugnana Sambandar; a **Bhaskara Kshetram** (sun's rays fall on the lingam for 10 days from Panguni 13, Mar–Apr).
- **Legend:** goddess's long penance → Shiva absorbs her as part of himself → **Swayamvara Tapasvini**. **Surya** worshipped here, dug the tank; Shiva restored his brightness → Bhaskara Kshetram. Four-yuga names: Brahmavanam / Sameevanam / Anandavanam / Muktivanam.
- **Festivals:** Panguni Suryapuja (10 days); Maha Shivaratri (Masi); Navaratri; Karthikai Mondays.

**Notes:** State is **Puducherry (Karaikal)**, not Tamil Nadu. Coordinates approximate. Worklist id `parvatheeswarar-karaikal`. Translations AI-drafted; native-speaker QA recommended.
