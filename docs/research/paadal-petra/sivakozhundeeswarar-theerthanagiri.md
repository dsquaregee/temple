# Sivakozhundeeswarar Temple, Theerthanagiri (Thiruthinainagar) — research notes

Master-list row 220 (Nadu Nadu). Confirmed: deity Sivakozhundeeswarar (Sivakozhundhu Easwarar), consort Oppila Nayaki (Neelayadakshi not seen in sources — not used), singer Sundarar.

- Place: Theerthanagiri (Teerthanagari), Cuddalore taluk, PIN 608801, via Alapakkam; ~25 km from Chidambaram, ~26–27 km from Cuddalore; buses Cuddalore–Chidambaram via Mettupalayam. Tevaram name Thiruthinai Nagar.
- One of the 276 Paadal Petra Sthalams; 5th in Nadu Nadu (shivatemples nn05). Sung by Sundarar (Tevaram).
- Sthala vriksham: kondrai. Theertham: Jambava theertham — linked with Jambavan of the Ramayana.
- Sthala purana: farmer Periyan and his wife fed at least one devotee every day; Shiva came as an old man, ate their millet (thinai) and helped cultivate their land in return. "Thinai" = millet — the old name Thinai Nagar.
- Architecture: east-facing three-tier rajagopuram.
- Festivals: 13-day Vaikasi brahmotsavam, Masi Shivaratri, Aippasi Annabhishekam, Arudra Darshanam (Margazhi).
- Century: 8 (Sundarar only). Dynasty: not documented — kept generic.
- Coordinates: 11.5692, 79.6774 — Wikidata Q16308740 (11°34′9.05″N 79°40′38.60″E), via search result text. Consistent with Theerthanagiri post office point (11.57, 79.68) and the stated distances from Chidambaram/Cuddalore. No other catalog entry here.
