# Tirupampuram — Seshapureeswarar Temple

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Seshapureeswarar** (Paampuranathar) — "lord of the serpents."
- **Location:** Tirupampuram, near Kuthalam, Tiruvarur district, Tamil Nadu. Approx 10.9025 N, 79.63 E (approximate). Historical names Paampuram, Seshapuri.
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam — sung by Sambandar, Appar and Sundarar.
- **Signature legend:** Shiva cursed the serpents for their pride; **Adisesha, Rahu, Ketu** and the nagas worshipped him on Maha Shivaratri and were released. One of **four Naga temples** of a one-day circuit (Kumbakonam, Tirunageswaram, Nagore, Tirupampuram = 3rd).
- **Fame:** Rahu–Ketu / **naga-dosha** parihara; sought for serpent-doshas and childlessness.
