# Tiruppugalur — Vardhamaneeswaram

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Vardhamaneeswarar** — a distinct Paadal Petra Sthalam **within the Agneeswarar (Agnipureeswarar) temple** complex at Tiruppugalur.
- **Location:** Tiruppugalur, near Nannilam, Nagapattinam district, Tamil Nadu. Approx 10.8667 N, 79.6000 E (approximate).
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam — Vardhamaneeswaram glorified by **Tirugnanasambandar**; the main Agneeswarar sanctum is itself a Tevaram sthalam, so two Paadal Petra shrines share one complex.
- **Signature legend (Sundarar's bricks):** Sundarar, needing funds for wife Paravai Nachiyar's festival, slept on ordinary bricks → turned to gold by morning. Custom today: house-builders offer a brick before starting construction.
- **Agni:** Agni worshipped Shiva here to be freed from a curse (→ Agneeswarar).
- **Inner precinct:** rich with images — 63 Nayanmars, Agni, Panchalinga, Appar, Vatapi Ganapati, Subramanya, Mahalakshmi, Kalasamhara.

**Notes:** Coordinates approximate; goddess name not asserted (varies by sanctum within the complex). The brick miracle is Sundarar's (not Appar's), per sources.
