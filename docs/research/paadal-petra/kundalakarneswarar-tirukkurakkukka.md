# Kundalakarneswarar (Kundaleswarar) Temple, Tirukkurakkukka (kundalakarneswarar-tirukkurakkukka)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Chola Nadu (north of the Kaveri). District: Mayiladuthurai
(Mayiladuthurai taluk; older sources/Dinamalar say Nagapattinam). Tradition: Shaivism.
Tevaram name: **Tirukkurakkukka** (திருக்குரக்குக்கா); village today Thirukurakkaval / Thirukurakka,
near Vaitheeswaran Koil. Counted as the 28th Tevaram shrine on the northern bank of the Kaveri
(Dinamalar/shivatemples).

- Deity: Shiva as Kundalakarneswarar / Kundaleswarar (Kunthaleswarar); the linga is described as a
  **sand linga** (Dinamalar listing). Consort: **Kundalambikai** (Kundalanayaki / Kunthalambigai) in a
  separate shrine (Dinamalar, shivatemples, TN Temples Project). Wikipedia instead gives the consort
  as Bala Tripura Sundari — conflicting; content uses Kundalambikai (three temple-side sources).
- Sung by: **Appar (Tirunavukkarasar)** per Dinamalar and blogs. No source checked names a
  Sambandar hymn here, so the Tevaram attribution is Appar only.
- Coordinates: **approximate ~11.185, 79.66** — derived from the textual location (about 13 km
  north of Mayiladuthurai, near Vaitheeswaran Koil). No sourced geo-coordinate found via search;
  refine against a mapping source.
- Defining legend: Hanuman. After the Lanka war Rama asked Hanuman to fetch a linga from
  Ayodhya; on his delay a sand linga was made (one account has Sita make it); a divine voice told
  Hanuman to install a linga of his own here and worship it. Shiva made one of his earrings
  (kundalam) vanish to test him; Hanuman, born with earrings, cut off one of his own and offered
  it — hence **Kundalakarneswarar** ("the one with the earring"). Wikipedia notes it as one of two
  places (with Rameswaram) where Hanuman is shown worshipping Shiva; popularly also "Shiva Bhaktha
  Anjaneya" temple. A variant has Shiva as an old mendicant begging the earring (not used).
- Name/"kurangu": local tradition (one blog) reads Kurakkukka as a softening of *kurangu* (monkey)
  + *kaa* — presented in content as a local reading, not established etymology. Local belief that
  two monkeys still visit in Chithirai (Apr–May) to worship with bilva leaves (Dinamalar listing).
- Architecture: modest village temple, **no rajagopuram** (blogs); Dravidian (Wikipedia); sacred
  water **Ganapathy Theertham**, a Kaveri channel (Dinamalar); separate Ambal shrine. Age claims
  "at least 1,200–1,400 years" (blogs, not authoritative).
- Festivals: Maha Shivaratri (Feb–Mar); Tirukarthikai (Nov–Dec); Hanuman Jayanti; Chithirai monkey
  visit belief; monthly Pradosham (generic).
- Access: ~13 km north of Mayiladuthurai; near Vaitheeswaran Koil (nearest railway); Mayiladuthurai
  Junction main railhead; Tiruchirappalli the nearest major airport (general knowledge). Often
  combined in a half-day circuit with Tiruvazhkolipputhur, Tirukkaruppariyalur and Tirupunkur.
- **Not verified:** founding dynasty/structural date (used century 7 for the Appar era, dynasty
  stated generically); exact coordinates; current timings (none found — content generic); consort
  (Wikipedia's Bala Tripura Sundari vs. Kundalambikai); whether Sambandar also sang here; the
  sand-linga detail and Rama/Ayodhya framing (single listing/blog, hedged as "tradition"/"is said");
  tank/festival specifics beyond the listing; district (Mayiladuthurai chosen as current); airport
  distance; exact festival dates.
