# Sivayoganathar (Yoganandeeswarar / Sivayoginathar) Temple, Tiruviyalur — Thiruvisanallur (sivayoganathar-tiruviyalur)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Chola Nadu (north of the Kaveri) (Tevaram name: Tiruviyalur). District: Thanjavur. Tradition: Shaivism.

- Deity: Shiva as Sivayoganathar / Yoganandeeswarar / Sivayoginathar; consort Soundara Nayaki (also given as Santha Nayaki). Names by yuga (Purathaneswarar, Vilvaranyeswarar, Yoganandeeswarar, Sivayoganathar) per temple tradition.
- Tevaram: hymn of Sambandar (First Thirumurai) per shivatemples.com / tntemplesproject. Counted in the Chola Nadu north-of-Kaveri sequence (listed as the 43rd). Generic Wikipedia text also cites Appar; no Appar hymn confirmed.
- Coordinates: **village-level ~11.01, 79.43** (Thiruvisanallur; single travel-distance site). Exact temple point not found — refine against a mapping source. Roughly 6.6 km straight-line / ~10 km by road from Kumbakonam.
- Legends: (1) eight Shiva yogis attained liberation and merged into the linga here (a variant says on Shivaratri Shiva made seven lights of them — number differs, so prose says "eight yogis merged into the linga" only); (2) a king from the Malayalam (Kerala) country who ill-treated many women was cursed; on a yogi's advice he bathed in the Kaveri, worshipped, and was freed; (3) Nandi turned back Yama approaching a devotee (fear-of-death belief); (4) tank called Jatayu Teertham, Jatayu said to have worshipped here.
- Architecture: east-facing, Dravidian/Chola; Wikipedia lists five towers; a sundial built into the outer wall (reported to show time fairly accurately 6 am–6 pm, seasonal variation). Wikipedia says "likely built in the time of Raja Raja Chola I". Administered by Thanjavur Palace Devasthanam.
- Festivals: Maha Shivaratri; Pradosham; Somavaram (Mondays). Nearby (separate site): Sridhara Ayyaval Mutt well, where the Ganga is said to have risen in his house well; thousands bathe on the Karthigai new moon (Nov–Dec; the "Ganga Akarshana" observance).
- Access: ~10 km by road from Kumbakonam (nearest railway); Tiruchirappalli nearest major airport (distance not sourced). Timings reported as 6–12 and 4–8 (variants: 6–12:30 / 3–8:30; 7:30–12:30 / 4–8).
- **Correction to seed hints:** town spelling Thiruvisanallur confirmed; temple is also known as Sivayoginathar / Yoganandeeswarar. The village Wikipedia article calls the shrine Chatur Kala Bhairavar, which conflicts with other sources and was ignored.
- **Not verified:** founding dynasty/builder (Rajaraja I link is tradition only); century 7 reflects the Sambandar era; exact temple coordinate; tank names and temple tree (sources conflict: vilva vs eight trees); the Ayyaval's dating (sources range 15th c. to c. 1720) — prose avoids dating him; the five towers (one source only); sundial details; distance to Tiruchirappalli airport; current timings and festival calendar; whether Appar also sang here.
