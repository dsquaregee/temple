# Tiruchattimutram — Sivakozhundeeswarar Temple

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Sivakozhundeeswarar** ("the tender/green sprout of Shiva"); goddess **Periyanayaki**.
- **Location:** Tiruchattimutram (Tirusakthimuttram), near Patteeswaram, ~7-8 km from Kumbakonam, Thanjavur district, Tamil Nadu. Approx 10.9333 N, 79.36 E (approximate).
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam — sung by Appar.
- **Signature legend:** **Parvati's penance** — stood on one foot and embraced the lingam (as at Kanchipuram) to show loving devotion is the path to grace. Prayers for **marriage / a worthy partner**.
- **Fabric:** first brick, rebuilt in granite under **Raja Raja Chola I**; inscriptions of Raja Raja I, Kulothunga III, Vijayanagara.
