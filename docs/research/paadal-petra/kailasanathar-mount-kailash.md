# Mount Kailash (Thirukkayilai / Nodithanmalai) — research notes

Master-list row 276 (Vada Nadu 5). Not a built temple: the sacred mountain itself is the sthalam. Kept as given: Appar and Sundarar. Sambandar may also have sung Thirukkayilai — NOT confirmed in search; not named.

- Mount Kailash (Kang Rinpoche), Ngari Prefecture, Tibet Autonomous Region, China. Height ~6,638 m; summit unclimbed by custom of reverence. Lake Manasarovar to the south.
- Tevaram names: Thirukkayilai / Thirukkayilayam; Sundarar calls it Nodithanmalai in his last pathigam.
- One of the 276 Paadal Petra Sthalams; Vada Nadu (northern country), with Srisailam, Indraneelaparuppatham, Anekathangapatham and Kedarnath.
- Appar (7th c.): in old age set out on foot to behold Kailash; when his body failed, Shiva (as a sage) bade him bathe in a pool; he rose from the tank at Thiruvaiyaru, where he was granted the vision of Kailash with the Lord and Goddess (Periya Puranam tradition; Thiruvaiyaru keeps "Appar's Kailasa darshan" in Aadi — presented as tradition). Appar sang hymns to the Lord of Kayilai.
- Sundarar (8th c.): at the end of his life Shiva sent a white elephant to bring him to Kailash; his friend Cheraman Perumal rode alongside on his horse; on the way Sundarar sang the Nodithanmalai pathigam (Periya Puranam tradition). Guru puja in Aadi (Swathi) — mention as Aadi only.
- Karaikkal Ammaiyar: Periya Puranam says she climbed Kailash on her hands out of reverence (not to tread the Lord's mountain with feet) — tradition.
- Also sacred to Buddhists, Jains (Ashtapada, Rishabhanatha's nirvana) and the Bon tradition — mention neutrally.
- Pilgrimage: Kailash Mansarovar Yatra (Govt of India MEA-organised, via Lipulekh pass in Uttarakhand or Nathu La in Sikkim); private routes via Nepal. Parikrama (kora) ~52 km, crossing the Dolma La at ~5,600 m. Season roughly June–September. Permits required; fitness checks.
- Coordinates: 31.0675, 81.3119 — Wikipedia "Mount Kailash" infobox (31°4′0″N 81°18′45″E).
- Century: 7 (Appar).
