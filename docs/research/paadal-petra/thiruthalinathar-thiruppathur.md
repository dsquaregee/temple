# Thiruthalinathar Temple, Thiruppathur (Thiruputhur), Sivaganga — research notes

Master-list row 199 (Pandya Nadu 6). Row facts confirmed (deity Thiruthalinathar, consort Sivakami, Appar + Sambandar). Some listings add Sundarar — not confirmed, not used.

- Place: Tiruppattur / Thiruppathur town, Sivaganga district (not the Tirupattur of Vellore/Tirupattur district). Tevaram name Thiruputhur. ~65 km from Madurai, ~20–25 km from Karaikudi, ~35 km from Sivaganga.
- 6th of the 14 Pandya Nadu Paadal Petra Sthalams (shivatemples pn06).
- Moolavar: Thiruthalinathar (Tiruttalinathar, Thiruthaleeswarar). Ambal: Sivakami.
- Theertham: Thali theertham, Sivagangai.
- Legends: Mahalakshmi (Thiru) worshipped Shiva after he danced the Gauri Thandavam here → Thiru-thali-nathar. Valmiki did penance here as an anthill (putru) → Thiru-putrur/Thiruputhur. Vasuki and Karkotaka worshipped → separate Thirunageswarar shrine in the outer (third) prakaram.
- Yoga Bhairavar: shrine in the second prakaram, facing west, in a rare seated posture of penance; temple popularly called the "Vairavan temple". The hall before the Bhairavar shrine is attributed (Tamil source) to the Marudhu brothers, with their statues.
- Inscriptions: two Pandya records of 868 and 878 CE in the regnal years of Maranjadaiyan (Varaguna II) (Wikipedia). Kulasekhara-era record (DHARMA title). ARE 1935: temple damaged in the 14th c. and repaired/reconsecrated by Visalayadevar of Kunrakkudi in the 46th year of Jatavarman Vira Pandya. (Content mentions damage neutrally, no attribution.)
- Architecture: four prakarams; vimana with stucco work (indianetzone). Gopuram tiers unknown.
- Festivals: customary — Bhairava Ashtami (especially Theipirai Ashtami) for Yoga Bhairavar is widely practised at Bhairava shrines; not specifically confirmed here; phrased generally.
- Coordinates: 10.133, 78.617 — Wikipedia "Tiruttalinathar Temple" infobox (10°08′N 78°37′E, minute precision only). Town article gives the same. Precision ~±1 km; temple is in the town centre. Flag as low-precision.
