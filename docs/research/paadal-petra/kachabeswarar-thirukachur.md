# Kachabeswarar Temple, Thirukachur Alakkoil (kachabeswarar-thirukachur)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Thondai Nadu (Chengalpattu district, Tamil Nadu). Tradition: Shaivism.

- Deity: Shiva as Kachabeswarar / Virundhitta Nathar (Virundhitta Eeswarar); the goddess is
  given as Anjanakshi in the catalog (sources vary: Wikipedia lists Kanniyumaiyal/Antanatchi
  for Kachabeswarar; the hill shrine Marundeeswarar has Irulneeki Ammai).
- Tevaram name: Tirukkachchur Alakkoil (Alakkoyil); associated with Sundarar.
- Coordinates: **~12.776, 79.997** (Thirukachur village, near Singaperumal Koil). Exact temple
  point not independently verified — refine against a mapping source when possible.
- Legend (defining story): Sundarar, faint with hunger, lay down in the hall by the temple
  tank; Shiva as an old Brahmin begged alms house to house and served him a full meal, then
  vanished — hence **Virundhitta Nathar** ("the Lord who set out the feast"). Second legend:
  Vishnu, as the tortoise (Kachchapa/Kurma) bearing Mount Mandara in the churning of the
  ocean, worshipped Shiva here; the village name derives from "kachchapa" (tortoise).
- Architecture: granite concentric rectangular enclosures; Kachabeswarar linga sanctum;
  Anjanakshi shrine; **Kurma Theertham** tank with a pillared hall (Sundarar's resting place);
  the hill shrine Alakkoil (~1 km away, Marundeeswarar) forms a twin pilgrimage with the
  village temple.
- Festivals: Brahmotsavam in Masi/Magam (Feb–Mar); second Brahmotsavam and Chitra Pournami in
  Chithirai (Apr–May); Maha Shivaratri; monthly Pradosham.
- Access: on the Chennai–Chengalpattu road near Singaperumal Koil; ~2 km from Singaperumal
  Koil railway station (Chennai Egmore–Chengalpattu line); Chennai airport ~50 km north.
- **Not verified:** exact structural dating beyond "Chola foundations with Vijayanagara–Nayaka
  additions"; the goddess's name (sources conflict, see above); precise coordinates; current
  timings (one source gives ~8:00–11:30 and 17:30–20:30; confirm locally).
