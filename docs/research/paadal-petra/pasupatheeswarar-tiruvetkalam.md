# Pasupatheeswarar (Pasupatheswarar) Temple, Tiruvetkalam (pasupatheeswarar-tiruvetkalam)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Chola Nadu (north of the Kaveri) (Tevaram name: Tiruvetkalam, திருவேட்களம்). District: Cuddalore (Annamalai Nagar, adjoining Chidambaram). Tradition: Shaivism.

- Deity: Shiva as Pasupatheeswarar / Pasupatheswarar; consort Nalla Nayaki (also given as
  Satgunambal).
- Tevaram: sung by **Sambandar and Appar** (Appar's decad in the 5th Tirumurai). No Sundarar
  hymn found in the sources consulted.
- Coordinates: **~11.3914, 79.7196** (Wikipedia 11°23'29"N 79°43'10.7"E; matches an
  independent GPS listing). Temple point, inside the Annamalai University campus.
- Defining legend: Arjuna's penance for the Pasupata weapon during the Pandavas' exile, in a
  bamboo forest. A boar attacks; Arjuna and a hunter (Shiva as Kirata, with Parvati) shoot
  at once; quarrel and contest; Arjuna is defeated, worships, the hunter is revealed as
  Shiva, who grants the Pasupata. Hence "Pasupatheeswarar". Local tradition adds the linga
  bears a mark of Arjuna's blow (one account). Resembles the Tiruvetakkudi legend
  (tntemplesproject). "Vetkalam" glossed as the hunting ground (editorial reading).
- Architecture: ~2 acres in concentric rectangular walls; three-tiered east rajagopuram;
  pillared hall and ardha mandapa before the sanctum; goddess shrine in the front hall
  facing south; pillars of the goddess shrine carved with Arjuna-legend scenes incl. hunter
  Shiva; image of Parvati with unbound hair; sthala vriksha **bamboo**.
- History: traditionally Chola-built; one source credits an earlier Pallava brick temple;
  renovated in stone in 1914 by A. Pethaperumal Chettiar of Kanadukathan.
- Festivals: Tamil New Year in Chithirai (April) is the most prominent; Maha Shivaratri and
  Pradosham generic.
- Access: inside Annamalai University grounds, Annamalai Nagar (PIN 608002), reached by the
  university main gate toward the rear, near the music college; distance to Chidambaram given
  variously as ~2 km to ~5 km. Railway/bus: Chidambaram. Airports: Puducherry/Chennai (generic).
- **Not verified:** builder and period of the structural temple (Chola vs Pallava sources
  differ; century 7 used for the Tevaram era); name of the temple tank (Krupa Theertham in
  one source, Esanya in another that may refer to a different temple — omitted from the
  content); whether the Arjuna festival is old or recent (omitted); temple timings (generic
  text used); exact distances to Chidambaram and airports (kept vague); the "mark of the
  blow" on the linga (reported as tradition); Sundarar hymns (not claimed).
