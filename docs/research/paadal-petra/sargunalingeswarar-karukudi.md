# Tirukkarukkudi (Marudhanallur) — Sargunalingeswarar Temple

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Sargunalingeswarar** (also **Karukudi Nathar**, **Brahmapureeswarar**); a **swayambhu sand lingam** (manal-lingam). Goddess **Advaita Nayaki** (also **Sarvalankara Nayaki**, **Kalyani Ambica**). (Worklist goddess "Advaita Nayaki" — confirmed.)
- **Location:** Marudhanallur (Tevaram **Tirukkarukkudi**), ~5–6 km south of Kumbakonam past Sakkottai toward Valangaiman, Thanjavur district, Tamil Nadu. ~10.9248 N, 79.4041 E (Wikipedia).
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam — hymned by Tirugnana Sambandar; Kaveri south bank (~69th on south bank).
- **Legends:** **Rama** worshipped here before Lanka, bidding Hanuman fetch a lingam from the north; Pandyan king **Sargunan** worshipped → name Sargunalingeswarar; **Brahma** worshipped → also Brahmapureeswarar.
- **Festivals:** Maha Shivaratri (Masi); Navaratri (Purattasi); Karthikai Mondays; Pradosham.
- **Caution:** distinct from Sarguneswarar at Karuveli (Tiruvarur), goddess Sarvangasundari.
