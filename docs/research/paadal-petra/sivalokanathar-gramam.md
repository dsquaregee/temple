# Sivalokanathar Temple, Gramam (Thirumundeeswaram) — research notes

Master-list row 234 (Nadu Nadu). Row confirmed: Sivalokanathar (Mundeeswarar, Mudiswarar) / Soundaryanayaki (Soundaranayagi; also Kanarkuzhali per Dinamalar); Appar. District: row says Villupuram; Dinamalar address is Ulundurpettai taluk (PIN 607203) — Ulundurpet taluk has been in Kallakurichi district since 2019. Content says "near Thiruvennainallur", no district named.

- Place: Gramam village; Tevaram name Thirumundeeswaram. Called Mouli-gramam in Parantaka I's day (vmis.in archive).
- Paadal Petra Sthalam, Nadu Nadu (shivatemples nn19). Appar — one pathigam in the 6th Tirumurai (Thiruthandagam). Only Appar confirmed. Shivatemples: one of the places where Appar did uzhavarappani (cleaning temple grounds) — tradition.
- Name legend: the dvarapalas Thindi and Mundi worshipped Shiva here → Thirumundeeswaram.
- History: Chola-period inscriptions: under Parantaka I the temple was converted to stone; gift of lamps by Rajaditya (Parantaka's son). Archive: rebuild by a general of Rajaditya c. 943 CE; Gramam was Rajaditya's headquarters. Wikipedia: present masonry Vijayanagara 16th c. HR&CE administered.
- Festivals: Chitra Pournami, Aani Thirumanjanam.
- Access: Gramam bus stop near temple.
- Coordinates: 11.8500, 79.4044 — Wikipedia infobox 11°51′00″N 79°24′16″E (and mirror), via search text. No other catalog temple in Gramam.
