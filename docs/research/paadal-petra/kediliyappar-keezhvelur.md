# Keezhvelur — Kediliappar Temple

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Kediliappar** (also **Akshayalingeswarar**); goddess **Vanamulai Amman**.
- **Location:** Keezhvelur (Thirukeezhvelur), near Nagapattinam (~10 km), Nagapattinam district, Tamil Nadu. Approx 10.7006 N, 79.7756 E (approximate).
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam — sung by Sambandar and Appar.
- **Architecture:** A **Maadakovil** — elevated temple traditionally attributed to **Kochengat Chola** (built high so elephants couldn't enter the sanctum); east-facing **seven-tiered rajagopuram**, Saravana Theertham opposite.
- **Legends/features:** Sthala vriksham = **ilanthai (jujube)**, said to have grown from a drop of amrita (ocean-churning). **Nataraja with right foot raised** — form revealed to sage Agastya.

**Notes:** Coordinates approximate; dating traditional (sources vary: "1000+ yrs"; Kochengat Chola attribution is legendary). Translations AI-drafted; native-speaker QA recommended.
