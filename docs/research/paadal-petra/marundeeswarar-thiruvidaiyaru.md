# Marundeeswarar (Idaiyatrunathar) Temple, T. Edaiyar (Thiruvidaiyaru) — research notes

Master-list row 228 (Nadu Nadu). Row facts confirmed. Consort: Gnanambigai and Sittridai Nayaki (master: "Sitridai Nachiyar" — same name). One Wikipedia mirror gives "Balambigai" — rejected.

- Place: T. Edaiyar (T. Edayar / T. Idayaaru), old name Thiruvidaiyaru; on the southern bank of the Then Pennai, on the Thirukkovilur–Thiruvennainallur road, ~5 km from Thiruvennainallur. Viluppuram district (Thiruvennainallur taluk).
- Paadal Petra Sthalam, 13th in Nadu Nadu (Dinamalar; shivatemples nn13). Sung by Sundarar — one pathigam, 7th Tirumurai (shivatemples). Dinamalar also names Tirunavukkarasar (Appar) — not corroborated; not used. Century 8.
- Moolavar: Marundeeswarar / Marudeeswarar; also Idaiyatrunathar (Idaiyattreeswarar), Sivanandeswarar.
- Theertham: Sittridai theertham; also the Pennai. Sthala vriksham: marutham (Terminalia arjuna).
- Legend: Suka (Shuka Brahma Rishi) overheard a secret teaching Shiva gave Parvati and was cursed; Shiva assured him he would be born as son of Vyasa and be freed by worshipping at Thiruvidaiyaru; he did penance beneath the marutha tree here and was released.
- History: original complex believed Chola; present masonry 16th c. Inscriptions: Rajaraja I (985–1014), Kulothunga I (1070–1120), Rajendra III (1246–1279); Pandya Maravarman Sundara Pandya (1216–1238); Vijayanagara Achyuta Raya, Sadasiva Raya (1542–1570), Rama Deva. HR&CE administered.
- Architecture: Dravidian, ~1 acre, concentric rectangular granite walls; three-tiered gopuram.
- Festivals: the most prominent festival falls in Thai (Jan–Feb) per Wikipedia (name garbled "Thayar Aaratu" — not named in content). Usual Shaiva festivals.
- Coordinates: 11.8931, 79.3367 — Wikipedia temple article (11°53′35″N 79°20′12″E) via search text. No other catalog entry in this village; Thiruvennainallur entry (kripapureeswarar) is distinct.
