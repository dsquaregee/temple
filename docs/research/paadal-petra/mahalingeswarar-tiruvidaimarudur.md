# Tiruvidaimarudur — Mahalingeswarar Temple (Madhyarjuna)

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Mahalingeswarar**, the lingam revered as jyotirmaya (radiant light); goddess **Brihatsundarakujambika**.
- **Location:** Tiruvidaimarudur, ~8 km from Kumbakonam, Thanjavur district, Tamil Nadu (on the Kumbakonam–Mayiladuthurai line). Approx 10.9908 N, 79.4486 E (approximate).
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam (Tevaram); the **central** of the three Marudur / arjuna temples = **Madhyarjuna**.
- **Fabric:** Among the largest delta temples — 4 gopurams, 3 concentric prakaras; oldest parts Chola (9th c.), 2nd precinct Vijayanagara, towers up to 16th-c. Nayaks. ~35 theerthams (Karunyamritha Tirtham best known). North-style Mookambika shrine within.
- **Legend:** Pandya king (often Varaguna) freed from a brahmahatya death-curse through the lord's grace — a kshetra of expiation and liberation.
