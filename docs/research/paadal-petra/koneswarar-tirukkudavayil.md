# Tirukkudavayil (Kudavasal) — Koneswarar Temple

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Koneswarar**; goddess **Periyanayaki** (Periyanayagi Ammai).
- **Location:** **Tirukkudavayil / Kudavasal**, midway between Kumbakonam (~18 km) and Tiruvarur (~20 km); Tiruvarur district. ~10.86 N, 79.52 E (approximate).
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam — the **94th** shrine of the Kaveri south bank; hymned by **Tirugnana Sambandar**. A maadakkovil attributed to Kochchenga Chola; one of the twelve temples tied to the origin of Kumbakonam and the Mahamaham (Brahma's pot-top fell here). Tank Amirtatheertham; 80-ft rajagopuram.
- **Festivals:** Masi Magam Brahmotsavam (Masi); Maha Shivaratri; Navaratri (Purattasi); Karthikai Mondays; Pradosham.
- **Distinctness:** distinct from `koneswarar-trincomalee` (the Eezha Nadu / Sri Lanka temple).
