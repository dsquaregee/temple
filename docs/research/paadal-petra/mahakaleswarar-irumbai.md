# Mahakaleswarar (Maakaleswarar) Temple, Irumbai (mahakaleswarar-irumbai)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Thondai Nadu (Tevaram name: Irumbai Maakaalam / Thiru Irumbai Makalam). District: Villupuram (Vanur taluk; some listings say Cuddalore — Vanur taluk is Villupuram). Tradition: Shaivism.

- Deity: Shiva as Mahakaleswarar / Maakaleswarar; consort Kuyilmozhi Ammai.
- Tevaram: sung by Tirugnana Sambandar (Dinamalar lists it as the 32nd Shiva temple of the
  Nadunadu/Thondai region praised in the Tevaram; one source also cites Sundarar and Pattinathar).
- Coordinates: **village-level ~12.007, 79.787** (Irumbai). Exact temple point not found in
  sources — refine against a mapping source when possible.
- Legend (the temple's defining story): the Siddha Kaduveli sat in penance under a tree during a
  drought; the king sent a dancer (devadasi) to disturb him, and rain returned when he rose. At a
  festival her anklet slipped and the Siddha refastened it; mocked by the crowd, he sang before
  Mahakaleswarar and the linga burst into pieces. When the king begged forgiveness he sang again
  and the fragments reunited; the king had the linga covered with a **copper plate**, which
  remains. Place name read from *iluppai* trees. One of three Makalam shrines said to be
  consecrated by Rishi Makalanathar (the others: Ujjain and Tirumakalam).
- Architecture: east-facing; ekadala-style vimana over the sanctum; modest walled courtyard rather
  than a tall gopuram; copper-sheathed split linga; goddess in her own shrine; tank Mangala
  Teertham.
- Festivals: Maha Shivaratri and Masi Magam (Masi, Feb–Mar); Tirukarthikai (Nov–Dec); Panguni
  Uthiram (Mar–Apr); monthly Pradosham.
- Access: Vanur taluk, Villupuram district; ~13 km from Pondicherry on the Pondicherry–Tindivanam
  road via Kiliyanur, short branch road (sources give ~8–13 km). Nearest railway Pondicherry;
  nearest major airport Chennai.
- **Not verified:** founding dynasty/century of the structural temple (medieval; century 7 used
  for the Tevaram era); exact temple coordinate; the age of the Kaduveli story (sources give
  "500–600 years ago", which sits awkwardly with the Tevaram-era shrine); the ekadala vimana and
  Mangala Teertham details are carried from the existing en entry; current timings.
