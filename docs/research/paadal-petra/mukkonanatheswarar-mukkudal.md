# Tiruppalliyin Mukkudal — Mukkonanatheswarar Temple

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Mukkonanatheswarar** (also **Tirunethranathar / Trinethranathar**, the three-eyed lord); goddess **Anjanakshi** (also **Mayilmevum Kanni**). (Worklist left goddess "?" — supplied; names vary across sources.)
- **Location:** Tiruppalliyin Mukkudal (historical **Kekkarai**), ~6–7 km NE of Tiruvarur, Tiruvarur district, Tamil Nadu. ~10.7984 N, 79.6572 E (Wikipedia).
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam — hymned by Appar; Kaveri south bank. Name *mukkudal* = a confluence of three waters.
- **Festivals:** Maha Shivaratri (Masi); Navaratri (Purattasi); Karthikai Mondays; Pradosham.
