# Aranyasundareswarar (Aranyeswarar) Temple, Keezhai Tirukkattuppalli (aranyasundareswarar-keezhai-tirukkattuppalli)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Chola Nadu (north of the Kaveri). District: Mayiladuthurai
(Sirkazhi taluk; older sources say Nagapattinam). Tradition: Shaivism.
Tevaram name: **Tirukkattuppalli** (திருக்காட்டுப்பள்ளி); "Keezhai" = lower/downstream, to
distinguish it from Mela (upper) Tirukkattuppalli (Agneeswarar, Thanjavur district), a
separate Paadal Petra Sthalam. Counted as the 12th Tevaram shrine on the northern bank of the
Kaveri (per shivatemples/Dinamalar).

- Deity: Shiva as Aranyeswarar / Aranyasundareswarar; swayambhu linga on a square avudayar.
  Consort: **Akilandeswari** (Akilandanayaki), shrine to the left of the Lord so both are seen
  from one spot (Wikipedia/Dinamalar).
- Sung by: **Tirugnanasambandar** (one pathikam). Sources checked do NOT confirm Appar here;
  the seed hint of a generic "Tevaram" attribution was narrowed to Sambandar only.
- Coordinates: **approximate ~11.145, 79.775** — derived from the textual location (about 2 km
  west of Tiruvenkadu on the road towards Elayamuthukulapuram; ~13 km from Sirkazhi). No sourced
  geo-coordinate found via search; refine against a mapping source.
- Defining legend: Indra slew Vishwaroopa; Vishwaroopa's father raised Vritra (Vritrasuran)
  in revenge; Indra killed him with the vajra made from sage Dadhichi's spine and incurred
  brahmahatti; his guru advised him to worship Shiva; he did penance in this forest and was
  absolved. Name Aranya = forest (the area was once forest).
- Architecture: no tall gopuram; understated entrance opening directly into a spacious outer
  prakaram; sanctum vimana of the **dwidala** type; Ambal shrine to the left of the main deity;
  seven lingas in the temple, one with two banas (Dinamalar). Date of the present
  structure unknown.
- Festivals: Maha Shivaratri (Feb–Mar); Aipasi Skanda Sashti (Oct–Nov); Arudra Darshan
  (Dec–Jan); Vinayaka Chaturthi (Aug–Sep); monthly Pradosham (generic).
- Timings reported: ~8:00–11:00 and 18:00–19:30 (Dinamalar, TN Temples Project); shivatemples
  gives 9:00–11:00 and 18:00–19:30.
- Access: ~2 km west of Tiruvenkadu; Sirkazhi (~13 km) nearest railway station; Mayiladuthurai
  junction; airports Tiruchirappalli/Chennai (general knowledge, no distance given).
- **Not verified:** founding dynasty/structural date (used century 7 for the Sambandar era);
  exact coordinates; current timings and contact numbers; the count of lingas (Dinamalar says
  seven; Dharisanam says Brahma created ten lingas and names the sage "Thatheesi" — conflicting
  traditions, content uses the Dinamalar seven-linga account hedged as "temple accounts");
  Templefolks says Sambandar's hymn refers to a "Dasalinga" form (not used); Appar's
  association (a search summary claimed it, not confirmed by any page — omitted); an alternate
  name Parthanpalli appears on one blog (not used); direction the temple faces (not found);
  nearest-airport distance.
