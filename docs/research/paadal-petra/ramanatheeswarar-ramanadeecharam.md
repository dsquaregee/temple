# Ramanadeecharam — Ramanatheeswarar Temple

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Ramanatheswarar** (Ramalingeswarar); goddess **Sarivar Kuzhali** (also Soodikaambal).
- **Location:** **Ramanadeecharam** (historical name Tiruramanatheechuram) at **Thirukkannapuram**, ~10 km from Nannilam, ~3 km from Thiruchengattangudi; Tiruvarur district. ~10.85 N, 79.68 E (approximate).
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam, Kaveri south bank — hymned by **Tirugnana Sambandar**.
- **IMPORTANT distinctness:** a small, separate Shiva shrine — NOT the Ramanathaswamy jyotirlinga at Rameswaram (`ramanathaswamy-rameswaram`), and NOT the Vishnu Divya Desam Sowriraja temple that shares the village of Thirukkannapuram (`sowriraja-thirukkannapuram`). (Master-list goddess draft "Parvathavardhini?" was WRONG — that belongs to Rameswaram; corrected to Sarivar Kuzhali.)
- **Festivals:** Maha Shivaratri (Masi); Navaratri (Purattasi); Karthikai Mondays; Pradosham.
