# Kadaimudinathar Temple, Tirukkadaimudi (kadaimudinathar-tirukkadaimudi)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Chola Nadu (north of the Kaveri) (Tevaram name: Tirukkadaimudi, திருக்கடைமுடி). Tradition: Shaivism.
District: Mayiladuthurai (Keezhaiyur, Tharangambadi taluk; some older listings say Nagapattinam).

- Deity: Shiva as Kadaimudinathar, also Anthi Samrakshaneeswarar; consort Abirami (Abhiramavalli). Both the Lord's and the goddess's shrines face **west**.
- Tevaram: sung by Sambandar (one pathigam per shivatemples.com). No source found confirming Appar or Sundarar hymns; the content says only "Sambandar".
- Coordinates: **approximate, village-level ~11.07, 79.77.** No source gave the temple's coordinates (the Wikipedia Keezhaiyur coordinates found belong to the Karaikal-area Keezhaiyur and the "Twin Temples, Keezhaiyur" belongs to a different site). Refine against a mapping source.
- Defining legend: Brahma, cursed by Shiva for pride, saw a swayambhu lingam beneath a **kiluvai tree** and worshipped it to be freed; the tree gave the place its name (Kiluvaiyur -> Keezhaiyur). Sage **Kanva** worshipped here and bathed in the nearby Kaveri; the place of his liberation is still called **Kanva Mahan Thurai**.
- Name: Kadaimudi explained as a corruption of Sadaimudi (Shiva's matted locks); "Anthi Samrakshaneeswarar" = protector at dusk/the end. (Interpretive; stated as "some read".)
- Architecture: west-facing sanctum; west-facing Abirami shrine; shrines to Nataraja, Bhairava and the Tevaram saints. Gopuram height/tiers, mandapas, tank not documented in sources found.
- Festivals: Arudra Darisanam (Margazhi, Dec-Jan); Karthikai Deepam (Nov-Dec); Maha Shivaratri and Pradosham generic.
- Access: Keezhaiyur, Tharangambadi taluk, PIN 609304; temple ~2 km from Keezhaiyur bus stop; reach via Mayiladuthurai / Sirkazhi; Mayiladuthurai Junction nearest major railway; Tiruchirappalli nearest major airport (distance approximate). Timings in sources differ (e.g. 6-12 / 4-8 and 6-1 / 3-7).
- Disambiguation: the Abhirami Pattar story belongs to Thirukkadaiyur (Amritaghateswarar), a different temple; the hindu-blog "Shodasalinga / dissolution" account is folklore and was not used.
- **Not verified:** founding dynasty and century (used 7 for the Tevaram era); gopuram/mandapa details; temple tank; exact coordinates; current timings; Appar/Sundarar hymns; distance from Mayiladuthurai/Sirkazhi.
