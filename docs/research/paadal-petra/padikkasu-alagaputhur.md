# Alagaputhur (Arisilkaraipudur) — Padikasu Nathar Temple

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Padikasu Nathar** (also **Swarnapureeswarar**, "lord of the golden city"); goddess **Soundara Nayaki** (also **Azhagammai** / Azhagambikai). (Worklist drafted goddess "Azhagammai?" — confirmed.)
- **Location:** Alagaputhur / Arisilkaraipudur (on the Arisil river), ~6–7 km from Kumbakonam, Thanjavur district, Tamil Nadu. ~10.9303 N, 79.4295 E (Wikipedia).
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam — hymned by Sambandar, Appar and Sundarar; Kaveri south bank. Sthala vriksham **vilvam**; tank **Amirtha Pushkarini**.
- **Legend:** birthplace of **Pugazh Thunai Nayanar** (of the 63 Nayanmars); in a famine Shiva placed a daily gold coin (**padikasu**) on the temple step to sustain his worship → name Padikasu Nathar.
- **Festivals:** Maha Shivaratri (Masi); Pugazh Thunai Nayanar festival; Pradosham; Karthikai.
