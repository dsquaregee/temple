# Tiruppayatrur (Tirupayathangudi) — Payatrunathar Temple

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Payatrunathar**; goddess **Kaviyangkanni Ammai** (also **Nethrambigai**, "goddess of the eye"). (Worklist drafted goddess "Mugizhambikai?" — corrected from sources.)
- **Location:** Tirupayathangudi (Tevaram **Tiruppayatrur**), ~14–15 km from Tiruvarur (via Surakkudi), between the Arasilar and Vettaru rivers, Nagapattinam district, Tamil Nadu (via Kangalancheri, PIN 610101). ~10.8000 N, 79.7300 E (approximate).
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam — hymned by Appar; Kaveri south bank. Tank **Karuna Theertham**.
- **Healing tradition:** especially revered for **eye ailments** — bathe in the Karuna Theertham and worship **Nethrambigai** (netra = eye) and Payatrunathar.
- **Festivals:** Maha Shivaratri (Masi); Navaratri (Purattasi); Karthikai Mondays; Pradosham.
