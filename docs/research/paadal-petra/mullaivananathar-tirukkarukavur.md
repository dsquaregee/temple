# Tirukkarukavur — Mullaivananathar / Garbarakshambigai Temple

- **Deity:** Shiva as Mullaivananathar ("lord of the jasmine forest"); goddess **Garbarakshambigai** ("guardian of the womb").
- **Location:** Tirukkarukavur, near Papanasam, Thanjavur district, Tamil Nadu (~10 km Papanasam, ~25 km Kumbakonam). Approx 10.9372 N, 79.2706 E (approximate).
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam — sung in the Tevaram.
- **Fame:** Pre-eminent temple for expectant mothers; consecrated ghee and castor oil given as the goddess's blessing for safe pregnancy.
- **Legend:** Vedhika, wife of sage Nidhruva, whose unborn child was cursed by a short-tempered visiting sage; the goddess protected the womb to a healthy birth — hence Garbarakshambigai.
- **Fabric:** Compact Dravidian temple, Chola-period building with later renovation; twin sanctums of Mullaivananathar and the goddess.
