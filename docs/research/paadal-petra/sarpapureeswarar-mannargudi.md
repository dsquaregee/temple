# Paamani (near Mannargudi) — Sarpapureeswarar Temple

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Sarpapureeswarar** (also **Naganathar**, "lord of the serpents' city"); goddess **Amirtha Nayaki**.
- **Location:** Paamani, ~3.5 km north of Mannargudi, Tiruvarur district, Tamil Nadu. Approx 10.6889 N, 79.4542 E (approximate).
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam — one pathigam by Sambandar. Historical name **Tiruppaadhaleeswaram**.
- **Associations:** naga (serpent) worship; prayers for relief from naga-dosha.
- **Caveat:** A small, lesser-documented delta shrine; detailed sthala-purana not widely recorded. Distinct from the famous Rajagopalaswamy (Vaishnava) temple of Mannargudi town.

**Notes:** Coordinates approximate; sourcing thin (shivatemples.com listing). Goddess Amirtha Nayaki, deity Sarpa Pureeswarar/Naganathar per that listing. Translations AI-drafted; native-speaker QA recommended. Prose original and deliberately modest where sources are sparse.
