# Thiruvaduthurai — Masilamaniswarar / Gomukteeswarar Temple

- **Deity:** Shiva as Masilamaniswarar ("flawless gem"), also Gomukteeswarar (granted liberation to a cow); goddess **Oppilamulaiyammai**.
- **Location:** Thiruvaduthurai, near Kuthalam, Mayiladuthurai district, Tamil Nadu (~20 km from Kumbakonam and Mayiladuthurai). Approx 11.0197 N, 79.5236 E (approximate).
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam — sung by Appar, Sambandar and Sundarar.
- **Mutt:** Seat of the **Thiruvaduthurai Adheenam** (from 16th c.), a major Shaiva Siddhanta monastic institution; patron of Tamil Saiva scholarship, music and publishing.
- **Tirumular:** Site strongly associated with the Siddha sage Tirumular (author of the Tirumantiram).
- **Epigraphy:** Chola-period temple with inscriptions from Parantaka I onward.
