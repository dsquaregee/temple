# Tiruidumbavanam — Sarguna Nathar Temple (Idumbavaneswarar)

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Sarguna Nathar** (also **Idumbavaneswarar**, "lord of all good qualities"); goddess **Mangalavalli** (also **Mangalanayaki**). (Worklist left goddess "?" — supplied from sources.)
- **Location:** Idumbavanam, Thiruthuraipoondi taluk, Tiruvarur district, Tamil Nadu, PIN 614703; ~18 km from Thiruthuraipoondi, southern delta. ~10.4500 N, 79.6800 E (approximate).
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam — hymned by Tirugnana Sambandar; southern Kaveri delta.
- **Legend:** the demon **Idumban** bathed in the temple tank, worshipped Shiva, and was granted salvation → name Idumbavanam ("grove of Idumban").
- **Festivals:** Maha Shivaratri (Masi); Navaratri (Purattasi); Karthikai Mondays; Pradosham.
