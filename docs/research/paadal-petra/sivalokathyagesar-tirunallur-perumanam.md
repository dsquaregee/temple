# Sivaloka Thyagesar (Shivaloka Thyagar) Temple, Tirunallur Perumanam (sivalokathyagesar-tirunallur-perumanam)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Chola Nadu (north of the Kaveri) (Tevaram name: Tirunallur Perumanam / திருநல்லூர்ப் பெருமணம்). District: Mayiladuthurai (Sirkazhi taluk; older sources list Nagapattinam district). Tradition: Shaivism.

- Deity: Shiva as Sivalokathyagesar / Shivaloka Thyagar; consort Venneetru Umaiammai (Tiruvenneetru Umaiammai), also Swetha Vibhuti Nayaki (Dinamalar, shivatemples.com, Wikipedia agree).
- Tevaram: sung by Sambandar. Hymn count for the temple not established in sources checked.
- Place name: now **Achalpuram** (Aachchaalpuram / Achchalpuram); earlier Nallur / Tirunallur Perumanam. Sources vary in spelling. *Perumanam* glossed as "great wedding" (Tamil peru + manam), the reference being Sambandar's wedding.
- Coordinates: **~11.329, 79.756** (Wikipedia: 11°19′44″N 79°45′20″E, Achalpuram Shivalokathyagar Temple). Not independently re-checked on a map.
- Defining legend: Sambandar's father Sivapada Hridayar chose Mangai Nallal (also Stotrapoornambikai), daughter of Nambandar Nambi of Nallur; wedding held at the temple with Tiruneelanakka Nayanar presiding over rites and guests incl. Muruga Nayanar. Sambandar prayed to be spared the entanglements of householder life; a brilliant beam of divine light (Shiva Jyothi) appeared and the Lord bade the saint come with all assembled; Sambandar sang the Namashivaya padigam (opening "Kadhalagi Kasindhu Kannir malgi", per tntemplesproject) and he, his bride, parents and all guests merged into the light. One source adds that Parvati distributed vibhuti; not used.
- Architecture: east-facing; five-tiered rajagopuram; metal-clad dwajasthambam; Nandi mandapam; hundred-pillared hall (nootrukkal mandapam) with a shrine to Sambandar shown with his bride; 11 theerthams, the Panchakshara Theertham opposite the east entrance the chief one; sthala vriksha mango (Dinamalar); believed Pallava foundation, renovated by medieval Cholas; mother shrine built 1210 CE.
- Festivals: Vaikasi Moolam (May–June), the wedding/merging reenacted yearly (Dinamalar; tntemplesproject). Maha Shivaratri and Pradosham stated generally.
- Access: ~12 km SE of Chidambaram and ~13 km NE of Sirkali (Dinamalar); Sirkali–Mahendrapuram buses pass through; nearest railway Sirkali; Mayiladuthurai ~37 km (tntemplesproject); Dinamalar timings 6 am–12 noon and 4–8 pm; Chennai nearest major airport (~200+ km by road, general knowledge).
- Corrections to seed: seed gave the town as "Acharapuram"; sources spell it **Achalpuram / Aachchaalpuram** (Tamil ஆச்சாள்புரம்). Content uses Achalpuram (the seed's spelling is a variant, not found in sources). The seed's goddess hint was not given; sources name Venneetru Umaiammai. A Dinamalar line saying Sambandar praised the Lord "with the Kallur Perumanam hymn" appears to be an error for Nallur Perumanam and is not used. Dinamalar lists the sthala vriksham as mango (single source).
- **Not verified:** bride's name (Mangai Nallal vs Stotrapoornambikai — sources give both and the content gives both); the exact details of the light/merging (accounts differ: beam of light, Parvati's vibhuti); Tevaram hymn count; dating of the Pallava foundation (tradition only; century 7 used for the Tevaram era); the 1210 CE date of the Ambal shrine (single source, Wikipedia); sthala vriksham (single source); current timings (reported Dinamalar/tntemplesproject figures, flagged as "commonly reported"); the exact temple coordinate; distances (Chidambaram 12 km SE per Dinamalar; other sources say 15 or 25 km); whether the festival is on Moolam star of Vaikasi (two sources agree, no official calendar checked).
