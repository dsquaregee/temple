# Tiruppullamangai — Alanthurainathar (Brahmapureeswarar) Temple

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Alanthurainathar** (Brahmapureeswarar / Pasupatinathar); goddess **Alliyankothai**. (Master-list goddess "Alliangothai?" — confirmed.)
- **Location:** **Pullamangai** at **Pasupathikoil**, on the Kudamurutti near Thanjavur; Thanjavur district. ~10.88 N, 79.27 E (approximate).
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam — hymned by **Tirugnana Sambandar**. One of the most celebrated **early-Chola** temples (~10th c.), renowned for its exquisite vimana sculpture. Name Alanthurai = 'banyan ghat'.
- **Festivals:** Maha Shivaratri (Masi); Navaratri (Purattasi); Karthikai Mondays; Pradosham.
