# Tirukkadavur Mayanam — Brahmapureeswarar Temple

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Brahmapureeswarar**; goddess **Malarkuzhal Minnammai** (shivatemples.com: Nimalakuchambikai).
- **Location:** **Tirukkadavur Mayanam** (Tirumayanam), ~1.5–3 km from the Amrita Kadeswarar temple at **Tirukkadaiyur**; Mayiladuthurai district (some sources Nagapattinam). ~11.02 N, 79.85 E (approximate).
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam — hymned by **Sambandar, Appar and Sundarar**. A **mayana koil** (cremation-ground shrine); one of the five **Mayanam** temples where Shiva humbled & recreated Brahma's ego (with Kachi/Kanchipuram, Kazhi/Sirkazhi, Naalur, Veezhi/Tiruveezhimizhalai).
- **Note:** distinct from the catalog's other Brahmapureeswarar shrines (Sirkazhi, Thirukkuvalai, Ambar).
- **Festivals:** Maha Shivaratri (Masi); Navaratri (Purattasi); Karthikai Mondays; Pradosham.
