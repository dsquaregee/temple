# Tirukkadikkulam (Karpaganatharkulam) — Karpaganathar Temple

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Karpaganathar** (Karpageswarar, the wish-granting lord; after the **karpaga/kalpaka** celestial tree); worshipped in a rare **hexagonal lingam**. Goddess **Soundara Nayaki** (also **Balasundari**). (Worklist goddess "Soundaranayaki" — confirmed.)
- **Location:** Karpaganatharkulam (Tevaram **Tirukkadikkulam**), ~18 km from Thiruthuraipoondi (via Thondiakadu), Tiruvarur district, Tamil Nadu, PIN 614703; ~29 km from Vedaranyam. ~10.4400 N, 79.7500 E (approximate).
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam — hymned by Tirugnana Sambandar; southern Kaveri delta.
- **Distinctive:** the mula-lingam is **hexagonal**; name evokes the wish-fulfilling tree.
- **Festivals:** Maha Shivaratri (Masi); Navaratri (Purattasi); Karthikai Mondays; Pradosham.
