# Tiruneelakudi — Neelakandeswarar Temple

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Neelakandeswarar** (TN Temples: Chozheeswaramudaiyar); goddess **Oppilamulaiyal** (Anupamasthani / Bhaktabeeshta Pradayini; one source: Umaiyammai/Azhagaambikai). (Worklist goddess "Oppilamulaiyal" — confirmed.)
- **Location:** **Tiruneelakudi**, N bank of the Nattaru, ~13–15 km from **Kumbakonam** on the Kumbakonam–Karaikal road; Thanjavur district. ~10.98 N, 79.48 E (approximate — no Wikipedia coord).
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam — hymned by **Appar and Sambandar**; a **Rahu parihara** shrine. Early Chola structure, later Pandya/Vijayanagara additions.
- **Festivals:** Maha Shivaratri (Masi); Navaratri (Purattasi); Karthikai Mondays; Pradosham; Rahu parihara days.

**Notes:** Coordinates approximate. Distinct from the catalog's other Neelakandeswarar shrines (Tirumannippadikkarai, Erukkattampuliyur). Reconciled remaining-PPS temple.
