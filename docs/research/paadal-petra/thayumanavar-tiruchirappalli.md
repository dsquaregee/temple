# Thayumanavar (Matrubhuteswarar) Temple, Rockfort, Tiruchirappalli (thayumanavar-tiruchirappalli)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Chola Nadu south of the Kaveri. Tradition: Shaivism.
- Deity: Shiva as Thayumanavar / Matrubhuteswarar; consort Mattuvar Kuzhalammai (also Sugandha Kundalambikai).
- At the foot of the Tiruchirappalli Rockfort; hill revered as Dakshina Kailayam.
- Legend: Rathnavathi, a devotee away from her mother, had none to help at childbirth; Shiva came in her mother's form and tended her → "Thayumanavar" (the one who became mother).
- Theppa float festival recorded since 1546.
- Coordinates: ~10.8297, 78.6983 (Wikipedia, Thayumanaswami Temple, Rockfort).
- Not verified: exact founding century of the structural temple (used 7 for the Tevaram era); step count varies by source (300/417).
