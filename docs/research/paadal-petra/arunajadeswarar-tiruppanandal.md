# Arunajadeswarar (Thaalavaneswarar) Temple, Tiruppanandal (arunajadeswarar-tiruppanandal)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Chola Nadu (north of the Kaveri) (Tevaram name: Tiruppanandal / Thiruppanandal). District: Thanjavur (Thiruvidaimarudur taluk). Tradition: Shaivism.

- Deity: Shiva as Arunajadeswarar (Aruna = red, jata = matted locks; also Thaalavaneswarar,
  "Lord of the palm forest"; other listed names Senjadaiappar, Panangateesar); consort
  Periyanayaki (Sanskrit Brihannayaki).
- Tevaram: counted as the 39th Shiva shrine on the northern bank of the Kaveri (Dinamalar /
  shivatemples nc39). Hymns credited to Sambandar (tntemplesproject); Wikipedia additionally lists
  Appar and Nambiyandar Nambi, and Arunagirinathar (Tiruppugazh) — the Wikipedia attribution
  carries a "citation needed" tag. Content text therefore says only "sung in the Tevaram".
  Historical names: Thadagaieswaram / Tiruttatakai-Isvaram, Thala Vanam.
- Coordinates: **approximate, town-level ~11.10, 79.44**. No source gave a geotag. Estimated from
  "midway between Kumbakonam and Gangaikonda Cholapuram" and ~18–21 km from Kumbakonam along the
  Kumbakonam–Anaikarai road; consistent with Wikipedia's geotag for nearby Tiruaappadi
  (11.0828, 79.4375), 2.5 km away. Refine against a mapping source.
- Defining legends:
  1. Thadagai: a woman devotee garlanded the Lord daily; once her upper garment slipped as she
     raised the garland; the Lord bent his head/shaft toward her to accept it, sparing her
     modesty, so the linga leans (tntemplesproject; Wikipedia).
  2. Kungiliya Kalaya Nayanar (one of the 63 Nayanmars, of Tirukkadaiyur, known for offering
     kungiliyam incense): the Chola king (named **Manimudi Chola** by tntemplesproject; storibuzz
     names the king differently, "Veerasena") tried to straighten the leaning linga with
     elephants and ropes and failed; Kalayar tied a rope around his own neck and pulled with the
     Panchakshara; the Lord straightened. Storibuzz adds a hand emerging from the linga to stop him.
     The 63-Nayanmar account is from the Periya Puranam (standard tradition).
  3. Parvati's penance in the palm grove and Shiva teaching her as Gnana Guru (indian-heritage.org).
- Correction to seed hint: the seed said "the Lord's matted hair leaned / jata leaned". Sources
  consistently describe the **linga** (not the jata) leaning; "Aruna Jadeswarar" is the name
  (Lord of the red matted locks). Content follows the sources.
- Architecture: seven-tier rajagopuram on the west and five-tier gopuram on the east
  (tntemplesproject); sanctum with Shiva linga; Periyanayaki shrine; sthala vriksham palm
  (Dinamalar: Panamaram); tirtham reported as Nagakannika Theertham below the sixteen-pillared
  hall (one source says Brahma Theertham); Darasuram sculpture of the Kalayar story shows the
  slant of the linga (poetryinstone). Kasi Mutt (Thiruppanandal Adheenam) founded by
  Kumaragurupara Desikar is within the temple complex (Wikipedia).
- Inscriptions: a tourism blog (2016) reports inscriptions of Kulothunga Chola I and Rajaraja Chola II;
  not independently confirmed, so the content only says "inscriptions of the Chola kings are
  reported".
- Festivals: no verified festival calendar. Generic Maha Shivaratri / Pradosham / Tamil-month
  festivals only. (A ta.wikisource page mentions a 10-day Chithirai Brahmotsavam but its
  attribution to this temple was unclear — not used.)
- Access: Kumbakonam–Anaikarai road; ~18 km (tntemplesproject) or ~21 km (other listing) from
  Kumbakonam; Thiruppanandal is in Thiruvidaimarudur taluk. Tevaram shrine Tiruaappadi ~2.5 km away.
  Nearest major airport Tiruchirappalli (~100 km — estimate, not sourced).
- **Not verified:** exact temple coordinates; which Tevaram saints sang here and the number of
  decads; the name of the king (Manimudi Chola vs other variants) and whether the legend is
  attached to this king historically; dynasty/date of the stone temple and inscriptions;
  existence and location of Nagakannika Theertham; sculptures in the temple depicting the stories;
  administration (Dharmapuram Adheenam vs Kasi Mutt — sources differ); festival dates; current
  timings (a 2016 blog lists ~6:00–12:00 and 16:00–21:00); distance from Kumbakonam; airport distance.
  Century 7 used for the Tevaram era (Sambandar/Appar).
