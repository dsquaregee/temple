# Tiruthalaiyalankadu (Thalayalangadu) — Nardhanapureeswarar Temple

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Nardhanapureeswarar** ("lord of the city of dance"; also **Aadavalleeswarar / Aadavallar**, "mighty in dance" — a Nataraja/cosmic-dance epithet); goddess **Balambikai** (also **Tirumadanthai Ammai**). (Worklist had "Aadavalleeswarar" as a name and goddess "Thirukuzhal Nayaki?"; sources give Aadavalleeswarar as the **lord** and the goddess as Balambikai — corrected.)
- **Location:** Thalayalangadu (Tevaram **Tiruthalaiyalankadu**), ~15 km from Tiruvarur, ~8 km east of Kudavasal (Chimizhi post, via Sembangudi, PIN 612603), Tiruvarur district, Tamil Nadu. ~10.8600 N, 79.6000 E (approximate).
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam — hymned by Appar; Kaveri south bank (~93rd on south bank). Tank **Sangu Theertham**; sthala vriksham vilva/pala (varies).
- **Festivals:** Thai amavasai; Maha Shivaratri (Masi); Aippasi Annabhishekam; Pradosham; Karthikai.
