# Thaalapureeswarar (Sapthapureeswarar / Sabdapureeswarar) Temple, Tirukolakka (thaalapureeswarar-tirukolakka)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Chola Nadu (north of the Kaveri). District: Mayiladuthurai
(formerly Nagapattinam), Tamil Nadu; address Tirukolakka – 609 110 (Keezhai Thirukolakka, beside
Sirkazhi). Tradition: Shaivism. Tevaram name: Tirukolakka (திருக்கோலக்கா), also Sapthapuri.

- Deity: Shiva as **Thaalapureeswarar** / Sapthapureeswarar / Sabdapureeswarar /
  **Thalamudaiyar** (Thiru Thalam Udayar); consort **Osaikodutha Nayaki** (also
  Dhwanipradhambal). Sacred tree: konrai (Cassia fistula). Tanks: Ananda Theertham (opposite the
  temple) and Surya Theertham (in front).
- Tevaram: hymns of Sambandar and Sundarar (per tntemplesproject). Dinamalar counts it as the
  15th Shiva temple on the northern bank of the Kaveri.
- Coordinates: **approximate ~11.230, 79.737** — NOT sourced. Placed ~1.5–2 km from the
  Sirkazhi Sattainathar temple (sources: 1–2 km), near the Ulagalandha Perumal temple
  (Kazheesirama Vinnagaram). Refine against a mapping source.
- Name origin: Thiru (Lakshmi) appeared in bridal finery (kolam) in the konrai grove (kaa) —
  tntemplesproject.
- Defining legend: child Sambandar sang here keeping time by clapping; his hands reddened; Shiva
  gave him a pair of golden cymbals (thalam) which made no sound until the Goddess infused them
  with sound — hence Thalamudaiyar and Osaikodutha Nayaki. Variant: "golden plates" instead of
  cymbals (Dinamalar). Another variant (shivatemples) of a mother offering a golden plate in
  thanksgiving after her mute son regained speech, kept at the temple — not used.
- Architecture: Dravidian; no detailed description found. Prakara shrines per Dinamalar:
  Vinayaka, Somaskanda, Subramania, Lakshmi, Sani (Saturn), Bhairava, Surya.
- Festivals: Chithirai Thiruvadhirai (Apr–May) is held at Sirkazhi Sattainathar temple with the
  milk festival on day 2; afterwards Sambandar comes to Tirukolakka where the golden plate/cymbals
  are given (Dinamalar). Maha Shivaratri and monthly Pradosham (general).
- Access: ~2 km from Sirkazhi (sources: 1–2 km), ~19–20 km from Chidambaram and Mayiladuthurai,
  ~240 km from Chennai (tamilnadu-favtourism-type blog); nearest railway Sirkazhi.
- **Not verified:** exact coordinates; temple layout, orientation, gopuram, age of the structure
  and any dynasty/century (century 7 used for the Tevaram/Sambandar era; "Chola and later" is a
  generic placeholder); the claim that the temple is "modest/small" is an editorial reading, not
  a sourced dimension; current timings (none found); whether a separate goddess shrine exists
  (en text says she is "honoured"); the Surya Theertham location; "Ulagalandha Perumal temple"
  proximity (one Tripadvisor review); the "15th on the northern bank" sequence (Dinamalar only).
