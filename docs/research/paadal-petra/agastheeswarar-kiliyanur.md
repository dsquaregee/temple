# Agastheeswarar Temple, Kiliyanur (Thirukkiliyannavur) — research notes

Master-list row 238 (Nadu Nadu). Row confirmed: Agastheeswarar / Akhilandeswari (separate shrine); Sambandar. District: row says Villupuram — correct (Vanur taluk per 2011 census; shivatemples address says Tindivanam taluk).

- Place: Kiliyanur village, about 18 km from Tindivanam on the Tindivanam–Puducherry road (shivatemples). Old spelling Killiyannavur; Tevaram name Thirukkiliyannavur. Kiliyanur Wikipedia: between Puducherry and Tindivanam.
- Paadal Petra Sthalam, Nadu Nadu — 23rd of Nadu Nadu (shivatemples nn23). Sambandar — pathigam in the 3rd Tirumurai. Only singer confirmed.
- Legends: Sage Agastya worshipped here (hence Agastheeswarar); sthala purana: Sukha Brahma Maharishi worshipped here for relief from severe stomach pain; Adisesha worshipped here. On Maha Shivaratri, tradition says Nandikeswara worships Goddess Akhilandeswari. (No parrot legend found — not used.)
- History: locally said ~1500 years old; built by Cholas; inscriptions say first built of brick, rebuilt in granite in the reign of Parantaka I (travel site — phrased "inscriptions are said to record"). 
- Sthala vriksham: Vanni.
- Coordinates: 12.1167, 79.7500 — Kiliyanur village, Wikipedia (12°7′0″N 79°45′0″E), via search text. APPROXIMATE (village point, rounded to the minute); no temple-specific coordinate found. No other catalog temple at Kiliyanur.
