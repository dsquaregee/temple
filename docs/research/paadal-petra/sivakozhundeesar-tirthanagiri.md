# Sivakozhundeesar Temple, Tirthanagiri (sivakozhundeesar-tirthanagiri)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Nadu Nadu (Tevaram name: Tiruthinainagar / Thiruthinai).
Tradition: Shaivism.

- Deity: Shiva as Sivakozhundeesar (Sivakozhundhu Easwarar / Sivakozhundeeswarar),
  worshipped as a swayambhu (self-manifested) linga.
- Consort: sourced name is **Oppila Nayaki** (also spelled Oppilaa Nayagi / Oppilanayaki),
  given consistently across Wikipedia, Dinamalar and shivatemples.com. **The task brief
  specified "Sivakami," but NO source names Sivakami for this temple** — the content uses
  the sourced Oppila Nayaki. Alternate Amman names appearing in one listing: Neelayadakshi,
  Karumkadamkanni, Ilangkombunaal (unreconciled; not used in prose).
- Place: Theerthanagiri, Via Alappakkam, Cuddalore district, PIN 608801; ~25 km from
  Chidambaram, ~27 km from Cuddalore town. 5th Shiva temple of the Nadu Nadu region in the
  Thevaram ordering.
- Hymnist: **Sundarar** (7th Tirumurai pathikam); his hymn names the place and deity in
  every verse — this is what establishes Paadal Petra status. Appar/Sambandar/Muyalagan
  links seen in some search snippets belong to OTHER temples (Tiruvattathurai, etc.) and
  were NOT attributed here.
- Legend (defining, but thin in sources): a devout farming couple remembered as **Periyan
  and his wife**, whose vow was to feed a servant of Shiva before taking their own meal;
  Shiva tested and blessed their steadfastness (a miraculous-harvest / never-failing-store
  motif). The full narrative was not found in detail — prose kept general. A Ramayana /
  Jambavan association is mentioned by one aggregator but was too thin to include.
- Architecture: east-facing **three-tiered rajagopuram**, gateway aligned with
  dwajasthambam, Nandi and balipeetam; swayambhu linga; koshta (niche) deities include
  Dakshinamurthi, Lingodbhava, Durga. A **Panguni solar alignment** (sun's rays reach the
  linga on days ~20–22 of Panguni) is noted by sources.
- Name gloss: "Sivakozhundu" ~ the tender shoot / sprout; glossed in prose as the Lord who
  is the tender shoot. (Interpretive, commonly given; not a hard source claim.)
- Festivals: no temple-specific festival calendar was found; prose kept to the general
  Shaiva observances (Maha Shivaratri, Pradosham, Margazhi, Chithirai) plus the Panguni
  solar days. Verify locally.
- Access: Cuddalore–Chidambaram buses via Alappakkam; nearest railheads Chidambaram and
  Virudhachalam; nearest airports Puducherry and Tiruchirappalli.

- **Not verified:** exact temple coordinate (used **town-level ~11.39, 79.45**, estimated
  from the Alappakkam/Chidambaram-corridor region — sources gave conflicting PIN-centroid
  points: Alapakkam S.O ~11.57/79.72, Alapakkam village Wikipedia ~11.37/79.43, and an
  indiamapia point ~12.66/79.99 that is clearly wrong; refine against a mapping source);
  consort name (brief said Sivakami, sources say Oppila Nayaki — used the sourced name);
  founding dynasty/century of the structural temple (medieval Chola-era patrons assumed;
  century 8 used for the Sundarar/Tevaram era); full Periyan legend narrative; festival
  dates (Tamil calendar); current timings.
