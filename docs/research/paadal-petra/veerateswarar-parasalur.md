# Keelaparasalur — Veerateswarar Temple (Ashta Veerattanam)

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Veerateswarar** (Daksha Samhara Murti); goddess **Ilam Kobanayal** (also Balambika). Alt temple name **Dakshapureeswarar**; place also Dakshapuram.
- **Location:** Keelaparasalur (Parasalur / Tirupparialur), ~13 km from Mayiladuthurai, Mayiladuthurai district, Tamil Nadu. Approx 11.0531 N, 79.75 E (approximate).
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam (Tevaram, Kaveri Then Karai; Sambandar) AND one of the **Ashta Veerattanam** — this one for the **destruction of Daksha's sacrifice**.
- **Legend:** Daksha denied Shiva his due at the yagna; Shiva's fierce aspect ended the sacrifice and chastened Daksha (later reconciled).
