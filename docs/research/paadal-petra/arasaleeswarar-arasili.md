# Arasaleeswarar (Arasilinathar) Temple, Thiru Arasili (arasaleeswarar-arasili)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Thondai Nadu (Tevaram name: Tiruvarasili, திருவரசிலி; counted as the 31st Thondai Nadu shrine). Tradition: Shaivism.

- Deity: Shiva as Arasaleeswarar / Arasilinathar (Lord of the arasu / peepal tree), small
  swayambhu linga worshipped under a canopy ("pandal") of 108 rudraksha beads; consort
  Periyanayaki in a separate shrine.
- Sung by Tirugnanasambandar (Sambandar) in the Tevaram (per Dinamalar / shivatemples).
- Location: village **Ozhindiyapattu (Ozhindhiampattu / Olunthiyapattu)**, Viluppuram district
  (near Puducherry), ~16 km from Puducherry on the Puducherry–Kilianur–Tindivanam road;
  a branch road runs ~2 km east from the main-road junction. Kilianur ~8 km, Irumbai
  Mahakalam ~8 km, Tiruvakkarai ~27 km.
- Coordinates: **12.0525, 79.7728** (12°03′09″N 79°46′22″E, from Wikipedia). NOTE: the seed
  hint (11.90, 79.55) was wrong (it is near Viluppuram town); Wikipedia's figure is
  consistent with the "16 km from Puducherry" description. Single-source; refine if possible.
- Sthala vriksham: arasu (peepal). Parikara sthalam for Pushya (Poosam) natal star (one source).
- Legends:
  1. Sage Vamadeva stood under an arasu tree longing for Shiva's grace; Shiva appeared as a
     swayambhu linga. Name "Arasili" from the tree.
  2. Childless Chalukya king Satyavardhan made a flower garden; blossoms kept vanishing;
     he saw a deer, shot an arrow; the linga was found bleeding; Shiva revealed he was the
     deer and granted the king children. The arrow scar remains on the linga and is covered
     with cloth during pooja.
  3. Folk etymology of Ozhindiyapattu: "ozhinthu" (lost) + "agapattathu" (regained).
- Architecture: three-tiered gopuram, one prakaram, ~2.5 acres (shivatemples/Dinamalar; other
  sources say ~1 acre — content says "roughly two acres"). Original Chola foundation;
  present masonry believed by 16th-century chieftains.
- Inscriptions: five inscriptions of medieval Cholas (Vikrama Chola, Kulothunga Chola) reported;
  texts not located.
- Festivals: 10-day Vaikasi Brahmotsavam (May–June) is the main one; Maha Shivaratri
  (Feb–Mar); Tirukarthigai (Nov–Dec); Pradosham.
- Timings per sources: ~6:30–12:00 and 4:30–19:30 (one source: 6:00–19:00); sources differ.
- Access: Puducherry (~16 km) is the nearest major town; few shops/eateries near temple.
- **Not verified:** the exact temple coordinate (single source); acreage (1 vs ~2.5 acres);
  exact inscription texts and dates; Satyavardhan's dynasty (source says Chalukya; not used
  in prose); gopuram/shrine details beyond three-tier gopuram; Poosam festival association
  (single source); nearest railway station and distance; current timings; whether Sundarar/
  Appar also sang here (sources say only Sambandar); Thondai Nadu river boundaries (general
  knowledge, not from sources).
