# Tiruvusathanam (Kovilur) — Mandirapureeswarar Temple

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Mandirapureeswarar** ("lord of counsel/mandira"); goddess **Periyanayaki** (also **Brihannayaki**, "great mother"). (Worklist goddess "Periyanayaki" — confirmed.)
- **Location:** Kovilur (Tevaram **Tiruvusathanam / Tiru Usaatthanam**), ~3 km N of Muthupettai on the Mannargudi road, ~22 km from Pattukottai; district varies **Tiruvarur / Thanjavur**, Tamil Nadu. ~10.4300 N, 79.5000 E (approximate, near Muthupettai).
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam — hymned by Tirugnana Sambandar; Kaveri (southern) delta.
- **Legend:** name = "place of consultation" — **Rama** sought Shiva's **counsel** here before building the Setu bridge to Lanka → Mandirapureeswarar.
- **Festivals:** Maha Shivaratri (Masi); Navaratri (Purattasi); Karthikai Mondays; Pradosham.
