# Tiruthalaichangadu — Sankaranyeswarar Temple

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Sankaranyeswarar** (worshipped in the **Somaskanda** form); goddess **Soundara Nayaki** (alt. **Bruhath Sundarambikai**). (Worklist drafted goddess "Soundaranayaki?" — confirmed.)
- **Location:** Thalaichangadu, Tharangambadi taluk, ~22 km from Mayiladuthurai, ~16 km from Sirkazhi, near Poompuhar; Mayiladuthurai district (older listings Nagapattinam), Tamil Nadu. ~11.1244 N, 79.7944 E (village; Wikipedia).
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam — hymned by Tirugnana Sambandar; Kaveri south bank (listed ~45th on south bank). Traditionally a Kochengat Chola foundation.
- **Legend:** **Vishnu** worshipped Shiva here and received his conch **Panchajanya** (sangu) → name Thalai-changadu ("head of the conch"); tank **Sangu Theertham**; sthala vriksham **purasu**. Puranam linked to Valampuranathar, Melaperumpallam; **Thalai Sanga Nanmathiyam** Divya Desam <1 km away.
- **Festivals:** Maha Shivaratri (Masi); Navaratri (Purattasi); Karthikai Mondays; Pradosham.
