# Adipureeswarar (Thyagaraja Swamy / Vadivudai Amman) Temple, Thiruvottiyur (adipureeswarar-tiruvottiyur)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Thondai Nadu (Tevaram name: Tiruvottiyur / Tiruvorriyur, திருவொற்றியூர்). Tradition: Shaivism.

- Deity: Shiva as **Adipureeswarar** (also Padampakkanathar, Thiruvottrieswarar; festival form
  **Thyagaraja** / Ananda Thyagesar); goddess **Vadivudai Amman** (Tripurasundari; Gnana Shakti,
  one of the three Tri-Shakti goddess temples of Chennai with Thiruvudai Amman at Melur and
  Kodiyidai Amman at Thirumullaivoyil). Separate Vattaparai Amman shrine/festival.
- Tevaram: sung by **all three saints, Appar, Sambandar and Sundarar** (one source counts 8
  pathigams) and also by Pattinathar. Counted among the Thondai Nadu Tevaram shrines.
- Coordinates: **~13.161, 80.299** (Wikipedia: 13°09′39″N 80°17′57″E). Temple on Thiruvottiyur
  High Road near Therady bus stop, north Chennai.
- Name/legend of the lingam: the first self-formed (swayambhu) lingam, the "Adi" lingam, appearing
  after the great dissolution; anthill form kept under a kavacham (armour), which is removed for
  three days from the Karthigai full moon so devotees see the natural anthill form (one source).
- Defining legend (Sundarar): already married to Paravai at Tiruvarur, he fell in love with
  Sangili Nachiyar at Tiruvottiyur; to win her trust he vowed not to leave her; asked Shiva to
  stand beneath the magizha tree (Shiva said to have hidden behind it, so the vow was truly to
  the Lord); on leaving, he lost his sight (restored later at other shrines). Re-enacted as
  **Makizhadi Sevai** on the 9th day of the Masi Brahmotsavam.
- Architecture: Dravidian; Pallava and Chola association; two tanks (Brahma Theertham, Adisesha
  Theertham); Thyagaraja shrine (dancing form, Ananda Thyagesar); magizha tree and wedding
  mandapam; Vattaparai Amman shrine.
- Festivals: Masi Brahmotsavam (10–11 days, Feb–Mar; procession before dawn, Nandi Nadanam on
  Makam new-moon day); Karthigai Pournami kavacham removal; Vattaparai Amman festival (Chithirai);
  Vasantha utsavam, Maha Shivaratri, Navaratri, Thai Poosam, Aadi Pooram, Skanda Shashti,
  Arudra darshan, Pradosham, Pournami days (Wikipedia list).
- Access: Thiruvottiyur High Road, Therady stop, north Chennai; city buses; Chennai suburban
  railway (Thiruvottiyur station, from general knowledge); Chennai airport across the city.
- Timings: pooja schedule Ushathkala 6:00, Kalasanthi 8:00, Uchikala 12:00, Sayaraksha 18:00
  (OneIndia); opening/closing hours not confirmed.
- **Not verified:** the "sand-lingam" legend in the brief (not found; the researched lingam story
  is the self-formed anthill Adi lingam instead); "Padampakka Nayaki" as the goddess's name (sources
  give goddess as Vadivudai Amman; Padampakkanathar is the Lord's name); Sangili's social
  background; exact dating and builders of the structural temple (Pallava/Chola per Wikipedia;
  a blog's "2000 years old" claim not used); gopuram heights; Kavacham dates beyond "three days
  from Karthigai full moon"; Aippasi Annabishekam (not found); Vattaparai Amman festival month
  (one source: Chithirai); distance to airport; Thiruvottiyur railway station (not in sources);
  precise opening hours; the Vadivudai Amman "brother's wife feeds a devotee" story (omitted).
