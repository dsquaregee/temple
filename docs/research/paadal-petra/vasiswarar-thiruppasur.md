# Vasiswarar (Vaseeswarar / Vacheeswarar) Temple, Thiruppasur (Thiruppachur) — research notes

Master-list row 254 (Thondai Nadu). Row facts confirmed: deity Vaseeswarar / Vacheeswarar (also Pasoornathar); consort Pasupathinayaki (Wikipedia) with Thangadhali (Thangaathali) — Dinamalar describes Thangadhali's shrine to the right of the Lord as the wedding form of the divine couple; content names both. Singers Appar and Sambandar (tntemplesproject: Appar 2 pathigams, Sambandar 1; one listing adds Sundarar — not confirmed, omitted). No correction.

- Place: Thiruppachur / Thirupasoor village, Tiruvallur district, about 4–5 km from Tiruvallur town on the Tiruvallur–Tiruttani road. 16th Thondai Nadu sthalam (shivatemples tn16).
- Name: "pachu/pasu" = bamboo; the area was a bamboo forest.
- Legend (Hindupedia, tntemplesproject): a cow seen pouring milk over a spot (a snake mound) in the bamboo forest; the spot was dug and a lingam found; a "vaasi" (axe-like tool) struck it and left a scar — hence Vaaseeswarar. Karikala Chola credited with building the temple after the discovery. Tradition: the king was attacked by a serpent sent by his enemies (Kurumbar chiefs) and saved after praying to Shiva — content keeps it neutral, no sectarian framing.
- Thangadhali: the goddess worshipped Shiva here and joined him to bless devotees (Hindupedia).
- Chola-era inscriptions present (tntemplesproject). Wikipedia: "southern bank of the Poondi reservoir" — doubtful geographically for a site 5 km W of Tiruvallur; omitted.
- century: 7 (Appar, Sambandar).
- Coordinates: 13.1408, 79.8761 — Wikipedia "Vaseeswarar Temple" infobox (13°08′27″N 79°52′34″E), matched by Bharatpedia mirror and AroundUs. Distinct from the Veeraraghava Perumal Divya Desam in Tiruvallur town (~79.91 E).
