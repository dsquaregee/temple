# Tarukavaneswarar (Paraithurainathar) Temple, Tirupparaithurai (tarukavaneswarar-tirupparaithurai)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Chola Nadu (south of the Kaveri). District: Tiruchirappalli
(Srirangam taluk; see discrepancy below). Tradition: Shaivism.

- Deity: Shiva as **Tarukavaneswarar / Paraithurainathar** (lingam); consort **Pasumpon
  Mayilambikai**, also called **Hemavarnambikai**.
- Tevaram name: **Tirupparaithurai** (திருப்பராய்த்துறை). Hymns of **Sambandar** and **Appar**
  (shivatemples.com lists one padigam each; Sundarar not listed). Dinamalar also lists
  Arunagirinathar (Tiruppugazh). Described as one of the Tevaram temples on the southern bank
  of the Kaveri.
- Coordinates: **village-level ~10.878, 78.563** (Bharatpedia gives 10°52′41″N 78°33′47″E; India
  Post office for PIN 639115 ~10.8826, 78.5676). Exact temple point not found — refine against
  a mapping source.
- Name: from the **parai** tree (one source: Streblus asper; another: Salvadora persica —
  botanical identity unresolved; prose says only "parai tree"). Kaveri here is honoured as
  **Akanda Kaveri**; it divides ~2 km away into Kaveri and Kollidam.
- Defining legend: the **Taruka (Daruka) forest** sages — purva-mimamsakas who believed Vedic
  ritual alone was supreme — were humbled when Shiva came as **Bhikshatana** with Vishnu as
  **Mohini**; the sages sent a snake, a tiger and an elephant against Shiva, who overcame each.
  One source calls this the starting point of the Bhikshatana story. Local belief: pouring water
  at the foot of the sacred parai tree behind the sanctum relieves illness (sources say cancer or
  skin ailments; prose says "illness, especially of the skin").
- Architecture: Dravidian (Wikipedia infobox). Sanctum with linga; shrines to Vinayaka, Murugan,
  Mahalakshmi and Durga. Sources conflict on the tree: one says the linga was found among parai
  trees and the temple built around it; another says the linga lies beneath the sacred tree in
  the outer prakaram behind the sanctum — prose states only that the tree grows behind the
  sanctum. Chola-era inscriptions are mentioned.
- Festivals: no temple-specific festival found. Prose uses Maha Shivaratri and monthly Pradosham
  (universal), plus the regional Aadi Perukku river festival (general Kaveri-bank observance, not
  attributed to a temple ceremony).
- Access: Trichy–Karur main road, ~16 km from Chatram bus stand, Trichy; Tirupparaithurai bus stop
  near the temple. Trichy–Palakkad line passes through the village area (Elamanur station);
  Tiruchirappalli Junction and airport are the main hubs. Reported timings ~6:00–11:30 and
  4:30–8:30 (other sites give different hours; phone +91 99408 43571 appears on one listing).
- **Corrections to seed hint:** none of substance. Goddess confirmed as Pasumpon Mayilambikai
  (Hemavarnambikai).
- **Not verified:** district (Wikipedia's temple page says Karur district; tntemplesproject,
  Bharatpedia and PIN records say Tiruchirappalli / Srirangam taluk — Tiruchirappalli used);
  gopuram form and tiers; whether the goddess shrine faces south; founding dynasty / structural
  century (Tevaram era used for century 7); the exact distance to a railway station and
  airport; current timings; named annual festivals; Arunagirinathar's hymn (Dinamalar claim).
