# Maragathachaleswarar (Eengoinathar) Temple, Tiruveengoimalai (maragathachaleswarar-tiruveengoimalai)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Chola Nadu (north of the Kaveri; Tevaram name: Thiruvengoimalai / Eengoimalai). District: Tiruchirappalli, Tamil Nadu. Tradition: Shaivism.

- Deity: Shiva as Maragathachaleswarar (Eengoinathar); consort Marakathavalli (also spelled
  Maragathavalli / Maragathambikai in sources).
- Tevaram: one decad (padigam) by Sambandar.
- Coordinates: **approximate ~11.003, 78.447** (hill near Musiri/Kulithalai). Exact temple point
  not found in sources — refine against a mapping source when possible.
- Legend (the temple's defining story): Vayu (wind) and Adisesha contested who was greater;
  Adisesha coiled round the cosmic mountain and Vayu's whirlwind broke fragments loose that
  fell in eight places. The fragment that fell here was an emerald and became the Maragatha
  (emerald) linga. Name legend: Sage Agastya, arriving late in the evening after worshipping at
  Kulithalai and Ayyarmalai (Iyermalai), became a fly by yogic power to reach the summit
  ("eengoi" ~ fly). Note: some web summaries render this as a bee; the entry follows the more
  common "fly" reading. Also associated: Siddha Bogar and king Musukunda Chakravarti.
- Architecture: small rocky hill, east-facing; ~500 steep, uneven rock-cut steps; three-tiered
  (unfinished) entrance gopuram; green-toned linga under a three-tiered vimana; Marakathavalli in
  her own shrine; Vinayaka and Murugan with consorts near the entrance arch.
- Festivals: Thai Poosam; Masi Magam; Panguni Brahmotsavam; Maha Shivaratri (local accounts of
  sunlight on the deity around Masi Shivaratri); monthly Pradosham. Traditional pilgrimage
  sequence: Kulithalai (morning), Iyermalai (afternoon), Tiruveengoimalai (evening), especially
  on a Monday in Karthigai.
- Access: ~7 km from Musiri (town bus); Musiri ~43 km from Tiruchirappalli; also reachable from
  Kulithalai across the Kaveri. Tiruchirappalli Junction nearest main railway; Tiruchirappalli
  International Airport (~50 km).
- **Not verified:** founding dynasty/date of the stone temple (Chola-era rebuilding is a general
  attribution; century 7 reflects the Sambandar era); exact temple coordinate; step count (~500,
  approximate); sunlight-on-deity claim (local accounts only); timings (sources disagree:
  shivatemples.com gives roughly 10:00-12:00 and 16:30-18:00, Dinamalar/Oneindia 09:00-18:00).
