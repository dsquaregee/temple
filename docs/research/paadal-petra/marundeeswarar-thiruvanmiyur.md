# Marundeeswarar Temple, Thiruvanmiyur (marundeeswarar-thiruvanmiyur)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Thondai Nadu (Tevaram name: Tiruvanmiyur; one source numbers it
25th in Thondai Nadu). Tradition: Shaivism.

- Deity: Shiva as **Marundeeswarar** ("Lord of Medicine"); other names: Palvannanathar,
  Vanmikanathar, Vedapureeswarar, Amudheeswarar. Goddess **Tripurasundari** (Thri-Pura-Sundari).
  Sthala vriksha: **Vanni**. Swayambhu linga, **west-facing**.
- Tevaram: sung by **Sambandar and Appar** (Tirunavukkarasar). (Sources that credit only some
  saints vary; the specific verses/decads were not located.)
- Location: Thiruvanmiyur, south Chennai, East Coast Road; western 5-tier gopuram is the main
  entrance beside Thiruvanmiyur bus terminus. Coordinates **12.9856, 80.2614** (Wikipedia
  12°59′08″N 80°15′41″E).
- Legends (all healing-related): (1) **Agastya** suffered severe stomach pain; Shiva appeared under
  the Vanni tree and taught him the medicinal herbs → "Marundeeswarar" (marundhu = medicine).
  (2) **Valmiki** worshipped Shiva here and stayed; place = Thiruvalmikiyur → Thiruvanmiyur;
  Valmiki shrine on the west side. (3) **Kamadhenu**, cursed by Vasishta, was freed by
  worshipping the swayambhu linga; milk whitened the linga → "Palvannanathar" (or, in another
  version, fleeing captors she struck the linga with her hooves; marks visible on the linga).
  (4) Linga said to have cured a curse upon **Indra**.
- Architecture: seven-tiered east rajagopuram and five-tiered west gopuram (sources disagree:
  one says "two seven-tiered gopurams and a huge tank"; another seven-tier east + five-tier west
  + five-tier inner); large mandapam with **36 carved pillars** housing Somaskanda; Tripurasundari
  shrine in the eastern outer prakaram; large tank; Vanni tree.
- Date: sources conflict — Chola masonry 9th c. (+ Vijayanagara additions), Chola
  patronage/expansion 11th c., or 16th-c. rebuilding by a Tuluva chieftain; Pallava-era origin;
  Rajendra Chola I visits 1012–1044 CE (one source, from inscriptions). Century set to **7**
  (Appar/Sambandar era); the dynasty line records the conflicting claims generically (the
  16th-c. Tuluva claim not used).
- Festivals: **Panguni Brahmotsavam** (Mar–Apr; ceremony beneath the Vanni tree on day 9),
  Maha Shivaratri (Feb–Mar), Skanda Sashti (Oct–Nov), Vinayaka Chaturthi, full-moon and
  Kiruthigai days.
- Access: Thiruvanmiyur bus terminus on ECR; autos/cabs.
- Timings found (conflicting): 7:00–12:00 and 17:00–21:00; 5:00–12:00 and 16:00–21:00;
  6:00–12:30 and 16:30–20:30. Content says "confirm locally".
- **Not verified:** exact temple coordinate beyond Wikipedia's; construction date/dynasty (see
  above); number and arrangement of gopurams; **Bhringi** association (no source found —
  omitted); a monthly Pradosham observance (no source — not asserted); the tank's name and any
  Theppam (float) festival (not found — omitted); the Tevaram verse text; the Panguni
  Brahmotsavam length and the nature of the day-9 Vanni-tree ceremony; Skanda Sashti /
  Vinayaka Chaturthi detail; the suburban railway station (Thiruvanmiyur MRTS) and the
  ~17 km airport distance are from general knowledge, not from the searched sources.
