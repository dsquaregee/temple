# Tiruvaimur — Vaimoornathar Temple

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Vaimoornathar** (Vaaimoornaathar), a **swayambhu** lingam; goddess **Paalin Nanmozhiyal** (Ksheeropa Vasani). (Worklist goddess "Paalin Nanmozhiyal" — confirmed by TN Temples Project.)
- **Location:** Tiruvaimur, near **Tirukkuvalai**, off the Nagapattinam–Tiruthuraipoondi road; lower Kaveri delta. ~10°37′N 79°43′E (≈10.6167, 79.7167, Wikipedia).
- **District:** Wikipedia lists **Tiruvarur district**; TN Temples Project lists **Nagapattinam**. Worklist used Nagapattinam; the district boundary runs nearby. Noted in body.
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam — Kaveri south bank, counted **124th**; tied in tradition to **Sundarar**. Hymned in the Tevaram.
- **Festivals:** Maha Shivaratri (Masi); Navaratri (Purattasi); Karthikai Mondays; Pradosham.
