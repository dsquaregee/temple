# Semponarkoil — Swarnapureeswarar Temple

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Swarnapureeswarar** ("golden-hued"), swayambhu lingam said to sparkle like gold.
- **Location:** Semponarkoil (Tiruchemponpalli; a.k.a. Sembanarkoil), near Mayiladuthurai, Nagapattinam/Mayiladuthurai district, Tamil Nadu. Approx 11.1233 N, 79.72 E (approximate).
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam — sung by Sambandar and Appar.
- **Legends/names:** **Lakshmipuri** (Mahalakshmi's penance), **Indirapuri** (Indra won the vajra vs Vruddhasura), **Kandapuri** (Murugan before Tharakasura); also where **Dakshayani** worshipped before Daksha's yagna.
- **Epigraphy:** Jyeshta Devi iconography → renovation under **Aditya Chola I**; inscriptions of Kulothunga III, Rajadhiraja I, Serfoji.
- **Distinct** from Andarkoil Swarnapureeswarar (Tiruvarur), which is a separate temple (also in the worklist).
