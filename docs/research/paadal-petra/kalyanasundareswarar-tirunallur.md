# Tirunallur — Kalyanasundareswarar Temple

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Kalyanasundareswarar**; goddess **Girisundari** (Parvatasundari / Kalyanasundari). (Master-list goddess "Girisundari" — confirmed.)
- **Location:** **Tirunallur (Nallur)**, near Papanasam, ~16 km from Kumbakonam; Thanjavur district (Valangaiman taluk; some sources Tiruvarur). ~10.93 N, 79.27 E (approximate).
- **Note:** distinct from **Sivalokathyagesar at Tirunallur Perumanam** (Chola-north) and **Kalyanasundareswarar at Tiruvelvikudi** (Chola-north) — both already in the catalog.
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam (20th, Kaveri south) — hymned by **Sambandar and Appar**. Linked to **Amaraneethi Nayanar** and sage Agastya.
- **Festivals:** Maha Shivaratri (Masi); Navaratri (Purattasi); Tiruvadhirai (Margazhi); Karthikai Mondays; Pradosham.
