# Vedapureeswarar Temple, Thiruverkadu (vedapureeswarar-thiruverkadu)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Thondai Nadu (Tevaram name: Thiruverkadu / திருவேற்காடு). Tradition: Shaivism.

- Deity: Shiva as Vedapureeswarar (lingam); consort the goddess Balambigai (Balambikai).
- Sung in the Tevaram: Tirugnana Sambandar (two padigams) and Appar (two padigams), 7th
  century — which makes it a Paadal Petra Sthalam of the Thondai country.
- Coordinates: **13.0714, 80.1147** (Wikipedia, 13°04′17″N 80°06′53″E; town-level, Thiruverkadu).
- Defining legends (two):
  1. **Agastya and the wedding vision.** When Shiva wed Parvati in the Himalayas, the whole
     world is said to have gathered to the north, and the sage Agastya was sent south to keep
     the earth in balance; so he could not witness the marriage. He prayed to Shiva here, and
     the Lord granted him a vision of himself and Parvati in their wedding form (Tirumana
     Kolam). A sculpture of the divine couple as bride and groom stands behind the sanctum.
  2. **The four Vedas as trees.** The four Vedas are said to have taken the form of trees (a
     grove/forest) to worship Shiva at this place — the source of the name "Verkadu"
     (sacred grove) and of the deity's name Vedapureeswarar ("Lord of the city of the Vedas").
- Sthala virutcham (temple tree): the Velvela (Vela) tree. Theertham: Veda Theertham (one
  source also names Velayudha Theertham).
- Architecture: east-facing rajagopuram (sources differ — five tiers per shivatemples.com,
  seven per Wikipedia), three prakaras (corridors), and an apsidal **Gajaprishta vimana**
  over the sanctum. Complex ~2 acres (one source ~1.5 acres). Reconstructed in 1973 under
  the technical direction of G. Yegneswaran; administered by the Tamil Nadu HR&CE Department.
- Festivals: four annual festivals; the chief is the Brahmotsavam in the Tamil month of
  Magam (Feb–Mar); Maha Shivaratri and the monthly Pradosham are observed.
- Access: Thiruverkadu, Tiruvallur district, in the western suburbs of Chennai (~18–20 km
  from the city centre); well served by Chennai suburban buses; nearest major railheads at
  Avadi / Ambattur / Chennai; Chennai International Airport ~25 km.
- Note on the town: Thiruverkadu is today widely known for the **Karumariamman temple**; the
  **Vedapureeswarar Shiva temple is the older shrine** and the one counted among the Tevaram
  Paadal Petra Sthalams — this entry's subject.
- **Not verified:** exact gopuram tier count (5 vs 7 across sources); founding dynasty/century
  of the structural temple (Chola origin attributed but marked "citation needed"; century 7
  used for the Tevaram era); exact temple-point coordinate (town-level used); current timings.
