# Padanjalinathar (Patanjaleeswarar) Temple, Tirukkanattumullur / Kanattampuliyur (padanjalinathar-tirukkanattumullur)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Chola Nadu (north of the Kaveri; listed on the northern bank, 32nd of that group by one count; counted as the 86th of the 276). District: Cuddalore (Kattumannarkoil taluk, Muttam post, PIN 608306). Tradition: Shaivism.

- Deity: Shiva as Padanjalinathar / Pathanchalinathar / Patanjaleeswarar; consort Kola Valai Kai Ambicai
  (also given as Kanaar-Kuzhali / Kanarkuzhali, Ambujakshi). The Tamil rendering of the goddess's
  name in the app (கோலவளைக்கை அம்பிகை) is a transliteration of the English-source spelling.
- Tevaram name: **Tirukkanattumullur** (திருக்கானாட்டுமுள்ளூர்). Sung by **Sundarar** (pathigam in the
  7th Tirumurai). No Sambandar/Appar hymn found. Wikipedia and shivatemples.com agree.
- Setting: shivatemples.com says northern bank of the **Kollidam**, ~9 km from Kattumannarkoil;
  Wikipedia says northern bank of the Kaveri (Kollidam is the Kaveri's northern branch, so the
  two are compatible). Content uses "northern bank of the Kollidam."
- Coordinates: **approximate ~11.23, 79.58** (derived from "~9 km from Kattumannarkoil", whose
  Wikipedia coordinate is 11.2727, 79.5555, and a position near the Kollidam). **No sourced
  coordinate found for Kanattampuliyur/Muttam — refine against OSM/Google before relying on it.**
- Defining legend: the sage **Patanjali** (Adisesha incarnate) was shown Shiva's cosmic dance at
  Chidambaram, then came here and begged to see it again; Shiva appeared and danced for him
  here, hence "Padanjalinathar." Dinamalar adds that Patanjali performed penance here on
  Shiva's advice. Seed hint "Patanjali worship" confirmed and sharpened to the second-vision story.
- Nava Puliyur link: Wikipedia lists this among the Nava Puliyur temples (Patanjali and
  Vyaghrapada); tntemplesproject argues against tying it to Vyaghrapada. The content mentions the
  puliyur name link lightly and attributes the temple chiefly to Patanjali.
- Architecture: east-facing, three-tiered rajagopuram; **Surya Pushkarini** tank outside the gateway;
  sthala vriksham **erukku**; Nataraja with left leg forward and body slightly back (Dinamalar);
  image of Patanjali and the Nalvar facing the main sanctum (Dinamalar). Classified Dravidian.
- Festivals (bharattemples/Wikipedia listing): Maha Shivaratri (Feb–Mar), Tirukarthikai (Nov–Dec),
  Aipasi Annabhishekam (Oct–Nov), Tiruvadhirai (Dec–Jan); monthly Pradosham added as general practice.
- Access: Muttam village ~2 km from the temple, served by buses from Chidambaram and
  Kattumannarkoil; ~9 km from Kattumannarkoil. Listing timings ~7:00–9:00 and 6:00–7:30
  (may have changed), so content gives general guidance only.
- Century: used **8** because the only Tevaram hymn is Sundarar's (traditionally 8th century), as in
  the Avinashiappar exemplar.
- **Not verified:** founding dynasty and century of the structural temple (one listing says only
  "1000–2000 years old"; content says "Chola and later" generically); exact temple coordinate;
  current timings; pathigam number/verse text; specifics of the goddess's shrine (position,
  gopuram); the exact nearest railhead distance (Chidambaram used as the practical railhead,
  distance not sourced); nearest airports (Puducherry/Tiruchirappalli/Chennai given as general
  road-distance options, not sourced).
