# Ponsei (Tirunanipalli) — Natrunaiyappar Temple

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Natrunaiyappar** ("the good companion"); goddess **Malayaan Madanthai** (a second form **Parvatharaja Puthri** also honoured). (Worklist drafted goddess as "Mangalanayaki?" — that belongs to Tirumangalakudi; corrected here.)
- **Location:** Ponsei / Tirunanipalli, ~4 km from Sembanarkoil, ~13 km from Mayiladuthurai; Mayiladuthurai district (older listings Nagapattinam), Tamil Nadu. ~11.1307 N, 79.7596 E (Wikipedia).
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam — hymned by Appar, Sambandar and Sundarar; Kaveri south bank.
- **Legend:** birthplace of **Bhagavati**, mother of Tirugnana Sambandar; the child-saint's hymn said to have turned barren fields fertile.
- **Festivals:** Maha Shivaratri (Masi); Tirugnana Sambandar festival; Pradosham; Karthikai.
