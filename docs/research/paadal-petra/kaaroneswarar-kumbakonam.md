# Kumbakonam — Kaaroneswarar (Kudanthai Karonam)

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Kaaroneswarar / Kayarohaneswarar** — identified (by many devotees and the temple's priests) as the **Kudanthai Karonam** of the Tevaram, at the **Kasi Viswanathar temple**, Kumbakonam.
- **Location:** Kumbakonam town, near the Mahamaham tank, Thanjavur district, Tamil Nadu. Approx 10.9602 N, 79.3789 E (approximate).
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam — sung by Appar and Sambandar.
- **Identity dispute:** The Tevaram "Karonam" is claimed by BOTH this (Kasi Viswanathar) temple and the nearby **Someswarar** temple; both priesthoods maintain their own claim. (Someswarar-Pazhayarai in the worklist is a separate matter and remains deferred.)
- **Name:** Kayarohaneswarar ("embracing the lingam", kaya+arohana) → Karonam; also linked to Rama absorbing Rudra's essence.
- **Fabric:** Chola + Nayak; western gopuram ~72 ft, 7 storeys. Tied to the 12-yearly **Mahamaham** festival.
