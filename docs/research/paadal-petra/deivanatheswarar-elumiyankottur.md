# Deivanatheswarar (Deivanayaka Easwarar / Arambeswarar) Temple, Elumiyankottur (deivanatheswarar-elumiyankottur)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Thondai Nadu (Tevaram name given in brief: Tiruvilambaiyankottur).
Tradition: Shaivism.

- Deity: Shiva as **Deivanatheswarar** (Wikipedia: "Deivanayaka Easwarar"; shivatemples.com:
  "Deivanaatheswarar"), also called **Arambeswarar / Rambeswarar** and **Chandrasekarar**.
- Consort: **Kanakakujambikai** (Wikipedia; shivatemples.com spells it "Kanaka Kuchambikai").
  Note an older shivatemples listing gave the goddess as "Kodenthu Mulaiammai" — flag below.
- One of the 275/276 **Paadal Petra Sthalams**, near **Perambakkam**, Tiruvallur district (some
  listings place it under Kanchipuram district — see flags). Praised by **Tirugnanasambandar**
  (Pathigam "Sambandar - 1"). Sambandar is traditionally 7th c. CE — **used century 7**.
- Legends (two, both from popular temple guides):
  1. **Rambha legend** (the sthala's name story): Rambha, foremost of the apsaras, did long
     penance before the Lord seeking the boon of **eternal youth and beauty**; the pleased Lord
     granted it and became **Arambeswarar/Rambeswarar**; the village was called **Rambaikottur**,
     which over time became **Elambayankottur → Elumiyankottur**.
  2. **Tripura Samhara** tie: Shiva's destruction of the three asura cities (gold/silver/iron)
     with one arrow, associated with this site as with others in the region.
  - Also: tradition that **Shiva appeared to the child-saint Sambandar here as an old man** and
    commanded him to sing.
- Sacred features: **sthala vriksham = jasmine (mullai/mallika)**; **temple tank = Mallika
  Pushkarini**; **cow puja** performed regularly (Wikipedia).
