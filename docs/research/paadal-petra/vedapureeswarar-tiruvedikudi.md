# Tiruvedikudi — Vedapureeswarar Temple

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Vedapureeswarar** (Vazhai Madu Nathar); goddess **Mangayarkarasi**. (Worklist draft goddess "Mangayarkarasi?" — confirmed.)
- **Location:** **Tiruvedikudi**, ~7 km from **Tiruvaiyaru**, ~11 km from Thanjavur; Thanjavur district. ~10.85639 N, 79.13722 E (Wikipedia exact).
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam — **Sambandar** (2 padigams) and **Appar** (2 padigams). The **4th of the Saptha Sthana** temples of Tiruvaiyaru (Chithirai festival). Sanctum in reversed configuration (goddess right, lord left). Legend: the Vedas did penance here to be purified.
- **Festivals:** Saptha Sthana (Chithirai); Maha Shivaratri (Masi); Navaratri (Purattasi); Karthikai Mondays; Pradosham.
