# Sangameswarar Temple, Bhavani (Thirunana) — sangameswarar-bhavani

Master-list row 209 (Kongu Nadu 1). Facts confirmed; no corrections.

- Place: Bhavani Kooduthurai, Bhavani town, Erode district, at the confluence of the Kaveri and Bhavani with the unseen Amudha (Amrita) river — the "Dakshina Triveni Sangamam". ~15 km from Erode.
- Deity: Sangameswarar; consort Vedanayaki. Tevaram name Thirunana.
- Tevaram: Sambandar — one pathigam (10 verses), 2nd Tirumurai. Also praised by Arunagirinathar in the Tiruppugazh. First Paadal Petra Sthalam of Kongu Nadu (shivatemples kn1).
- Complex also holds a shrine of Adikesava Perumal with Soundaravalli Thayar (Wikipedia; Dinamalar treats it as a separate listing).
- British collector William Garrow (Wikipedia: "William Karo", c. 1804) — tradition: saved from a collapsing bungalow after the goddess appeared in a dream; gifted an ivory cradle/cot bearing his signature (other accounts say a gold offering). Content mentions the ivory gift as "remembered" tradition.
- Festivals: Aadi Perukku (main festival per Erode district site); new-moon ancestral rites at the confluence.
- Coordinates: 11.4349, 77.6828 (Wikipedia article coordinates 11°26′5.6″N 77°40′58.1″E; Wikidata Q7417808 agrees within ~5″).
- Access: Erode Junction ~15 km; Coimbatore airport the nearest airport.
