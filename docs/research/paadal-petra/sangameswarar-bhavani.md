# Sangameswarar Temple, Bhavani (sangameswarar-bhavani)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Kongu Nadu (Tevaram name: Thirunana). Tradition: Shaivism.

- Deity: Shiva as Sangameswarar (swayambhu linga); consort Vedanayaki (Vedambikai).
- Site: Kooduthurai, Bhavani, Erode district — the confluence of the Kaveri and Bhavani with
  the mythical underground third stream (Amudha; called Agaya Gangai in some accounts and
  Saraswati in others). Called Dakshina Triveni Sangamam. Because the river flows north to
  south here, the spot is held especially holy for ancestral/last rites.
- Tevaram: praised by Sambandar as Thirunana; Arunagirinathar also sang it in the
  Tiruppugazh. Blogs count it the 3rd Kongu Nadu Tevaram sthalam (261st overall) — numbering
  varies by source, not used in the content.
- Legend (Kubera): on pilgrimage among Shiva shrines Kubera saw tiger, deer, cow, elephant
  and snake drinking together at the confluence; a heavenly voice told him to worship Shiva.
  Variant: when Kubera tried to lift the linga a jet of water (Amudha) burst out and joined
  the Bhavani and Kaveri. Also worshipped by Vishvamitra and Parasara. Folk claim of a cave
  link to Chidambaram (explicitly unverified tradition).
- Architecture: separate Vishnu shrine with consort Soundaravalli inside the complex;
  six daily services of worship. Compound wall attributed (local blog) to a Vijayanagara-era
  governor, Valarasa Wodeyar, under Krishnadevaraya. Very little verifiable architectural
  detail (towers, mandapas, orientation) was surfaced — content is deliberately general.
- Inscriptions (blog listing, incomplete): a 1640 CE record, a Vijayanagara-period record,
  an 18th-century Mysore Wodeyar-era record.
- 1804 colonial anecdote: a British collector (name varies: "William Garrow" / "Karo")
  reportedly offered a gold plate to the goddess, dated 11/01/1804. Wikipedia marks it
  "citation needed"; the content mentions it only as unconfirmed local memory.
- Festivals: annual Brahmotsavam in Chithirai (Apr–May) per one source; Maha Shivaratri,
  Pradosham (general); new-moon ancestral rites at the confluence (general, inferred from the
  last-rites sources).
- Coordinates: **town-level ~11.447, 77.684** (Wikipedia for Bhavani town: 11°26′50″N
  77°41′02″E; other sources 11.446–11.452, 77.679–77.693). Not the exact temple point —
  refine against a mapping source when possible.
- Access: ~15 km (one source 16 km) from Erode (nearest major railway junction); ~1 km from
  Bhavani bus stand; buses from Erode, Salem, Tiruppur, Coimbatore; Coimbatore airport
  ~95–105 km (sources differ).
- Timings (sources conflict): erode.nic.in 6:00–12:30 and 4:00–9:00; shivatemples.com 5:00–1:00
  and 4:00–8:00; Oneindia lists 4:00 AM–9:00 PM. Content says "confirm locally".
- **Not verified:** century/dynasty of the structural temple (century 7 used for the
  Tevaram/Sambandar era; dynasty kept general); gopuram/sanctum orientation and dimensions;
  whether the Vishnu shrine is Adikesava Perumal (one source says so, not confirmed); Aadi
  Amavasai specifics (no source surfaced); the Kubera/Amudha variants and the Chidambaram
  tunnel are tradition; the 1804 anecdote; exact temple coordinate; current timings. Sourcing
  is THIN on architecture — recommend human review.
