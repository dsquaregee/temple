# Mahalakshminathar (Mahalakshmeeswarar) Temple, Tirunindriyur (mahalakshminathar-tirunindriyur)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Chola Nadu (north of the Kaveri) (Tevaram name: Tirunindriyur, திருநின்றியூர்). Tradition: Shaivism.
District: Mayiladuthurai (Thirunindriyur village, Mayiladuthurai taluk; Dinamalar lists Nagapattinam, an older district name).

- Deity: Shiva as Mahalakshminathar / Mahalakshmeeswarar; also Lakshmipureeswarar, Jamadhagneesar, Parikeshwarar. Goddess **Loka Nayaki (Ulaga Nayaki)**.
- Tevaram: sung by all three — Sambandar (1 pathigam), Appar (1), Sundarar (2, one only partially surviving) per shivatemples.com; Wikipedia concurs that all three sang here.
- Coordinates: **approximate, village-level ~11.17, 79.69** (between Mayiladuthurai and Sirkazhi, ~7 km from Mayiladuthurai). No source gave coordinates; the Wikipedia/Thirunindravur hits are a different place near Chennai. Refine against a mapping source.
- Legends: (1) Mahalakshmi, consort of Vishnu, worshipped Shiva here (hence Mahalakshmeeswarar; the town name read as Tiru = Lakshmi + nindra + ur, "where Lakshmi stayed"). (2) Chola king travelling daily to Chidambaram: torches went out each night in the forest and relit after crossing; a buried linga was found and a temple built; Dinamalar derives the name from thiri (wick) + nindra (stopped). (3) Parashurama (relieved of matricide sin), Jamadagni, Indra, Agastya and Airavata also worshipped. The king in (2) is unnamed in sources.
- Architecture: Tevaram-age Maadakoil attributed to Kochchengat Chola (tradition, "believed to"); Nagarathar community renovation about a century ago gave the present layout; three-tier rajagopuram (Dinamalar); tank Neela Poigai (Dinamalar; shivatemples gives Neelamalar Poigai). Sanctum/goddess shrine orientation not verified.
- Festivals: Aani Tirumanjanam (Jun-Jul), Maha Shivaratri (Feb-Mar), Tirukarthikai (Nov-Dec) per Dinamalar; Pradosham generic.
- Access: ~7 km from Mayiladuthurai on the Sirkazhi road (one source says ~9 km); ~6.4 km from Vaitheeswarankoil; Mayiladuthurai nearest major railway; Tiruchirappalli nearest major airport (approximate). Timings in sources differ (6-11 / 4-8 vs 7-1 / 4-7:30).
- Corrections to seed hints: none contradicted; the "Vishnu connection" is Lakshmi's worship (Vishnu's consort), not a separate Vishnu shrine.
- **Not verified:** founding dynasty (used century 7 for the Tevaram era; Kochchengat Chola link is tradition only); gopuram/sanctum measurements; shrine orientations; exact coordinates; current timings; temple tank name variant; the king's identity in the torch legend.
