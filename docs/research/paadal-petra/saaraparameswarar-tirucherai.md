# Tirucherai — Saara Parameswarar Temple

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Saara Parameswarar** (Gnanaparameswarar; also Senneriyappar); goddess **Gnanavalli** (Gnanambikai). (Master-list goddess "Gnanavalli?" — confirmed.)
- **Location:** **Tirucherai**, near Kudavasal; Thanjavur district (some sources Tiruvarur). ~10.87 N, 79.47 E (approximate).
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam (95th, Kaveri south) — hymned by **Sambandar and Appar**. Legend: sage **Markandeya** installed the **Rnavimochana Lingeswarar** (debt-relief) here.
- **IMPORTANT distinctness:** this is the **Shiva** temple at Tirucherai — distinct from `saranatha-thirucherai` (the Saranatha Perumal Vishnu Divya Desam of the same town) and from the catalog's Gnanaparameswarar temple at Tirumeignanam.
- **Festivals:** Maha Shivaratri (Masi); Annabishekam (Aippasi); Tiruvadhirai (Margazhi); Navaratri (Purattasi); Karthikai Mondays; Pradosham.
