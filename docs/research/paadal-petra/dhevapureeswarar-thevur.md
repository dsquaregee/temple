# Thevur — Deva Pureeswarar Temple

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Deva Pureeswarar** (Dhevapureeswarar; also **Devaguru Nathar**, **Kathalivanesar** "lord of the plantain forest"); goddess **Madhurabashini** (also **Thenmozhiyaal**, "sweet-spoken"). (Worklist drafted goddess "Madhuramozhi Ammai?" — corrected to the sourced names.)
- **Location:** Thevur (Devur), 4 km south of Kizhvelur on the Nagapattinam–Tiruvarur road, ~16 km from Nagapattinam; Tiruvarur district (some listings Nagapattinam), Tamil Nadu. ~10.7259 N, 79.7314 E (Wikipedia).
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam — hymned by Tirugnana Sambandar (2nd Thirumurai); Kaveri south bank.
- **Legend:** the **devas** worshipped Shiva here to be freed of the sin of slaying an asura (Vritra/Vruddhasura) → name Thevur ("city of the devas"), lord Deva Pureeswarar.
- **Festivals:** Maha Shivaratri (Masi); Navaratri (Purattasi); Karthikai Mondays; Pradosham.
