# Tiruvarur — Thoovainathar Temple (Arur Paravaiyunmandali)

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Thoovainathar** (also Thoovanayanar); goddess **Panchin Mennadiyal** (Sanskrit **Mridupada Nayaki**, "cotton-soft feet"). (Worklist left goddess "?" — supplied.)
- **Location:** Tiruvarur East Street, within Tiruvarur town, Tiruvarur district, Tamil Nadu. Historical name **Arur Paravaiyunmandali** (the mansion-quarter of **Paravai Nachiyar**, wife of Sundarar). ~10.7730 N, 79.6400 E (town-level, approximate).
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam — hymned by Tirugnana Sambandar; Kaveri south bank (~89th on south bank by one count).
- **Association:** historical name ties the shrine to the Sundarar–Paravai story of Tiruvarur.
- **Festivals:** Maha Shivaratri (Masi); Navaratri (Purattasi); Karthikai Mondays; Pradosham.
