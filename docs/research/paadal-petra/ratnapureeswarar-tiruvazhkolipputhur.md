# Ratnapureeswarar (Manickavannar) Temple, Tiruvazhkolipputhur (ratnapureeswarar-tiruvazhkolipputhur)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Chola Nadu (north of the Kaveri). District: Mayiladuthurai
(Mayiladuthurai taluk; older sources/Dinamalar say Nagapattinam). Tradition: Shaivism.
Tevaram name: **Tiruvazhkolipputhur** (திருவாழ்கொளிபுத்தூர்); modern/Wikipedia spelling
**Tiruvalaputhur** (Manickavannar Temple). PIN 609205. Not to be confused with the Rathnapureeswarar
Temple at Thirunattiyathangudi (Tiruvarur district), a different shrine.

- Deity: Shiva as **Manickavannar** / **Ratnapureeswarar** (Manicka Vannar, Rathna Pureeswarar).
  Consort: **Vandamar Poonguzhali** (Dinamalar also gives "Brahma Kunthalambica"). "Vandamar
  poonguzhali" = she whose flowered tresses bees settle upon (Tamil gloss, used in prose).
- Sung by: **Sambandar and Sundarar** (Wikipedia, Dinamalar, shivatemples).
- Sacred tree: **vaagai** (Dinamalar, blogs). Shrine of **Ashta Naga Vinayagar** under the tree,
  built to resemble an anthill (Tamilnadu tourism blog).
- Coordinates: **approximate ~11.10, 79.74 (low confidence)** — derived only from textual
  distances (~16 km from Mayiladuthurai, ~11.6 km from Vaitheeswaran Koil, branch road at
  Elanthoppu on the Tiruppanandal route). No sourced geo-coordinate found; refine against a mapping
  source.
- Legends (two, from a single blog account, hedged as "tradition" in content): (1) Shiva hid
  **Arjuna's sword** in a tree hollow here — *vaal* (sword) + *koli* (hidden) + *puthur* — giving
  "Vaalkolipputhur"; (2) the serpent **Vasuki**, who served at the churning of the ocean (Shiva
  swallowed the venom), dwelt in hollows beneath the vaagai tree (*puthur* ~ anthill town) and sought
  pardon here; Shiva blessed him and wore him as an ornament.
- **Seed hint corrected:** the brief's "gem/manickam connection" is not backed by any sourced gem
  legend. "Manickavannar" (ruby-hued) and "Ratnapureeswarar" (lord of the city of gems) are names
  only; content says explicitly that no separate gem tale is firmly recorded and interprets the names
  as the radiance of the Lord's form (an interpretive gloss, not a sourced one).
- Architecture: sanctum **three-tier vesara vimana**; mandapas in an older Tamil style (single blog
  description, hedged "according to temple descriptions"); blog suggests the temple pre-dates the 7th
  century and was rebuilt under Cholas and Vijayanagara (not authoritative, hedged). Dravidian.
- Festivals: no site-specific festival found. Content uses generic Shaiva calendar: Maha Shivaratri
  (Feb–Mar), monthly Pradosham, Tirukarthikai (Nov–Dec), and advises asking locally.
- Timings reported (Dinamalar): 7:30–11:30 and 16:30–20:00.
- Access: ~16 km from Mayiladuthurai; ~11.6 km from Vaitheeswaran Koil (nearest railway, per blog);
  road from Vaitheeswaran Koil towards Tiruppanandal to Elanthoppu, then a branch road. One of four
  Tevaram shrines (with Tirukkurakkukka, Tirukkaruppariyalur, Tirupunkur) visitable in a half-day
  trip from Vaitheeswaran Koil. Tiruchirappalli = nearest major airport (general knowledge).
- **Not verified:** exact coordinates; founding dynasty/structural date (century 7 used for the
  Sambandar/Sundarar era; dynasty stated generically); the Arjuna-sword and Vasuki legends (single
  blog, no corroboration); the vimana description; any gem/ruby legend; temple-specific festivals;
  current timings; district (Mayiladuthurai chosen as current); airport distance.
