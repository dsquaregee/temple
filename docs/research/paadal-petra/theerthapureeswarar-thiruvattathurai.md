# Theerthapureeswarar Temple, Thiruvattathurai (Thirunelvayil Arathurai) — research notes

Master-list row 216 (Nadu Nadu 1; shivatemples nn01). Correction/addition: the consort is given as Anandanayaki in the master list; Dinamalar names her Tiripurasundari — both names are used (Anandanayaki / Tiripurasundari). Moolavar also called Anandeeswarar (Dinamalar) and Arathurainathar.

- Place: Tiruvattathurai / Tiruvarathurai, Tevaram name Thirunelvayil Arathurai (also Tiruvatturai); Tittagudi taluk, Cuddalore district, PIN 606111. About 6 km SW of Pennadam (shivatemples); reached via the Virudhachalam–Tittagudi route through Pennadam to Kodikalam, then a branch road. Wikipedia: on the Thozhudur–Vridhachalam bus route, ~20 km from Thozhudur.
- One of the 276 Paadal Petra Sthalams; first of the Nadu Nadu shrines in the traditional order. Sung by Thirugnana Sambandar (7th c.). Dinamalar also lists Appar and Sundarar plus later poets (Ramalinga Adigal etc.) — not independently confirmed for the Tevaram; only Sambandar named in content.
- Legend (Periya Puranam tradition): the child Sambandar, walking barefoot from shrine to shrine (after Pennadam), grew tired; Shiva appeared in dreams to the people/temple staff of Arathurai and had them present to Sambandar a pearl palanquin (muthu chivigai), a pearl umbrella (and, in some tellings, other emblems). Sambandar received them here. 
- A relief panel in the temple (Poetry in Stone, described as ~1000 years old) shows the palanquin and an umbrella bearer — the gift scene.
- Theertham: Neela Malar Poigai and the Vellar (Vada Vellaru), which flows south of the temple. Dinamalar: one of seven "Thurai" temples on the Vellar, a tradition tied to the Saptarishis.
- Festivals: not specifically sourced — use customary Shaiva observances (Maha Shivaratri, Pradosham, Arudra Darshanam) + confirm locally.
- Coordinates: 11.390, 79.190 — APPROXIMATE: postal directory point for the Tiruvattathurai branch post office (11.39 N, 79.19 E; only 2 dp). No infobox/map coordinate found in search results; flagged for verification. Distinct from the Pennadam temple (11.4025, 79.2567).
