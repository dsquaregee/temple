# Pallavaneswarar Temple, Poompuhar (pallavaneswarar-tiruppallavaneecharam)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Chola Nadu (north of the Kaveri) (Tevaram name:
Tiruppallavaneecharam / Tiruppallavaneechuram, திருப்பல்லவனீச்சரம்). District: Mayiladuthurai
(Sirkazhi taluk; older sources and the postal address still say Nagapattinam district).
Tradition: Shaivism.

- Deity: Shiva as Pallavaneswarar (swayambhu linga); consort Soundaryanayaki. Utsavar
  Somaskandar. Sthala vriksha: jasmine (mallikai) and punnai; theerthams: Janavi, Sangama
  theertham, Kaveri.
- Tevaram: sung by Sambandar (Book III hymn; cited as III.112 in one source). Counted as the
  10th Tevaram Shiva temple on the northern bank of the Kaveri.
- Place: Poompuhar (Puhar, ancient Kaveripoompattinam / Kaveri Poompattinam), the early Chola
  port capital; ~19-21 km from Sirkazhi, ~24 km from Mayiladuthurai (sources vary 23-28 km).
  Sea ~3 km east of the temple today.
- Coordinates: **approximate, town-level ~11.142, 79.851** (Wikipedia gives Poompuhar town as
  11 deg 08' 38" N, 79 deg 51' 18" E; another source 11.139, 79.849). Exact temple point not
  found — refine against a mapping source.
- Defining legend(s): (a) A Pallava king worshipped and attained salvation here, hence the
  name Pallavaneecharam / Pallavaneswarar; Kubera and Kalava Maharishi also said to have
  worshipped. (b) Birthplace of the Shaiva saint-poet **Pattinathar** (born Tiruvenkadar to
  Sivanesar and Gnanakamalambikai; sea-trader; opened a treasure chest to find a husk dung-cake
  and a message; renounced; attained liberation at Tiruvottriyur). Regarded as an
  incarnation of Kubera; separate north-facing Pattinathar shrine. The "broken needle" message
  is the traditional text of the story, included in the prose; the dung-cake detail is in
  the cited sources.
- Architecture: east-facing, five-tiered rajagopuram, single prakaram, two corridors,
  **no flagstaff (kodimaram)**; swayambhu linga; Murugan standing with Valli and Deivanai
  **without his peacock**; two Durga images in the koshta (no Mahisha under one); shrines to
  Pattinathar (north-facing), Vinayaka, Subramania, Chandikeswara. Small temple.
- Festivals: Aadi (Jul-Aug) **Pattinathar festival, 12 days** — the principal festival (an
  "Adiyar utsavam"; no Brahmotsavam for Shiva); Vaikasi Visakam (May-Jun); Maha Shivaratri;
  monthly Pradosham.
- Access: nearest railway Mayiladuthurai (or Sirkazhi ~20 km); nearest airport listed:
  Tiruchirappalli. Timings reported ~6:30-12:00 and 4:30-7:30 (verify).
- **Seed-hint correction:** the brief's "Pallava/Chandra connection" was not found in any
  source; the verified associations are a Pallava king, Kubera and Kalava Maharishi, and
  Pattinathar. No Chandra legend was used.
- **Not verified:** founding dynasty / builders and century of the structural temple (century
  7 used for the Sambandar/Tevaram era); exact temple coordinate; current timings; bus
  frequency; distance to Tiruchirappalli airport; a local claim (one blog) that the Murugan
  idol came from Tiruchendur and was recovered from the sea after Dutch seizure in 1648 —
  not used; whether the Pattinathar tale's "foster-son Marudavanar" element is traditionally
  placed here (omitted).
