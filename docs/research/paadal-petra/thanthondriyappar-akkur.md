# Akkur (Tiru-Aakkur) — Thanthondriappar Temple

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Thanthondriappar** ("the self-manifested lord"), swayambhu lingam; goddess **Valnedunkanni**.
- **Location:** Akkur, ~16 km east of Mayiladuthurai (on the Mayiladuthurai–Tharangambadi road), Mayiladuthurai district, Tamil Nadu. Approx 11.0825 N, 79.7814 E (approximate).
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam — sung by Sambandar and Appar; named **Thon-Thondri Maadam** in the Tevaram. A **Maada Kovil** (elevated temple, traditionally Kochengat Chola).
- **Legends:** lingam rose on its own while a tank was dug (→ Thanthondriappar); a Chola king cured of disease after feeding 1,000 people for 48 days, then built the Maada Kovil. Birthplace of **Sirappuli Nayanar** (one of the 63). Murugan praised in Arunagirinathar's Tiruppugazh.
