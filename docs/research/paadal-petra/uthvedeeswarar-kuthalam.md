# Kuthalam (Tiruthuruthi) — Uthvedeeswarar Temple

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Uthvedeeswarar** (also **Vedhasuwarar / Sonnavararivar**); goddess **Amirtha Mughizhambikai**. (Worklist drafted the goddess as "Parimala Soundaranayaki"; sources consistently give Mughizhambikai — corrected here.)
- **Location:** Kuthalam (historical **Tiruthuruthi**), ~9 km from Mayiladuthurai on the Kumbakonam road, Mayiladuthurai district, Tamil Nadu. Approx 11.1000 N, 79.5500 E (town-level, approximate).
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam — hymned by Appar, Sambandar and Sundarar; counted among the Shiva shrines on the southern bank of the Kaveri (listed as the 37th on the south bank / 154th overall by common counts).
- **Legend:** goddess born of Sage Bharathama's sacrificial fire worships a lingam in the Kaveri; Shiva emerges and takes her hand. Lord's **Padukas** (sandals) venerated. Sthala vriksham **Uddhala** tree; river **Manjalaru** (Kaveri branch).
- **Festivals:** Maha Shivaratri (Masi); Panguni Uthiram; Karthikai Mondays; Pradosham.
