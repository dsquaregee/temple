# Thiruvanchikulam Mahadeva Temple, Kodungallur (Thiruvanjaikalam) — research notes

Master-list row 208 (Malai Nadu 1 — the only Paadal Petra Sthalam of Malai Nadu/Kerala). Row facts confirmed.
Consort given as Umadevi (Wikipedia) = Umayammai (row). No correction.

- Place: Thiruvanchikulam, about 2 km south of Kodungallur town, Thrissur district, Kerala (Kottappuram side; postal
  address "Kodungallur–Ernakulam Highway, Kottappuram, Kodungallur, Thrissur 680664"). Tevaram name Thiruvanjaikalam (Anjaikkalam).
- One of the 276 Paadal Petra Sthalams; Malai Nadu. Sung by Sundarar. Century 8.
- Deity: Shiva as Mahadeva (Sadashiva form, facing east); Tamil Tevaram name Anjaikalathappar. Consort Umadevi.
- Associations: Cheraman Perumal Nayanar (Chera king, Sundarar's friend; Chera capital at Kodungallur/Mahodayapuram).
  Tradition: Sundarar departed for Kailash from here on a white elephant sent by Shiva; Cheraman Perumal followed on horseback
  (whispering the Panchakshara into the horse's ear in the Periya Puranam — not used). Event remembered on Aadi Swathi (July–Aug).
  Sundarar sang a final pathigam on the way, which Varuna (sea god) carried back to Thiruvanjaikalam.
- Images of Sundarar and Cheraman Perumal are kept at a Bhagavathi temple and brought to this temple during the Swathi festival (Wikipedia).
- Architecture: Kerala style; namaskara mandapam with 16 pillars before the sreekovil; good collection of murals; protected by ASI.
  Copper roof not confirmed — not used.
- Festivals: 8-day Maha Shivaratri festival in Kumbham (Feb–Mar), with Aanayottam (elephant race); Aadi Swathi (Sundarar's
  ascension). One source names a 10-day Vaikasi brahmotsavam — conflicting, not used.
- Coordinates: 10.2103, 76.2067 — Wikipedia "Thiruvanchikulam Temple" infobox (10°12′37″N 76°12′24″E) via search text.
  Distinct from kodungallur-bhagavathy entry (10.2269, 76.1962), ~2 km apart.
- Getting there: Kodungallur town 2 km; railheads Irinjalakuda / Thrissur / Ernakulam; Cochin International Airport ~30 km.
