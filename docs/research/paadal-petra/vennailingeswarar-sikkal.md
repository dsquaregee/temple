# Sikkal — Navaneetheswarar (Vennailingeswarar) Temple

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Navaneetheswarar / Vennailingeswarar** (the butter lingam); goddess **Velnedungkanni**.
- **Location:** Sikkal, near Nagapattinam (~7 km), Tamil Nadu. Approx 10.7958 N, 79.8089 E (approximate).
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam (Tevaram, Kaveri Then Karai group). Famed **Singaravelan (Murugan) shrine within the same temple**.
- **Legends:** Lingam made of butter from Kamadhenu's milk by sage Vasishta (→ Navaneetheswarar). Murugan received the **vel** from the goddess here before destroying Surapadman (slain at Tiruchendur the next day). Name "Sikkal" ~ "tangled place" (obstacles in construction).
- **Festival:** **Skanda Shashti** (Aippasi); the vel-giving the day before Surasamharam — the bronze Singaravelan said to perspire.
