# Tiruvettakkudi — Tirumeniazhagar Temple

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Tirumeniazhagar** (Sundareswarar); goddess **Soundaranayagi** (Santhanayagi). (Worklist draft goddess "Soundaranayaki?" — confirmed.)
- **Location:** **Tiruvettakkudi**, near **Karaikal** (via Karaikal–Poraiyar road to Varichakudi ~3 km, then a branch road); **Karaikal district, Puducherry** (Kaveri delta). ~10.95 N, 79.80 E (approximate).
- **Note:** distinct from the brick Tirumeniazhagar temple at Mappadugai/Pandaravadai (Nagapattinam), which is NOT the Paadal Petra Sthalam, and from the Chola-north Tirumeniazhagar at Tirumayendirapalli (already in catalog).
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam — hymned by **Tirugnana Sambandar**. Legend: Arjuna's penance; Shiva came as a hunter (Vedan) → Tiru-Vetta-Kudi.
- **Festivals:** Maha Shivaratri (Masi); Navaratri (Purattasi); Karthikai Mondays; Pradosham.
