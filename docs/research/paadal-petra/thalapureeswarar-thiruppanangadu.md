# Thalapureeswarar Temple, Thiruppanangadu — research notes

Master-list row 247 (Thondai Nadu). Row facts confirmed: two presiding lingams, Thalapureeswarar (Talapurisvarar / Panangatteeswarar) and Kirupanatheswarar (Kirubapureeswarar / Krupanatheswarar); consorts Amirthavalli and Kirubanayaki. Singer: Sundarar only (Wikipedia, Dinamalar). Century 8.

- Place: Thiruppanangadu (Tiruppanangadu), single-village gram panchayat in Vembakkam block, Cheyyar area, Tiruvannamalai district; PIN 604 410. Historical name Vanpartthan Panangattur (Wikipedia). ~16 km from Kanchipuram (shivatemples tn09). Dinamalar counts it as the 19th Tevaram shrine of Thondaimandalam.
- Paadal Petra Sthalam of Thondai Nadu; sung by Sundarar.
- Two lingams: Agastya installed Thalapureeswarar; his disciple Pulastya installed Kirupanatheswarar nearby on a square pitha. Both worshipped as one Shiva; Agastya's lingam given ritual precedence. One account: both under a gajaprishta (elephant-back) vimana; Thalapureeswarar visible through an aperture from beside the Kirupanatheswarar shrine (detail not used, sources vary).
- Agastya worshipped with palm fruit (panai) — name "Panangadu" = palm forest. Sthala vriksham palm; one of five temples with palm as sacred tree (shivatemples). Inscriptions in the temple reportedly warn against cutting living palms (shivatemples — reported).
- Theerthams: Jada Gangai and Sundarar Theertham (Dinamalar).
- Devotees seeking children offer palm fruit; prayers for marriage, eye ailments (Dinamalar).
- Inscriptions: Vijayanagara records (SII vol. 22): Kampana Udaiyar endowments of ghee and milk (c. 1370s, families of shepherds assigned); Virupaksha II, Saka 1310 / 1388 CE, appointing a man to supply water for the deity's bath and maintain a flower garden; deity named Tiruppanangadudaiya-Nayanar; village in Kaliyur-kottam of Jayangondasolamandalam (Chola-era territorial name).
- Festivals: not found — customary observances only, confirm locally.
- Coordinates: 12.8012, 79.6126 — geographic.org PIN/place listing for Tiruppanangadu 604410 (12.801175, 79.612639). Village/post-office point, not temple-specific; consistent with ~16 km from Kanchipuram by road. **Needs a map check.** No other catalog temple in the village.
- Not used: a Sundarar "food and water" legend surfaced in search but belongs to Thirukurukavur (shivatemples nc13), not this temple.
