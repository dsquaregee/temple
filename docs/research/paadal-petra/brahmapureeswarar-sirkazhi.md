# Brahmapureeswarar (Sattainathar / Thoniappar) Temple, Sirkazhi (brahmapureeswarar-sirkazhi)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Chola Nadu (north of the Kaveri). District: Mayiladuthurai
(formerly Nagapattinam), Tamil Nadu. Tradition: Shaivism. Tevaram name: Sirkazhi
(சீர்காழி), also Brahmapuram; the town is credited with twelve ancient names (Kazhi,
Brahmapuram, Venupuram, Venguru, Thonipuram, Kazhumalam, Pugazhi, Poontharai, Sirapuram,
Puravam, Shanbai, Kochivayam — lists differ slightly between sources).

- Deity: Shiva in three forms — **Brahmapureeswarar** (linga), **Thoniappar** (Uma
  Maheswarar on the boat/thoni, with goddess **Periya Nayaki**) and **Sattainathar**
  (Bhairava / Vatukanathar). Goddess of the main shrine: **Thirunilai Nayaki**
  (Tirunilainayaki); her shrine stands near the Sambandar shrine on the outer prakara.
- Coordinates: **town-level ~11.233, 79.733** (Wikipedia gives the temple as 11°14′N 79°44′E;
  Sirkazhi town 11°14′20″N 79°44′10″E). Exact temple point not confirmed — approximate.
- Three-tier shrine: ground = Brahmapureeswarar; middle = Thoniappar with Periya Nayaki;
  top = Sattainathar. **Source conflict:** Wikipedia, thanjavurparampara and shivatemples
  use this order; two hotel-chain blogs list Sattainathar on the ground. We follow the
  majority/Wikipedia order and state the discrepancy in the en/ta text.
- Defining legend: Sambandar (child of Sivapada Hridayar), left on the bank of the temple
  tank while his father bathed, cried for his parents; Shiva and Parvati appeared and Parvati
  fed him milk of wisdom (gnanappal) from a golden cup; he then sang his first hymn,
  *Todudaiya Seviyan* (opens the Tevaram). Sirkazhi is his birthplace.
- Other legends: Brahma worshipped Shiva here (hence Brahmapuram); during the pralaya flood
  Shiva and Uma floated on a thoni (raft/boat) and found Sirkazhi unharmed (hence Thoniappar);
  Sattainathar as Bhairava, lord of law (*sattam*) — litigants pray to him. (A further tradition
  of Shiva wearing Vishnu's skin to explain "Sattai" appears in one source; not used.)
- Tanks: Brahma Theertham (also Gnana Theertham); 22 or 23 theerthams associated with the place.
- Festivals: **Chithirai Thiruvadhirai** Brahmotsavam (Apr–May); the Gnanappal (milk) festival on
  its 2nd day; Sambandar then goes in procession to Tirukolakka (Dinamalar) where the golden
  cymbals are remembered; Maha Shivaratri; monthly Pradosham; Ashtami Bhairava worship (general).
- Access: Sirkazhi on the Chennai–Chidambaram–Mayiladuthurai road; 17 km from Chidambaram,
  58 km from Kumbakonam, ~260 km from Chennai; own railway station (Chennai–Mayiladuthurai line);
  temple in the town centre.
- **Not verified:** founding dynasty and century of the structural temple (Chola/medieval patrons
  not firmly sourced; century 7 used for the Tevaram/Sambandar era); exact temple coordinate;
  gopuram heights and dimensions (not found); whether all three saints (Sambandar, Appar,
  Sundarar) sang here — Sambandar's hymns are the principal ones; current timings (sources vary:
  ~6:00–12:00/12:30/13:00 and ~16:00–21:00); the Vishnu-skin explanation of "Sattai"; distances
  to the bus stand and railway station; nearest airport (Chennai assumed far; Tiruchirappalli
  not checked). "Sattainathar" meaning "lord of the law" is a folk gloss reported by one source.
