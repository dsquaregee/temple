# Pasupatinathar (Pasupatheeswarar) Temple, Pandanallur (pasupatinathar-tirupanthanainallur)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Chola Nadu (north of the Kaveri) (Tevaram name: Tirupanthanainallur,
திருப்பந்தணைநல்லூர்; also written Thiruppanthanainallur / Pandanainallur).
District: Thanjavur (Thiruvidaimarudur taluk), Tamil Nadu. Tradition: Shaivism.
Listed as the 35th Tevaram Shiva sthalam on the northern bank of the Kaveri.

- Deity: Shiva as Pasupatinathar / Pasupatheeswarar; consort **Venubhujambikai (Venu Bhujambikai),
  Tamil name Veyurutholi Ammai**, also given as Kambanatholi Ammai. **Correction/variance:** Wikipedia
  gives only "Mangalambika"; Templenet and Dinamalar give Venubhujambikai/Veyurutholi — used the
  latter two (consistent across two sources). Sthala vriksha: sarakkonrai. Tank: Surya Theertham
  (Wikipedia names Kamadhenu Theertham outside the entrance; Templenet "Suryapushkarini" inside
  with a central mandapam — Dinamalar/TN Temples Project say Surya Theertham; used Surya Teertham).
- Tevaram: Sambandar and Appar (Wikipedia, Templenet; Dinamalar mentions Appar only).
- Coordinates: **approximate ~11.12, 79.52** (Wikipedia temple infobox 11°7′N 79°31′E; Pandanallur
  post office 11.132, 79.515). Exact temple point not found — refine against a mapping source.
- Defining legend: the name — Parvati wished to play ball (pandu) with her companions; Shiva made
  balls of the four Vedas; she played so long the Sun delayed and the sages could not complete
  evening rites. Parvati took cow (pasu) form with Murugan as calf and worshipped Shiva here until
  relieved; Vishnu tended the cow as a herdsman (and its milk fed Sage Kanva). The Vedas "came to
  rest" (pandu vandhu anaintha nallur) -> Pandanallur. Versions differ on whether Shiva cursed
  Parvati (Templenet: ball game with Lakshmi and Saraswati, curse into cow form) — prose is kept
  to the common elements.
- Linga: swayambhu, reported white, with a cow-hoof mark, no abhisheka (Wikipedia); Dinamalar
  describes an anthill-form swayambu. Prose hedges as "tradition describes".
- Architecture: Dravidian; two prakaras; gopuram at the entrance (**sources conflict on tiers:
  Wikipedia three-tier vs one source seven-tier — tiers deliberately not stated**); oldest masonry
  Chola, ~11th c.; inscriptions of Vikrama Chola, Rajadhiraja II, Kulottunga III, Rajaraja III and
  Vijayanagara gifts. Nruthi Ganapathi shrine.
- Festivals: Masi Magam (Feb–Mar), Panguni Uthiram (Mar–Apr); Shivaratri, Thirukarthigai, Aippasi;
  Pradosham; Sun's rays on the linga for three days (19th–21st) in Aavani (Dinamalar, one source).
- Village note: home of the Pandanallur style of Bharatanatyam (Meenakshisundaram Pillai, 1869–1964).
- Access: Kaveri delta north of Aduthurai, between Kumbakonam and Mayiladuthurai.
- **Not verified:** exact temple coordinates (approximate used); gopuram tier count; goddess name
  (variants listed above); nearest-airport/rail distances (stated generally); current timings
  (listings conflict: 6am–12 / 4–8pm vs 7am–11:59pm, so given generically); sun-ray dates (one
  source, Aavani 19–21); Vishnu-as-herdsman and Kanva details (single-source folk tradition);
  sthala vriksha identity (sarakkonrai given untranslated); dress/photography rules are the
  general Tamil Nadu temple rules. A "Kanchi" connection in the seed hint was not found in any
  source and is omitted. Do not confuse with Pasupatheeswarar temples at Avoor, Pasupathikovil,
  Nallavur, Karur or Thirukondeeswaram.
