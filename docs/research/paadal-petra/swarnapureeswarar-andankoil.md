# Andankoil (Tirukkaduvaaikkaraiputtur) — Swarnapureeswarar Temple

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Swarnapureeswarar** ("lord of the golden city"); goddess **Swarnambikai** (also Sivambigai, **Sivasekari**, Poornambikai).
- **Location:** Andankoil (Tevaram **Tirukkaduvaaikkaraiputtur** / Thiru Kaduvai Karai Thenputhur), Valangaiman taluk, Tiruvarur district, Tamil Nadu, PIN 612804. ~10.7600 N, 79.3800 E (approximate, near Valangaiman).
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam — hymned by Appar; Kaveri south bank.
- **Legend:** tied to **Tiruvarur Thyagaraja temple** — minister **Kandadeva** came here for stone/lime and built the temple at Shiva's command in a dream.
- **Festivals:** Maha Shivaratri (Masi); Navaratri (Purattasi); Karthikai Mondays; Pradosham.
- **Caution:** distinct from Semponarkoil Swarnapureeswarar (goddess Suguntha Kundalambikai) and Alagaputhur (Padikasu Nathar/Swarnapureeswarar, goddess Azhagammai).
