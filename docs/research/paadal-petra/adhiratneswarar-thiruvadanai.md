# Adhiratneswarar Temple, Thiruvadanai — research notes

Master-list row 202 (Pandya Nadu 9). Row facts confirmed: Adhiratneswarar (Aadanainathar, Ajagajeswarar); Snehavalli; Sambandar.

- Place: Tiruvadanai / Thiruvadanai, taluk headquarters in Ramanathapuram district; ~45 km from Karaikudi via Devakottai. PIN 623407.
- 9th of the 14 Pandya Nadu sthalams in shivatemples' order (pn09).
- Moolavar: Adhiratneswarar; also Aadanainathar, Ajagajeswarar ("Lord of the goat-elephant"). Ambal: Snehavalli (Anbayiravalli? — not used); associated with Sukra (Venus) for navagraha worship (shivatemples).
- Sambandar's pathigam: in the 2nd Thirumurai (1 pathigam).
- Legend: Vaaruni, son of Varuna, cursed by a sage (Durvasa in some versions — not used) to have an elephant's body and a goat's head; worshipped Shiva here and was restored. Aadu (goat) + aanai (elephant) → Thiru-aadu-aanai → Thiruvadanai. Theerthams: Varuna and Vaaruni theerthams; Surya Pushkarini (Dinamalar). Sthala vriksham: vilvam.
- Surya: tradition that Surya worshipped Shiva here (indianetzone says in a blue-gem image — single source, presented only as "tradition says Surya worshipped here"); separate Surya Chandikeswarar shrine (indianetzone).
- Architecture: nine-tiered rajagopuram, ~130 ft, with stucco figures (indianetzone; single source — phrased "said to rise about 130 feet").
- Coordinates: 9.7833, 78.9194 — Wikipedia "Tiruvadanai" town infobox (9°47′0″N 78°55′10″E); satellites.pro 9°47′00″N 78°55′07″E agrees. Town point; the temple is the town's central landmark. Not a temple-specific point.
