# Adhimooleswarar (Moolanathar) Temple, Tiruppatrurai (adhimooleswarar-tiruppatrurai)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Chola Nadu (north of the Kaveri). Tevaram name: Tiruppatrurai
(திருப்பாற்றுறை; also written Tirupalathurai / Tiruppalatthurai). District: Tiruchirappalli.
Tradition: Shaivism.

- Deity: Shiva as Adhimooleswarar (also Adhimoolanathar / Moolanathar / Thirumoolanathar), a
  swayambu (self-manifested) linga; consort **Nithyakalyani** (also listed as Mekalambika /
  Mohanayaki — sources differ on the name; Nithyakalyani used per brief and Dinamalar).
- Tevaram: sung by Sambandar (shivatemples.com lists a Sambandar pathikam). Counted among the
  Paadal Petra Sthalams on the northern bank of the Kaveri (Dinamalar numbers it 59th).
- Coordinates: **~10.8458, 78.7692** (Wikipedia: 10°50′45″N 78°46′09″E; infobox location
  "Panayur"). Approximate; verify against a map before relying on it for navigation.
- **Correction to seed hint:** the seed said "town Thiruppattur". Thiruppattur (Tiruchirappalli
  district) is a different place with the Brahmapureeswarar (Vaippu Sthalam) temple. This temple
  is at Tiruppatrurai / Tirupalathurai on the Kollidam bank near Panayapuram, ~9 km east of
  Tiruvanaikaval; the city field is therefore "Tiruppatrurai". Do not confuse with the
  Parangipettai (Cuddalore) Aadhimooleswarar temple that also surfaces in Dinamalar searches.
- Defining legends: (1) A Chola king hunting chased a bird into a bush; his arrow struck an
  anthill that oozed milk; Shiva appeared in a dream, the king built the temple. The milk gives
  the name *Pattrurai* (paal = milk, thurai = riverbank place). (2) The short-lived sage
  Markandeya came to pray for longer life; with no water to bathe the linga, milk welled up from
  the top of the linga itself (Dinamalar, Tamil). Sources mention milk only, never blood.
- Architecture: east-facing sanctum with swayambu linga; Nithyakalyani shrine faces **south**
  (devotees say this tempers the fear of Yama); shrine of **Sankaranarayana** behind the
  sanctum in place of Lingodbhava; Chola-period inscriptions (Parantaka I, Vikrama Chola; one
  record of the 8th year of Parakesarivarman re-engraving older land grants). One tourism blog
  suggests construction by Parantaka I (10th c.) — hedged, not used as a firm claim.
- Festivals: Maha Shivaratri (Masi, Feb–Mar), Tirukarthikai (Nov–Dec), Margazhi Thiruvadirai
  (Arudra Darshan), annual Brahmotsavam, Pournami (full-moon) poojas to Nithyakalyani; Pradosham.
- Access: on the Kollidam bank, ~9 km east of Tiruvanaikaval, ~2 km from Panayapuram; limited
  public transport (auto/taxi/private vehicle advised). Tiruchirappalli Junction (rail) and
  Tiruchirappalli airport.
- **Not verified:** exact temple coordinate (Wikipedia point only); gopuram tiers / precise layout
  and prakara details; month of the Brahmotsavam; whether Appar/Sundarar also sang here (only
  Sambandar confirmed); founding dynasty/king (the Chola king of the legend is unnamed;
  structural date medieval Chola — century 7 used for the Tevaram era); current timings (sources
  conflict: 7–10:30/4:30–8, 7–12/4–7, 5:30 am–8 pm rituals); distance to the airport.
