# Pasupatheeswarar (Kalyana Pasupateeswarar / Aanilaiyappar) Temple, Karur (pasupatheeswarar-karur)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Kongu Nadu (Tevaram name: Karuvur Aanilai). Tradition: Shaivism.

- Deity: Shiva as Pasupatheeswarar / Aanilaiyappar (swayambhu lingam, slightly leaning);
  goddesses Soundaranayaki (Alankaravalli) and Kripanayaki (Ichcha / Kriya Shakti per TN
  Temples Project); Mangalambika also listed as a prominent shrine in one source.
- Counted among the seven Kongu Nadu Tevaram shrines; Wikipedia/others credit Sambandar and
  Sundarar; Karuvur Thevar (Tiruvisaippa) and Eripatha Nayanar are avatara-sthala links;
  Arunagirinathar also praised (Dinamalar).
- Antiquity: Karur (Karuvur) a Sangam-era city, linked with Vanji. Inscriptions show the temple
  existed under Rajendra Chola (1012–54 CE), who gifted land; oldest masonry ascribed to the
  11th-century Cholas. (One Wikipedia history page says "7th century Chola" — conflicts, not used.)
- Coordinates: **town-level ~10.958, 78.077** (Karur centre). Exact temple point not found.
- Architecture: Dravidian; five-tiered rajagopuram (one source ~120 ft); concentric granite
  walls (sources variously say 2 or 3 prakarams); carved puranic scenes; shrines of Pasupatheeswarar,
  Soundaranayaki, Kripanayaki/Mangalambika, Adikesava Perumal; theertham Brahma Teertham and the
  Amaravati river.
- Legend: Brahma's pride; creation entrusted to Kamadhenu; on Narada's advice she worshipped a
  lingam under an anthill in the Vanji forest — hence Aanilai. Karuvur Thevar: Lord leaned to
  hear his songs / saint merged with the Lord, leaving the lingam inclined.
- Festivals: Panguni Brahmotsavam and three days of sun rays on the lingam (Panguni, mid-Mar to
  mid-Apr); Eripatha Nayanar festival; Navaratri; Arudra Darshan (Margazhi); Maha Shivaratri;
  Pradosham.
- Access: Karur on the Amaravati; Karur Jn railway in town; Tiruchirappalli ~75–82 km;
  Coimbatore ~125 km by road (Wikipedia, 127 km).
- **Not verified:** whether Appar's hymns also cover Karur (sources name Sambandar and Sundarar
  only); the Aanilai etymology beyond the Kamadhenu account; the "ablution not performed" claim
  (one source — omitted); rajagopuram height (one source); exact prakaram count; Mangalambika vs
  Kripanayaki naming; Pugazh Chola Nayanar / Karuvur Thevar–Rajaraja traditions (not searched,
  omitted); exact temple coordinate; timings (6–12:30 & 4–9 per TN Temples Project vs 6–11 & 4–8
  per Dinamalar); distance of railway station from the temple.
