# Kalyana Pasupatheeswarar Temple, Karur (Karuvur Anilai) — pasupatheeswarar-karur

Master-list row 211 (Kongu Nadu 3). Facts confirmed; no corrections.

- Place: Karur town (Sannathi St, Madavilagam), on the south bank of the Amaravathi, a tributary of the Kaveri. Tevaram name Thirukkaruvur Anilai (Aanilai).
- Deity: Pasupatheeswarar / Pasupathi / Aanilai Appar (Kalyana Pasupatheeswarar); consort Alankaravalli, also Soundaryanayaki, Kripanayaki.
- Hymns: Sambandar (Tevaram). One source also lists Sundarar — not corroborated; content names Sambandar only. Karuvur Thevar (Karuvurar, the siddha) sang the Thiruvisaippa (9th Tirumurai); Arunagirinathar the Tiruppugazh.
- Legend: Kamadhenu (pasu, cow) worshipped Shiva here → Pasupatheeswarar; "Aanilai" — the cow's abode. Lord said to tilt to one side to hear Karuvurar's songs.
- Karur is the birthplace of Eripatha Nayanar and Karuvur Thevar; ruled by Pugazh Chola Nayanar (Periya Puranam: Eripatha cut down the royal elephant that scattered flowers meant for Shiva; the king offered his own life).
- Sun's rays fall on the lingam a few days a year (sources disagree: 3 days in Panguni vs 5 days in Masi) — content says "in Masi–Panguni".
- Tanks: Brahma theertham; the Amaravathi.
- Coordinates: 10.9606, 78.0785 (Wikipedia infobox 10°57′38″N 78°04′43″E, via search result text).
- Access: Karur Junction railway station in town; Tiruchirappalli airport (~80 km) nearest; Coimbatore also reachable.
