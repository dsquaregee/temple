# Sirukudi — Mangaleswarar (Sookshmapureeswarar) Temple

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Mangaleswarar** (also **Sookshmapureeswarar**); goddess **Mangala Nayaki**.
- **Location:** Sirukudi, ~3 km west of Tirupampuram, Tiruvarur district, Tamil Nadu. Approx 10.9050 N, 79.6033 E (approximate).
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam — sung by Sambandar.
- **Signature legend:** Parvati won a dice game on Kailasa; Shiva turned "subtle" (sookshma) and vanished; she worshipped a **lingam made of a handful of sand (sirupidi)** here and he re-manifested and took her back → Sookshmapuri / Sookshmapureeswarar; village name **Sirukudi** from "sirupidi".
- **Association:** marital harmony / reconciliation.
