# Tiruppanaiyur — Soundareswarar (Thaalavaneswarar) Temple

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Soundareswarar**, also **Thaalavaneswarar** ("lord of the palmyra/palm forest"); goddess **Soundaranayaki**. Self-manifested (swayambhu) lingam.
- **Location:** Tiruppanaiyur, near Tiruvarur, Tiruvarur district, Tamil Nadu. Approx 10.8783 N, 79.5550 E (approximate).
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam — sung by Sundarar. Sthala vriksham = **palmyra (panai)**, which names the village.
- **Legends:** young **Karikala Chola** and his mother sheltered here (she prayed to Vinayaka); lingam installed by sage **Parasara**.
