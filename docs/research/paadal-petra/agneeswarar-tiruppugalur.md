# Tiruppugalur — Agneeswarar (Agnipureeswarar) Temple

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Agneeswarar** (Agnipureeswarar); goddess **Karundharkuzhali** (also Choolikambal).
- **Location:** **Tiruppugalur**, lower Kaveri delta; Nagapattinam district. ~10.87 N, 79.75 E (approximate).
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam (Kaveri south bank) — hymned by **Appar, Sambandar and Sundarar**. Present stone temple Chola, ~10th c. Legend: **Agni** did penance here and raised the **Agni Theertham** that still encircles the shrine.
- **Note:** the Tiruppugalur complex holds **two Paadal Petra shrines** — this **Agneeswarar** temple and the neighbouring **Vardhamaneswarar** shrine (`vardhamaneeswarar-tiruppugalur`, already in the catalog). Distinct also from the catalog's other Agneeswarar temples (Melaithirukkattupalli, Kanjanur, Tirukkollikadu).
- **Festivals:** Maha Shivaratri (Masi); Navaratri (Purattasi); Karthikai Mondays; Pradosham.

**Notes:** Coordinates approximate. Genuinely-missing PPS temple found via strict circuit-reconciliation (the town-matcher had masked it behind the neighbouring Vardhamaneswarar shrine).
