# Tirukkondeeswaram — Pasupatheeswarar Temple

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Pasupatheeswarar** (also **Jurahareswarar**, 'fever-remover'); goddess **Shanthanayaki** (Santha Nayagi). (Worklist goddess "Santhanayaki" — confirmed.)
- **Location:** **Tirukkondeeswaram** (Tevaram Tirukkondeechuram), ~17 km N of Tiruvarur, S bank of the **Mudikondan**, ~2.5 km E of **Nannilam**; Tiruvarur district. ~10.88003 N, 79.62923 E (Wikipedia exact). Area once Vilvaranyam.
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam — hymned by **Appar**. Legends: Parvati as Kamadhenu, horn-mark on the lingam → Pasupateeswarar; Shiva cured Agastya's fever → Jurahareswarar.
- **Festivals:** Maha Shivaratri (Masi); Navaratri (Purattasi); Karthikai Mondays; Pradosham.
