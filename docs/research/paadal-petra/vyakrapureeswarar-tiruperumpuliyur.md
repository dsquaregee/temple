# Vyakrapureeswarar Temple, Tiruperumpuliyur (vyakrapureeswarar-tiruperumpuliyur)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Chola Nadu (north of the Kaveri), Thanjavur district (Thiruvaiyaru taluk). Tevaram name: Tiruperumpuliyur (திருப்பெரும்புலியூர்). Tradition: Shaivism.

- Deity: Shiva as Vyakrapureeswarar (Vyagrapureeswarar), presiding lingam held to be
  self-manifested (svayambhu); consort **Soundara Nayaki / Soundaryanayaki** (east-facing shrine,
  standing posture).
- Tevaram: Sambandar, one decad (shivatemples lists "Tirugyanasambandar - 1"). Appar/Sundarar: not
  found.
- Coordinates: **approximate ~10.908, 79.075** (village-level; derived from "about 4–5 km
  NW/W of Thiruvaiyaru"). No sourced coordinate found — refine against a mapping source.
- Legend (defining story): sage **Vyaghrapada** (Tamil *Pulikkal Munivar*, "tiger-footed
  sage"), who learned of Nataraja of Thillai from his father (Mathiyanthinar / Madhyandina) and
  worshipped him daily at Chidambaram. To gather flowers from high branches in the dark before
  the bees touched them, Shiva gave him a tiger's feet (to climb) and a tiger's eyes (to see at
  night). Shrines where he worshipped are the **Puliyur** temples; Wikipedia lists Tirupperumpuliyur
  among the **Nava Puliyur** (worshipped by Patanjali and Vyaghrapada). Vyaghrapada and Patanjali
  are remembered as witnesses of Nataraja's dance (Chidambaram tradition).
- Architecture: east-facing **three-tiered rajagopuram** with well-preserved carvings; goddess
  Soundara Nayaki in an east-facing shrine, standing; **Navagraha shrine with the planets all
  facing the central Sun (Surya)**; sacred tree **sarakondrai** (golden shower tree, Cassia
  fistula); holy water the Kaveri.
- History: a Tamil blog says a brick temple may predate the 7th century, rebuilt in Chola and
  Vijayanagara periods (single non-scholarly source); village name held "over 1500 years".
  Century set to 7 (Sambandar's Tevaram era).
- Festivals: no temple-specific festival found. Maha Shivaratri and monthly Pradosham stated
  generically (standard for Shiva temples).
- Access: Perumpuliyur, Thillaisthanam Post, via Thiruvaiyaru, Thanjavur District, PIN 613203;
  ~4–5 km from Thiruvaiyaru (sources: "4 km NW", "5 km", "4 km west of Tiruneithanam"). Thanjavur
  railhead; Tiruchirappalli airport.
- **Correction / caution:** the Dinamalar page (id=657) found in search describes a Muruga shrine
  and 800-year-old vanni tree and appears to be Thirupulivanam (Kanchipuram), not this temple;
  the three-tier gopuram detail was cross-checked with the Tiruperumpuliyur Wikipedia/blog text
  and Thirupulivanam details were NOT merged in.
- **Not verified:** exact coordinates; precise distance to Thiruvaiyaru; Appar/Sundarar hymns;
  named annual festivals and dates; timings; the pre-7th-century brick temple and
  Chola/Vijayanagara rebuilding (single blog source); the Nataraja-shrine detail (Vyaghrapada and
  Patanjali flanking Nataraja) — source text ambiguous about whether it refers to this temple or
  to Chidambaram, so it was kept to the legend and NOT stated as a shrine here; the exact
  composition of the Nava Puliyur list (sources differ on five vs nine).
