# Oondreeswarar Temple, Tiruvenpakkam (oondreeswarar-tiruvenpakkam)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Thondai Nadu (Tiruvallur district). Tradition: Shaivism.
Now located in Poondi village; Tevaram name Tiruvenpakkam (also remembered as Pazhampathi).

- Deity: Shiva as Oondreeswarar (also spelled Oondreswarar), worshipped as a linga. The
  name comes from "oondru / oondru-kol" (a staff/club to lean on), from the Sundarar
  legend below.
- Consort: Minnoli Amman (Minnoli Nayagi / Minnoli Ammai), identified with Parvati.
  Name read as "lightning mother" (minnal = lightning), from the legend.
- Counted among the Paadal Petra Sthalams (Thondai Nadu group); hymn attributed to
  Sundarar (one pathigam). Placed in the Tevaram era — used century 8 (Sundarar's age,
  consistent with the Avinashiappar exemplar's treatment of Sundarar). Note: one source
  phrases the canon as "7th-century"; 7 or 8 both acceptable per task.
- Defining legend (Sundarar): having lost his sight, Sundarar came here and sang for his
  vision. Shiva gave him a staff (oondru-kol) to lean on — hence Oondreeswarar. Still
  blind and not knowing who gave it, the saint threw the staff aside in distress and it
  struck/broke the horn of Nandi; the temple's Nandi is shown with a broken horn to this
  day. The goddess, prevented from walking at his side, sent a flash of lightning before
  him to show the way (on his way toward Kanchipuram) — hence Minnoli, "lightning mother."
  By tradition his sight was restored afterward at Kanchipuram (Ekambareswarar) and
  Tiruvarur (sources vary on where/which eye).
- History: original complex believed Chola-era; present masonry structure 16th century.
  The temple was relocated in 1942 to its present site to make way for the Poondi
  reservoir. Original site reported on the banks of the Kuchasthalai River / near a lake
  at Tiruvalampudur (sources vary in phrasing).
- Architecture: east-facing Dravidian temple, now on the northern bank of the Poondi
  reservoir; linga in sanctum; Nandi with broken horn before it; Minnoli Amman in her own
  shrine. Festival deity (urchavar) named as Somaskandar by one source.
- Coordinates: **13.208, 79.879** (from Wikipedia's temple coordinates 13°12′29″N
  79°52′43″E) — a temple-level point, not merely town-level.
- Access: Poondi, on the Tiruvallur–Tiruttani road, ~11 km from Tiruvallur (nearest
  railway junction); Chennai (~60 km) nearest international airport.
- Festivals (Dinamalar): Masi Maha Shivaratri (Feb–Mar); Aipasi Annabishekam (Oct–Nov);
  Tirukarthikai (Nov–Dec). Wikipedia notes four annual festivals incl. a Brahmotsavam;
  full list unconfirmed — kept to the above plus a general Brahmotsavam mention.

**Not verified:** exact century/dynasty of the original structural temple (local tradition
= Chola original, 16th-century masonry; used century 8 for the Tevaram/Sundarar era);
precise original site name (Kuchasthalai riverbank vs Tiruvalampudur lake — sources vary);
the full annual festival calendar (Wikipedia says four; only three named in sources, plus
a Brahmotsavam); exact detail of where Sundarar's sight was restored (Kanchipuram and/or
Tiruvarur — sources differ); current timings; number of gopuram tiers / dimensions.
