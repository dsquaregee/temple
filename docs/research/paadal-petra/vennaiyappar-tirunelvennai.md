# Swarnakadeswarar (Vennaiyappar) Temple, Tirunelvennai (vennaiyappar-tirunelvennai)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Nadu Nadu (Tevaram name: Nelvennai / Tirunelvennai;
village of Neivanai). Tradition: Shaivism.

- Deity: Shiva as Swarnakadeswarar (Sanskrit) / Vennaiyappar (Tamil); consort
  Neelamalar Kanni Ammai (also spelt Neelamalar Kanniammai).
- Counted among the 275 Paadal Petra Sthalams; listed as the **10th Shiva temple of
  the Nadu Nadu region** praised in the Tevaram. Sung by **Tirugnana Sambandar**
  (3rd Tirumurai) — Sambandar places the temple in the **7th century**, hence
  `century: 7`.
- Moolavar is a **swayambhu (self-manifested) linga**, enshrined **under a canopy made
  of rudraksha beads** (the shrine's defining feature; sourced).
- **Sanakadi Rishis** said to have worshipped here — local temple belief; **no textual
  source found** (Wikipedia/guides note it as a belief).
- Name **"Swarnakadeswarar" = "the one who bestowed the golden pot,"** traced by temple
  tradition to a shrine **inscription** — **owner-supplied; not confirmed in web
  sources.** Written into the note and the `legends` section as tradition, not fact.
- **Maha Shivaratri sun alignment:** a temple listing (shivatemples.com) states the
  sun's rays fall on the deity during Mahashivratri (month of Masi). The further
  detail that the rays fall **at the Lord's feet** (owner-supplied) was **not
  confirmed** — prose says only that the rays reach/fall upon the Lord.
- **Coordinates: town-level estimate ~11.78, 79.21** (Neivanai / Tirunelvennai lies on
  the Tirukkovilur–Ulundurpet road via Eraiyur and Elavanasur, PIN 607201 area).
  **Exact temple point not found** in sources — refine against a mapping source when
  possible.
- Access: on the Tirukkovilur–Ulundurpet road via Eraiyur and Elavanasur; Ulundurpet /
  Tirukkovilur nearest towns; Villupuram nearest major railway junction. Off the main
  highways.
- **Not verified:** founding dynasty/century of the structural temple (used century 7
  for the Sambandar/Tevaram era; later patrons not firmly sourced); exact temple
  coordinate; the "golden pot" inscription and the gloss of Swarnakadeswarar; the
  "at the feet" detail of the Shivaratri sun; owner-listed festivals **Tirukarthikai
  and Arudra Darshan** for this specific temple (not found in sources — kept general
  as part of the ordinary Shaiva calendar); current timings.
