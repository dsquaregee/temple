# Kapardeeswarar (Karpaganathar) Temple, Tiruvalanchuzhi (karpaganathar-tiruvalanchuzhi)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Chola Nadu south of the Kaveri. Tradition: Shaivism.
- Deity: Shiva as Kapardeeswarar / Karpaganathar; consort Periyanayaki.
- Famed for Swetha Vinayakar, the white sea-foam Ganesha; worship offered to the kavacham, not the fragile image.
- Sung by Appar & Thirugnana Sambandar; Chola base, 16th-c. Thanjavur Nayak additions.
- ~6 km from Kumbakonam, near Swamimalai.
- Coordinates: ~10.9509, 79.3215 (Tiruvalanchuzhi; refine against a map).
- Not verified: exact structural founding century (used 7, Tevaram era); precise temple coordinate.
