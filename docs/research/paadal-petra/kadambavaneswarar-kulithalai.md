# Kadambavaneswarar (Kadambanathar) Temple, Kulithalai (kadambavaneswarar-kulithalai)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Chola Nadu (south of the Kaveri) (Tevaram name: Tirukkadambanthurai / திருக்கடம்பந்துறை). District: Karur (town of Kulithalai, on the Kaveri). Tradition: Shaivism.

- Deity: Shiva as Kadambavaneswarar; consort **Mutrila Mulaiammai** (Tamil முற்றிலா முலையம்மை; spelled Muttrilamulaiyammai elsewhere). Self-manifested (swayambhu) lingam facing **north**; seven celestial maidens (Sapta Kannikas) shown worshipping from behind.
- Tevaram: hymns by Appar (Thirunavukkarasar); Dinamalar listing also cites Arunagirinathar (a later poet). Counted as the 65th shrine on the southern bank of the Kaveri (Dinamalar).
- Historical names: Kadambanthurai, Kuzhithandalai, Tirukkadambanthurai. Sacred tree: kadamba; tirtham: Kaveri.
- Coordinates: **town-level ~10.935, 78.418** (Kulithalai). Exact temple point not found in the sources — refine against a mapping source when possible.
- Legends (two traditions): (1) Shiva appeared before sage Kanva in the kadamba forest, giving the name Kadambanthurai (Wikipedia temple article). (2) Seven celestial maidens prayed to Shiva in the kadamba forest for protection from the demon Doomralochana; Shiva slew the demon and was called Lord of the Kadamba Forest (Wikipedia Kulithalai article; TN Temples Project gives a similar version in which the Sapta Kannikas fight the demon in Parvati's place). Prose presents both as tradition; the maidens-behind-the-linga detail is from Dinamalar.
- Architecture: modest temple with a single gopuram, entrance mandapa, two circumambulatory corridors; largely Nayak / post-Vijayanagara in character (Sriram V blog also asserts a Pallava-era foundation — not used).
- Festivals: Thai Poosam — deities of nearby Shiva temples brought to the Kaveri, ~2 lakh gather (Tamil Nadu Urban Tree); Maha Shivaratri and monthly Pradosham (general).
- Pilgrimage circuit: Kadambar Koil (Kulithalai) in the morning, Ratnagiri / Ayyarmalai in the afternoon, Thiruvengoimalai (Eengoimalai) in the evening (shivatemples.com; Team-BHP).
- Access: Kulithalai on the Tiruchirappalli–Karur road and rail line; ~34 km from Tiruchirappalli, ~42 km from Karur (shivatemples.com); nearest airport Tiruchirappalli.
- **Seed hint corrections:** the consort is Mutrila Mulaiammai (the seed's "Muktambikai" guess is not supported); the kadamba-forest legend is a sage Kanva / Doomralochana tradition, not a single authoritative story; no source ties the maidens to the "Sapta Matrikas" (they are the Sapta Kannikas).
- **Not verified:** founding dynasty and century of the structure (Nayak-era appearance; foundation unsourced); exact temple coordinate; current timings (sources differ: 6:00–1:00 and 4:30–8:30, or 5:00–7:45 pm); the goddess shrine's location within the enclosure and the Kaveri bathing practice (generic prose); Arunagirinathar's connection (single listing); the 2020 temple-land dispute (not used).
