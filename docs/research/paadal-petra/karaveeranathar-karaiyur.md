# Tirukkaraveeram (Karaveeram) — Karaveeranathar Temple

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Karaveeranathar** (also **Brahmapureeswarar**); goddess **Pratyaksha Nayagi** (also **Prathyaksha Minnammai**, **Karaveera Nayagi**). (Worklist drafted goddess "Pratyaksha Minakshi?" ≈ Prathyaksha Minnammai — corrected.)
- **Location:** Karaveeram (Tevaram **Tirukkaraveeram**), ~7–10 km west of Tiruvarur on the Kumbakonam road, Tiruvarur district, Tamil Nadu. ~10.8088 N, 79.5805 E (Wikipedia).
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam — hymned by Tirugnana Sambandar; Kaveri south bank. Sthala vriksham **karaveeram** (oleander / pon-arali).
- **Legend:** sage **Gautama** merged into the oleander tree in his devotion → lord Karaveeranathar; goddess **Pratyaksha** ("who appears before the devotee").
- **Festivals:** Maha Shivaratri (Masi); Navaratri (Purattasi); Karthikai Mondays; Pradosham.
