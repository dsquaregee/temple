# Chayavaneswarar (Sayavaneswarar) Temple, Tiruchaikkadu (chayavaneswarar-tiruchaikkadu)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Chola Nadu (north of the Kaveri) (Tevaram name: Tiruchaikkadu / Tirucchaykkadu; also Sayavanam, Chayavanam). District: Mayiladuthurai (formerly Nagapattinam), near Poompuhar (Kaveripoompattinam). Tradition: Shaivism.

- Deity: Shiva as Chayavaneswarar (Sayavaneswarar; also Rathna Chayavaneswarar); consort **Kuyilinum
  Nanmozhi Ammai** (also recorded as Kokilavani / Goshambaal).
- Tevaram: hymns by Sambandar and Appar (Wikipedia). One of the Paadal Petra Sthalams on the northern
  bank of the Kaveri. Not found in sources: a Sundarar hymn.
- Place: village of **Sayavanam**, ~2 km from the Poompuhar estuary; ~17 km from Sirkazhi and ~20 km
  from Mayiladuthurai (AroundUs; distances vary by source). Wikipedia/AroundUs coordinate
  **11.13056, 79.79000** (used; sourced, temple-level).
- Name/sthala vriksham: once a forest of **saya grass (korai)** — hence Sayavanam / Chayavaneswarar; sacred
  tree/plant: saya.
- Tirthams: the **Kaveri** itself and **Airavata Teertham**.
- Defining legend: Indra's mother **Aditi** came to earth to worship Chayavaneswarar; missing her, Indra
  traced her and resolved to uproot the entire temple and carry it to his realm with his elephant
  **Airavata**. As the lifting began, Parvati intervened with a voice sweeter than a nightingale's
  (kuyil), whence "Kuyilinum Nanmozhi Ammai"; Shiva appeared and told Indra to abandon the plan.
  Airavata Teertham was formed where the elephant dug in its tusks.
- Murugan (local tradition, single blog source): the image was carried off from Tiruchendur, later
  recovered from the sea near Poompuhar; here Murugan holds a **bow and arrow** instead of the vel.
- Kasi-equivalent: Tiruvaiyaru, Mayiladuthurai, Thiruvidaimarudur, Thiruvenkadu, Chayavanam and
  Srivanchiyam are counted as equivalents of Kasi (Wikipedia).
- Architecture: structural temple of the medieval Chola period (tradition: originally by Kochengat Chola);
  east-facing, **three-tier rajagopuram** and an **octagonal Dravida vimana** over the sanctum
  (single travelogue source); prakara mandapas added in the Maratha period; shrine of **Iyarpagai Nayanar**
  with his wife in the outer prakara, and a shrine for the three Saivite saints; tntemplesproject notes a
  "unique arch".
- Festivals: **not found in sources**; content uses general Shaiva observances and says to confirm locally.
- Access: nearest railway Sirkazhi / Mayiladuthurai; Poompuhar ~2 km. Timings not found.
- **Corrections to seed hints:** the seed suggested Chandra/moon worship and a "Kaveri sangam" legend; searches
  found **no Chandra legend**. The researched defining legend is Indra / Aditi / Airavata and the
  nightingale-voiced goddess. The Kaveri is a sacred tirtham here; a confluence-with-sea link is not
  stated in sources for the temple itself (the sea meets the Kaveri at nearby Poompuhar).
- **Not verified:** founding by Kochengat Chola; octagonal vimana and three-tier gopuram; Murugan-from-the-sea
  tradition; festival calendar (Masi, Maha Shivaratri etc.); temple timings; Chandra worship (no source);
  Iyarpagai Nayanar's link beyond the shrine (his hometown is Poompuhar, not confirmed in the sources used);
  nearest airport (stated as Tiruchirappalli, approximate).
