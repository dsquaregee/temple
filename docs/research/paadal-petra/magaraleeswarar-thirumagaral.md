# Thirumagaraleeswarar (Magaraleeswarar) Temple, Thirumagaral — research notes

Master-list row 245 (Thondai Nadu). Row facts confirmed: Magaraleeswarar / Thirumagaraleeswarar; consort Thiribhuvana Nayaki (Tribhuvananayaki), south-facing; singer Sambandar (one pathigam of 11 verses, Dinamalar).
Note: the alternative name "Agneeswarar" in the master list was not confirmed as a deity name (the tank is Agni Theertham); content does not use it.

- Place: Magaral (Thirumagaral) village, Kanchipuram district (Uthiramerur taluk area), on the northern bank of the Seyyar (Cheyyar) river; ~12–14 km north of Uthiramerur.
- 7th Thondai Nadu sthalam (shivatemples tn07).
- Temple faces east; five-tiered rajagopuram. Swayambhu lingam whose form resembles the tail of a monitor lizard (udumbu).
- Legend: the lingam is held to be the form in which Shiva appeared to the Chola king Rajendra (Rajendra Chozha) here. A temple source on Uthiramerur says Magaral is the place where Makaran (an asura) was slain — name link, tradition.
- History: original complex believed Chola; present masonry largely Vijayanagara/Nayak, 16th c. (Wikipedia).
- Theertham: Agni Theertham; sthala vriksham: lime tree (elumichai) (Dinamalar).
- Coordinates: 12.7183, 79.7547 — Wikipedia "Thirumakaraleeswarar temple" infobox (12°43′06″N 79°45′17″E).
- Festivals: not specifically confirmed; customary Shaiva observances, confirm locally.
