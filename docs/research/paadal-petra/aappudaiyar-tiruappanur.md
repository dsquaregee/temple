# Aappudaiyar (Aappanathar) Temple, Tiruappanur (aappudaiyar-tiruappanur)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Pandya Nadu (Tevaram name: Thiru Aappanoor). Tradition: Shaivism.

- Deity: Shiva as Aappudaiyar / Aappanathar (also Annavinodhar, Idabapureswarar/Vidabeshwarar in
  some listings); consort **Kuzhalvaimozhi Ammai** (owner-supplied name). Sources give the
  goddess variously as Sugantha Kundalambikai / Kuravankamazhum Kuzhalammai — all share the
  "kuzhal" element; `deity` lists the owner-supplied name only.
- Sung by Tirugnana Sambandar; counted as the second Tevaram shrine of the Pandya country.
- Location: Sellur, on the north bank of the Vaigai in Madurai city, ~5 km from the Meenakshi
  temple. Coordinates: **town-level ~9.944, 78.137** (Sellur, Madurai). Exact temple point not
  found in sources — refine against a mapping source when possible.
- Legend (the name): a Pandya king (Solandhagan / Cholanthakan in some tellings) heading to the
  Meenakshi temple found the Vaigai in flood; a wedge (**aappu**) became a Shiva linga and
  helped him cross — hence Aappudaiyar. Variant: Shiva turned river sand into rice in a famine
  (Annavinodhar).
- Architecture: Pandya foundation, Nayak enlargement; east-facing; **Somaskanda plan** with a
  Subrahmanya shrine between the Shiva and Ambal shrines, a Vinayaka under a fig between them;
  sources say the Shiva shrine has no gopuram of its own; sthala vriksham kondrai (golden
  shower); theertham the Vaigai.
- Festivals: **Panguni Uthiram** (Mar–Apr) — images of Meenakshi and Sundareswarar are brought
  to Tiruappanur from the Madurai temple; Maha Shivaratri; monthly Pradosham.
- Access: city buses 17, 17A, 17C from Periyar bus stand, stop "Tiruvaappudaiyar Kovil";
  Simmakkal stop across the river; Madurai Junction / Madurai airport.
- Timings as listed (conflicting): 6:30–11:30 / 4:30–9:00 and 6:00–11:00 / 5:00–8:00 — JSON
  uses a rounded "roughly 6:00–11:30 / 4:30–8:00" with a confirm-locally note.
- **Not verified:** the goddess's name (see above); exact century of the structural temple
  (Pandya foundation with Nayak additions, patrons not firmly sourced — century 7 used for the
  Sambandar/Tevaram era); the "no gopuram" detail rests on a single source (indianetzone) and
  is hedged in the prose ("accounts note"); king's name varies by source; exact temple
  coordinate; current timings.
