# Koneswaram Temple, Trincomalee (Thirukonamalai) — research notes

Master-list row 192 (Eezha Nadu 1). Row facts confirmed (deities listed as Konanatha Swami / Koneswarar and Mathumai). No correction.

- Place: Swami Rock (Konamalai), a cliff promontory within Fort Frederick, Trincomalee, Eastern Province, Sri Lanka.
  Tevaram name Thirukonamalai.
- One of the 276 Paadal Petra Sthalams; Eezha Nadu (with Ketheeswaram). Sambandar sang a pathigam (Thirukonamalai).
  Wikipedia also names Sundarar as mentioning it — not used (Sundarar's reference not confirmed as a pathigam). Century 7.
- Antiquity: traditions/claims trace the shrine to the centuries BCE; Kulakottan, a traditional figure, credited in an
  inscription/chronicle tradition with building a pagoda — presented as tradition only.
- Destruction: Portuguese, 1622–1624 (sources vary: 14 April 1622 at Tamil New Year under Constantino de Sá de Noronha; complete by 1624);
  a fort (later Fort Frederick) built from its debris. Described as a "thousand-pillared" shrine.
- 1950: workers digging a well unearthed buried statues believed to belong to the old temple. Present temple built by the
  local Hindu community; reconstruction from 1952 (Society for Restoration of Koneswaram), inaugurated 1963; further work 1982.
  Ancient pillars/artefacts recovered from the sea below the rock incorporated.
- Legend: Ravana, for his ailing mother, tried to lift/move the temple (or the hill); Shiva made him drop his sword, leaving the
  cleft "Ravana Vettu" (Ravana's cut) on the cliff.
- Lovers' Leap: popular name of the cliff edge (avoid elaborating).
- Festivals: not specifically sourced. Maha Shivaratri + annual festival — keep general, confirm locally.
- Coordinates: 8.5825, 81.2456 — Wikipedia "Koneswaram Temple" infobox (8°34′57″N 81°14′44″E) via search text.
  Distinct from Aathi Koneswaram at Thampalakamam (different temple).
- Getting there: through Fort Frederick to the tip of Swami Rock; Trincomalee railway station (line from Colombo);
  China Bay domestic airport; Bandaranaike International Airport (Colombo) main gateway.
