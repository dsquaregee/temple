# Chandramouleeswarar Temple, Thiruvakkarai (chandramouleeswarar-thiruvakkarai)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Thondai Nadu (Villupuram district, Tamil Nadu; Vanur taluk). Tradition: Shaivism.

- Deity: Shiva as Chandramouleeswarar (Piraisoodiya Emperuman); consort Vadivambikai /
  Amirthambikai (Amirthambigai).
- Tevaram name: Tiruvakkarai; praised by Sambandar and Sundarar (Dinamalar counts it the 30th
  Thondai Nadu Tevaram shrine).
- Coordinates: **~12.0278, 79.6522** (Thiruvakkarai village). Exact temple point not
  independently verified — refine against a mapping source when possible.
- Legend (defining story): the asura **Vakrasura**, who gained boons through penance to Shiva
  and then harassed the devas, was slain here; the goddess **Vakrakali** is honoured as the
  fierce mother who ended him, and her image is covered in sandalwood paste at the new moon.
  The river below the temple (Varaha) keeps the memory in its name. Details vary between
  sources.
- Architecture: east-facing **seven-tiered rajagopuram**; at the foot of a low hill on the
  river bank; Chandramouleeswarar sanctum, Vadivambikai/Amirthambikai shrine, separate
  Vakrakali shrine; Dravidian stone walls with medieval inscriptions. Chola-era structure
  (~10th century) with endowments from queen Sembiyan Mahadevi.
- Festivals: Chitra Pournami (Chithirai); Maha Shivaratri; Vinayaka Chaturthi; Vijayadasami;
  Karthigai Deepam; new-moon (Amavasya) sandalwood anointing of Vakrakali with large crowds.
- Access: Vanur taluk, ~30 km from Tindivanam and ~29 km from Villupuram; reachable from
  Puducherry/Auroville; next to the National Fossil Wood Park (trees ~20 million years old).
- **Not verified:** exact coordinates; details of the Vakra legend beyond the outline above;
  current timings (sources differ: one gives continuous 6:00–20:00, another 6:00–12:00 and
  17:00–20:00; confirm locally).
