# Sakkottai — Amirthakalasanathar Temple

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Amirthakalasanathar** (Amirthakadeswarar) — "lord of the pot of nectar"; goddess **Amirthavalli**.
- **Location:** Sakkottai, ~4.5 km from Kumbakonam (Kumbakonam–Needamangalam road), Thanjavur district, Tamil Nadu. Approx 10.9383 N, 79.4206 E (approximate).
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam — **Sundarar** sang ten verses (7th Tirumurai).
- **Names/legends:** amrita descended in a kalayam (pot) → Amirthakalasanathar / old name **Kalayanallur**; later name **Sakkottai** from Sakkiyar-kottai (Buddhist-era past, noted in Tevaram). Linked to **Sakya Nayanar** (worshipped by casting a stone daily before his Shaiva conversion).
- **Fabric:** Chola (9th c.) + Thanjavur Nayak additions. Festivals: Maha Shivaratri, Masi Magam, Margazhi Tiruvadhirai.
