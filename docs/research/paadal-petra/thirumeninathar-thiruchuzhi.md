# Thirumeninathar Temple, Tiruchuli (Thiruchuzhiyal) — thirumeninathar-thiruchuzhi

Master-list row 205 (Pandya Nadu 12).

**Correction:** the master list gives singers "Sambandar, Sundarar". Sources (shivatemples pn12, Wikipedia, Dinamalar) give only **Sundarar — 1 pathigam (7th Tirumurai)**. No source links Sambandar. Content names Sundarar only; `century` = 8.

- Place: Tiruchuli (Thiruchuzhiyal), Virudhunagar district; ~15 km east of Aruppukottai, ~45 km south of Madurai; on the Virudhunagar–Manamadurai road; Tiruchchuli railway station on the Virudhunagar–Manamadurai line via Aruppukottai.
- Deity: Shiva as Thirumeninathar, also Bhoominathar; also called Pralaya Vidangar, with a shrine of Pralaya Vidangar. Consort: Thunaimalai Nayaki.
- Legend: in an age of deluge (pralaya) Shiva hurled/planted his trident and the floodwaters drained into the earth (or were drawn back); variant ties it to the Pandya king Bruhat Balan, freed of brahmahatti. Alternate names Chuzhigai / Chuzhikapuri (link to "suzhi", whirl, is inference — content phrases as "the name is linked by tradition…"? — not used).
- Tank: Kavvaikadal (Kauvaikkadal), opposite the temple (reported dry in 2024).
- Tradition (Dinamalar): Sundarar stayed in a mutt here.
- Ramana Maharshi was born at Tiruchuli (30 Dec 1879); his birthplace house, Sundara Mandiram, stands near the temple.
- Coordinates: **unresolved for the temple itself.** Wikipedia's temple infobox (9.700, 78.483) is ~35 km ENE of Tiruchuli town and is a bad geocode. Used the Tiruchuli town-centre point from Wikipedia's Tiruchuli article: 9.5348, 78.2008 (the temple stands in the town; railway station 9.5379, 78.1989). Needs a map check.
