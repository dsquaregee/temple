# Tirukkottaru — Airavateswarar Temple

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Airavateswarar** (the lord worshipped by **Airavata**, Indra's white elephant); goddess **Vandar Poonkuzhali** (also **Sugandha Kundalambikai**). (Worklist left goddess "?" — supplied from sources.)
- **Location:** Tirukkottaru (historical **Tirukkottaaru**), ~14 km from Karaikal, ~5 km from Tirunallaru, ~12 km E of Peralam, ~23 km from Mayiladuthurai. District varies: **Tiruvarur (Tamil Nadu)** in most listings; Karaikal in one. ~10.8900 N, 79.7800 E (approximate).
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam — hymned by Tirugnana Sambandar; Kaveri south bank.
- **Legend:** sage **Durvasa**'s garland → Indra → placed on **Airavata**; by the garland's slighting and curse, Airavata is parted from grace and here worships Shiva to regain it → name Airavateswarar.
- **Festivals:** Maha Shivaratri (Masi); Navaratri (Purattasi); Karthikai Mondays; Pradosham.
