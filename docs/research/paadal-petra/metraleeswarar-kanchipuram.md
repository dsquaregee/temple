# Thirumetrali Nathar (Metraleeswarar) Temple, Kanchipuram (Tirukkachi Metrali) — research notes

Master-list row 240 (Thondai Nadu). Row facts confirmed in part: deity Thirumetrali Nathar / Metraleeswarar; singers Appar and Sundarar (one pathigam each).
Correction: the master list gives the consort as "Thirumetrali Nayaki"; sources name the goddess Kamakshi (shivatemples, Wikipedia) or Parasakthi, in her own east-facing shrine (Dinamalar). Content uses "Kamakshi (Parasakthi)".

- Place: Thirumetrali Street, Pillaiyarpalayam, Kanchipuram 631501; ~1 km from Kanchipuram bus stand; near the Vegavathi river.
- Counted as the 2nd Thondai Nadu sthalam on shivatemples.com (tn02).
- Pathigams: Tirunavukkarasar (Appar) 1, Sundarar 1.
- Name: Lord faces west (merku) → Metrali / Mettraleeswarar (Dinamalar).
- Second lingam: Odha-Urugeesar (Othavurugeesar); the rajagopuram and main entrance face this shrine; Nandi in front of it receives Pradosham worship.
- Legend (shivatemples): Vishnu wished to attain the form of a linga; Shiva told him to worship the west-facing swayambhu linga; Vishnu did penance on the bank of the Vegavathi facing it; Sambandar, on pilgrimage, sang a pathigam from a distance; moved by the hymn Vishnu melted ("urugu") into a linga with his feet still visible — Odha-Urugeesar. Wikipedia variant: Shiva himself was charmed by Sambandar's singing and turned west to listen, hence Thirumetrali. Present as tradition, both strands.
- Administered by Tamil Nadu HR&CE.
- Coordinates: 12.835, 79.692 — Wikipedia "Metraleeswar temple" infobox (12°50′06″N 79°41′30″E → 12.8350, 79.6917). Distinct from Kachabeswarar (12.838, 79.701) and Kailasanathar points.
- Festivals: not specifically confirmed; Pradosham at the Odha-Urugeesar Nandi; customary Shaiva observances, confirm locally.
