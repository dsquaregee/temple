# Tirukkalar — Parijata Vaneswarar Temple

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Parijata Vaneswarar** (lord of the parijata grove); also praised as **Kalarmulainathar / Kalarumalainathar** ("lord of the dance floor"); goddess **Amudhavalli**.
- **Location:** Tirukkalar, near Mannargudi, Tiruvarur district, Tamil Nadu. Approx 10.5717 N, 79.5542 E (approximate).
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam — sung by Sambandar and Appar.
- **Signature legend:** Sage **Durvasa** brought the **parijata** flower from devaloka to see Shiva's dance; Shiva performed the **Brahma Tandavam** here. Durvasa said to have done the first consecration. Place name ~ "dance floor".
- **Fabric:** Dravidian temple; tall **five-tiered east rajagopuram** (~80 ft).
