# Pralayakaleswarar (Sudarkozhundeesar) Temple, Pennadam — research notes

Master-list row 217 (Nadu Nadu 2; shivatemples nn02). CORRECTION: singers — the master list gives Appar only; shivatemples lists Thirunavukkarasar 1 pathigam and Sambandar 1 pathigam. Both named in content. Consort names: Kadanthai Nayaki, also Amodhana/Amudhanayaki.

- Place: Pennadam (Pennagadam / Pennakadam), Cuddalore district, PIN 606105; about 18 km SW of Virudhachalam on the Virudhachalam–Tittagudi road. Tevaram name: the town Kadanthai; the temple Thoongaanai Maadam ("the sleeping-elephant shrine").
- One of the 276 Paadal Petra Sthalams; Nadu Nadu 2 in the traditional order. Appar and Sambandar (7th c.).
- Moolavar: Sudarkozhundeesar ("the radiant flame"), also Pralayakaleswarar. Ambal: Kadanthai Nayaki (Amodhanayaki).
- Architecture: the sanctum roof (vimana) is gajaprishta — shaped like an elephant's back — hence Thoongaanai Maadam. Nandi (Pradosha Nandi / Adhikara Nandi) faces away from the sanctum, towards the town/entrance.
- Legend (Pralaya): when the deluge threatened Pennadam, Shiva bade Nandi turn around and swallow the waters, saving the temple and people — hence Pralayakaleswarar and the turned Nandi. Folk belief: the bull guards the town from floods.
- Legend (Appar): Appar, returned to Shaivism from Jainism, prayed here to be marked with Shiva's emblems; he was blessed with the marks of the bull (rishabha) and trident (soolam) on his shoulders. (Tradition / Periya Puranam.)
- Name (tradition): Penn (celestial maidens), Aa (Kamadhenu) and Kadam (the elephant Airavata) worshipped here → Pennagadam. Present as tradition.
- Saints: birthplace of Kalikamba Nayanar (one of the 63). Meykandar's birth here is claimed but marked citation-needed — not used.
- Coordinates: 11.4025, 79.2567 — Wikipedia "Sudarkozhundeeswarar Temple, Pennadam" infobox (11°24′09″N 79°15′24″E). Distinct from Thiruvattathurai (6 km SW).
- Festivals: not specifically sourced — Pradosham (apt, Pradosha Nandi), Maha Shivaratri, Arudra; confirm locally.
