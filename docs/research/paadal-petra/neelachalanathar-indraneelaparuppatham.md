# Indraneelaparuppatham (Neelachalanathar) — research notes

Master-list row 273 (Vada Nadu 2). Id marked `?` in the master list (uncertain identification); id used without the `?`. IDENTIFICATION IS TRADITIONAL AND DISPUTED: templenet (and koyil.com lists) place Indraneelaparvatam "near Kathmandu", Nepal; the Veludharan blog places it at Badrinath (Chamoli, Uttarakhand), noting the Nepal claim. Master list follows the Badrinath-region identification; content presents it as tradition and mentions the Nepal alternative.

- Sung by Thirugnana Sambandar — one pathigam (Tamil Wikisource commentary note: "ஞானசம்பந்தரால் பாடப்பட்டது. இதற்கு ஒரே பதிகம் உளது"). Templenet: Sambandar sang it from Kalahasti; none of the Nayanmars visited (tradition).
- Sambandar's phrase: the blue mountain that Indra worships ("இந்திரன் கைதொழு நீல் பருப்பதம்"). Name explained by clouds resting on the peak giving it a blue look.
- Deity: Neelachalanathar; Goddess: Neelambigai (Wikisource note; templenet). Theertham: Indra Theertham (templenet).
- Legends: Indra worshipped Shiva here; Arjuna performed penance and received the Pashupata weapon; the demon Mukasura (the boar of the Kirata episode) died here.
- Wikisource note: said to lie on the way to Kedarnath.
- No built Tevaram-era temple is recorded at the site.
- Coordinates: 30.72, 79.40 — Nilkantha peak above Badrinath, Wikipedia "Nilkantha (mountain)" (30°43′12″N 79°24′00″E; mountain-forecast 30.72 N 79.40 E). Used as the point of the traditional Badrinath-region identification (the "blue mountain" Neelkanth). Distinct from the catalog's Badrinath temple (30.7433, 79.4938). Flag: identification unsettled.
