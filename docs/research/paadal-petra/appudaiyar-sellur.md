# Appudaiyar Temple, Sellur (Thiru Aappanoor) — research notes

Master-list row 195 (Pandya Nadu 2). Row facts confirmed; no corrections.

- Place: Sellur, a neighbourhood of Madurai on the north side of the Vaigai, reached from Simmakkal across the river. Tevaram name Thiru Aappanoor (Thiruappanur).
- One of the 276 Paadal Petra Sthalams; 2nd Pandya Nadu shrine in the traditional order (shivatemples pn02). Sung by Thirugnana Sambandar (a pathigam on Thiru Aappanoor). No other singer confirmed.
- Moolavar: Aappudaiyar; also called Annavinodhar and Vidabeshwarar (Idabeswarar). Lingam.
- Ambal: Kuravankamazh Kuzhali (Kuravamkamazhkuzhalal), Sanskrit equivalent Sugandha Kundalambigai ("she whose tresses are fragrant with kuravam flowers").
- Sthala vriksham: kondrai (Indian laburnum). Theertham: the Vaigai river.
- Legend 1 (name): Pandya king Cholanthakan would not eat before worshipping Shiva; on a journey (hunting in the forest / unable to reach Madurai, Vaigai in flood) he collapsed with hunger; a minister pointed to a wooden wedge/post (aappu) and called it a lingam; the king worshipped it and Shiva manifested in it — "Aappudaiyar", the Lord of the wedge.
- Legend 2: in a famine, a priest grew a little paddy for the offering; resentment; Shiva told him to cook the sand of the Vaigai, which turned into rice — Annavinodhar.
- Local tradition counts five Madurai-area Shiva temples as the pancha bhoota set; Sellur = water (one source only, presented as local tradition).
- Architecture: modest; one source says there is no tall gopuram; the gateway carries a stucco image of Shiva and Parvati on the bull (Rishabharudar). Begun by the Pandyas, expanded by the Nayaks; Ramnad Sethupathis contributed.
- Festivals: customary Shaiva observances (Maha Shivaratri, Pradosham, Arudra Darshanam, Aippasi Annabhishekam is apt but not confirmed specifically — not used). Keep festivals general + confirm locally.
- Coordinates: 9.9306, 78.1228 — Wikipedia "Thiru Aappanoor" infobox (9°55′50″N 78°07′22″E). Bharatpedia gives 9°55′10″N 78°07′10″E (which would be south of the river, inconsistent with Sellur) — not used. Distinct from Koodal Azhagar and Meenakshi entries (both south of the river).
