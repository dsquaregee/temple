# Sundareswarar Temple, Tirukkalikkamur (sundareswarar-tirukkalikkamur)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Chola Nadu (north of the Kaveri) (Tevaram name: Tirukkalikkamur; also Kalikamoor / Tirukkalikkamoor). District: Mayiladuthurai (formerly Nagapattinam), Sirkazhi block. Tradition: Shaivism.

- Deity: Shiva as Sundareswarar (also Vilvavana Nathar); consort Azhagammai (also recorded as
  Sundarambal / Azhaguvana Mulaiyammai).
- Tevaram: hymn of Sambandar (listed as Sambandar, first collection / "Pathigam Sambandar-1");
  one of the Paadal Petra Sthalams on the northern bank of the Kaveri. Not found in sources: any
  hymn by Appar or Sundarar here.
- Place: village of **Annappanpettai** (Thennampattinam post, Mangaimadam), ~14 km from Sirkazhi on the
  Sirkazhi–Thiruvenkadu route; town buses Sirkazhi–Konaiyampattinam pass through.
- Coordinates: **approximate, village-level ~11.17, 79.79** (Mangaimadam / Thiruvenkadu area). Wikipedia's
  "Annappanpettai" page gives 10.84, 79.20 but that is a different Annappanpettai (Thanjavur
  district) — do not use. Refine against a mapping source.
- Defining legend: the sage **Parasara** worshipped Shiva here (carved/depicted in the temple); at the
  sage's request Shiva stayed on as Sundareswarar; the area was dense with vilva trees, hence the name
  Vilvavana Nathar; Shiva granted darshan to the sage Sakti's son (Parasara). The place name
  Kalikamoor is explained as the place where Shiva removes the hardships of *kali* (strife).
- Architecture: a **small** temple with **no rajagopuram and no kodimaram (flagstaff)**; a graceful
  **arch at the entrance**; Navagrahas stand upright **without their vahanas**; Vinayaka as Selva
  Siddhi Vinayaka; goddess in her own shrine.
- History: tradition says the temple might date to the 6th century CE, was reconstructed in stone by the
  Cholas in the 10th century CE, and extensively renovated by the Marathas (single-source, treat as
  tradition). Century 7 used for the Tevaram era.
- Festivals: not found in sources. Content uses only general Shaiva observances (Maha Shivaratri,
  Pradosham, Tamil-calendar utsavams) and says to confirm locally.
- Access: nearest railway Sirkazhi / Mayiladuthurai; reported timings ~8:00–10:00 and 17:00–19:30.
- **Corrections to seed hints:** goddess researched as Azhagammai. A Wikipedia-derived snippet claimed
  Azhagammai alone goes to the sea for Masi Maham theerthavari and a fisherman's-net story; a second
  search attributed the fishermen/Parvati story to a different temple (Tiruvettakudi), so it was
  **left out**. Any link to Eyarkon Kalikkama Nayanar (born at Tiruperumangalam) is not supported.
- **Not verified:** exact temple coordinate; founding century/dynasty (6th-century origin, Chola 10th-century
  rebuild, Maratha renovation are single-source tradition); festival calendar; Masi Maham theerthavari
  practice; whether the sanctum faces east; precise timings; nearest airport distance (stated as
  Tiruchirappalli/Chennai, approximate).
