# Sishtagurunatheswarar (Pasupatheeswarar) Temple, Thiruthuraiyur — research notes

Master-list row 230 (Nadu Nadu). Row facts confirmed (Dinamalar, shivatemples nn15): Lord Sishtagurunathar / Pasupatheeswarar; Ambal Poongothai Nayaki and Sivaloka Nayaki. Wikipedia gives the consort as "Thyagavalliammai" and says the shrine was praised by Appar — both contradicted by the other sources (Sundarar's pathigam on Thiruthuraiyur, extolling the Then Pennai); not used.

- Place: Thiruthuraiyur (Tirutturaiyur), Panruti taluk, Cuddalore district, PIN 607205; ~9–10 km north of Panruti via Pudupettai and Chinnapettai; ~16 km from Villupuram, ~35 km from Cuddalore (tntemplesproject). Near the Then Pennai.
- Paadal Petra Sthalam, Nadu Nadu (15th per shivatemples numbering nn15). Sung by Sundarar; his hymn extols the sanctity of the Then Pennai and the grace of the Lord of Thiruthuraiyur. Century 8.
- Theertham: Surya Pushkarini (Dinamalar).
- Legend (shivatemples): coming from Thiruvennainallur, Sundarar could not cross the flooded Then Pennai; he prayed and sang; Shiva and Parvati came as an elderly couple and ferried him across, then gave him darshan as Rishabaroodar (on the bull). Name Sishtagurunathar — the Lord as guru who taught (traditional explanation: Sundarar asked for "thavaneri", the path of penance, and the Lord instructed him) — present as tradition.
- Arulnandi Sivachariyar (disciple of Meykandar; one of the four Santhana acharyas): area associated with his birth; a memorial to him is nearby (visitor review + shivatemples) — present as "remembered as".
- Access: regular buses from Panruti.
- Coordinates: 11.860, 79.550 — **APPROXIMATE ESTIMATE**, no published coordinate found (Wikipedia snippet without coordinates; no village coordinate in search results; OSM/Wikidata unreachable).
