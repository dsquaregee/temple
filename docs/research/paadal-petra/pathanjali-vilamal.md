# Tiruvilamar (Vilamal) — Pathanjali Manoharar Temple

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Pathanjali Manoharar** ("he who charmed the sage Patanjali"); goddess **Madhurabhashini** (also **Thenmozhi Ammai**; **Yazhinum Menmozhiyaal**, "words softer than the lute"). (Worklist drafted goddess "Mridubhashini" — corrected to Madhurabhashini/Thenmozhi.)
- **Location:** Vilamal (Tevaram **Tiruvilamar**), on the Odampokki river, ~1–3 km from Tiruvarur, Tiruvarur district, Tamil Nadu. ~10.7800 N, 79.6300 E (approximate). Tank **Agni Teertham**.
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam — hymned by Tirugnana Sambandar; Kaveri south bank.
- **Theme:** name ties the lord to **Patanjali**, sage of the Yoga Sutras, a witness of Shiva's cosmic dance.
- **Festivals:** Maha Shivaratri (Masi); Navaratri (Purattasi); Karthikai Mondays; Pradosham.
