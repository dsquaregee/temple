# Tiruppereyil (Ogai Perayur) — Jagadeeswarar Temple

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Jagadeeswarar** ("lord of the universe"); goddess **Jagan Nayaki** (also **Pennamirtha Nayaki**). (Worklist left goddess "?" — supplied from sources.)
- **Location:** Ogai Perayur (Tevaram **Tiruppereyil**), ~13 km from Tiruvarur, ~21 km from Mannargudi, Tiruvarur district, Tamil Nadu. ~10.8000 N, 79.5300 E (approximate).
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam — hymned by Appar; Kaveri south bank (~114th). Sthala vriksham **narthai** (citron); tank **Agni Theertham**.
- **Name/history:** **Pereyil** = "great rampart"; tradition of an early-Chola fort here when Tiruvarur was a seat of Chola power.
- **Festivals:** Margazhi Tiruvadhirai; Maha Shivaratri (Masi); Karthikai Mondays; Pradosham.
