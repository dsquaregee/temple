# Karkadeswarar Temple, Tirunthudevankudi / Nandankoil (karkadeswarar-tirunthudevankudi)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Chola Nadu (north of the Kaveri), Thanjavur district (Kumbakonam taluk; near Thiruvidaimarudur and Thiruvisanallur). Tradition: Shaivism.

- Deity: Shiva as **Karkadeswarar** (Karkateeswarar; older tradition also Oushadeeswarar); consort **Arumarundhu Nayaki** (also Apoorvanayaki / Arumarundammai — "lady of the rare medicine"). Utsavar: Somaskandar (Dinamalar).
- Tevaram name: **Tirunthudevankudi** (Tirundudevankudi / Thirundhuthevankudi); historical names Karkateeswaram and Nandankoil (officially Nandaangkovil). Sung by **Tirugnanasambandar**; Dinamalar lists it as the 42nd shrine on the Kaveri's northern bank. Shivatemples.com page: nc42.
- Coordinates: **approximate ~10.98, 79.44** (village area between Kumbakonam and Thiruvidaimarudur; Thiruvidaimarudur town is ~10°59′N 79°28′E). No sourced temple coordinate found. Refine against a mapping source.
- Defining legend: gandharva mocks sage Durvasa and is cursed to be born a crab; told to worship Shiva here with a lotus from the temple tank daily; Indra (offering 1,008 lotuses daily) finds one missing, sees the crab at the linga and strikes with his sword; Shiva opens a hole in the linga so the crab can enter; the gandharva is freed, Indra is corrected and pardoned (the name Tirunthudevankudi read as the place where the Devan was "set right"); sword mark said to be visible on the linga. Variant (shivatemples.com): the crab is Parvati in disguise. Further tradition (thanjavurparampara): the Lord, Oushadeeswarar, took an old man's form to cure a Chola king's infectious disease; explains goddess name Arumarundhu Nayaki.
- Architecture: water-filled moat on three sides, east open; brick temple later rebuilt in stone in the Chola period; sanctum with Karkadeswarar linga (crab hole, sword mark); separate goddess shrine; Yoga Chandra idol (Karkataka rasi / Ashlesha link); tank called Karkata Theertham; Somaskandar utsavar.
- Festivals: Maha Shivaratri (Feb–Mar); Tirukarthikai (Nov–Dec); monthly Pradosham (generic). Devotional sources: Ashlesha (Aayilyam) / Karkataka-rasi births and Moon-related relief.
- Access: Nandankoil ~7–10 km from Kumbakonam (sources conflict: 10 km from Kumbakonam bus stand; 7 km; ~2 km from Thiruvidaimarudur; ~1.5–2 km from Thiruvisanallur); Kumbakonam nearest railway/bus hub; Tiruchirappalli nearest airport (~100 km, own estimate). Postal: Nandankovil, Thiruvisalur Post, Kumbakonam Taluk, PIN 612105. Hours conflict (9:00–1:30 & 4:00–7:00 vs 8:30–6:30).
- Seed corrections: none to deity/goddess/Tevaram name. Wikipedia lists the goddess as Apoorvanayaki with Arumarundhu Nayaki as an alternate name; prose uses Arumarundhu Nayaki (Apoorvanayaki) per the brief. The legend is a Durvasa/gandharva/Indra story, not a plain "crab worship" story.
- **Not verified:** exact temple coordinate; builder/century of the stone temple (Chola period only); the "golden crab emerging during Aadi Amavasya abhishekam" claim (Vasishta Mahatmiyam, flagged citation-needed on Wikipedia; omitted); the Navapashana Theertham and nangai sthala vriksham listed by Dinamalar (conflicting with Karkata Theertham tank name elsewhere; not used); abhishekam water as a remedy (devotional sites; omitted); the Oushadeeswarar/Chola king legend (single secondary source); current timings and exact distance from Kumbakonam; Tiruchirappalli distance.
