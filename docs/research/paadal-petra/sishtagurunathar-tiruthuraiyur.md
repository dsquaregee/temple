# Sishtagurunathar (Pasupatheeswarar) Temple, Tiruthuraiyur (sishtagurunathar-tiruthuraiyur)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Nadu Nadu (Tevaram name: Tiruthuraiyur / Tirutturaiyur).
Tradition: Shaivism.

- Deity: Shiva as Sishtagurunathar (Sishtagurunatheswarar) / Pasupatheeswarar; also
  praised as **Thava Neri Aaludayar** ("master of the way of penance"). Consort:
  **Poongothai Nayaki, also Sivaloka Nayaki** (owner-supplied; matches
  shivatemples/dinamalar). **Note:** Wikipedia names the consort **Thyagavalliammai** —
  a discrepancy between sources; the owner-supplied Poongothai/Sivalokanayaki was used.
- Counted among the 275 Paadal Petra Sthalams; listed as the **15th Shiva temple of the
  Nadu Nadu region** praised in the Tevaram. Sung by **Sundarar** (1 pathikam) —
  Sundarar places it in the **8th century**, hence `century: 8`.
- **Legend (the temple's defining story):** Sundarar, travelling from Tiruvennainallur,
  reached the river in flood and could not cross. He prayed and sang a pathikam; Shiva
  and Parvati appeared as an elderly couple and ferried him across; a celestial voice
  then bade him look up and he beheld them as **Rishabarudar** on the bull. In another
  account he asked the Lord to teach the rules of penance, so the Lord is praised as
  **Sishta Gurunathar** and **Thava Neri Aaludayar**. (shivatemples / tntemplesproject.)
- Architecture (thin; kept general): entered through a **single gateway without a
  rajagopuram** (sourced, shivatemples); path to flagstaff, balipitha and Nandi; a
  **large Shiva linga** in the sanctum; sthala vriksha the **kondrai** (Indian laburnum);
  temple tank **Surya Pushkarini** (sthala theertham, said to lie outside the premises).
- **Chithirai sun alignment:** the evening sun falls on the deity in the first week of
  Chithirai (Apr–May) — Dinamalar.
- Festivals (Dinamalar listing): **Vaikasi Brahmotsavam** (10 days, May–Jun, around
  Vaikasi Visakam); **Aipasi Skanda Sashti + Annabhishekam** (Oct–Nov); **Maha
  Shivaratri** (Masi, Feb–Mar); monthly Pradosham.
- Timings (Dinamalar): **6:00–10:00 a.m. and 5:00–8:00 p.m.** (single listing — phrased
  as "roughly"; confirm locally). Phone listed: +91-4142-248498.
- Access: **~9–10 km north of Panruti** via Pudupettai and Chinnapettai; PIN 607205,
  Panruti Taluk, Cuddalore district. Panruti nearest town/railway; Cuddalore (district
  HQ) ~40 km.
- **Coordinates: town-level estimate ~11.85, 79.55** (Panruti town is ~11.77, 79.55; the
  village lies ~9–10 km north). **Exact temple point not found** in sources — refine
  against a mapping source when possible.
- **Not verified:** founding dynasty/century of the structural temple (used century 8
  for the Sundarar/Tevaram era; later patrons not firmly sourced); exact temple
  coordinate; consort name discrepancy (Poongothai/Sivalokanayaki vs. Wikipedia's
  Thyagavalliammai); architectural/stylistic history beyond the single-gateway note.
