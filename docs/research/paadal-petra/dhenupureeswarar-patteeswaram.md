# Dhenupureeswarar Temple, Patteeswaram (dhenupureeswarar-patteeswaram)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Chola Nadu south of the Kaveri. Tradition: Shaivism.
- Deity: Shiva as Dhenupureeswarar (Thenupureeswarar); consort Gnanambikai (also Palvalai Nayaki).
- Famous for its majestic standing eight-armed Durga shrine.
- Legend: Patti, calf of Kamadhenu, made a sand linga and bathed it in milk → village "Patteeswaram"; Parvati's penance here served by Patti.
- Sung by Thirugnana Sambandar; ~9 km SW of Kumbakonam, ~3 km SE of Swamimalai.
- Coordinates: ~10.9436, 79.3348 (Patteeswaram village; temple point not separately found — refine against a map).
- Not verified: exact structural founding century (used 7, Tevaram era); precise temple coordinate.
