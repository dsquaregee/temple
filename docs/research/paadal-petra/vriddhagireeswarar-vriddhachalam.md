# Vriddhagireeswarar Temple (vriddhagireeswarar-vriddhachalam)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Nadu Nadu. Tradition: Shaivism.

- Deity: Vriddhagireeswarar (Pazhamalainathar); Vriddhambikai (Periyanayaki).
- Vriddhachalam, Cuddalore district (Tevaram: Tirumudukunram; a large major temple). Coords 11.516, 79.324. Praised by all three Tevaram saints.
- Legends: the ancient hill (Pazhamalai) of Brahma's creation; and Sundarar's gold, given up by the river and returned by the temple tank.
