# Vriddhagireeswarar Temple, Vriddhachalam (Thirumuthukundram) — research notes

Master-list row 224 (Nadu Nadu). Confirmed: Pazhamalainathar / Vriddhagireeswarar; consort Periyanayaki (Vriddhambigai), with Balambigai (Ilaya Nayaki) also listed; sung by Appar, Sambandar and Sundarar. No corrections.

- Place: Vriddhachalam (Virudhachalam) town, Cuddalore district; temple in the town centre near the Manimuktha river. Tevaram name Thirumuthukundram ("the old hill"); Sanskrit Vriddhachalam / Vriddhagiri.
- One of the 276 Paadal Petra Sthalams; 9th in Nadu Nadu (shivatemples nn09).
- Deity names: Vriddhagiriswarar, Pazhamalainathar, Vriddhachaleswarar, Mudukundreeswarar (five names per Dinamalar). Ambal: Periyanayaki / Vriddhambigai, Balambigai.
- Five sages including Vibhasitthu worshipped here (Dinamalar). Not used in detail.
- Sundarar legend: for his pathigam, Shiva gave him 12,000 gold coins; Sundarar feared carrying them; the Lord had him cast them into the Manimuktha with a promise he would receive them from the tank at Tiruvarur (Kamalalayam).
- Theerthams: Manimuktha river (near north gopuram), Nityananda koopam, Agni, Chakra, Kubera theerthams.
- Architecture: five prakarams (enclosures); five Vinayaka shrines including Aazhathu Vinayagar (sunken shrine). Gopuram count not confirmed — not stated.
- History: inscription of Uttama Chola's reign (c. 982 CE) records Sembiyan Mahadevi's construction of parts of the temple (central shrine and sub-shrines); several Chola-period inscriptions.
- Festivals: three yearly festivals; brahmotsavam culminating on Masi Magam is the chief, with large crowds bathing in the Manimuktha.
- Century: 7 (Appar, Sambandar).
- Coordinates: 11.5169, 79.3195 — Justdial map listing for Sri Virudhagireeswarar Temple (11.5168624, 79.3194533), via search text; consistent with the town-centre point (Wikipedia 11.50, 79.33) and the temple's central location. Wikipedia/Wikidata temple point 11°18′N 79°12′E is ~25 km SW, outside the town — rejected as erroneous. UNVERIFIED against an independent map — flag in report. Distinct from Rajendrapattinam (11.424, 79.348). No other catalog entry in Vriddhachalam.
