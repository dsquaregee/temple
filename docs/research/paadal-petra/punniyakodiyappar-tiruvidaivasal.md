# Tiruvidaivasal — Punniyakodiyappar Temple

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Punniyakodiyappar**; goddess **Abirami** (Thanjavur Paramapara; worklist goddess "Abirami" — confirmed). The worklist's alt name "Idaivainathar" is unverified in sources (one search flagged it as belonging to a different temple), so the body leads with Punniyakodiyappar and keeps Idaivainathar only as a parenthetical alt in the deity field.
- **Location:** **Kovilur**, near the Vennar bridge, Koradacheri–Koothanallur road, Tiruvarur district. ~10.8833 N, 79.1343 E (single-aggregator figure — approximate).
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam — hymned by **Sambandar**; its lost Tevaram verse was **recovered in 1917**, completing the roll at **275**. Sthala vriksham **kasturi arali**.
- **Festivals:** Maha Shivaratri (Masi); Navaratri (Purattasi); Karthikai Mondays; Pradosham.
