# Panankatteeswarar Temple, Panaiyapuram (panankatteeswarar-panankattur)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Nadu Nadu (Tevaram name: **Puravar Panankattur**).
Tradition: Shaivism. Village Panaiyapuram, Villupuram district, Tamil Nadu.

- Deity: Shiva as **Panankatteeswarar / Panankatteesar**. Consort **Sathyambikai**, also
  **Puravammai** (and Meyyambal). Classed as the 20th of the Nadu Nadu Paadal Petra
  Sthalams; praised by **Tirugnana Sambandar**, padigam in the **Second Thirumurai**.
- **CONSORT DISCREPANCY (flagged):** the task brief named the consort **Amirthavalli**.
  Multiple sources (shivatemples.com, Dinamalar, tntemplesproject.in) give the Panaiyapuram
  goddess as **Sathyambikai / Puravammai** — *not* Amirthavalli. Amirthavalli is the consort
  of a *different* temple, **Tiruppanangadu / Tiruppanankattur** (Thalapureeswarar, a
  separate "Panankatteeswarar" associated with Sundarar, near Tiruvannamalai/Kancheepuram).
  I used the **sourced name (Sathyambikai/Puravammai)** and flag the brief's Amirthavalli as
  a probable mix-up of the two temples. Confirm with the owner if Amirthavalli was intended.
- Sthala vriksham: **palm (panai)** — the name Panankattur = "palm forest."
- Legend (defining story): **Surya**, after attending **Daksha's yagam**, lost his radiance
  and regained it by worshipping Shiva here. For the first several days of **Chithirai**
  (Tamil new year, April) the sunrise falls on the sanctum (the Lord) and then on the
  goddess — a built-in solar alignment.
- Origins: **Pallava era** (before the hymns), **rebuilt in stone under the Cholas**.
  Century set to **7** for the Tevaram/Sambandar era.
- Coordinates: **TOWN-LEVEL, approximate — ~12.01, 79.54** (flag). No exact temple point
  found. Panaiyapuram/Panayapuram BO postal listing gives ~12.01, 79.54 (PIN 605601 in that
  listing; shivatemples gives PIN 605603); near **Mundiyampakkam** (~12.0, 79.52) and
  **Vikravandi**. **Refine against a mapping source** when possible.
- Access: ~13 km from **Villupuram** (nearest major railway junction / bus hub), near
  Vikravandi and Mundiyampakkam, off the Villupuram highways.
- **Prose kept general** on architecture, the goddess shrine's layout, and the festival
  roster beyond the Chithirai sun-alignment — village-shrine detail is thin in the sources.
- **Heritage note (not used in prose, unverified/undated):** a report said NH45C widening
  threatened the sanctum; local objection led to an alternative route. Date unclear — do not
  present as current.
- **Not verified:** exact temple coordinate; precise structural dating beyond
  "Pallava origin, Chola stone rebuild"; full festival calendar; current timings; the exact
  wording/identity tying "Puravar" to the goddess name Puravammai.
