# Kalyana Vigirtheeswarar Temple, Venjamakoodal — research notes

Master-list row 215 (Kongu Nadu 7; shivatemples kn7). Row facts confirmed, with naming variants:
moolavar Kalyana Vigirtheeswarar / Vikriteswarar / Vigirthanatheswarar; Ambal Vigirthanayaki / Vikritambika / Vigirtheswari,
also Panmozhi Ammai (Panner Mozhi Ammai, Dinamalar). No correction needed.

- Place: Venjamakoodal (Venjamangudalur), Karur district, about 16 km south-west of Karur on the way to Aravakurichi; on the
  east bank of the Kudaganaru, at its confluence with the Chittaru (one source).
- One of the 276 Paadal Petra Sthalams, Kongu Nadu. Sung by Sundarar; Arunagirinathar also sang (Murugan, Tiruppugazh). Century 8.
- Name: a hunter king Venchan is said to have worshipped Shiva here (Wikipedia) — Venjan + koodal (confluence).
- Legend: Sundarar came asking for gold; Shiva appeared as an old man/priest; in one account the Lord pledged his two sons
  (Vinayaka and Murugan) to an old woman — Parvati herself — to raise the gold for Sundarar.
- Architecture: 5-tier rajagopuram (Wikipedia); temple said to be more than 1,200 years old (Wikipedia) — treated as approximate.
- Festivals: not specifically sourced; customary Shaiva observances (Maha Shivaratri, Pradosham, Arudra) + confirm locally.
- Coordinates: 10.8153, 77.9956 — Wikipedia "Venjamakoodal" infobox (10°48′55″N 77°59′44″E) via search text; consistent
  with ~16 km SW of Karur.
- Getting there: Karur railway junction (~16 km); Tiruchirappalli airport (~95 km).
