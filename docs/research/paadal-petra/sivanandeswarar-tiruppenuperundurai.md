# Tiruppenuperundurai — Sivanandeswarar Temple

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Sivanandeswarar** (Pranaveswarar); goddess **Mangalambikai** (also Malayarasi Ammai).
- **Location:** **Tiruppenuperundurai**, near **Nachiyarkoil**, S bank of the Arisilar, ~10–12 km from Kumbakonam; Thanjavur district. ~10.93 N, 79.46 E (approximate).
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam (64th, Kaveri south) — hymned by **Tirugnana Sambandar**. Sthala vriksham Vanni; tank Mangala Theertham. Historical name distinguishes it from Manickavasagar's Tirupperundurai.
- **Note:** distinct from the catalog's `sivanandeswarar-tirukkandalam` (Thondai Nadu).
- **Festivals:** Maha Shivaratri (Masi); Navaratri (Purattasi); Karthikai Mondays; Pradosham.
