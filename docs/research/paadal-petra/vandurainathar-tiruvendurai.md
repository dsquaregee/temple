# Tiruvendurai — Vandurainathar Temple

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Vandurainathar** (Vanduthurainathar / **Brahmapureeswarar** / Pramareswarar); goddess **Vel Nedunganni** (Venedunkanni; Sathyadayi). (Master-list goddess "Vandamar Poonkuzhali?" was WRONG — that belongs to the Thirukkuvalai Brahmapureeswarar. Corrected to Vel Nedunganni.)
- **Location:** **Cherivadi Vaikal** (Tevaram **Tiruventhurai**), ~10 km from Mannargudi toward Thiruthuraipoondi; Tiruvarur district. ~10.60 N, 79.52 E (approximate).
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam — hymned by **Sambandar**. Legend: sage **Bringhi** as a bee; Ardhanariswarar.
- **Festivals:** Maha Shivaratri (Masi); Navaratri (Purattasi); Karthikai Mondays; Pradosham.
