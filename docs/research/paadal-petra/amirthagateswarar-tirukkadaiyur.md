# Tirukkadaiyur — Amritaghateswarar–Abirami Temple

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Amritaghateswarar** ("lord of the pot of nectar"), worshipped as **Kalasamharamurti** (conqueror of Time/Death); goddess **Abirami**.
- **Location:** Tirukkadaiyur, near Tharangambadi, Mayiladuthurai district, Tamil Nadu (~21 km E of Mayiladuthurai). Approx 11.0192 N, 79.8517 E (approximate).
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam (Tevaram) AND one of the **Ashta Veerattanam** (eight sites of Shiva's heroic deeds).
- **Legend:** Markandeya saved from Yama — Shiva emerges from the lingam, kicks down Yama (Kalasamhara), then restores him; marks of Yama's noose shown on the lingam.
- **Abirami Pattar:** Abirami shrine linked to the saint Abhirami Pattar, author of the **Abhirami Anthadhi**.
- **Longevity rites:** Pre-eminent site for **Sashtiabdhapoorthi** (60th) and **Sadabhishekam** (80th) birthday ceremonies.
- **Fabric:** Large Chola-era temple, Thanjavur Nayak expansions; 5 courtyards; eastern gopuram 11 storeys (~46 m).
