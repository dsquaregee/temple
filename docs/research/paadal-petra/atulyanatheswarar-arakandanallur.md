# Atulyanatheswarar Temple, Arakandanallur (atulyanatheswarar-arakandanallur)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Nadu Nadu. District: Villupuram, Tamil Nadu. Tradition: Shaivism.

- Deity: Shiva as Atulyanatheswarar (a hill linga); consort Azhagiya Ponnazhagi (Azhagiya Nayaki).
- Town formerly Araiyani Nallur / Tiru-Araiyani-nallur, on the north bank of the Thenpennai
  (South Pennai) river, close to Tirukkovilur.
- Coordinates: 11.974, 79.220 (Wikipedia temple point 11°58′28″N 79°13′13″E).
- Legend: Sambandar found the temple shut by people of another faith, worship halted; he
  sang in praise and the doors opened, the rituals restored. 7th-c. Tevaram; Paadal Petra
  Sthalam.
- Architecture: set on a granite hill; seven-tier gopuram. Pallava origins (early rock-cut
  caves attributed to the Pallavas), Chola expansion, present masonry largely of the
  Vijayanagara period (16th c., per Wikipedia). Dinamalar instead gives a round "1000–2000
  years." Tirtham: the Thenpennai river.
- Festivals: Vaikasi Brahmotsavam (May–Jun, 10-day); Maha Shivaratri; Tirukarthikai (Nov–Dec);
  monthly Pradosham.
- Not verified: the Ramana Maharshi association with Araiyaninallur (reported by one mirror,
  unconfirmed by the others — omitted from the content); exact dynasty phasing; festival
  dates (Tamil calendar); transport distances.
