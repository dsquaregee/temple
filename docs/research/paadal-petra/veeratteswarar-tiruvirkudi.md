# Tiruvirkudi — Veeratteswarar Temple (Ashta Veerattanam)

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Veeratteswarar** (Jalandhara Samhara Murti); goddess **Elavar Kuzhali**.
- **Location:** Tiruvirkudi, near Nannilam, Tiruvarur district, Tamil Nadu. Approx 10.7806 N, 79.7083 E (approximate).
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam (Tevaram) AND one of the **Ashta Veerattanam** — this one for the destruction of **Jalandhara** (Jalandhara Samharam). Historical name Tiruvirkudi Veerattam.
- **Legend:** Shiva traces a circle → radiant discus (chakra) that severs Jalandhara; weapon later given to Vishnu.
- **Fabric:** Dravidian temple, Chola-period; five-tiered rajagopuram.
