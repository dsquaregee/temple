# Karai Tirunatheswarar (Sathyanatheswarar) Temple, Kanchipuram (karai-tirunatheswarar-kanchipuram)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Thondai Nadu (Tevaram name: Tirukkachi Nerikaraikkadu / Kachi Nerikkaaraikkadu). District: Kancheepuram. Tradition: Shaivism.

- Deity: Shiva as Karai Tirunatheswarar (also Sathyanatheswarar / Sathyanathaswamy), a
  **swayambhu linga**; consort Pramarambikai (Kamakshi in some accounts).
- Tevaram: one decad by Tirugnana Sambandar (Wikipedia list places it in the 1st Tirumurai;
  shivatemples.com says the 3rd Tirumurai — sources conflict).
- Coordinates: **locality-level ~12.835, 79.703** (Tirukalimedu, north-east Kanchipuram). Exact
  temple point not found in sources — refine against a mapping source when possible.
- Legend (the temple's defining story): the area was once a thorny forest of *kaarai* shrub
  ("Kaarai Kaadu"), where the Lord manifested as a self-born linga. Tradition adds that the planet
  Mercury (Budhan) worshipped here and was freed of his afflictions, and that Indra (the site is
  also called "Indra Puri") worshipped the deity and raised the sacred water for his bathing.
  Local devotion treats it as a Mercury (Budha) parihara shrine.
- Architecture: modest neighbourhood temple; **west-facing sanctum** (uncommon); goddess in her
  own shrine; temple tank locally known as Veppankulam.
- Festivals: Wednesday worship (Mercury); Maha Shivaratri; monthly Pradosham.
- Access: Tirukalimedu, north-east Kanchipuram, by the road opposite the Kanchipuram head post
  office; Kanchipuram ~72 km from Chennai by bus and rail.
- **Not verified:** founding dynasty/century of the structural temple (Pallava-era city; century 7
  used for the Tevaram era); exact coordinate; the Veppankulam identification and Indra-tank
  story (carried from existing en entry); the Mercury-grace tradition's sourcing; Tirumurai
  number; current timings (shivatemples.com gives roughly 6:00 am–12:00 noon and 4:00–6:00 pm).
