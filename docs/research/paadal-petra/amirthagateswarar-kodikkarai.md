# Kodikkarai (Point Calimere) — Amirthagateswarar / Kodi Kuzhagar Temple

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Amirthagateswarar** (Amrithakadeswarar / **Kodi Kuzhagar**), a **swayambhu** lingam; goddess **Anjanakshi** (Mai Thadanganni / Maithadankanni). (Worklist goddess "Maiyar Tadankanni" ≈ Mai Thadanganni — Anjanakshi is the primary name per Dinamalar & TN Temples.)
- **Location:** **Kodikkarai — Point Calimere**, the southern tip of the delta, ~11 km beyond Vedaranyam, by the wildlife sanctuary; Nagapattinam district. ~10.285 N, 79.852 E.
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam — hymned by **Sambandar and Sundarar**. Legend: a drop of the churned-ocean amritam, borne by Vayu, fell here and became the lingam (amrita-ghata). Navagrahas set in a straight line. Opens ~1 hr morning & evening (remote).
- **Festivals:** Maha Shivaratri (Masi); Navaratri (Purattasi); Karthikai Mondays; Pradosham.
