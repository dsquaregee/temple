# Tiruthengur — Vellimalainathar Temple

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Vellimalainathar** ("lord of the silver mountain"); goddess **Periyanayaki** (also **Brihannayaki**). (Worklist goddess "Periyanayaki" — confirmed.)
- **Location:** Tiruthengur (often **Thiruthangur**), ~18 km from Tiruvarur, ~22 km from Mannargudi, Tiruvarur district, Tamil Nadu. ~10.6487 N, 79.6258 E (Wikipedia).
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam — hymned by Tirugnana Sambandar; Kaveri south bank.
- **Legend:** **Sukracharya** (lord of **Sukra/Venus**, = *velli*, silver) regained his lost sight by worship here → Vellimalainathar; the **Navagrahas** each installed a lingam — a Navagraha / Sukra-dosha shrine.
- **Festivals:** Maha Shivaratri (Masi); Navaratri (Purattasi); Karthikai Mondays; Pradosham; Friday (Sukra) worship.
