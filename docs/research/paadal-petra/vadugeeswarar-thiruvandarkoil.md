# Vadugeeswarar (Vadukeeswarar / Panchanatheeswarar) Temple, Thiruvandarkoil (Thiruvadugur) — research notes

Master-list row 231 (Nadu Nadu). Row facts confirmed: Lord Vadukeeswarar / Panchanatheeswarar; Goddess Thiripurasundari, also Vadugur Nayaki (Vaduvagirkkanniammai). Singer Sambandar (pathigam in 1st Tirumurai).

- Place: Thiruvandarkoil (Tevaram: Thiruvadugur / Vadugur), Puducherry UT (address Puducherry 605102), on the Villupuram–Puducherry highway, ~21 km east of Villupuram (shivatemples) — i.e. ~20 km west of Puducherry town.
- Paadal Petra Sthalam, 16th in Nadu Nadu (Dinamalar). Sambandar only.
- Legend (shivatemples): the demon Mundasura troubled the world; Shiva sent Vaduka Bhairava (one of the eight Bhairavas) to slay him; to be cleansed of the sin of the killing (brahmahathi), Bhairava worshipped the Lord here — hence Vadukeeswarar and the name Vadugur / Thiruvandarkoil. Name also explained as "Andar Koil".
- Goddess Thiripurasundari in a separate shrine; depicted with Mahalakshmi attributes (Wikipedia).
- History: Chola inscriptions — Rajaraja I (985–1014), Kulothunga I (1070–1120), Rajendra III (1246–1279). Maintained by the Archaeological Survey of India (Wikipedia). No firm construction date.
- Architecture: compound walls, east-facing entrance, no gopuram (Wikipedia).
- Festivals: special worship of Bhairava on Karthigai Ashtami (Kalabhairava Ashtami); a one-day Chithirai festival.
- Coordinates: 11.9191, 79.6573 — Wikipedia "Vadukeeswarar temple" (11°55′8.7″N 79°39′26.3″E) via search text; mirror agrees. No other catalog entry in this village.
