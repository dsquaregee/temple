# Sethalapathy (Tiruthiladaipathi) — Mathi-Muktheeswarar Temple

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Mathi-Muktheeswarar** (Muktheeswarar, "lord of mukti/liberation"); goddess **Porkodi Nayaki** (also **Swarnavalli**, **Maragathavalli** — all "golden creeper"). (Worklist drafted goddess "Swarnavalli" — confirmed, with Porkodi Nayaki primary.)
- **Location:** Sethalapathy (Tevaram **Tiruthiladaipathi**; also "Thilatharpanapuri" in some sources), ~3 km from Poonthottam, Tiruvarur district (older listings Nagapattinam), Tamil Nadu, PIN 609503; ~25 km from Mayiladuthurai. ~10.9439 N, 79.6357 E (Wikipedia).
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam — hymned by Tirugnana Sambandar (2nd Thirumurai); Kaveri south bank.
- **Theme:** name Muktheeswarar = grant of liberation; village name linked to **tila (sesame) / tarpana** ancestral rites of the delta (stated cautiously — full sthala purana not fully sourced).
- **Festivals:** Maha Shivaratri (Masi); Navaratri (Purattasi); Karthikai Mondays; Pradosham.
