# Uchinathar (Uchinatheswarar) Temple, Sivapuri (uchinathar-sivapuri)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Chola Nadu (north of the Kaveri) (Tevaram name: Tirunelvayil, திருநெல்வாயில்). District: Cuddalore (near Chidambaram, close to Annamalai University). Tradition: Shaivism.

- Deity: Shiva as Uchinathar / Uchinatheswarar (Sanskrit: Madhyaneeswarar); goddess
  **Kanakambikai** (shivatemples.com, dinamalar, tntemplesproject). Wikipedia gives the consort
  as "Uchinayagi" — treated as a variant; Kanakambikai used per the seed and the other sources.
- Tevaram: **Sambandar**, ten verses. Sambandar is said to have come daily to worship here
  while staying at Chidambaram.
- Coordinates: **11.3729, 79.7134** (Wikipedia 11°22'22.4"N 79°42'48.3"E). Neighbouring
  Tirukkazhippalai Palvannanathar temple is at 11.3710, 79.7141 (a few hundred metres away).
- Name: "Tirunelvayil" from *nel* (paddy) — the place was ringed by rice fields.
- Defining legend: Shiva, in the guise of a temple priest at noon, fed the hungry Sambandar
  and his party; hence Uchinathar ("uchi" = midday), Madhyaneeswarar in Sanskrit. Sources
  differ on details (one says they were on the way to Sambandar's wedding, another toward
  Achalpuram). Secondary tradition: sage Agastya was shown the wedding of Shiva and Parvati
  here (tntemplesproject).
- Architecture: east-facing, three-tiered rajagopuram (Wikipedia; a different shivatemples
  page gives five tiers — three used), concentric rectangular walls; the main deity is a
  small, swayambhu linga with Shiva-Parvati images behind it; separate shrine to Kanakambikai;
  tank **Krupa (Kripa) Samudram**; sthala vriksha listed as nelli (gooseberry) and moongil
  (bamboo).
- Festivals: Vaikasi Visakam (May–June) named as most prominent (one source); Maha Shivaratri
  and Pradosham generic.
- Access: ~3-5 km from Chidambaram (sources vary); about 3 km south-east of Chidambaram
  railway station; near Annamalai University; Tirukkazhippalai Palvannanathar temple a few
  hundred metres away. Airports: Puducherry/Chennai (generic).
- **Seed correction/notes:** a source states there are two temples at Sivapuri, the northern
  one being Tirunelvayil (Uchinathar) — the entry covers this one. One search result linked
  "Thonniappar" and the milk-cup legend to Sirkazhi, not here — not used.
- **Not verified:** founding dynasty and period of the structural temple (century 7 used for
  the Sambandar era); whether the Sambandar-meal story's circumstances are as told (flagged in
  the text as tradition); the second sthala vriksha ("moongil" read as bamboo); Agastya
  tradition (single source); timings (one source: 6-11 am and 5-7:30 pm — generic text used
  in content); exact distances; the Kanva and Neelanakka Nayanar associations (not claimed).
