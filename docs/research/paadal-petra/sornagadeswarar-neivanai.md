# Sornagadeswarar (Swarnakadeswarar) Temple, Neivanai (Thirunelvennai) — research notes

Master-list row 225 (Nadu Nadu). Corrections: consort's alternative name "Brihannayaki" not found in any source — omitted. District: the temple address is Ulundurpet taluk (formerly Villupuram district; Ulundurpet taluk is now in Kallakurichi district) — row's "Kallakurichi" kept.

- Place: Neivanai village (Tevaram name Thirunelvennai; Dinamalar "Nelvennai"), Ulundurpet taluk. Postal address: Neivanai village, Koovadu post, via Eraiyur, PIN 607201. About 15 km from Ulundurpet; reached via Eraiyur on the Tirukkovilur–Ulundurpet road, ~4 km from Eraiyur through Vadakurumbur; also by a turn off the Ulundurpet–Villupuram road.
- Paadal Petra Sthalam, 10th in Nadu Nadu. Sung by Thirugnana Sambandar (pathigam in the 3rd Tirumurai). Only singer confirmed.
- Moolavar: Swarnakadeswarar / Sornagadeswarar; also called Vennaiyappar (shivatemples.com). Name explained from temple inscriptions as the Lord who granted a golden pot (swarna-kadam).
- Ambal: Neelamalar Kanni (Neelamalar Kanniammai), in a separate east-facing shrine close by.
- Legends: (1) Dinamalar — the region's people, rich from their paddy, forgot God; Varuna's incessant rain flooded the land, tanks breached; Shiva came as a youth, had the villagers bring their sacks of paddy and built a dam (anai) of paddy (nel) — hence Nel-anai > Neivanai. (2) Sambandar reached the place at night and could not find the way; the Lord sent the Goddess to guide him to the temple. (3) The Sanakadi sages are believed to have worshipped here.
- Sambandar's hymn describes Thirunelvennai as ancient, renowned and rich in water.
- Age: Dinamalar "1000–2000 years" (vague; not used). Century 7 from Sambandar.
- Festivals: Karthigai Deepam (Nov–Dec), Maha Shivaratri (Feb–Mar), Arudra Darshanam (Dec–Jan).
- Coordinates: 11.825, 79.174 — APPROXIMATE. No temple/village coordinate found in search results (Wikipedia article snippet has none; OSM/Wikidata unreachable). Used the indiamapia point for Kuvadu (Koovadu), the branch post office (PIN 607201) whose postal area includes Neivanai (11.82525 N, 79.17425 E). Needs a map check for the temple point.
