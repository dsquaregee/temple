# Atcheeswarar Temple, Achirupakkam (atcheeswarar-achirupakkam)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Thondai Nadu (Tevaram name: Tiruvachirupakkam / திருவச்சிறுபாக்கம்). Tradition: Shaivism.

- Deity: Shiva as Atcheeswarar ("Lord of the Axle"; Tamil *achu* = axle), a self-manifested
  (swayambhu) east-facing linga; a second sanctum for **Umayatcheeswarar** (Umai Atcheeswarar).
  Goddesses: **Ilangili Ammai** and **Umayambigai**, each in a separate shrine.
  (The brief's "Soundaryanayaki" was not found in any source; not used.)
- Sung by Tirugnanasambandar (Tevaram); sources count it the 29th Thondai Nadu Tevaram shrine
  (numbering varies; not used in content).
- Coordinates: Wikipedia temple infobox ~12.4009, 79.8176 (town article gives 12.3959, 79.8091,
  ~1 km off). Used **12.401, 79.818**. Village-level; exact gate not independently confirmed.
  (Seed 12.46, 79.80 was ~6 km north of sourced value.)
- Defining legend: on his way to destroy the demons of Tripura, Shiva's chariot axle broke here
  because he had not first worshipped Vinayaka; Vinayaka repaired it. The place where the axle
  broke ("achu iru pakkam") became Achirupakkam. Second tradition: the axle of a Pandya king's
  chariot, carrying earth from the Ganga for a dam on a local river, also broke here (appears on
  a Wikipedia mirror; not cross-checked). Third variant (indianetzone): Vinayaka, angry at not
  being given a role, destroyed the chariot's linchpin — not used in content.
- Shrine of **Achumuri Vinayaka** outside the temple, facing west (Dinamalar); Arunagirinathar's
  Tiruppugazh is said to name him Achiru Vinayaka. Devotees pray to him to overcome obstacles.
- Architecture: east-facing, five-tiered entrance gateway (shivatemples.com); two sanctums for the
  Lord; two goddess shrines. No dimensions or dynasty found.
- Festivals: 10-day Chithirai Brahmotsavam (Apr–May); special pujas on new-moon and full-moon
  days (Dinamalar, shivatemples.com).
- Timings (listings differ): 6:00–12:00 & 16:00–20:00 (shivatemples tn28.php); 6:30–12:00 &
  16:00–20:30 (mirror); 6:00–11:00 & 16:00–20:30 (Dinamalar).
- Access: Achirupakkam on NH 45 (Chennai–Tindivanam GST Road), ~96 km from Chennai; Madurantakam
  taluk (listed under Kancheepuram in older sources; now Chengalpattu district, PIN 603301).
- **Not verified:** founding dynasty/century of the structure (7th-century used for the Tevaram
  era; "1000–2000 years old" claim disregarded); gopuram height; number of Sambandar decads;
  Maha Shivaratri/Pradosham at this temple specifically (stated as general Shiva-temple
  practice); the Pandya-king variant; distances to Chengalpattu (~40 km) and Chennai airport
  (~80 km) are approximate estimates, not sourced; nearest railway station not confirmed (only
  Chengalpattu junction named); current timings. Note: English Wikipedia titles the temple
  "Aksheeswaraswamy Temple, Acharapakkam" (spelling variant of the same village); the
  Athitheeswarar Temple (Vaniyambadi) result is unrelated and was not used.
