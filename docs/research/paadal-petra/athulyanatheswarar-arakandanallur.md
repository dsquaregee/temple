# Athulyanatheswarar (Atulya Nadheswarar) Temple, Arakandanallur — research notes

Master-list row 227 (Nadu Nadu). **Correction:** consort is Azhagiya Ponnazhagi / Azhagiya Ponnammai (Sanskrit Soundarya Kanakambigai), not "Arunthavanayaki (Soundaryanayaki)" — no source names Arunthavanayaki. District: Thirukoilur taluk — Viluppuram district per Wikipedia; Thirukoilur taluk has been in Kallakurichi district since 2019. Row's "Villupuram" left as given in notes; content says "near Thirukkovilur".

- Place: Arakandanallur (Tevaram: Thiruvaraiyaninallur / Araiyani Nallur), town panchayat on the north bank of the Then Pennai (Pennaiyar), opposite Thirukkovilur.
- Paadal Petra Sthalam, Nadu Nadu (shivatemples nn12). Sung by Thirugnana Sambandar (pathigam in the 3rd Tirumurai). Only singer confirmed.
- Moolavar: Atulyanatheswarar ("the incomparable Lord"; Tamil Oppilamaniyeswarar per master list — Tamil equivalent of the Sanskrit name).
- History: built on a granite hill; complex ~2 acres; original believed Pallava, expanded by Cholas, present masonry Vijayanagara (16th c.). Three rock-cut caves on the hill attributed to the Pallava period (one source says "10th century" — not used). HR&CE administered.
- Architecture: Dravidian; seven-tier rajagopuram (Wikipedia); a mirror says main gopuram faces west (unconfirmed — omitted). Tank: Then Pennai (river) listed as theertham.
- Legends: the temple had been shut by followers of another faith, stopping worship; Sambandar sang his pathigam, the doors opened and rituals were restored (tradition). Sambandar, travelling to Tiruvannamalai, was held here; tradition says he worshipped Annamalaiyar from here (Arunachala visible from the hill) — Wikipedia: he set up Annamalaiyar as a lingam here.
- Ramana Maharshi: in late August 1896, the young Venkataraman, on his way from Madurai to Tiruvannamalai, stopped at this temple on the rock (Arunachala faintly visible); biographers record a vision of dazzling light filling the temple (gururamana.org timeline; Osborne-derived biographies).
- Festivals: 10-day Vaikasi Brahmotsavam (May–June), Maha Shivaratri, Karthigai Deepam.
- Coordinates: 11.9744, 79.2203 — Wikipedia temple article (11°58′28″N 79°13′13″E) via search text. Town article 11°58′58″N 79°13′41″E (consistent). Distinct from Veerattaneswarar Thirukkovilur (11.9714, 79.2106) and Trivikrama Thirukkovilur (11.967, 79.202).
