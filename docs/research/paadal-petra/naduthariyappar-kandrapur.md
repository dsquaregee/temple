# Tirukandrapur (Kanrappur) — Naduthariyappar Temple

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Naduthariyappar** (Nadutariappar/Naduturaippar); goddess **Srivalli Nayaki** (also **Maathumai Nayaki** / Madhumai Ammai). (Worklist goddess "Maathumai Nayaki" — kept as alt; Srivalli Nayaki primary per Wikipedia.)
- **Location:** Kanrappur (Tevaram **Tirukandrapur**; spellings vary — Kanrappur/Kandrapur/Tirukkanraappur), ~16–17 km from Tiruvarur, Tiruvarur district, Tamil Nadu. ~10.6792 N, 79.6898 E (Wikipedia).
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam — hymned by Appar; Kaveri south bank.
- **Legend:** the **swayambhu** lingam could not be cut/broken (an axe-blow left it unharmed) → name of the steadfast lord.
- **Festivals:** Maha Shivaratri (Masi); Navaratri (Purattasi); Karthikai Mondays; Pradosham.
