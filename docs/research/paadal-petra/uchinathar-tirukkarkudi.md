# Uchinathar (Ujjivanathar) Temple, Tirukkarkudi (uchinathar-tirukkarkudi)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Chola Nadu (south of the Kaveri). District: Tiruchirappalli
(Uyyakondan Tirumalai / Karkudimalai / Thirumalainallur, on the Trichy–Vayalur road).
Tradition: Shaivism. Distinct from the other Uchinathar temple at **Sivapuri** (separate site).

- Deity: Shiva as **Uchinathar / Ujjivanathar (Ujjeevanathar)**, also called Tirukkarkkudi
  Paramesvarar; consort **Anjanakshi** (sources also list the names Balambikai and
  Neelanedunkanni for the goddess).
- Tevaram name: **Tirukkarkudi** (திருக்கற்குடி). Sources say it is sung by the three Nayanmars
  (Appar, Sambandar, Sundarar); one source calls it the 4th Tevaram temple on the Kaveri's southern
  bank. Individual saints not itemised in prose.
- Coordinates: **~10.816, 78.659** (Wikipedia: 10°48′57″N 78°39′33″E for the Uyyakondan Thirumalai
  temple). Treat as temple-level but confirm against a mapping source.
- Name: hill/village known as Uyyakondan Tirumalai after the Uyyakondan channel (believed Chola,
  9th century); older names Karkudi / Karkudimalai / Thirumalainallur. "Karkudi" glossed in
  prose as the settlement on the rock (kal = stone) — an interpretation, not a quoted source gloss.
- Defining legend: sage **Markandeya** took refuge at the temple, embraced the linga, and was granted
  eternal youth (sixteen, Chiranjeevi); the shrine is said to give longevity and prosperity to
  those who live nearby. Maharshis Upamanyu and Karan and Arunagirinathar are also said to have
  worshipped here.
- Architecture: east-facing **five-tiered gopuram**; steps cut into the hill (one source: 65;
  another listing: 100 — prose notes the discrepancy); **Gnana Vavi** tank to the left after the
  entrance; sanctum on the hill top.
- History/epigraphy: earliest inscriptions on pillars of the inner enclosure; one of Parantaka I's
  34th regnal year (941 CE); later inscriptions of Uttama Chola (971–988 CE) and Sembiyan
  Mahadevi. Wikipedia says the original complex is believed built by the Cholas.
- Festivals: **Panguni (Mar–Apr) Brahmotsavam** is the most prominent; Shiva festivals generally.
- Access: on the Trichy–Vayalur road, western outskirts of Tiruchirappalli (~5 km from the city);
  Tiruchirappalli Junction and airport are the main hubs.
- **Corrections to seed hint:** none. Goddess Anjanakshi confirmed. Town given as Uyyakondan
  Tirumalai.
- **Not verified:** exact step count (65 vs 100); whether processions circle the hill during the
  Brahmotsavam (stated generically); which of the three saints sang here; the Balambikai /
  Neelanedunkanni names as separate shrines vs alternate names; whether the goddess has a separate
  shrine; current timings (none found; generic prose); exact temple coordinates; distances to
  railway/airport; the "kal = rock" etymology.
