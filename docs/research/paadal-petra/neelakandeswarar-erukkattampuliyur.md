# Neelakandeswarar (Swetaranyeswarar) Temple, Erukkattampuliyur (neelakandeswarar-erukkattampuliyur)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Nadu Nadu (old town name: Erukkattampuliyur; present name:
Rajendrapattinam). Tradition: Shaivism.

- Deity: Shiva as **Neelakandeswarar** (sourced alternate **Swetaranyeswarar /
  Swedaranyeswarar**, "Lord of the white forest"). The task brief gave the alternate as
  "Swedharka Vaneswarar" — the same white-forest idea (sweta/shveta + vana/aranya); the
  sourced form Swetaranyeswarar was used in prose. Sources disagree on which is the primary
  moolavar name (shivatemples.com → Neelakandeswarar; tntemplesproject → Swetaranyeswarar).
- Consort: **Neelamalar Kanni** (sourced as Neelamalarkanni Ammai) — matches the brief.
- Place: Rajendrapattinam, Virudhachalam taluk (one PIN directory says Kattumannarkoil
  taluk), Cuddalore district, PIN 608703; near the Virudhachalam–Jayamkondam road and close
  to Srimushnam; ~15 km from Virudhachalam. 4th Shiva temple of the Nadu Nadu region in the
  Thevaram ordering (shivatemples nn04).
- Hymnist: **Sambandar** (Tirugnana Sambandar) — one source states he sang a pathigam here;
  used century 7 for the Sambandar era. (Not every aggregator names the hymnist; treated as
  the best-sourced attribution.)
- Puliyur group: one of the **Pancha Puliyur** (five) / **Nava Puliyur** (nine) temples
  sanctified by the tiger-footed sage **Vyaghrapada** (gathered flowers before dawn for
  Nataraja at Chidambaram; granted tiger feet/eyes). Source count varies (5 vs 9) — prose
  states both.
- Royal legend: **Rajaraja Chola** prayed here for an heir and was blessed with **Rajendra
  Chola**, who renovated/endowed the temple; hence the name **Rajendrapattinam**. (Widely
  repeated local tradition; not independently corroborated history.)
- Nayanar: honoured as the **avatara sthalam (birthplace) of Tiru Neelakanta Yazhpanar**,
  one of the 63 Nayanmars (a yazh/lute player).
- Also noted: Murugan here sung by Arunagirinathar (a Tiruppugazh temple) — mentioned but
  not elaborated in prose.
- Architecture: **five-tiered rajagopuram**; earliest fabric Chola (one source: 12th
  century), present form largely in the **Nagarathar** style.

- **Not verified:** exact temple coordinate (used **town-level ~11.425, 79.343**, from the
  Wikipedia "Rajendirapattinam" village point 11°25′29″N 79°20′36″E; other sources gave
  conflicting PIN-centroid points — refine against a mapping source); which of
  Neelakandeswarar / Swetaranyeswarar is the formal primary name; the Sambandar vs other
  hymnist attribution and exact Tirumurai placement; Rajaraja/Rajendra prayer legend as
  history; festival calendar (kept general); dynasty phasing; current timings.
