# Koteeswarar (Kodeeswarar) Temple, Tirukkottaiyur — Kottaiyur (koteeswarar-tirukkottaiyur)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Chola Nadu (north of the Kaveri) (Tevaram name: Tirukkottaiyur). District: Thanjavur (near Kumbakonam). Tradition: Shaivism. Distinct from the Koteeswarar at Tirukkodikaval (Tirukkodikka).

- Deity: Shiva as Koteeswarar (self-manifested linga); consort **Pandadu Nayaki** (also Panthaadu Nayaki; Sanskrit Kanthuka Kreedambal — "the goddess who plays with a ball").
- Tevaram: **Appar (Tirunavukkarasar)** — hymn in the Sixth Thirumurai, reported as shared between Tirukkottaiyur and Tiruvalanchuzhi. (Shrine-guide listing gives Appar only; no Sambandar/Sundarar hymn found.)
- Coordinates: **~10.967, 79.358** (Wikipedia: 10°58′01″N 79°21′30″E, elevation ~52 m; treated as approximate temple/village point).
- Name: koti = crore (Koteeswarar); kottai = seed (Kottaiyur). Linga said to have appeared beneath a castor (eranda) plant among fallen seeds; sage Eranda associated.
- Legends: (1) Amrita Kalasam — Brahma's pot of the seeds of all life plus Vedas/Puranas drifted in the deluge; Shiva as a hunter broke it with an arrow; drops of nectar fell at several places incl. here (another account says sandal paste/chandanam); linked to Kumbakonam / Mahamaham origin; (2) prince Suruchi, son of Sathyarthi, cursed into a devil's form, restored after worship and bathing in the temple spring; (3) goddess's name explained as tossing away devotees' curses and sorrows; (4) Markandeya and Padirayogi named as worshippers.
- Architecture: five-tiered rajagopuram (the notable feature); self-manifested linga; goddess in a separate shrine; Vinayagar and Murugan shrines flank the sanctum entrance; temple spring and tank. Sapta Sthanam listing of Kumbakonam noted in sources but not used in prose.
- Festivals: Masi Maham (Feb–Mar; tank procession, milk abhishekam) described as most prominent; Margazhi Tiruvathirai; Maha Shivaratri; Panguni Uthiram; arrow festival in Purattasi.
- Access: ~5 km from Kumbakonam on the Kumbakonam–Swamimalai road (nearest railway: Kumbakonam; nearest major airport Tiruchirappalli, distance not sourced). PIN 612002. Timings reported 6–12 and 3 (or 4)–8; daily pujas listed 7:30, 11:30, 6:00, 8:00.
- **Researched facts:** goddess is Pandadu Nayaki (not a Panchavarnavalli-type name, which belongs to Uraiyur) and the hymn is Appar's (Sixth Thirumurai); no Sambandar hymn found for this shrine.
- **Not verified:** founding dynasty/builders and century of the structural temple (century 7 = Appar era); precise temple coordinate; Masi Maham vs Maha Shivaratri being the same event (sources unclear; prose lists them separately); whether the tank procession occurs at Masi Maham in current practice; amrita vs chandanam variant; Eranda/Padirayogi details (single-source); specific sculptural details and dimensions; distance to Tiruchirappalli airport; current timings.
