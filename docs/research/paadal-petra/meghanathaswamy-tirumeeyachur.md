# Tirumeeyachur — Meghanathaswamy Temple (Lalithambikai)

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Meghanathaswamy** (Meganathar); goddess **Lalithambikai** — the devotional focus.
- **Location:** Tirumeeyachur, ~2 km from Peralam, near Nannilam, Tiruvarur district, Tamil Nadu. Approx 10.9322 N, 79.6256 E (approximate).
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam — sung by Sambandar. Major Devi centre.
- **Signature legend:** Tied to the **Lalita Sahasranama** (1000 names of the Mother): ashta vachinees recited her names to pacify Parvati; Agastya & Hayagriva associated. Also: Garuda's birthplace; Yama worshipped for longevity; Indra regained powers.
- **Architecture:** Raised **Maadakovil** (traditionally Kochengat Chola), **Gajaprishta** (elephant-backed) vimana; main shrine dated c. late-10th c. (Sembiyan Mahadevi era); fine Chola Gangadhara panel in the goddess's shrine.
- **Note:** The complex also holds the Sakalabhuvaneswarar Ilankoil (separate Paadal Petra, written separately).
