# Anekathangapatham (Gaurikund) — research notes

Master-list row 274 (Vada Nadu 3). Id marked `?` (identification traditional); used without `?`. Identification of Anekathangapatham with Gaurikund is traditional (shivatemples tn04 note: "two Paadal Petra Shivasthalams in the name of Anekatangaapatham — one near Gowrikund on the way to Kedarnath, another at Kanchipuram (Kachchi Anekathangaapatham, sung by Sundarar)"). Templenet groups Gowrikund among the four Himalayan Tevaram shrines.

- Singer: Sambandar (master list; templenet/koyil Vada Nadu lists). Not independently confirmed by a snippet naming the pathigam; tradition says Sambandar sang the northern shrines from Kalahasti.
- Deity: Anekathangapatheswarar; Goddess: Gowri — per master list, NOT confirmed in search.
- Name: "anekatham" = elephant (Tamil literary word); at Kanchi the name is tied to Vinayaka worshipping Shiva. At Gaurikund, local tradition links Parvati and Ganesha (Parvati's bath; Ganesha set as guard) — present as local tradition only, lightly.
- Gaurikund: Rudraprayag district, ~1,980 m (6,502 ft); base of the Kedarnath trek. Folklore: Parvati performed penance here to win Shiva; their wedding placed at nearby Triyuginarayan. Hot spring (kund) — spring largely lost in 2013 floods; a small hot-water stream remains. Gauri Devi temple at the site.
- Sonprayag: confluence of Mandakini and Son, ~5 km below; vehicles stop at Sonprayag.
- Coordinates: 30.6333, 79.0167 — Wikipedia "Gaurikund" (30°38′N 79°1′E, minute precision only). Flag: approximate. Distinct from Kedarnath entry (30.7352, 79.0669).
