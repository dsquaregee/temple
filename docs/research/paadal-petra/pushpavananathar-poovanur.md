# Poovanur — Pushpavananathar Temple

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Pushpavananathar** (also **Chaturanga Vallabha Nathar**); goddess **Karpagavalli** (also Rajarajeswari). (Worklist goddess "?" — resolved to Karpagavalli from sources.)
- **Location:** **Poovanur**, between Kumbakonam and Mannargudi, near Needamangalam; Tiruvarur district (Kaveri north-bank tract). ~10.80 N, 79.44 E (approximate).
- **Note:** distinct from the **Tiruppoonturutti** Pushpavananathar (Thanjavur), which is already in the catalog.
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam — hymned by **Appar and Sambandar**. Legend: Shiva & Uma chose the flowering grove → Poovanur ('flower-forest town').
- **Festivals:** Maha Shivaratri (Masi); Navaratri (Purattasi); Karthikai Mondays; Pradosham.
