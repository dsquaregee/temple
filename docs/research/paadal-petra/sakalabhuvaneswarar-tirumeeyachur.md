# Tirumeeyachur — Sakalabhuvaneswarar Temple (Ilankoil)

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Sakalabhuvaneswarar**; goddess **Mekalambikai** (Mekhalambikai).
- **Location:** Within the **Meghanathaswamy temple complex** at Tirumeeyachur (northern prakara), near Peralam/Nannilam, Tiruvarur district, Tamil Nadu. Approx 10.9324 N, 79.6259 E (approximate).
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam — sung by **Appar** (distinct from the Meghanathar shrine, sung by Sambandar). Two Tevaram shrines share one complex.
- **Ilankoil ("young temple"):** originated as the **balalayam** — the temporary shrine kept in worship while the main Meghanathar temple was built. Sakalabhuvaneswarar faces east, Mekalambikai faces south.
- **Legends:** birth of Vali & Sugreeva; Surya forgiven here; Garuda from Kashyapa's wife's egg.
