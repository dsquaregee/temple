# Agasthiyampalli — Agastheeswarar Temple

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Agastheeswarar**; goddess **Soundaranayagi** (also Baagampriya Nayagi). (Worklist draft goddess "Mangalambikai?" was unverified — corrected to Soundaranayagi / Baagampriya Nayagi from sources.)
- **Location:** Agasthiyampalli (Agasthiyar Kovil), ~2–3 km south of **Vedaranyam**, on the road to **Kodikkarai (Point Calimere)**, near the sea, Nagapattinam district. ~10.355 N, 79.852 E (approximate).
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam — hymned by **Tirugnana Sambandar**. Legend: sage **Agastya** camped here and saw Shiva–Parvati's wedding (shared with the Vedaranyam shrine); village named for his stay.
- **Festivals:** Maha Shivaratri (Masi); Navaratri (Purattasi); Karthikai Mondays; Pradosham.
