# Nageswaraswamy Temple, Kumbakonam (nageswaraswamy-kumbakonam)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Chola Nadu south of the Kaveri. Tradition: Shaivism.
- Deity: Shiva as Nageswaraswamy; consort Periyanayaki. Early Chola masterpiece (Aditya I, ~9th c.), famed vimana sculpture.
- Sun's rays fall on the linga in Chithirai → "Surya Kottam / Keel Kottam".
- Named for serpent kings (Adisesha, Dakshan, Karkotaka) worshipping Shiva; also has a notable Rahu association in local practice.
- Sung by Thirugnana Sambandar; in central Kumbakonam, part of the Mahamaham town.
- Coordinates: ~10.9602, 79.3845 (Wikipedia, Nageswaraswamy Temple, Kumbakonam).
- Not verified: exact construction date (sources give 9th vs 12th c.; used 9th / Aditya Chola per most sources). Distinct from Tirunageswaram (Rahu) and Adi Kumbeswarar.
