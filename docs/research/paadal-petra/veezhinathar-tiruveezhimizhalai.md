# Tiruveezhimizhalai — Veezhinathar Temple

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Veezhinathar**; goddess **Sundarakuchambikai** (Azhagiya Nayagi).
- **Location:** Tiruveezhimizhalai, near Needamangalam, Tiruvarur district, Tamil Nadu. Approx 10.8003 N, 79.5186 E (approximate).
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam — sung by Sambandar, Appar and Sundarar.
- **Signature legend:** The **famine / padikasu miracle** — during a drought, Shiva gave Sambandar and Appar a daily gold coin to feed the people; one version: Sambandar's coins were lower in value, equalised after his hymn. Seats of the two saints shown in the temple (near maha-mandapam / western side).
- **Association:** Annadanam (feeding) and charity especially apt here.

**Notes:** Coordinates approximate; goddess name given as Sundarakuchambikai. Translations AI-drafted; native-speaker QA recommended. Prose original; legend cross-checked against Wikipedia (Veezhinathar Kovil), TN Temples Project, and Periyapuranam retellings.
