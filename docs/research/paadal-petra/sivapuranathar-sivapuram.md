# Sivapuram — Sivagurunathaswamy Temple

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Sivagurunathaswamy** (Sivapuranathar); goddesses **Aryambal** and **Singaravalli** (two goddess shrines).
- **Location:** Sivapuram, ~6 km east of Kumbakonam, Thanjavur district, Tamil Nadu. Approx 10.9683 N, 79.4350 E (approximate).
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam (Tevaram). Historical names Kuberapuram, Bhookailas, Shenbagaranyam; sthala vriksham shenbagam (champak).
- **Legends:** **Kubera** (cursed by Nandi, born as king Dhanapati) regained his devas-place here → Kuberapuri; **Vishnu as Varaha** worshipped with lotuses; town name from **countless lingams underground**; Sambandar did angapradakshinam (would not step on the sacred ground).
- **Festival:** **Aippasi Annabhishekam** (Oct–Nov) foremost.
