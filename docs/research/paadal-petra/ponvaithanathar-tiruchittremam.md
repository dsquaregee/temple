# Tiruchittremam — Ponvaithanathar Temple

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Ponvaithanathar**; goddess **Akhilandeswari**. (Master-list goddess "Periyanayaki" was WRONG — that belongs to the *different* Puradhanavaneswarar temple at Tiruchittrambalam, Pattukottai. Corrected to Akhilandeswari per Wikipedia/Dinamalar/TN Temples.)
- **Location:** **Sitthaimur**, near **Aalathampadi** between Thiruvarur and Thiruthuraipoondi; Tiruvarur delta. ~10.64 N, 79.63 E (approximate). Historical name **Tiruchchittremam**; the 106th shrine of the Kaveri south bank.
- **District:** Wikipedia **Tiruvarur**; TN Temples Project & Dinamalar **Nagapattinam**. Noted in body.
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam — hymned by **Appar and Sundarar**. Legend: the lord left a daily gold coin for devotee Sankaran's wife → Ponvaithanathar.
- **Festivals:** Maha Shivaratri (Masi); Navaratri (Purattasi); Karthikai Mondays; Pradosham.
