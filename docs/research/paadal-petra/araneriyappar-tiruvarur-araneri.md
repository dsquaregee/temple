# Tiruvarur Araneri — Araneriyappar (Achaleswarar) Temple

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Araneriyappar** (Achaleswarar); goddess **Vandarkuzhali** (Achaleswari). (Master-list goddess "Vandarkuzhali?" — confirmed.)
- **Location:** Within the **Thyagaraja temple complex at Tiruvarur** (its Agni / south-east corner); Tiruvarur district. ~10.772 N, 79.636 E (approximate).
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam, Kaveri south bank — hymned by **Appar**. Legend: **Naminandi Adigal** lit the temple lamps with water. Sthala vriksham pathiri; tank Sangu Theertham.
- **Note:** distinct from `thyagaraja-thiruvarur` (the main Thyagaraja temple) and `thoovainathar-tiruvarur`.
- **Festivals:** Tiruvadhirai (Margazhi); Maha Shivaratri (Masi); Navaratri (Purattasi); Karthikai Mondays; Pradosham.
