# Pazhampathinathar (Vriddhapureeswarar) Temple, Thiruppunavayil (Thiruppunavasal) — research notes

Master-list row 200 (Pandya Nadu 7). Row facts confirmed: Pazhampathinathar = Vriddhapureeswarar; Periyanayaki; Sambandar + Sundarar.

- Place: Thiruppunavasal (Tevaram: Thiruppunavayil), village in Pudukkottai district near the coast; the Pambar river flows south of the temple and meets the sea ~3 km east. About 25–30 km south of Avudaiyarkoil.
- 7th of the 14 Pandya Nadu sthalams (shivatemples pn07).
- Moolavar: Vriddhapureeswarar / Pazhampathinathar (both "the ancient one / Lord of the ancient town"). Ambal: Periyanayaki, separate east-facing shrine (Karunai Nayaki also listed by one source).
- Lingam on a very large avudaiyar (pedestal), said to be among the largest in circumference.
- Singers: Sambandar (probably on the journey from Vedaranyam to Madurai); Sundarar visited with his friend Cheraman Perumal.
- Legend: Brahma lost his creative power after failing to explain the Pranava; on Parvati's counsel he worshipped here and installed a four-faced (chaturmukha) lingam, regaining his role. Name "Vriddhapuri" = ancient town.
- Other shrines: Pancha Vinayakar, the nine sons of sage Kapila, Dakshinamurthy, Murugan with Valli and Deivanai, Brahma, Chandikeswarar, Gajalakshmi.
- Architecture: rajagopuram about 65 ft; Dinamalar attributes the building to a king Sundara Pandya, blending Pandya and Chola styles.
- Coordinates: 9.8942, 79.0636 — Wikipedia "Thiruppunavasal" village infobox (9°53′39″N 79°03′49″E); village point, the temple is the village's central landmark. Not a temple-specific point.
- Festivals: not specifically confirmed; customary Shaiva observances, confirm locally.
