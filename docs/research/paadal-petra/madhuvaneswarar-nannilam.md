# Nannilam — Madhuvaneswarar Temple

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Madhuvaneswarar** ("lord of the honey/madhu-forest"), swayambhu lingam; goddess **Madhuvana Nayaki**.
- **Location:** Nannilam town, Tiruvarur district, Tamil Nadu (~17 km from Tiruvarur). Approx 10.8631 N, 79.6042 E (approximate).
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam — Appar praised it; Sundarar sang nine verses (naming the Chola builder); alt name **Nannilathu Perunkovil**.
- **Architecture:** a **Maada Kovil** (traditionally Kochengat Chola).
- **Legends:** name from a madhu-tree forest; Devas turned into **honeybees** to worship the lingam (Vrithrasura episode); also **Devaranyam** — Devas sheltered from Jalandhara (defeated at nearby Virkudi).
