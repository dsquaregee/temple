# Anekatangapadeswarar Temple, Kanchipuram (anekatangapadeswarar-kanchipuram)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Thondai Nadu. District: Kanchipuram, Tamil Nadu. Tradition: Shaivism.
(Note backfilled by the lead session; the drafting agent was interrupted before writing it.)

- Deity: Shiva as Anekatangapadeswarar (Anegabeswarar); worshipped in the Kanchipuram tradition with Kamakshi.
- Tevaram: Tiru Anekatangavadam, one of the ancient Kanchipuram Shiva shrines. Coords 12.835, 79.707 (town-level, Kanchipuram).
- Legend: tied above all to Ganesha, who is said to have set up and worshipped the linga here (the name links to the elephant form).
- Not verified: exact temple coordinate (distinct Kanchi shrine); goddess-name specifics; dynasty/century (Tevaram era, 8); festival dates.
