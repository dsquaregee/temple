# Dayanidheeswarar Temple, Tiruvadakurangaduthurai (dayanidheeswarar-tiruvadakurangaduthurai)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Chola Nadu (north of the Kaveri), Thanjavur district (near Thiruvaiyaru). Tevaram name: Tiruvadakurangaduthurai (திருவடகுரங்காடுதுறை). Tradition: Shaivism.

- Deity: Shiva as Dayanidheeswarar (also Valeeswarar / Valinathar / Kulaivanangunathar per
  tntemplesproject/shivatemples); consort **Jata Makuta Nayaki** (separate sanctum).
- Tevaram: praised by Sambandar (Wikipedia: "Sambandar has sung praises of the temple").
  Whether Appar/Sundarar also sang here: not found.
- Coordinates: **~10.922, 79.197** (Wikipedia infobox 10°55′19″N 79°11′49″E). Treat as
  approximate: sources also say "about 5 km from Tiruvaiyaru", which does not sit easily with
  these coordinates; refine against a mapping source.
- Location note (correction to seed hint "Town Aduthurai"): this is the **north-bank**
  Vadakurangaduthurai (village now often called *Aduthurai Perumal Koil*), NOT Thenkurangaduthurai
  (Aduthurai on the Kumbakonam–Mayiladuthurai road, Abatsahayeswarar, south bank). `city` is
  given as "Tiruvadakurangaduthurai (Aduthurai)".
- Legend (defining story): two Ramayana traditions. (1) Rama, Hanuman and the vanaras, travelling
  south, reached this bank of the Kaveri (a crossing/port); for a safe river crossing Hanuman
  worshipped Shiva and established the **Dayanidhi lingam**. A variant says Hanuman came to atone
  for damaging Sita's sand lingam at Rameswaram on his return from Lanka. (2) **Vali** kept his
  strength in his tail; it was injured while he struck at Ravana and lost its power; he worshipped
  Shiva here and was restored, so Shiva is also **Valinathar / Valeeswarar**. Bas-relief of Vali
  worshipping Shiva in the temple.
- **Correction to seed hint (Vali–Sugriva):** the **Sugriva** story (Shiva hid Vali among parijata
  plants) belongs to Thenkurangaduthurai (Abatsahayeswarar), the south-bank namesake; it is not
  used here. Here the monkey figures are Hanuman and Vali.
- Architecture: east-facing, **five-tier gateway tower**; main shrine in three parts (sanctum,
  ardha mandapa, long maha mandapa); **three-tier vimana**, described as nagara form with a
  vesara-style sikhara (blog sources); goddess in separate sanctum; Vali bas-relief.
- History: one account says a Pallava-period structure, rebuilt by Pandyas and Cholas; Chola-period
  inscriptions are present (tourism page). Century set to 7 (Sambandar's Tevaram era).
- Festivals: special adornment/worship of Jata Makuta Nayaki on every full-moon day (Wikipedia
  snippet). Maha Shivaratri and monthly Pradosham stated generically (standard for Shiva temples),
  not sourced to this temple.
- Access: Kaveri delta, Thanjavur district, near Thiruvaiyaru; Thanjavur / Kumbakonam railheads;
  Tiruchirappalli airport. Local name "Aduthurai Perumal Koil".
- **Not verified:** exact temple coordinate (see above); distances from Thiruvaiyaru, Thanjavur
  and Kumbakonam; Appar/Sundarar hymns; named annual festivals and dates; timings; the Pallava
  founding claim and nagara/vesara vimana description (single blog sources); Tamil/Vali variant
  traditions come from non-scholarly sources.
