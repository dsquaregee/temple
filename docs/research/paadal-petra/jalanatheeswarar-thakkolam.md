# Jalanatheeswarar Temple, Thakkolam (jalanatheeswarar-thakkolam)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Thondai Nadu (Tevaram name: Thiruvooral).
Tradition: Shaivism.

- Deity: Shiva as **Jalanatheeswarar** ("Lord of the waters"), worshipped as an
  **earth / sand linga (prithvi linga)** honoured as **Theenda Thirumeni** ("the form
  not touched by hand"). Consort: **Giriraja Kanyambika** (per task brief); sources give
  **Giriraja Kannikambal** / Giriraja Kanniammai — treated as spelling variants of the
  same name, reconciled in the data as "Giriraja Kanyambika (Giriraja Kannikambal)".
- One of the Paadal Petra Sthalams; praised in the 7th-century Tevaram, hymns attributed
  to **Sambandar** (Thirugnana Sambandar).
- **Legend:** name "Jalanatheeswarar" from water that is said to have flowed from Nandi's
  mouth and risen from the earth beneath the deity (hence Lord of waters, and the earth
  linga). Site linked to **Daksha's yagna and Veerabhadra's intervention**; village name
  "Thakkolam" traced to Daksha's dying cry. Other worshippers in lore: Kamadhenu, Indra,
  Chandra (Moon), Yama, and the seven Saptha Kannikas (celestial maidens).
- **Architecture:** Dravidian; towered **gopuram** entrance. **Rajagopuram built 1543**
  by Vijayanagara king **Veera Prataba Sadasiva Raya**. Foundation attributed to the
  **Pallavas**; present masonry largely **Nayaka period (16th c.)**. Enclosure size
  reported inconsistently (≈1 acre to 6 acres — not used in prose).
- **History / inscriptions:** records of Pallava **Aparajita(varman)** and Chola
  **Aditya I** (grants of land, gold, livestock); Chola-era expansion. Founding date
  disputed in sources (one says **876 CE / Aparajitavarman**; another says **6th century
  Pallava**; a devotional source even credits Rajaraja Chola II — low confidence).
- **Battle of Takkolam (949 CE):** fought at the town between **Cholas and Rashtrakutas**;
  Chola prince **Rajaditya** (son of Parantaka I) killed by an arrow. The temple is the
  Tevaram shrine of the town, distinct from the battle but a strong historical anchor.
- **Current condition:** recent reporting describes neglect — a collapsed wall, overgrown
  tank, last kumbabhishekam 15+ years ago, restoration pending HR&CE approval. Handled
  gently in prose ("has at times stood in need of care/restoration").
- **Coordinates:** **~13.0158, 79.7369** — temple point from Wikipedia (13°00′57″N
  79°44′13″E). Town point ~13.0167, 79.7314. Temple-level coordinate used.
- Access: Ranipet district, west of Kanchipuram, between Arakkonam and Kanchipuram
  (nearest towns with rail/bus); Chennai nearest major airport.

- **Not verified / flagged:**
  - **Founding century disputed** (6th-c Pallava vs 876 CE Aparajitavarman vs a Chola-II
    claim). Used **century 7** to anchor the Tevaram/Sambandar era; prose presents the
    Pallava foundation + Chola + 1543 Nayaka rajagopuram layering as sourced.
  - **District:** Wikipedia's temple article still says Vellore; the town article and
    current sources say **Ranipet** (carved out of Vellore in 2019) — Ranipet used.
  - Consort name spelling (Kanyambika vs Kannikambal vs Kanniammai).
  - Enclosure size (sources range widely; omitted from prose).
  - Timings (single visitor guide: 4:30am–12pm, 5pm–8pm — not widely confirmed; flagged
    in-text and kept general).
  - Detailed festival calendar beyond Pradosham / Guru Peyarchi / Maha Shivaratri.
