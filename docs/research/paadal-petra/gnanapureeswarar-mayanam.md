# Tirunaalur Mayanam — Gnanapureeswarar Temple

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Gnanapureeswarar** (also **Gnanaparameswarar**, **Palasavanesar**); goddess **Gnanambikai** (also **Periyanayagi**). (Worklist goddess "Gnanambikai?" — confirmed.)
- **Location:** Tirunaalur Mayanam, ~3 km from Kudavasal, ~17 km from Kumbakonam, Kudavasal taluk, Thanjavur district, Tamil Nadu. ~10.8550 N, 79.5250 E (approximate).
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam — hymned by Tirugnana Sambandar; a **Mayana Kovil** (cremation-ground shrine) of Shaiva literature; Kaveri south bank.
- **Theme:** name joins **jnana** (saving knowledge) with the mayana (cremation ground) — Shiva beyond death.
- **Festivals:** Maha Shivaratri (Masi); Navaratri (Purattasi); Karthikai Mondays; Pradosham.
