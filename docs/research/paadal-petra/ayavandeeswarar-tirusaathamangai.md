# Tirusaathamangai (Seeyathamangai) — Ayavandeeswarar Temple (Ayavanthi)

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Ayavandeeswarar** (temple called **Ayavanthi**); goddess **Malarkkannammai** (also **Irumalarkannammai**; Sanskrit **Ubaya-Pushpa-Vilochani**, "she of the two flower-eyes"). (Worklist drafted goddess "Malarkanni?" — confirmed; name varies across sources.)
- **Location:** Seeyathamangai (Tevaram **Tirusaathamangai**), ~13 km from Nagapattinam on the Nagore–Kumbakonam road, near the Nagapattinam–Tiruvarur boundary (one listing Nannilam taluk, Tiruvarur), Tamil Nadu. ~10.8300 N, 79.7800 E (approximate).
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam — hymned by Tirugnana Sambandar; Kaveri south bank.
- **Festivals:** Maha Shivaratri (Masi); Navaratri (Purattasi); Karthikai Mondays; Pradosham.
