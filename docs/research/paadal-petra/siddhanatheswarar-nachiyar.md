# Tirunaraiyur (Nachiyar Koil) — Siddha Natheswarar Temple

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Siddha Natheswarar** (Siddhanatheswarar, "lord of the siddhas"); goddess **Soundara Nayaki** (also **Azhagambikai** / Soundarya Nayaki). (Worklist drafted goddess "Soundaryavalli?" — corrected to Soundara Nayaki.)
- **Location:** Tirunaraiyur (historical **Tirunaraiyur Chittechuram**; also **Narapuram**), beside **Nachiyar Koil** (<1 km), ~8–10 km from Kumbakonam on the Tiruvarur road, Thanjavur district, Tamil Nadu. ~10.9185 N, 79.4480 E (village-level, approximate).
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam — hymned by Sambandar and Sundarar; Kaveri south bank.
- **Legend:** sage **Medhavi**'s penance → Shiva conveys the wish to Vishnu → Lakshmi born as Vanjulavalli, the **Nachiyar** of Nachiyar Koil (shared Shaiva–Vaishnava story). Siddha **Gorakkar** cured of a curse-born skin disease by the lord's abhishekam oil → Siddha Natheswarar.
- **Festivals:** Maha Shivaratri (Masi); Navaratri (Purattasi); Karthikai Mondays; Pradosham.
