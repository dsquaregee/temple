# Azhagiyanathar (Abhirameswarar) Temple, Tiruvamathur (azhagiyanathar-tiruvamathur)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Nadu Nadu. District: Villupuram, Tamil Nadu. Tradition: Shaivism.
(Note backfilled by the lead session from the committed content + aggregators.)

- Deity: Shiva as Azhagiyanathar (Abhirameswarar); consort Muthambikai.
- Tiruvamathur, near Villupuram. Coords 11.968, 79.466 (town-level). A Paadal Petra Sthalam
  sung by all three Tevaram saints (Sambandar, Appar, Sundarar).
- Legend: the name Abhirameswarar is tied to Rama, who is said to have worshipped the Lord here.
- Not verified: exact temple coordinate; dynasty/century (Tevaram era, 7); festival dates;
  goddess-name variants.
