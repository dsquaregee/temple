# Manikandeswarar Temple, Tirumalpur (manikandeswarar-tirumalpur)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Thondai Nadu. District: Ranipet, Tamil Nadu. Tradition: Shaivism.
(Note backfilled by the lead session; the drafting agent was interrupted before writing it.)

- Deity: Shiva as Manikandeswarar (Maalvanangeeswarar); consort Karunai Nayaki (Anjanakshi).
- Tirumalpur, Thondai country; a Paadal Petra Sthalam. Coords 12.957, 79.674 (town-level).
- Legends: Vishnu (Tirumal) worshipped Shiva here and had his Sudarshana chakra restored to its edge
  (hence Maalvanangeeswarar, "whom Vishnu worshipped"); and Parvati's sand-linga raised in atonement on the river bank.
