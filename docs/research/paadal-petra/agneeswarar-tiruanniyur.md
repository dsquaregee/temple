# Tiruanniyur — Agneeswarar Temple

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Agneeswarar**; goddess **UNVERIFIED** — most sources name only "Parvati"; local/some listings give **Karpagambikai** (master-list draft also "Karpagambikai?"). Treated as tentative; prose refers to her as the Goddess/Parvati.
- **Location:** **Tiruanniyur (Anniyur)**, Kudavasal taluk, Tiruvarur district. ~10.86 N, 79.55 E (approximate — no Wikipedia coord).
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam, Kaveri south bank — hymned in the Tevaram.
- **Distinctness:** two Tevaram temples are named Tiru-Anniyur; this is the **Agneeswarar** shrine (Kudavasal taluk, Tiruvarur), DISTINCT from the catalog's `aabathsahayeswarar-tiruanniyur` (the northern Anniyur / Ponnur, Mayiladuthurai, goddess Periya Nayaki).
- **Festivals:** Maha Shivaratri (Masi); Navaratri (Purattasi); Karthikai Mondays; Pradosham.

**Notes:** Coordinates approximate. Goddess name unverified — flagged for native-speaker / primary-source confirmation.
