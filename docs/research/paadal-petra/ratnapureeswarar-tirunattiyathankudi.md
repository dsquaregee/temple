# Tirunattiyathankudi — Rathnapureeswarar Temple

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Rathnapureeswarar** (Manikkavannar); goddess **Maamalar Mangai** (Ratnapureeswari; also Mangalambikai). (Worklist goddess "Maamalar Mangai" — confirmed by shivatemples.com.)
- **Location:** **Tirunattiyathankudi**, ~14 km from Tiruvarur; Tiruvarur district. ~10.72 N, 79.56 E (approximate — no Wikipedia coord).
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam — hymned by **Sundarar**. The **avatara sthalam of Kotpuli Nayanar** (one of the 63 Nayanmars). Legend of Chola Ratnendra dividing gems (doubtful historically).
- **Note:** distinct from the catalog's **Manikkavannar at Tirumarugal** and **Ratnapureeswarar at Tiruvazhkolipputhur**.
- **Festivals:** Kotpuli Nayanar guru puja; Maha Shivaratri (Masi); Navaratri (Purattasi); Karthikai Mondays; Pradosham.
