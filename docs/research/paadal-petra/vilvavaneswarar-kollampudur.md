# Tirukkollampudur — Vilvaranyeswarar Temple

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Vilvaranyeswarar** (Vilvavaneswarar, "lord of the bael/vilva forest"); goddess **Soundara Nayaki**. (Worklist goddess "Soundaranayaki" — confirmed.)
- **Location:** Tirukkollampudur (historical **Kollamputhur**; Chellur via Kudavasal), ~22–24 km from Kumbakonam near Koradacheri/Nidamangalam, Tiruvarur district, Tamil Nadu. ~10.8147 N, 79.4600 E (village-level, approximate — same village as Pereyil Jagadeeswarar).
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam — hymned by Tirugnana Sambandar; one of the **Pancha Aranya Kshetrams** (the **vilva** / bael forest). River **Vettaru** nearby.
- **Legend:** **Sambandar** crossed the flooded Vettaru here by the power of his devotion/song to reach the shrine.
- **Festivals:** Maha Shivaratri (Masi); Navaratri (Purattasi); Karthikai Mondays; Pradosham.
