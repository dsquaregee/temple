# Oondreeswarar (Oondreswarar) Temple, Poondi (Thiruvenpakkam) — research notes

Master-list row 255 (Thondai Nadu). Row facts confirmed: deity Oondreeswarar; consort Minnoli Ammai (Minnoli Amman); singer Sundarar. No correction.

- Tevaram name Thiruvenpakkam. 17th Thondai Nadu sthalam (shivatemples tn17).
- Relocation: the original temple stood at Tiruvilamputhur on the Kosasthalaiyar (Kuchasthalai) river. The Poondi reservoir project (begun 1942, for Chennai's drinking water) took the land; the temple was dismantled and rebuilt at its present site at Poondi on the northern bank of the reservoir, consecrated on 5 July 1968 (shivatemples). Bharatpedia says moved 1942 — content says "when the reservoir was built in the 1940s ... rebuilt and consecrated in 1968".
- Wikipedia: original complex built by the Cholas; masonry 16th century (before the move); shivatemples ~11th c. Content: "Chola origins".
- Legend: Sundarar, having lost his sight (after breaking his vow to Sangiliyar at Thiruvottiyur — Periya Puranam), came here; the Lord gave him a walking stick (oondrukol) — hence Oondreeswarar. Wikipedia: Sundarar did not know who gave the stick and threw it away in anguish; believed to have broken Nandi's (right) horn — Nandi's right horn is broken. The goddess appeared as lightning (minnal) to light his way toward Kanchipuram — Minnoli Ammai. (At Kanchipuram he regained sight of one eye — well-known Periya Puranam tradition.)
- Sculpture: Sundarar standing with the stick in his left hand, shown sad with sightless eyes (Dinamalar).
- century: 8 (Sundarar only).
- Coordinates: 13.2081, 79.8786 — Wikipedia "Oondreswarar Temple" infobox (13°12′29″N 79°52′43″E); Poondi village article 13°12′35″N 79°52′44″E consistent. About 11 km from Tiruvallur on Tiruvallur–Tiruttani road (Wikipedia; may be via Poondi road).
