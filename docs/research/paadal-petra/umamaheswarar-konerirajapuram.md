# Umamaheswarar (Tirunallam) Temple, Konerirajapuram (umamaheswarar-konerirajapuram)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Chola Nadu south of the Kaveri. Tradition: Shaivism.
- Deity: Shiva as Umamaheswarar; consort Mangalanayaki (sources also: Degasoundari/Angavala Nayaki). Tevaram name: Tirunallam; sung by Appar & Sambandar.
- Rebuilt in stone 10th c. under Chola queen Sembiyan Mahadevi; famed for a colossal Chola bronze Nataraja in its own enclosure (cannot be removed without dismantling).
- Near Kuthalam, Mayiladuthurai district.
- Coordinates: ~11.0167, 79.60 (approximate, Konerirajapuram village — refine against a map).
- Not verified: exact consort name (sources vary); precise temple coordinate; the "largest bronze Nataraja" claim is widely stated but not scholarly-settled.
