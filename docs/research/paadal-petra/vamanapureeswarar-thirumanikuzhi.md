# Vamanapureeswarar (Vamaneeswarar) Temple, Thirumanikuzhi — research notes

Master-list row 232 (Nadu Nadu). Row confirmed: Vamanapureeswarar / consort Ambujakshi, Cuddalore district, Sambandar.

- Place: Thirumanikuzhi village, Cuddalore district, on the bank of the Kedilam (Gedilam) river, ~10 km west of Cuddalore town on the Panruti road; Thiruvahindrapuram (Divya Desam) is ~3 km east.
- Paadal Petra Sthalam, Nadu Nadu — 17th of the Nadu Nadu temples (shivatemples nn17). Sung by Thirugnana Sambandar (one pathigam); he calls the place "Udhavi Manikkuzhi". Only singer confirmed.
- Moolavar: Vamanapureeswarar / Vamaneeswarar; also Udhavi Nayagar, Manikka Varadar. Consort: Ambujakshi (Pangayakkanni), also Udhavi Nayaki; her shrine faces north in the first precinct.
- History: complex ~2 acres, concentric walls; five-tier rajagopuram on the east. Inscriptions reported of Rajaraja Chola I, Kulothunga Chola I, Rajendra Chola III (Wikipedia, lightly sourced). Inscriptions also use the name "Udhavi". Administered by HR&CE.
- Legends: (1) Vishnu, after the Vamana avatar (subduing Mahabali), came here as a brahmachari ("maani") and worshipped Shiva to be freed from a dosha; name Manikkuzhi linked to this ("maani" + "kuzhi", pit). (2) Vishnu asked that his worship not be disturbed; Shiva set Bheema Rudra, one of the Ekadasa Rudras, as guardian of the screen — a cloth screen with Bheema Rudra hangs before the moolavar; worship is offered first to the screen and the lingam is seen only when the screen is drawn aside at the deeparadhana. (3) A northern merchant (named Atiri/Athri in sources) attacked by robbers called on Shiva and was rescued — hence Udhavi Nayagar / Udhavi Nayaki ("the Lord/Lady who helped").
- Festivals: Maha Shivaratri (Masi), Navaratri (Purattasi).
- Coordinates: 11.7374, 79.6836 — Wikipedia temple infobox 11°44′14.7″N 79°41′0.9″E via search text; village article 11°44′14″N 79°41′01″E consistent. Distinct from Devanatha, Thiruvahindrapuram (11.7447, 79.7089).
