# Bhaktajaneswarar Temple, Tirunavalur (bhaktajaneswarar-tirunavalur)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Nadu Nadu. District: Villupuram, Tamil Nadu. Tradition: Shaivism.
(Note backfilled by the lead session; the drafting agent was interrupted by a rate limit
before writing it. Facts drawn from the committed temple content + WebSearch aggregators.)

- Deity: Shiva as Bhaktajaneswarar (Lord of the devotees); consort Manonmani.
- Anciently Jambupuri / Tirunama Nallur; on the north bank of the Kedilam river.
- Coordinates: 11.78, 79.33 (town-level; refine to the temple point if possible).
- **Birthplace of the saint Sundarar** (Nambi Arooran). The town is tied to three of the 63
  Nayanmars: Sundarar; his father Sadaiyanar; and the chieftain Narasinga Munaiyaraiyar who
  raised him. A Paadal Petra Sthalam of Nadu Nadu, praised in the Tevaram.
- Not verified: precise dynasty phasing (Chola origins with later additions); festival dates
  (Tamil calendar); exact temple coordinate.
