# Tirukkaruvili Kottittai (Karuveli) — Sarguneswarar Temple

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Sarguneswarar** (Sarguna-Natheswarar, "lord of all good qualities"); goddess **Sarvangasundari** (also **Sarvanga Nayaki**; standing image ~5.5 ft). (Worklist goddess "Sarvangasundari" — confirmed.)
- **Location:** Karuveli (Tevaram **Tirukkaruvili Kottittai**; village also **Sarguneswarapuram**), near Koonthalur, ~22 km from Kumbakonam; district varies **Tiruvarur / Thanjavur**, Tamil Nadu. ~10.9407 N, 79.5313 E (Wikipedia).
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam — hymned by Appar; Kaveri south bank.
- **Legend:** **King Sargunan** worshipped here and attained liberation → village name. Tank **Yama Theertham** (outside the temple); worship held to bestow **moksha**.
- **Festivals:** Maha Shivaratri (Masi); Navaratri (Purattasi); Karthikai Mondays; Pradosham.
- **Caution:** distinct from Karukudi Sargunalingeswarar (Marudhanallur), whose goddess is Sarva-Alankara Nayaki.
