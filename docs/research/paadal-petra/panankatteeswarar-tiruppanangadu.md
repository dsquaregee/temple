# Tiruppanangadu — Panankatteeswarar (Talapurisvarar) Temple

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Panankatteeswarar / Talapurisvarar** (plus a second form **Kirubapurisvarar**); goddesses **Kirubanayaki** and **Amirtavalli**. (Worklist goddess "Amirthavalli?" — present; primary consort Kirubanayaki per Wikipedia.)
- **Location:** **Tiruppanangadu**, ~16 km from Kanchipuram, near Cheyyar; **Tiruvannamalai district** (Thondai Nadu). ~12.70 N, 79.55 E (approximate — no Wikipedia coord). PIN 604410.
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam — hymned by **Sundarar**. Name = 'forest of palms'; sthala vriksham the **palm**. Legend: sage Agastya & the palm fruit; worship begins at Agastya's lingam, then his disciple Pulasthyar's.
- **Important disambiguation:** this is the **Thondai Nadu** Tiruppanangadu — NOT **Puravar Panankattur** (Panaiyapuram, Villupuram), the separate Nadu-Nadu PPS already in the catalog as `panankatteeswarar-panankattur`.
- **Festivals:** Brahmotsavam (Masi); Annabishegam (Aippasi); Arudra Darshan (Margazhi); Maha Shivaratri; Pradosham.
