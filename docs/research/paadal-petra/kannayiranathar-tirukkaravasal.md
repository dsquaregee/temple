# Tirukkaravasal (Tirukarayil) — Kannayiranathar Temple

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Kannayiranathar** (Kannayiramudayar) — "lord of a thousand eyes."
- **Location:** Tirukkaravasal / Tirukarayil, near Tiruvarur (sources split Tiruvarur / Nagapattinam district), Tamil Nadu. Approx 10.6825 N, 79.7039 E (approximate).
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam — sung by Sambandar and Appar; 119th south-bank Cauvery shrine (Dinamalar).
- **Legends/tanks:** King **Muchukunda** worshipped an **emerald (maragatha) lingam** from Indra. **Brahma Teertham** (Brahma freed from a curse). **Sesha Teertham** — Adisesha regained sight; waters believed to cure eye/skin ailments. **Kadukkai Vinayakar** (merchant's nutmeg → kadukkai/myrobalan).

**Notes:** Coordinates approximate; district varies by source. Goddess name not asserted (sources inconsistent). Translations AI-drafted; native-speaker QA recommended. Prose original; facts cross-checked against Wikipedia (Kannayariamudayar Temple, Thirukkarayil), TN Temples Project, Dinamalar.
