# Vamanapureeswarar (Vamaneeswarar) Temple, Tirumanikuzhi (vamanapureeswarar-tirumanikuzhi)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Nadu Nadu. District: Cuddalore, Tamil Nadu. Tradition: Shaivism.

- Deity: Shiva as Vamanapureeswarar (Vamaneeswarar); consort Ambujakshi (also given as
  Manonmani / Pangayakkanni / Udavinayaki across sources).
- Coordinates: 11.737, 79.684 (Wikipedia, 11°44′14.7″N 79°41′0.9″E).
- Sung by Sambandar (Thevaram; shivatemples lists Sambandar — 1 padigam). Paadal Petra Sthalam.
- Legends: linked to Vamana and Mahabali — a lamp-wick story in which a rat that tugged the
  wick is reborn as Mahabali; and Vamana placing his foot. The Sun God is said to have
  worshipped here.
- Architecture: five-tiered east-facing gopuram; three concentric granite-walled precincts;
  Ambujakshi's shrine faces north. Later Chola inscriptions (Rajaraja I 985–1014,
  Kulothunga I 1070–1120).
- Festivals: Purattasi Navarathri (Sep–Oct); Masi Maha Shivaratri (Feb–Mar); Pradosham.
- Not verified: the exact goddess name (sources vary — used Ambujakshi); timings (two
  conflicting ranges); the precise founding century (Tevaram era used, century 7).
