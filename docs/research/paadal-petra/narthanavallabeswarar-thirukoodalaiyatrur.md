# Narthana Vallabeswarar Temple, Thirukoodalaiyatrur — research notes

Master-list row 218 (Nadu Nadu). Row facts confirmed; no corrections. Consort is also called Purikuzhal Nayaki (Purikuzhalaambikai); the temple has two Ambal shrines, Gnanasakthi and Parasakthi.

- Place: Thirukoodalaiyathoor (Thirukoodalaiyatrur) village, Cuddalore district, between Srimushnam and Sethiyathope, west of Chidambaram. Stands at the confluence of the Vellar and the Manimuktha (Manimutharu) — revered as Dakshina Prayagai (the southern Prayag).
- One of the 276 Paadal Petra Sthalams; counted 3rd in Nadu Nadu (shivatemples nn03). Sung by Sundarar only — one pathigam.
- Moolavar: Narthana Vallabeswarar (Nerikattunayakar, "the Lord who showed the way"). Ambal: Gnanasakthi and Parasakthi in two shrines; also Purikuzhal Nayaki.
- Legend: Sundarar, on his way to Vriddhachalam (Thirumuthukundram), passed this village by without worshipping; Shiva met him on the road as an old Brahmin and showed him the way, then vanished; Sundarar realised and sang his pathigam marvelling that he had not known the Lord had come along that road.
- Theerthams: Brahma, Agastya, Katyayana theerthams; the river confluence.
- Sun's rays fall on the lingam on the first three days of Panguni.
- History: original shrine believed Chola; present masonry largely Nayak (16th c.) per Wikipedia.
- Festivals: Masi Magam brahmotsavam (13 days per Dinamalar), four yearly festivals; Panguni sun-worship days; Shivaratri, Pradosham.
- Century: 8 (Sundarar only).
- Coordinates: 11.4158, 79.4772 — Wikipedia infobox (11°24′57″N 79°28′38″E). Manimuktha mouth per Wikipedia 11.417, 79.464 (~1.4 km W) — consistent. No other catalog entry in this village.
