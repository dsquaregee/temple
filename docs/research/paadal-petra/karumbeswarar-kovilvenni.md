# Kovilvenni — Venni Karumbeswarar Temple

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Karumbeswarar** ("lord of sugarcane"); goddess **Soundaranayagi**. Lingam worshipped as a **bound sheaf of sugarcane stems**.
- **Location:** Kovilvenni, near Needamangalam, Tiruvarur district, Tamil Nadu. Approx 10.7822 N, 79.4206 E (approximate).
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam — sung by Sambandar and Appar. Historical name **Tiruvenniyur** → Venniyur → Koil Venni.
- **Legend:** two sages found a swayambhu lingam amid sugarcane; Shiva decreed sugarcane (karumbu) his covering and name, nandiyavartham his sthala vriksham.
- **Belief:** offering of sugar here believed to relieve **diabetes**.

**Notes:** Coordinates approximate; "all four yugas" and "1,300 years" are traditional claims, not dated history. Translations AI-drafted; native-speaker QA recommended. Prose original; facts cross-checked against Wikipedia (Venni Karumbeswarar Temple), TN Temples Project.
