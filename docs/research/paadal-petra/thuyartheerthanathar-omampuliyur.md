# Thuyar Theertha Nathar (Pranava Vyakrapureeswarar) Temple, Omampuliyur (thuyartheerthanathar-omampuliyur)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Chola Nadu (north of the Kaveri). District: Cuddalore
(Kattumannarkoil taluk). Tevaram name: Tiruvomampuliyur (திருஓமாம்புலியூர்). Tradition: Shaivism.
Listed as the 31st Paadal Petra shrine on the northern bank (Kaveri vada karai).

- Deity: Shiva as **Thuyar Theertha Nathar** (also Pranava Vyakrapureeswarar, Pranava Vyagrapureeswarar);
  consort **Poongodi Nayaki** (also Pushpalathambikai / Pushpakalathambikai).
- Tevaram: **Sambandar, 1 hymn; Appar (Tirunavukkarasar), 1 hymn.** Sambandar renders the name
  "Om-Ampuliyur" (the sacred syllable Om); Appar "Omampuliyur / Homampuliyur", from the homa smoke.
- Coordinates: **approximate ~11.22, 79.56** — the only figure found is for the Omampuliyur post
  office (PIN 608306), not the temple. It sits uneasily with "7 km from Kattumannarkoil"
  (Kattumannarkoil is further north), so treat it as village-level only; refine against a map.
- Defining legend: Parvati asked Shiva to explain the meaning of the Pranava (Om); Shiva, as
  Dakshinamurthy, imparted the true meaning here, hence "Om-Aam-Puliyur" / Pranava-vyagrapuram.
  A variant (hindu-blog) says Shiva cursed Parvati to be born on earth when her attention wandered;
  she prayed to Saraswati and did penance under an ilanthai tree. Sage **Vyaghrapada** (tiger-footed)
  worshipped here; one of the five "Puliyur" shrines where he is said to have sought the dance
  darshan at Chidambaram. **Guru Parihara sthalam** ("Gurumurthy sthalam"); abhishekam is done
  first to Dakshinamurthy, then to the Moolavar (per one blog source).
- **Correction / handling of seed hint:** the sources do not explain "Thuyar Theertha" — the
  "Lord who ends/removes sorrow" gloss is the name's plain Tamil meaning (துயர் = sorrow,
  தீர்த்த = one who ended/cured), not a sourced legend. No sourced sorrow-removal story was
  found; the prose treats it as the Lord's name and invites those with troubles. The name
  "Thuyar Theertha" is not tied by any source to a specific episode.
- Architecture: east-facing, **three-tiered rajagopuram**; front mandapa has images/panels of the
  sthala puranam; **Dakshinamurthy enshrined inside the sanctum** (one of few such temples; one
  source says two Dakshinamurthy shrines — not reconciled); koshta figures Vinayagar,
  Dakshinamurthy, Annamalaiyar/Lingodbhava, Brahma, Vishnu, Durga; a panel of Shiva giving
  Vishnu the chakra; Murugan (Arumugha) shrine in the courtyard. Sthala vriksham: ilanthai
  (Elandai). Teertham listed as Kollidam / Kolli teertham / Gowri teertham.
- Festivals (secondary source): Thai Poosam, Masi Magam, Navaratri, Sivaratri; Guru Peyarchi
  (Jupiter transit) celebrated (Dinamalar). Pradosham is generic.
- Timings (TN Temples Project / Dinamalar): 6:00–12:00 and 16:00–20:00. Phone listed +91 4144 264 845.
- Access: northern bank of the Kollidam, ~15 km east of Gangaikonda Cholapuram; listed ~19 km from
  Mayiladuthurai, ~43 km from Kumbakonam; one source says ~30 km from Chidambaram and ~7 km
  from Kattumannarkoil (inconsistent — the prose avoids these figures except the ~15 km from
  Gangaikonda Cholapuram). Nearest rail/airport are inferences.
- **Not verified:** founding dynasty/century (century 7 used for the Tevaram era; "Chola and
  later" is a generic assumption); exact temple coordinates; the "sorrow-removing" etymology of
  Thuyar Theertha; the cursed-Parvati variant (single source); whether there are one or two
  Dakshinamurthy images; the festival list (secondary site); distances to Kattumannarkoil and
  Chidambaram; nearest railway/airport; which bank/river exactly (sources say Kollidam; Wikipedia
  says only "north of the Kaveri"); King Sadhanandan leprosy tradition (single source, omitted).
