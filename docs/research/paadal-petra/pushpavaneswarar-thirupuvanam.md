# Pushpavaneswarar Temple, Thiruppuvanam — pushpavaneswarar-thirupuvanam

Master-list row 204 (Pandya Nadu 11). Facts confirmed; no corrections.

- Place: Thiruppuvanam (Thirupuvanam), Sivaganga district, on the bank of the Vaigai, ~20 km SE of Madurai on the Madurai–Rameswaram road.
- Deity: Shiva as Pushpavaneswarar (Poovananathar); consort Soundaranayaki.
- Tevaram: Appar, Sambandar and Sundarar — said to be the only one of the 14 Pandya Nadu sthalams with hymns by all three.
- Sambandar legend: unable to cross the river (ground/sand full of lingams per some tellings) he sang from the far bank; Nandi blocked his view, and the Nandi is shown leaning slightly aside. The spot where the three saints sang is the Moovar Mandapam across the river.
- Ponnanaiyal legend: a devotee (dancer) wishing to make a gold image of the Lord; Shiva came as a siddha alchemist and turned her metal vessels to gold; told in the Thiruvilaiyadal Puranam (Madurai's sacred sports).
- Sthala purana: ashes of the dead turned to flowers here; immersed in the Vaigai; place held holier than Kasi — ancestral rites (tarpanam) on the Vaigai banks.
- Architecture: seven-tier rajagopuram; tank adjacent; sacred waters Vaigai, Manikarnikai, Vasishta, Indra theerthams.
- Festivals: Vaikasi Visakam (brahmotsavam), Aadi mulaikottu, Navaratri, Aippasi kolattam.
- Dating claims in search results (Aditya I) appear to be copied from another article — not used.
- Coordinates: 9.8269, 78.2567 (Wikipedia infobox 9°49′37″N 78°15′24″E, via search result text).
