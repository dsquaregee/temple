# Tirumarugal — Manikkavannar (Ratnagireeswarar) Temple

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Manikkavannar** (Tamil, "gem-hued") = **Ratnagireeswarar** (Sanskrit); self-manifested (swayambhu) lingam.
- **Location:** Tirumarugal, Nannilam area, Tiruvarur/Nagapattinam district, Tamil Nadu (~6-7 km from Tiruppugalur). Approx 10.8422 N, 79.6281 E (approximate).
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam — sung in the Tevaram; sthala vriksham = marugal tree.
- **Signature legend:** Sambandar revives a **snakebitten bridegroom** with a hymn and solemnises the marriage, **Shiva standing witness** when the union is doubted. Nearby Ganesha = **Vitam-teertha Vinayakar** ("vitam" = poison) who aided the cure.
- **Associations:** marriage, children, healing / relief from snakebite-poison.

**Notes:** Coordinates approximate; district varies in sources (Tiruvarur/Nagapattinam). Goddess name not asserted (sources inconsistent).
