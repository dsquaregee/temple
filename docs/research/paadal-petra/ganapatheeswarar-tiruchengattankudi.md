# Tiruchengattangudi — Ganapatheeswarar Temple

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Ganapatheeswarar** (also **Uthirapasupatheeswarar**); goddess **Vaata Thirukkuzhal Nayaki** (Vaaitha Tirukuzhal Umai Nayaki).
- **Location:** Tiruchengattangudi, near Tirumarugal, Tiruvarur district, Tamil Nadu. Approx 10.8056 N, 79.6472 E (approximate).
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam (Tevaram); birthplace of **Siruthonda Nayanar** (one of the 63 Nayanmars).
- **Siruthondar legend:** Shiva, as a guest, asks for the family's son (Seeralan) as food; the family consents; Shiva restores the boy and elevates Siruthondar — the Periyapuranam's most severe test of devotion. Sculptural panel in the temple.
- **Vatapi Ganapati:** Village famed for the Vatapi Ganapati icon, traditionally linked to Paranjothi / Narasimhavarman I's campaign against Chalukya Pulikesi II. Sthala-purana: Ganesha worshipped Shiva here after slaying Gajamukhasura; place name ~ "the ground turned red."
