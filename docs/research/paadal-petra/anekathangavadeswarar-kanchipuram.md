# Anekathangavadeswarar (Anekathangapadeswarar) Temple, Kanchipuram (Kachi Anekathangapadam) — research notes

Master-list row 242 (Thondai Nadu). Row facts confirmed: deity Anekathangavadeswarar / Anekathangapadeswarar (Anegabeswarar); no separate Ambal shrine — Kamakshi common consort; singer Sundarar (pathigam in the 7th Tirumurai).

- Place: Pillaiyarpalayam, Kanchipuram 631501, on the way from the town to the Kailasanathar temple (via Putheri Street); a very small campus close to Kailasanathar.
- 4th Thondai Nadu sthalam on shivatemples.com (tn04).
- Name: Tevaram and inscriptions use Anekatangapadam; "Kachi" prefix distinguishes it from the northern Anekathangapatham identified with Gaurikund (Sambandar) — already in catalog as anekathangapatheswarar-gaurikund.
- Legend: Vinayaka (Ganesha) installed and worshipped the lingam here, with Shiva's blessing, before defeating the asura Kesi and marrying Vallabhai (Kesi's daughter in one account; in another a girl raised by sage Marichi and seized by Kesi). Kanchi Puranam: worship here releases one from rebirth. "Aneka" — "Anega(ba)" link to Vinayaka (Anegabeswarar). Kubera tradition not confirmed — omitted.
- Timings vary in sources (one says only a short morning window) — content says confirm locally; small temple may keep short hours.
- Coordinates: 12.8419, 79.6911 — Wikipedia "Anekadhangavadeswarar temple" infobox (12°50′31″N 79°41′28″E). Distinct from kailasanathar-kanchipuram (12.8479, 79.6788) and metraleeswarar (12.835, 79.6917).
- Festivals: Vinayaka Chaturthi plausible but not confirmed; content names customary Shaiva observances, confirm locally.
