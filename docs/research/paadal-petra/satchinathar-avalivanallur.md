# Avalivanallur — Satchinathar Temple

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Satchinathar** ("the witness lord"); goddess **Soundaranayaki**.
- **Location:** Avalivanallur, near Kumbakonam, Tiruvarur district, Tamil Nadu. Approx 10.9289 N, 79.4511 E (approximate).
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam — sung by Sambandar and Sundarar. Also a **Pancha Aranya Sthalam** (five forest temples near Kumbakonam); old setting = Pathiri Vanam (trumpet-flower forest), sthala vriksham pathiri.
- **Signature legend:** A priest back from Kashi couldn't tell his sickness-worn wife from her healthy sister; **Shiva appeared as witness** to affirm the truth → Satchinathar ("Satchi" = witness).
