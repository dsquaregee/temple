# Vigirthanatheswarar (Kalyana Vikirteeswarar) Temple, Venjamakoodal (vigirthanatheswarar-venjamakoodal)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Kongu Nadu (Karur district; Tevaram name Venjamakoodal /
Venjamaakudal). Tradition: Shaivism. **THIN SOURCING — prose kept general and factual.**

- Deity: Shiva as Vigirthanatheswarar (Vikriteswarar / Vigirtheeswarar / Kalyana
  Vigirtheeswarar); swayambu linga. Consort: sources give **Panner Mozhi Ammai**
  (= Panmozhi Nayaki) per Dinamalar; **Vigirtheswari / Vikritambika** per other listings.
  The owner's brief name "Madhuramozhi Nayaki" was **not found in any source**; used as
  given alongside Panmozhi Nayaki, flagged for owner confirmation.
- Counted as the fifth Kongu Nadu shrine praised in the Tevaram (Dinamalar); Sundarar
  sang a pathigam here.
- Location: east bank of the Kudaganaru river, ~16 km south-west of Karur on the way to
  Aravakurichi.
- Coordinates: **~10.89, 77.97 — NOT found in any source; estimated from "16 km SW of
  Karur"** (Karur ~10.96, 78.08). Low precision; **must be verified against a mapping
  source before relying on it.**
- Architecture: five-tiered rajagopuram; Shiva and Ambal shrines face east; linga ~5 ft,
  goddess image ~2.5 ft; shrines to Panchalingams, Bhairava, 63 Nayanmars and others;
  deity said to wear a snake ornament (nagabharanam), swayambu.
- Legend: Sundarar receives gold from Shiva. Versions: Shiva (as an old priest, per
  Wikipedia) / pledges his sons Vinayaka and Murugan to an old woman (Parvati) for gold,
  which he gives Sundarar (Dinamalar). Hunter king Venjan/Venchan worshipped here and gave
  the name ("koodal" = confluence/meeting); Indra worshipped to be freed of sin. Source
  texts are partly Wikipedia-derived and flagged as lacking inline citations — traditional
  accounts, not verified history.
- Age: "1000–2000 years" (Dinamalar) / "over 1200 years" (Wikipedia); structural temple
  patrons not recorded. Century 8 used for the Tevaram/Sundarar era.
- Festivals (Dinamalar): 10-day Masi Brahmotsavam (Feb–Mar), Margazhi Tiruvadhirai (Dec–Jan),
  Aipasi Annabhishekam (Oct–Nov), Tirukarthikai (Nov–Dec), Maha Shivaratri (Feb–Mar).
- Access: Karur nearest rail/base; Tiruchirappalli and Coimbatore airports (a few hours by
  road, not measured).
- **Not verified:** exact coordinates; goddess name "Madhuramozhi Nayaki"; founding
  dynasty and patrons; legend details (conflicting versions); current timings; exact
  road/bus access; whether the swayambu/nagabharanam claim is precisely as stated.
