# Tiruthandalaineeneri (Thandalaicherry) — Neenerinathar Temple

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Neenerinathar** (Neelneri Nathar); goddess **Gnanambikai** ("mother of wisdom"). (Worklist goddess "Gnanambikai?" — confirmed.)
- **Location:** Thandalaicherry (Tevaram **Tiruthandalaineeneri**), ~3–4 km N of Thiruthuraipoondi toward Tiruvarur, Tiruvarur district, Tamil Nadu (Velur post, PIN 614715). ~10.5600 N, 79.6300 E (approximate).
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam — hymned by Tirugnana Sambandar; Kaveri south bank (~110th). Sthala vriksham **kurundham**; tank **Omaka Theertham**.
- **Legend:** birthplace of **Arivattaya Nayanar** (of the 63 Nayanmars) — his daily food offering spilled; in despair he raised his sickle, and Shiva stayed his hand and received the offering.
- **Festivals:** Maha Shivaratri (Masi); Arivattaya Nayanar festival; Navaratri (Purattasi); Karthikai Mondays; Pradosham.
