# Onakantheeswarar Temple, Kanchipuram (Onakanthanthali) — research notes

Master-list row 241 (Thondai Nadu). Row facts confirmed: Onakantheeswarar; no separate Ambal shrine — Kamakshi is the common consort of Kanchipuram's Shiva temples; singer Sundarar (hymn in the 7th Tirumurai).

- Place: Panjupettai (Panchupettai), Kanchipuram, PIN 631502; reached from the Kanchipuram–Arakkonam road past Sarva Theertham tank and the Panjupettai electricity sub-station.
- 3rd Thondai Nadu sthalam on shivatemples.com (tn03).
- Three Shiva sanctums: Oneswarar and Kantheswarar (worshipped by the asuras Onan and Kanthan) and Jalandhareswarar (Salandheeswarar), worshipped by the asura Jalandhara.
- Name legend: Onan and Kanthan — described as commanders of the asura king Banasura (Vanasura) in one source; Wikipedia says they obtained a lingam in the forest here and became devotees. Present as tradition.
- Sundarar legend: Sundarar, in need, sang here asking for wealth; the Lord hid in a tamarind tree while he sang (at the fifth stanza), and as he continued, golden tamarind fruits fell. Devotees believe singing this pathigam brings prosperity.
- No separate Parvathi shrine (Kamakshi common shrine tradition).
- HR&CE administered.
- Coordinates: 12.8506, 79.6942 — Wikipedia "Ona Kantheeswarar Temple" infobox (12°51′02″N 79°41′39″E). Distinct from other Kanchipuram entries.
- Festivals: not specifically confirmed; customary Shaiva observances, confirm locally.
