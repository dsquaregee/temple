# Ona Kantheeswarar Temple, Kanchipuram (onakantheeswarar-kanchipuram)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Thondai Nadu (Tevaram name: Tiru Onakanthan Thali). Tradition: Shaivism.

- Deity: Shiva as Ona Kantheeswarar, a swayambhu (self-manifested) linga. Consort: Parvati worshipped
  as **Kamakshi**, following the Kanchipuram tradition in which Kamakshi is the common Goddess shrine
  for the city's Shiva temples. A sanctum image behind the linga shows Shiva and Parvati in the
  wedding pose (**tirumana kolam**).
- Counted a Paadal Petra Sthalam; praised in the Tevaram, with the hymn attributed to **Sundarar**
  (shivatemples lists pathigam: Sundarar 1).
- Location: **Panjupettai** quarter of Kanchipuram; shrines raised on an elevated platform.
- Name legend: from two asuras/figures, **Onan and Kaanthan**, remembered as commanders of the
  army of the asura king **Banasura (Vanasuran)**, who obtained a linga and worshipped Shiva here;
  the Lord became Ona Kantheeswarar and the shrine Tiru Onakanthan Thali. Accounts vary: some tie
  the two to building the temple; the two worshipped lingas are called Oneshwar and Kantheshwar,
  and a **third linga/asura (Jalandeeswaran)** is also associated.
- Architecture: small **three-tiered rajagopuram**; **three Shiva lingas in three separate sanctums**
  is the distinctive feature; shrines on an elevated structure.
- Festivals: no large car-festival calendar surfaced; general Shaiva observances (Maha Shivaratri,
  monthly Pradosham). Administered by the Tamil Nadu HR&CE board.
- Access: in Kanchipuram, ~75 km from Chennai (nearest international airport); rail and road served.
- **Coordinates: town-level ~12.839, 79.705** (Panjupettai / Kanchipuram area). Exact temple point
  not found — refine against a mapping source when possible.
- **Not verified:** founding dynasty/century of the structural temple (used **century 8** for
  Sundarar's era; dynasty left general); exact temple coordinate; current timings; festival calendar.
  A minority source ("Onakantheshvara & Jalandareshvara", washerman/Muktishvara account) conflicts
  with the mainstream story and was **not** used. "Lover of the Sravana constellation" name-gloss is
  folk etymology and was not asserted.
