# Tirupperuvelur — Abhimukteswarar Temple

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Abhimukteswarar** (Avimukteswarar / **Priyanathar**); goddess **Baagampriyal** (also Niraikonda Nayaki). (Worklist goddess "?" — resolved to Baagampriyal / Niraikonda Nayaki from sources.)
- **Location:** **Tirupperuvelur** at **Manakkal Ayyampettai**, ~10 km from Tiruvarur, ~30 km from Kumbakonam; Tiruvarur district. ~10.82 N, 79.56 E (approximate — no Wikipedia coord).
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam — hymned by **Sambandar and Appar**.
- **Festivals:** Maha Shivaratri (Masi); Navaratri (Purattasi); Karthikai Mondays; Pradosham.
