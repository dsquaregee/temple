# Arulsomanathar (Somanathaswami) Temple, Tiruneedur (arulsomanathar-tiruneedur)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Chola Nadu (north of the Kaveri). District: Mayiladuthurai
(formerly listed under Nagapattinam). Tevaram name: Tiruneedur (திருநீடூர்; also Needur).
Tradition: Shaivism.

- Deity: Shiva as Arulsomanathar / Somanathaswami (also Karkateswarar; processional form
  Somaskandar; "Gaana Nirthana Shankarar" in the Indra legend). Consort: **Veyuru Tholiyammai**
  (Veyurutholi Ammai), also Aalalasundara Nayaki. The seed hint "Vedanayaki" was **not found in
  any source** — the goddess is Veyuru Tholiyammai; used that.
- Tevaram: praised by Appar (Tirunavukkarasar) and Sundarar (sources: one pathikam each / two
  pathikams). Sambandar is not credited with this shrine in the sources found. Counted as one of
  the Chola Nadu north-bank shrines (shivatemples.com nc21).
- Historic place-name **Sapthapuri**; area said to have been a grove of magizham (Magilavanam).
- Coordinates: **approximate, village-level ~11.105, 79.607** (about 5 km from Mayiladuthurai,
  town centre ~11.10, 79.65). No sourced temple point found — refine against a mapping source.
- Legends (seed hint said "Chandra/Soma worship" — research shows several traditions; the
  **crab/Manmasudhan story is the best-attested defining legend** and Chandra is a secondary one):
  1. Demon **Manmasudhan** (also spelled Thanmasudhan) cursed to be born a crab; Narada sent him
     to Needur; bathed in the Kaveri, worshipped, and Shiva absorbed him into the linga through a
     hole; the crab's footprint/hole is shown on the linga. Hence **Karkateswarar**; special for the
     Kataka (Cancer) rasi. (Wikipedia gives the variant "born as cancer/nandu".)
  2. **Indra** shaped a linga of wet Kaveri sand to worship Shiva and see his dance; Shiva danced
     as **Gaana Nirthana Shankarar**; the sand linga is believed to be the present one.
  3. **Chandra** (Soma) regained his lustre after worshipping here and creating the **Chandra
     Theertham** (hence Somanathar), on guru Brihaspati's advice.
- Architecture: east-facing; **no rajagopuram**; stucco entrance arch with Rishabarudar flanked by
  Vinayaka and Murugan; **single-tiered gopuram** at the inner prakaram entrance; two prakarams;
  sanctum, ardha mandapa, maha mandapa. Shrines: Sivalokanathar, Kailasanathar, Ananda
  Thandavamurthy, Ganesha, Murugan, Seven Mothers (Saptamatrikas); Bhadrakali (also a processional
  image). Sthala vriksham: magizham (Mimusops elengi). **Nine theerthams** incl. Chandra
  Theertham. Processional images include Indra, Bhadrakali and **Munaiyaduvar Nayanar**.
- Festivals: **Karkataka Puja on Aadi full-moon day** (Jul–Aug) commemorating the crab legend;
  Vinayaka Chaturthi (Aavani), Navaratri, Skanda Shashti, Thirukarthikai, Makara Sankranti, Maha
  Shivaratri; sun-ray worship of the linga for a few days in Aavani (tradition).
- Access: ~4.8 km from Mayiladuthurai (regular buses); Mayiladuthurai Junction is the railway
  station; neighbouring Tevaram shrine Tiruanniyur (Ponnur) is nearby.
- **Not verified:** exact temple coordinate; founding dynasty/structural date (medieval Chola-era
  is inferred from Wikipedia's reference to "Later Chola Temples"; century 7 used for the Tevaram
  era); current timings (sources give 7:00–12:30 / 4:00–8:30 and 6:00–13:00 / 15:00–19:00); whether
  Sambandar also sang here; the Chandra legend's exact form (sources vary); direction/distance
  wording to Ponnur; exact Aavani sun-ray dates; whether the Munaiyaduvar link is a shrine or only a
