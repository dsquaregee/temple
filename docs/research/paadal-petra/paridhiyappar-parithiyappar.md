# Tiruparidhiniyamam (Parithiappar Koil) — Parithiappar Temple

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Parithiappar** (also **Bhaskareswarar**; parithi/bhaskara = the sun); goddess **Mangalambikai** (also **Mangalanayaki**). (Worklist goddess "Mangalanayaki" — confirmed.)
- **Location:** Parithiappar Koil (Tevaram **Tiruparidhiniyamam** / Parithi Niyamam), ~16–19 km from Thanjavur toward Pattukottai, Orathanadu taluk, Thanjavur district, Tamil Nadu, PIN 614904. ~10.6772 N, 79.2373 E (Wikipedia).
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam — hymned by Sambandar, Appar and Sundarar; Kaveri south bank (~101st on south bank).
- **Distinctive:** a **sun shrine** — Surya stands worshipping before the lingam; three **Chandikeswarar** images in the prakara. Town of the Tirukkural commentator **Paridhi**.
- **Festivals:** Maha Shivaratri (Masi); Suryapuja; Navaratri (Purattasi); Karthikai Mondays; Pradosham.
