# Tiruvalivalam — Manathunainathar Temple

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Manathunainathar** ("companion of the mind"; Sanskrit **Hrudaya Kamala Nathar**, "lord of the heart's lotus"); goddess **Maazhai Onkanni** (also **Madhyayadhakshi**, Valaiyangkanni). (Worklist left goddess "?" — supplied.)
- **Location:** Valivalam, ~18–19 km from Tiruvarur, near Tirukaravasal, Nagapattinam district, Tamil Nadu. ~10.7500 N, 79.7500 E (approximate).
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam — hymned by **all three** great saints (Sambandar, Appar, Sundarar); Kaveri south bank (~121st). Sthala vriksham **punnai**; tank **Sankara/Chakkara Theertham**.
- **Theme:** the lord as the inner companion of the heart/mind.
- **Festivals:** Maha Shivaratri (Masi); Navaratri (Purattasi); Karthikai Mondays; Pradosham.
