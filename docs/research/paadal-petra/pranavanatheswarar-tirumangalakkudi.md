# Pranavanatheswarar (Prananatheswarar / Prananadeswarar) Temple, Tirumangalakkudi (pranavanatheswarar-tirumangalakkudi)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Chola Nadu (north of the Kaveri) (Tevaram name: Tirumangalakkudi, திருமங்கலக்குடி).
District: Thanjavur (one source lists Mayiladuthurai; Thanjavur used, matching Wikipedia). Tradition: Shaivism.

- Deity: Shiva as Pranavanatheswarar. Sources spell it **Prananatheswarar / Prananadeswarar /
  Prananatheeswarar** ("Prana-natha", lord of life); "Pranavanatheswarar" (pranava = Om) is the
  slug spelling requested by the owner and is kept in the name. Wikipedia also names the shrines
  as Mangalapureeswarar and Mangalanayagi. Consort: **Mangalambigai (Mangalanayagi)**.
- Tevaram: one decad each by Sambandar and Appar per shivatemples.com (Dinamalar credits
  Sambandar; a blog also names Sundarar, which is not used). Listed as 92nd Paadal Petra
  Sthalam in one source and 275th in another list (numbering differs; not used).
- Coordinates: **11.024, 79.471** (Wikipedia, Prananadeswarar Temple). Approximate; refine
  against a mapping source when possible.
- "Pancha Mangala Kshetra" (shivatemples.com): place name, goddess Mangalambigai, Mangala
  Vimanam, Mangala Vinayaka, Mangala Theertham. Also Surya and Chandra theerthams.
- Legends: (1) Navagrahas cursed with leprosy; on Brahma's advice they worshipped Shiva,
  Parvati and Vinayaka here and were healed (tntemplesproject; Thanjavur Parampara has a variant
  where Brahma cursed them). Sun god also said to have worshipped here. (2) Chola minister
  **Alaivanar / Aalaivanar (also Analavanan)** built the temple from tax revenue without the
  king's leave (king named as Kulothunga Chola in some accounts); punished/beheaded; wife prayed
  to Mangalambigai and he revived at the temple. Hence the goddess is a protector of
  mangalyam; mangala sutra thread given as prasadam to women (tntemplesproject).
- **Seed hint correction:** the brief suggested a Sukran (Venus) link to Suryanar Koil. No
  source found ties Sukran specifically to this temple. Sources instead describe the
  Navagraha leprosy legend and Sun worship; one source says the planets at Suryanar Koil are
  those "belonging to" this temple, another says there is no special Navagraha shrine here. The
  prose says only that the nine planets and the Sun are linked to the place, and that Suryanar
  Koil is nearby.
- Architecture: east-facing, five-tiered rajagopuram (blogs); Wikipedia: Dravidian, "three
  precincts", many halls; blogs say two prakarams (conflict, so prose is non-specific).
  Wikipedia lists Mangalapureeswarar and Mangalanayagi shrines as most prominent. Administered
  by Thiruvaduthurai Adheenam.
- History: Wikipedia/tntemplesproject minister legend; a tourism blog credits Kulothunga Chola
  with an 11th-century renovation. Century set to 7 for the Tevaram era.
- Festivals: Panguni Brahmotsavam (Mar–Apr; sacred marriage; tourism blog says 10 days, wedding
  at night on the 7th day; Dinamalar mentions a wedding on the 7th day without the month);
  Aani Thirumanjanam, Vinayaka Chaturthi, Navaratri, Margazhi Thiruvadhirai, Aadi Perukku
  (Kaveri worship). Pradosham/Maha Shivaratri general.
- Access: about 2 km from Aduthurai (another source says 1 km), ~10 km from Kumbakonam, on the
  Mayiladuthurai–Kumbakonam route. Dinamalar timings: 6:30–12:30 and 4:00–8:30.
