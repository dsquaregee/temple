# Narthanavallabeswarar Temple (narthanavallabeswarar-koodalaiyathur)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Nadu Nadu. Tradition: Shaivism.

- Deity: Narthanavallabeswarar (Nerikkattunayakar); Gnana Shakti (Purikuzhalaambikai).
- Koodalaiyathur, Cuddalore district (Tevaram: Tirukkoodalaiyatrur; at the Vellaru-Manimutharu confluence, the "Dakshina Prayagai"). Coords 11.4158, 79.4772.
- Legend: Brahma prayed to witness Shiva dance, and the Lord danced before him here (hence Narthanavallabeswarar).
