# Avur — Pasupatheeswarar Temple

- **Deity:** Shiva as **Pasupatheeswarar**; goddesses **Mangalanayaki** and **Pankajavalli** (twin sanctums). (Master-list goddess "Mangalanayaki?" — confirmed.)
- **Location:** **Avur (Aavoor)**, ~12 km SW of Kumbakonam; Thanjavur district. ~10.92 N, 79.30 E (approximate).
- **Tradition:** Paadal Petra Sthalam — hymned by **Tirugnana Sambandar**. Kamadhenu freed of Brahma's curse → Pasupatheeswarar.
- **Festivals:** Maha Shivaratri (Masi); Navaratri (Purattasi); Karthikai Mondays; Pradosham.
