# Valeeswarar Temple, Kuranganilmuttam (valeeswarar-kuranganilmuttam)

Region: Paadal Petra Sthalams — Thondai Nadu (Tevaram name: Tirukkuranganilmuttam).
Tradition: Shaivism.

- Deity: Shiva as Valeeswarar, worshipped as a **swayambhu (self-manifest) linga**;
  consort **Irayarvalai Ammai** (spellings vary: Erayaar Valayammai, Iraiyaarvalaiyammai,
  "Valayeswari"). The consort is in a separate sanctum (one source: south-facing).
- One of the 275/276 Paadal Petra Sthalams; praised in the Tevaram, the hymn attributed
  to **Sambandar** (Thirugnana Sambandar) per shrine listings.
- **Sthala vruksham:** Ilandhai (Indian jujube / "Indian apple").
- **Name origin / defining legend:** *kurangu* (monkey) + *anil* (squirrel) + *muttam*
  (crow). Tradition: the Lord was worshipped simultaneously in three animal forms —
  **Vali** the monkey-king of the Ramayana (prayed to be freed of his monkey shape),
  **Indra** as a squirrel, and **Yama** as a crow. Some accounts add Hanuman; relief
  panels of the worship scenes are reported. A Dinamalar version omits Vali (Indra
  cursed; Shiva stays as swayambhu). An ecoheritage page gives an alternate older name
  "Thiruilanathai" with Brahma worshipping — variant, noted.
- **Architecture:** a **Pallava-era rock-cut cave temple**, small in scale. Local/heritage
  pages attribute the excavation to **Mahendravarman I (c. 600–630 CE)**, but Puratattva
  notes **no Pallava inscription has been found** in the temple or village, so the
  attribution is uncertain. Earliest village inscription cited: Rashtrakuta Krishna III;
  village named "Tirukkurananganimuttam" in a Kulothunga III (Chola) inscription.
- **Location:** village in the Palar (Paalaru) valley, **south of Kanchipuram** (sources
  say ~7–12 km), near **Mamandur**, reached via **Thoosi/Dusi** (Thoosi Post). River:
  southern bank of the Palar.
- Coordinates: **town-level approximate ~12.66, 79.66** (Kuranganilmuttam / Dusi-Mamandur
  area, south of Kanchipuram). No exact temple point found in sources — refine against a
  mapping source when possible.
- Access: Kanchipuram (~10–12 km) nearest town / transport hub (rail + bus); Chennai
  nearest major airport. Small village shrine — private vehicle + local directions advised.
