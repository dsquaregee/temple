# Catalog expansion — researched candidates beyond the launch 42

The launch catalog (42 temples, 8 circuits) is complete and deployed. This note
researches the **next tier of candidates** for a post-launch content expansion,
holding to the same selection filter as the original 42 (from
`01-remaining-catalog.md`): *canonical, well-documented, historically or
architecturally significant temples of South India, avoiding sites with access
restrictions or contested sensitivities.* It also acts on circuits the research
synthesis named as first-class but the catalog has not yet built out
(**Pancharama Kshetras**, **Navagraha temples**, further **Divya Desams**).

Facts below were verified against public sources (Wikipedia, ASI, temple/state
tourism) in July 2026; periods are given as documented and hedged where scholarship
is divided. Nothing here is built yet — this is a **research proposal for owner
sign-off** before any content work (Phase-1 gate discipline). Proposed content
`id`s follow the existing kebab convention.

## Cluster A — Pancharama Kshetras (proposed new circuit, 5 temples, Andhra Pradesh)

Five ancient Shiva temples tied by one legend — Kartikeya shattering Tarakasura's
invincible Shivalinga into five fragments, each enshrined so it could not rejoin.
Named as a target circuit in the synthesis. Collectively referred to as the
Pancharamas since at least the 12th c.; historian K. V. Soundara Rajan attributes
the group to **Eastern Chalukya** patronage (Chalukya Bhima I, 888–918 CE), with
most structures dated **10th–11th c.**

| # | id | Deity | Place / district | Notes |
|---|----|-------|------------------|-------|
| 1 | `draksharama-bhimeswara` | Bhimeswara (Shiva) | Draksharamam, Konaseema | Also one of the 18 **Maha Shakti Peethas** (Manikyamba) |
| 2 | `kumararama-bhimeswara` | Bhimeswara (Shiva) | Samalkota, Kakinada | Eastern Chalukya (Chalukya Bhima); tall two-storey vimana |
| 3 | `amararama-amaralingeswara` | Amaralingeswara (Shiva) | Amaravati, Palnadu | On the Krishna; beside the ancient Buddhist site |
| 4 | `ksheerarama-ramalingeswara` | Ramalingeswara (Shiva) | Palakollu, West Godavari | Tall white gopuram |
| 5 | `somarama-someswara` | Someswara (Shiva) | Bhimavaram, West Godavari | Lingam said to change colour with lunar cycle |

*Fit:* fills the named circuit; extends coastal-Andhra / Eastern-Chalukya coverage
the catalog is thin on. All active worship, exterior photography feasible.

## Cluster B — Navagraha temples of the Kaveri delta (proposed new circuit, 9 temples, Tamil Nadu)

The nine planetary temples around Kumbakonam / Mayiladuthurai, present masonry
built under the **Medieval Cholas (7th–11th c.)**. In eight, the presiding deity is
Shiva with a shrine to the planet; Suryanar Kovil alone enshrines the graha (Surya)
as principal. A hugely popular devotional circuit — strong multi-session retention.

| Planet | id | Temple | Place |
|--------|----|--------|-------|
| Surya (Sun) | `suryanar-kovil` | Suryanar Kovil | near Kumbakonam |
| Chandra (Moon) | `thingalur-kailasanathar` | Kailasanathar | Thingalur |
| Angaraka (Mars) | `vaitheeswaran-koil` | Vaitheeswaran Koil | Vaitheeswaran Koil (famed healing shrine) |
| Budha (Mercury) | `thiruvenkadu-swetaranyeswarar` | Swetaranyeswarar | Thiruvenkadu |
| Guru (Jupiter) | `alangudi-apatsahayeswarar` | Apatsahayeswarar | Alangudi |
| Shukra (Venus) | `kanjanur-agneeswarar` | Agneeswarar | Kanjanur |
| Shani (Saturn) | `thirunallar-dharbaranyeswarar` | Dharbaranyeswarar | Thirunallar (Karaikal, Puducherry) |
| Rahu | `thirunageswaram-naganathar` | Naganathar | Thirunageswaram |
| Ketu | `keezhaperumpallam-naganathaswamy` | Naganathaswamy | Keezhaperumpallam |

*Fit:* deepens the Chola Kaveri-delta core (already the catalog's anchor) and gives
the app a second marquee TN circuit after Arupadai Veedu. *Note:* Thirunallar sits
in the Puducherry UT (Karaikal enclave), still within the South-India scope.

## Cluster D — Early Deccan & Kalinga strata (the "oldest" end of the criteria)

The catalog's oldest layer is Pallava/Chalukya 7th–8th c.; these push earlier or into
under-represented dynasties and architectural schools.

| id | Deity | Place / state | Period / dynasty · significance |
|----|-------|---------------|-------------------------------|
| `alampur-navabrahma` | Shiva (9 shrines) + **Jogulamba** | Alampur, Telangana | **7th–9th-c. Badami Chalukya**, early Nagara in the Deccan; Jogulamba is a Maha Shakti Peetha; ASI-protected, oldest complex in the state |
| `srimukhalingam` | Mukhalingeswara (Shiva) | Srikakulam, AP | **Eastern Ganga, 8th c.**, Kalinga style; former Kalinga capital; ASI |
| `srikurmam` | Kurmanatha (Vishnu as Kurma) | Srikakulam, AP | Rare tortoise-avatar shrine; Eastern Ganga; richly inscribed |
| `banavasi-madhukeshwara` | Madhukeshwara (Shiva) | Banavasi, Karnataka | **Kadamba** — Karnataka's earliest kingdom; among the oldest temple towns in the state (later Chalukya/Hoysala layers) |
| `itagi-mahadeva` | Mahadeva (Shiva) | Itagi, Koppal, Karnataka | **Kalyani Chalukya, 1112 CE**; inscribed "Emperor among Temples" (Devalaya Chakravarti); "finest after Halebidu" (Cousens) |
| `lakkundi-kasivisvesvara` | Kasivisvesvara (Shiva) | Lakkundi, Gadag, Karnataka | Fully-developed Kalyani Chalukya style; twin sanctums (Shiva + Surya) |

*Fit:* adds Eastern Chalukya, Eastern Ganga/Kalinga, Kadamba, and Kalyani Chalukya —
four architecturally pivotal schools the launch set lacks, directly serving the
"oldest temples of India" search intent that is the app's top-of-funnel.

## Deliberately deferred (flagged, not recommended near-term)

- **Sabarimala Ayyappa (Kerala)** — nationally significant, but under active
  litigation and seasonal/gender access dispute; the selection filter explicitly
  avoids contested sensitivities. Revisit only with owner direction.
- **Heavily-reconstructed modern rebuilds** (e.g. Yadadri; Murudeshwar's colossus)
  — weaker fit with the "oldest / heritage" framing; lower priority.

## Recommended next batch (if a single expansion is chosen)

Highest value-per-temple and most on-criteria: **two complete new circuits plus the
early-strata trio.**

1. **Pancharama Kshetras** (5) — clean circuit, new region (coastal Andhra / Eastern Chalukya).
2. **Navagraha temples** (9) — clean circuit, deepens the Chola delta; high retention.
3. **Alampur Navabrahma, Srimukhalingam, Itagi Mahadeva** (3) — the oldest/most
   architecturally pivotal singletons (Badami Chalukya Deccan-Nagara, Eastern
   Ganga/Kalinga, Kalyani Chalukya).

That is **17 temples → catalog 59, circuits 10**. Clusters C and E remain a ready
backlog for the batch after.
