# Temple photo/video audit (Wikimedia Commons)

Two-stage selection, July 2026:

1. **Automatic quality gate** (gen-video.mjs): JPEG/PNG, ≥ 2 MP, landscape,
   CC/PD license. A hero image needs ≥ 1 qualifying photo; a slideshow video
   needs ≥ 3 (fewer → hero only, no video). All 10 v1 temples pass with 6
   photos each. The machine-generated per-run status lives in
   `photo-audit-auto.md` (regenerated by the script; not hand-edited).
2. **Editorial audit** (this document): every hero photo and candidate pool
   reviewed by eye. The resolution-ranked search favored things it cannot
   see — stereo-pair scans rendered as side-by-side duplicates, archival
   black-and-white photos, a burned-in camera datestamp, construction
   clutter, and in one case a photo of a *different* Kanchipuram temple
   (Kamakshi Ambal). Seven temples were re-curated with exact Commons titles
   (the `CURATED` map in `packages/content/scripts/gen-video.mjs`), then
   heroes and videos were regenerated.

## What curation removed

- **Stereo-pair scans** (image duplicated side by side): were the top-ranked
  "photos" for airavatesvara and jambukeswarar and most of the
  arunachaleswarar pool — the highest-megapixel files in each batch.
- **Archival B&W photography** (1961 street scene, ekambareswarar) — fine
  history, wrong register for a devotional hero image.
- **Burned-in datestamp** (ramanathaswamy-rameswaram gopuram).
- **Construction clutter** (arunachaleswarar courtyard with barriers and gas
  cylinders).
- **Wrong temple**: a Kamakshi Ambal Temple gateway surfaced under the
  ekambareswarar search; every curated title was verified to name the right
  temple.

## Known acceptable imperfections

- The ekambareswarar hero has the photographer's faint watermark (CC BY-SA
  attribution is also carried in `hero.credit` / `video.credit`).
- Photos are what Commons offers: mixed lighting styles across temples.
  Commissioned photography can replace any of this later — same bucket
  paths, no code changes.

## Catalog-parity pass (42 temples, 2026-07)

The catalog grew from 10 to 42 temples. A dry-run (`VIDEO_DRYRUN=1`, no cloud
credentials) audited Commons coverage for all 42: **41 have enough qualifying
photos for a hero + video; 1 is hero-only** (Gokarna). Three temples that the
automatic gate had skipped for too few photos were re-curated by eye, exactly
as the first ten were:

| Temple | Outcome | Hero (reviewed by eye) |
|---|---|---|
| thiruttani-murugan | ✍️ curated (3) | Rajagopuram seen from the approach road (only clean landscape); two tower portraits fill the video |
| pazhamudircholai-murugan | ✍️ curated (3) | `Solaimalai.jpg` — the gopuram against its wooded hill |
| gokarna-mahabaleshwara | ✍️ curated (1) | `Mahabaleshwara Temple.JPG` — stone shikhara over the Konkan tiled roof; **hero only** |

What the resolution-ranked search surfaced for these three (and why each was
rejected): stereo-pair "3D" scans of Gokarna; a **wrong temple** (the
Mahabaleshwara of Chamundi Hills, Mysuru); a blurry temple-interior with a
"Foreigners Not Allowed" sign; an ear-piercing ceremony (Thiruttani); roosting
bats and the **Shrungagiri Shanmukha temple in Bangalore** (Pazhamudircholai).

**Hero/video decoupling.** The gate now separates the two media: a single
dignified photo yields a hero image even when there aren't enough for a
slideshow, and the hero is written to every locale doc regardless of narration
audio (previously a locale with no audio silently got no hero). Gokarna is the
first temple to use the hero-only path; commissioned photography can add the
rest later with no code change.

**Still automatic (not yet eye-reviewed).** The other 29 new temples pass the
automatic gate but have **not** had the per-image editorial review the 13
curated temples received. A few auto-picks look suspect from their metadata
alone (e.g. `padmanabhaswamy` leads with a 245 MP file — almost certainly a
panorama/stereo stitch) and should be spot-checked before, or curated after,
the first credentialed video run.
